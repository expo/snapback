## Carrier and derivation

The carrier is `Constraint::Capabilities(BTreeSet<String>)`, encoded once in the lexicographically first table's existing `constraints` array, for example `{"Capabilities":["timeline"]}`. This generalizes F2's `Constraint::Recorded` carrier rather than adding a separate top-level field an older schema decoder could ignore. Published 0.2.33 strictly parses this enum and rejects `Capabilities`: it knows only `Unique`, `Cap`, `AppendOnly`, `Immutable`, `Text`, and `FinalExistsOnInsert`. Both its device `Backend` decode and its persisted engine-schema decode take that path.

[The shared codec](../crates/snapback4-core/src/schema/capabilities.rs) derives the required set at **serialization**, covering compiled backends, raw Rust schemas, schema hashes, stored generations and kept device backends. The single `CAPABILITIES` registry contains:

- `recorded-attribution`: any table has nonempty `recorded` fields.
- `timeline`: the schema has a nonempty `timelines` list.

**F1 adds one registry tuple here:** `("maintained-column", |s| <its schema predicate>)`. The registry is also the decoder's allowlist; no separate capability enum, carrier or admission-site update is needed. F1 still owns its normal schema representation changes, including the private wire field list if it adds a top-level `Schema` field.

The decoder rejects unknown names, missing requirements, an empty carrier, and duplicate carriers with `E_SCHEMA_CAPABILITY`. It removes the carrier after validation, so this wire metadata does not become a physical constraint or change successor classification. The private remote serde derive preserves the existing streaming codec and field encoding; no content-buffer replay or JSON tree is introduced. Input, stored-schema, initial-install, adoption and kept-backend error wrappers preserve the stable code. The sync card and `SERVER.md` explain the device upgrade requirement; `why E_SCHEMA_CAPABILITY` routes to its dedicated sync paragraph.

No required capability means no carrier and the existing serialized bytes. The old and new compilers' complete capability-free `/schema` response texts compare byte for byte in the published-package test. The conformance corpus is unchanged.

Orientation used `snapback4/AGENTS.md`, `NOT-DOING.md`, `JOURNAL.md`, `rules/RULES.md`, LLP 3000 §4/§7, LLP 2000.000 §5.1/§7.3, LLP 3000.007.003 §3.2–§3.3, and the current sync/deployment cards. The constraints retained here are the fence around Snapback 4, unchanged corpus serialization, one implementation across hosts, and the measured WASM ratchet. No new design decision or LLP is proposed.
