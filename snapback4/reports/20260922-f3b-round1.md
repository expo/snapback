## General device read-path gap for a supervisor ticket

`core/src/engine/read.rs::trust_synced_read` treats rows in synced tables as
server-authorized partition images. An ordinary predicted write that the writer
cannot read is stored in that same table with pending:true. Point reads, scans,
merges and existence probes can therefore expose a foreign private provisional
row to its predicting principal and report complete:true until settlement or
refusal restores the confirmed base. This predates declared timelines. This lane
prevents timeline-owned foreign outputs from entering the overlay, but does not
change general prediction/read authorization. Ticket the general distinction
between locally predicted rows and server-delivered rows, with the same
pre-settlement read/probe coverage on memory/WASM and native SQLite.
