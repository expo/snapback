## Retired init surface

`init --recipe feeds` refuses before filesystem work with exit status 2 and
`E_RECIPE_RETIRED`. The same refusal covers `--prefix`, `--authors`, `--edges`
and all four `--public-*` bridge mappings, including orphaned recipe options.
The refusal prints a timeline declaration, `size authors 1008, edges 5921`, the
empty-data requirement and `snapback4 guide feeds`.

The installer, bridge installation, recipe-only preflight and unused generated
`src/following.ts` template were removed. Ordinary default, online, Expo and web
init paths preserve their behavior. Unknown recipe names keep the ordinary usage
refusal. `E_RECIPE_BRIDGE` and `E_RECIPE_CAPS` had no remaining emitters and were
removed from both the code registry and diagnostic routing; the retained recipe's
runtime FEED codes remain documented. Sparse-filter and wide-merge diagnostics
now recommend the declaration rather than the retired command.

capacity. The witness follows 500 authors, observes pushed backfill, verifies no
promotion at 50 followers and promotion at 51, then checks shared posts, idempotent
reposting, distinct event identity, joined cards, complete 20-row pagination,
ordering, no gaps/duplicates, exclusion of unfollowed originals, removal with a

promotion after follower loss, a later follow and unrepost.

Init tests assert the retirement code/rewrite across empty and existing default,
Expo, online and web apps, and compare all existing file bytes. All ordinary
starters still compile. Recipe compatibility tests now construct existing-module
fixtures through the retained library rather than invoking a retired installer.
