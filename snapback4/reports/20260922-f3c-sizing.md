## Contract and implementation

The spelling is exactly the requested `size authors 1008, edges 5921`, placed
after the edges mapping. Either key may be omitted, and either order works.
Omitted keys resolve to 1000 authors and 100000 edges. Parsing delegates all
numeric and coupling validation to `FollowingCaps::parse`, including the
positive-u32 rule, author × 32 overflow bound, edges ≤ authors × 1000, and
route capacity for a full follower group plus its author. Malformed clauses
receive `E_TIMELINE_SIZE`, a rewrite and `guide: feeds`.

Expansion calls `following_sized`. Admission requires the mapped follows
table's global cap to equal the declared edges; endpoint caps remain 1000.
The resolved author and edge caps are persisted in timeline metadata and
participate in semantic redefinition checks. Default fields are omitted from
serialization and defaulted on read, preserving the earlier default timeline
encoding. No existing IR or value encoding changed.

Backfill visits clamp(authors, 200, 865) source events, including shared ones
in its membership count. It retains the 1000-event source scan, per-actor cap,
and the recipe's 159-write promotion reserve. The application's profiles cap
remains independent. A lower profile cap emits `N_TIMELINE_PROFILE_CAP` with
its reason and rewrite. Both new codes are registered in `guide::CODES`.
Activation scans at most authors + 1 profiles and refuses atomically above the
bound. A populated timeline refuses either size change with
`E_TIMELINE_REDEFINE`; its rewrite says the first scope sizes the timeline
before data exists. Empty timelines can be resized.

`check --cost` prints authors, edges, pulls and count rows, the backfill bound,
activation bound, derived writes and sized stream bounds. The feeds/timelines
card and executable language card show the Clique-sized declaration.

Read-only orientation used LLP 3000, LLP 2000.000 and
LLP 3000.007.003 §3.3, plus the Snapback 4 command sheet, NOT-DOING,
journal tail, adopted rules and current feeds/timelines cards. The user’s
commission extends the default-only sizing in the existing implementation;
no design was substituted and no LLP hand-off is needed.

--edges 5921` with all four public bridge mappings, then verifies the exact
installed recipe and bridge bytes against the harness inputs.

1008-author/5921-edge pair:

- Posts, reposts, duplicate reposts, removals, refollow, purge, profile deletion
  and re-registration, with paginated event and card reads.

  copies exactly 765 pushed events; pagination and meter cuts match.
- Caller pull-slot refusal, promotion pull-slot refusal, atomic rollback,
  permanent promotion and the saturated author's own-copy fallback.
- A missing private pull count in restored history refuses and rolls back.
- Self-follow and missing-profile refusals match.
- Time ties, event-id ordering, tombstone filtering and page filling match.
- The explicitly documented tombstoned repost-id exception remains isolated.

New traces register authors 1001–1008, compare state after each, and verify
1009 refuses identically without a profile or mode residue; visit exactly
200 and 865 events at authors 50 and 1008; prove the unchanged 1000-event
per-actor source cap; and create exactly 5921 edges through ordinary
mutations before the next edge refuses identically. The edge trace's ring has
at most 30 edges per endpoint, so it reaches the global cap first.

The two existing differences remain the only exceptions: the manual recipe's
extra follow-edge write, and a tombstoned caller-selected repost event id.
Successful and refused traces retain write accounting and rollback assertions.
The harness compares edge sets to avoid quadratic row comparisons without
weakening its measurement of the duplicate edge's writes.

Additional checks cover parser boundary parity, defaults and reversed key
order, resolved table caps, application profile-cap notes, mismatched edge
caps, activation at and above authors, event-free activation, populated
