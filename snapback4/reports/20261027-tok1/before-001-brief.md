Jukebox Restorations
A jukebox workshop restores vintage coin-operated jukeboxes, and the shop foreman opens each restoration with photos of the cabinet, record changer and lighting plus the quoted price. The owner must accept the quote within 30 days or the jukebox is returned, and a jukebox restored but not collected within 45 days of its ready notice starts a storage charge. Owners see only their own jukeboxes and quotes, technicians see the live shop board as jukeboxes move from teardown to changer rebuild, rewiring and cabinet polish, and only the master technician can approve replacing an original selector mechanism, recording the owner's written consent.
