Bandstand Restorations
A heritage carpentry firm restores Victorian park bandstands, and the project clerk opens each project with photos of the roof, columns and railings plus the quoted price. The town must accept the quote within 60 days or the estimate lapses, and the bandstand must be inspected within 12 months after reopening, with the project flagged until the inspection is recorded. Towns see only their own projects and inspections, carpenters see the live yard board as bandstands move from dismantled to repaired, painted and reopened, and only the master restorer can approve replacing an original cast column, recording the heritage officer's approval.
