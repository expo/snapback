Drone Pilot Certifications
A flight school certifies commercial drone pilots, and the registrar logs each exam with a scan of the candidate's photo identification and the exam fee. A candidate who fails may retest within 30 days at half price, and a certificate expires two years after it is issued. Candidates see only their own exams and certificates, instructors see the live field board as exams move from booked to written, flight test and emergency drill scored, and only the school manager can approve a retest beyond the second attempt, recording the reason and the instructor's recommendation.
