Exotic Bird Transport
An avian transport company moves parrots and exotic birds to new homes, and the coordinator opens each move with a scan of the bird's health certificate and the quoted price. Handlers see each move's bird, cage size, route and feeding notes, but not the owner's card, balance or the price on the same move, which only the coordinator and the company owner see. Owners see only their own birds and moves, the move board updates live as birds move from booked to caged, in transit and delivered, and a bird cannot travel on a health certificate older than ten days. Only the company owner can write off a balance, with the reason recorded.
