## Decisions

- Use explicit handler custody rather than an ambient upload continuation window. A global continuation flag could falsely attribute an unrelated promise reaction.
- Keep production/default schedule coalescing and make all-boundary admission explicit. Jobs run at final advanced now; historical logic uses periodStart/periodEnd. Actions receive deadlines from current admission, not historical windows.
- Preserve sweep semantics; describe advisory program-rule refusals and historical query/persona/code exemptions accurately. Explicit cases assert final authorized state.
- `qualify-linux --consumer-only` is used alongside the separately requested full five-crate and affected-client tests. The full qualifier internally runs scripts/test.sh, which this workstream was explicitly told never to run. Consumer evidence is not a publication receipt.
