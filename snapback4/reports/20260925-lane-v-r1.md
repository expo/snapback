## Decisions, limits and validation

Overflow remains per-row once a bucket fills, including after later deletions;
this avoids a growing directory, overflow scan or compaction during a mutation.
Oversized individual rows cost their own size plus a capped primary bucket.
The old unbounded format requires one full-load migration, which can inspect the
old bucket's full size; later single-row saves cannot perform that migration.
Legacy per-row stores are not rewritten. Old-version writers opening new packed
stores remain outside the qualified rollback path, as in round 0.

Authority binding is private to `createApp`; raw low-level clients without that
session owner and legacy unstamped journals retain their prior behavior. New
stamped records are never inferred into another epoch. Ordinary token rotation
within the same epoch/principal remains valid. A recovered record cancelled for
stale authority never enters the outbox; it therefore has no terminal server
receipt. The live caller receives `E_AUTH`.

No API, refusal code, server meter, page size or Wasm budget was added or raised.
No larger pages or streaming apply; packing remains the measured optimization.
No outstanding reproducible failures of these fixes are known. The limitations
above are retained explicitly rather than represented as additional coverage.

The baseline tail was checked: its earlier `cargo test` failure is not attributed
to this change. During targeted development, two initial assertions were wrong
(the raw closed client's query is `E_CLOSED`, and the migration fixture initially
used nonmatching hashes); those tests were corrected. The forced overflow abort
also exposed an already-aborted transaction being aborted again, which is fixed.
The first authority test fixture also reused a bearer that the tested logout
correctly retired; each race now uses a fresh guest session. An initial
