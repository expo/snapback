# P1-CE — Postgres change evidence, 2026-10-23

Base: `46c6cdd056`, branch `sb4-pg/p1-ce`. Local commits only; no push.

The Postgres tail now records the same shared net-image footprint as SQLite.
The existing commit CTE inserts evidence and deletes sequences `<= seq - 1000`;
both depend on the successful commit row. These are two additional modifying
CTE clauses and two parameters inside the existing tail command. There is no
additional prepare, exchange, writer transaction or hot-path DDL. The recorder
resets per member, and savepoints checkpoint it alongside tail/head state.
Rollback widens conservatively, exactly as SQLite already does. This applies
settled semantics; no additional design decision was needed.

`/changes` retains its single read transaction. The SDK stream now obtains
partition images/head, pending receipt acknowledgements and preservation/change
classification through `Engine::certified_stream_at`, in one read transaction
on both stores. Missing, pruned, incomplete, malformed, oversized and explicitly
unknown evidence certifies neither preservation nor revocation. It remains
`preserved: null`; a known empty list retains its distinct revocation meaning.
Doctor and the release guide now admit certified changes and ordinary sync
within the existing Postgres profile. No other named refusal was removed.

## Snapshot and conformance witnesses

- The actual `/changes` check pauses after reading head 1. A second adapter
  connection completes an ownership transfer at sequence 2 before the first
  reads evidence. The answer remains head 1/preserved notes; the next snapshot
  sees head 2/revocation.
- The actual certified SDK stream similarly holds its snapshot while a second
  connection commits an ownership transfer. Its page, receipts and evidence
  stay at sequence 1. A subsequent stream sees revocation at sequence 2.
- Reverse-order reads obtain evidence at head 2, commit sequence 3 on another
  connection, then read head 2 again and cannot see evidence for sequence 3.
  These witnesses await completed real adapter commits inside the held snapshot;
  they do not rely on scheduler timing or harness SQL to manufacture a race.
- All 12 change-evidence tests pass. Shared writer cases use fixed expected
  footprints for insert, ordinary update, owner update and delete, including
  JSON-escaped NUL data, admission refusals, uncovered writes, rollback and
  recovered savepoints. Pruning retains sequence 1 at exactly 1,000 and removes
  it at 1,001; pruning and writes roll back together. Existing bounded reader,
  explicit-unknown, signed-boundary, additive-column and attach-shape contracts
  pass on the locale cluster. Member/tail failure and terminal NUL-bind cases
  retain their atomicity assertions.
