### Oracle evidence correction

The recipe does **not** universally admit tombstoned repost-id reuse. Its private inbox has `unique byReaderEvent: readerId, eventId`, so C's surviving copy causes `E_RULE` even with a fresh physical copy id. The real-engine test first exposed this at the recipe assertion (meter: 3 attempted writes). With C's already-hidden copy absent, the recipe commits; F3 refuses with `FEED_EVENT_REMOVED` and rolls back. The separate exception test pins that admitted case. The F3 paired HTTP test pins the same refusal in both worlds. This narrows the ruling's explanation without changing its requested behavior or relaxing the general oracle.
