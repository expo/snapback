## Alternatives and remaining scope

The accepted alternatives and counterexamples in LLP 3000.025 §4 remain:

- Unrestricted scans after effects: a blind write transfers/expires a hidden
  row; retained ordinal 1 versus 2 gives E_BOUND versus STOP.
- A live cap alone: cap 4 permits 12 physical rows across expiry waves; a
  physical cap would block inserts behind dead rows and still needs fixed
  worst-case work reservation.
- Sweep inside the mutation: 10,000 deletes exceed the write ceiling and
  rollback restores the backlog; free work or separate commits break the
  meter or atomicity.
- Expiry-first access: it loses ordinal ordering; ordinal-first still walks
  dead rows. A bounded summary index remains a separate unbuilt design.
- Free expired-row traversal: ten million dead candidates make a one-row
  result unbounded.
- Query-selected ids as absence proof: incomplete pages, horizons or changed
  authority can omit a live blocker.
- Prediction justified by a cap: device-only unswept backlog can refuse
  before enqueue even after the server has swept.
- Dependency/evaluation-order analysis: protecting only explicit rule tables
  misses the page table, hidden gates, transitive maintenance and nested
  effects. Whole initial source statements remain the sufficient rule.

No new latency claim or prototype is needed. This round measures correctness,
regression sensitivity, tests and the rebuilt device artifact. Historical
10,000-row backlog timings remain in LLP §5. No ER design question remains;
DC owns delivery queue recovery, and the supervisor owns landing. No push or
