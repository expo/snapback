## Alternatives and scope

The accepted design and its rejected options are unchanged (LLP 3000.025 §4):

- Unrestricted uniform scans: a blind write can transfer/expire a hidden row;
  retained ordinal 1 versus 2 produces E_BOUND versus STOP.
- Existing live cap alone: cap 4 permits 12 unswept physical rows across three
  expiry waves. A new physical cap would block inserts behind dead rows and
  still need population-independent work reservation.
- Sweep inside the mutation: 10,000 deletions exceed the write ceiling;
  rollback restores the backlog. Free deletion or separate commits break the
  meter or atomicity.
- Expiry-first index: ordinal ordering is lost; ordinal-first still traverses
  the backlog. A bounded summary index remains a separate unbuilt design.
- Free expired-row traversal: ten million expired candidates make a one-row
  result unbounded.
- Query-selected ids as absence proof: incomplete pages, horizons or changed
  authority can omit a live blocker.
- Prediction from a live cap: a device-only unswept backlog can refuse before
  enqueue even after the server has swept.
- Dependency closure/evaluation-order analysis: must cover the page table,
  hidden gates, transitive maintenance, expressions and repeated filters.
  Checking whole initial source statements is the accepted sufficient rule.
