## Alternatives and remaining scope

The accepted alternatives and counterexamples in LLP 3000.025 §4 remain:

- Unrestricted scans after effects: blind transfer/expiry of a hidden row
  distinguishes retained ordinal 1 from 2 through E_BOUND versus STOP.
- A live cap alone: cap 4 leaves 12 physical rows across expiry waves; a
  physical cap blocks inserts behind dead rows and still needs fixed work
  reservation to avoid the same near-ceiling oracle.
- Sweep inside the mutation: 10,000 deletes exceed the write ceiling;
  rollback restores the backlog. Unmetered work or separate commits violate
  the meter or atomicity.
- Expiry-first access: loses ordinal ordering; ordinal-first still traverses
  expired candidates. A bounded summary index is a separate unbuilt design.
- Free expired-row traversal: ten million dead candidates make a one-row
  result unbounded.
- Query-selected ids as absence proof: incomplete pages, device horizons or
  changed authority can omit a live blocker.
- Prediction justified by a cap: device-only unswept backlog can refuse
  before enqueue even after the server swept.
- Tracking only explicit dependencies: misses the page table, hidden gates,
  transitive maintenance and nested effects; the initial whole source
  statement rule remains the sufficient condition.
