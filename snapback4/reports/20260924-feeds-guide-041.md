## Documented forms

No language form was added. The compiled examples teach these existing forms:

- Global: `view activity:` combines the post and repost projections;
  `return activity last 20 by .at after c { *, card: posts[.post] }` reads it.
- Following: `view following: activity where .actor = viewer or exists follows[viewer, .actor]`;
  `return following last 20 by .at after c` reads the caller's inbox.
- Trending: `likeCount: count likes` maintains the count;
  `return posts where .at > now - 1d and .at <= now and not .removed last 10 by .likeCount, .at after c`
  reads the bounded ranking.
- Compatibility, only at the end: `maintain oldFeed: timeline` with mapped
  sources and `return oldFeed[viewer] after c last 20`.

## Tests and measurements

Two new tests in the `guide` target:

- `feeds::builder_path_precedes_internals_and_older_spellings` pins the section
  order, keeps implementation terminology below “How it works”, and refuses
  legacy declaration/recipe/bridge/promotion advice on the current path.
- `feeds::published_following_matches_the_builder_contract` runs the exact
  published Following block. With 65 original events it proves own events are
  immediately visible and ordered newest first, a follow installs 50 immediately
  and reports incomplete, background work completes the backfill, later follower
  delivery is asynchronous, removal hides before cleanup, and unfollow hides on
  the next read. It also pins that pending new-event delivery alone does not
  change the completeness of delivered contents.

The existing Trending oracle, legacy Clique-size execution, atomic rollback,
bridge controls, diagnostic discovery, compile checks and source census remain.
The text expectations changed only for intentionally rewritten prose or the
renamed compatibility heading.
