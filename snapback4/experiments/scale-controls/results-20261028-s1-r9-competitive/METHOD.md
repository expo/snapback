The Part 1 setup repair applies to every arm: after the existing native/auth probe, W4 primes 32 distinct counters sequentially; exact primes the shared counter through 32 identities; hot1 primes both sets, 64 writes. Every write goes through that system’s own authenticated mutation API, with an exact setup counter delta. These operations precede the quiet/timing/counter window. Reads receive no mutation primer. This is a declared prewarmed workload, not a cold-start product fix.

Every measurement starts a fresh service, uses 10 s warm-up plus 20 s measurement and open intended-arrival pacing, 32 writers or 128 readers, cap 32. PG uses logical CPUs `0-2,6-8`, app/proxy `3-4,9-10` and generator `5,11`. Link A adds 250 µs per direction; B adds 500 µs. RTT and live thread affinity are witnessed before/after each cell. Before each cohort the host must have ten consecutive seconds with one-minute load below 1.5 and no unrelated process above 0.3 core. A one-second census runs throughout the trial. Aggregate unrelated CPU above one core for more than two cumulative seconds makes the attempt contaminated; it is retained, excluded and rerun once. A second contaminated attempt stays ineligible. Clean product failures are never replaced.

Writes require intended-arrival p99 ≤100 ms, reads ≤50 ms, measured errors ≤0.1%, and writes require the durable counter delta to equal every ACK including warm-up. Unsent/expired arrivals stay in the histograms and error denominator. Every repetition must pass. An owner exit, terminal latch, drain failure or counter mismatch fails the repetition. No durable state is repaired to convert a failure into a pass.

Capacity blocks predeclare five repetitions before timing. Arm order starts at a deterministic block-hash offset and rotates by one arm per repetition; each role’s required rungs are visited in slots. Adaptive blocks complete each qualifying/first-failing bracket. Matched candidate/main controls accompany advocate bracket completion. All clean candidate observations at a capacity rate remain effective, including these additional controls. An unresolved ineligible selected repetition makes that rate inconclusive, even if its initial five-repetition block passed; excluded attempts are retained and cannot be promoted into capacity evidence. The sole exception is the RFC’s pinned-competitor rule: after a failed pin, an asserted S1-C binary/image/settings/affinity check and one full five-repetition rerun are allowed; only the original pin is superseded, and both results are published. A rerun is not allowed again.

Convex is run at its pin, the next rung and enough additional rungs to complete its bracket. Once a completed five-repetition rung fails below a candidate deciding rung, implausible higher points are skipped with an explicit reason. Every repetition must pass, so one clean miss disqualifies that rung. W4/A has no Convex pinned pass, so it is tested directly at the candidate deciding rungs; a failure there is a direct comparison bound, not a claim that Convex has zero global capacity. A lower candidate frontier triggers another direct check if needed.

CPU/WAL accounting

A 100 ms PID/birth sampler follows every private PG family, including background workers and observed exiting backends, plus app and proxy. Whole-cohort CPU differences divide by all ACKs including warm-up. The historical measured-window CPU estimate interpolates generator launch +100 ms scheduler lead +10 s warm-up over 20 s and divides by successful measured-arrival ACKs, including drain completions. The frozen generator lacks an exact native measurement-start timestamp, so the latter has launch/interpolation/tick uncertainty. WAL/sync snapshots span warm-up, measurement, drain and surrounding ordinary maintenance and divide by all ACKs; their outer edges differ slightly from CPU sampling and are reported. PG statistics publication can lag. Failed-cohort per-ACK costs include refusal/void work and cannot be called the intrinsic cost of successful writes. Matched-load R9/main comparisons use the same rate, identities and accounting method. Components and nested diagnostic clocks are not added together as residence time.

The matched cost ledger joins native arms by block, repetition, phase, cell and rate, and requires both selected attempts to be eligible. Each metric uses the identical numeric pair subset for both arm medians and reports its pair count; the ratio divides those paired medians. Clean product failures remain included. Per-arm summaries separately retain all selected observations, including an ineligible attempt that cannot enter a cost pair.

One-CPU information, if run after the core campaign, changes only each owned private PG family to logical CPU 0 and an aggregate `cpu.max` quota of `100000 100000`. The postmaster, existing children and later inherited children are covered; app/proxy/generator placement remains fixed. CPU statistics and pressure are snapshotted before/after each trial. These two-cell observations do not qualify the RFC’s all-cell one-CPU gate or an “every scenario” claim.
