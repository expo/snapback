# E_QUARRY_IMMUTABLE

E_QUARRY_IMMUTABLE: An immutable patch provably changes a stored value or supplies a fresh clock/id. Omit it from the patch; insert it when creating the row. The static refusal requires a lifetime-free ordinary table without read-through and proven readability: a public/allow read rule, a system job, or conflict-key pins that establish the private read rule. Without that proof, private targets receive N_IMMUTABLE_PATCH because hidden immutable fields may be ignored. A literal or constant expression differing from a pinned key or the exact row just inserted is refused. Distinct fresh id captures differ too. A fresh clock on an immutable time column cannot preserve its original timestamp on later conflicts. An upsert with a proven fresh unused unique identity is insert-only and stays quiet. Exact target keys, current exact snapshots and proven equal values from an unconditional insert stay quiet. Intervening writes or uncertain control flow prevent snapshot proof; uncertainty receives N_IMMUTABLE_PATCH instead of a refusal. Source writers elsewhere do not constrain historical, seeded or adopted values. External IR keeps the established privacy exception: an unreadable target ignores immutable patch fields without comparing hidden values. guide writes.
