# E_REFERENCE_ACTION

E_REFERENCE_ACTION means an `on delete` declaration the column cannot carry. `on delete` belongs on a reference column (its type names a table) between two ordinary durable tables: ephemeral tables, engine-maintained views and receipt or timeline bookkeeping keep their own lifetimes. `cascade` and `set null` write another table's rows, so their target may not be append only, hold field-owned (`written by`) columns, belong to a timeline, or have writes without a fixed price; a cascade may not reach its own table again (a cycle, including a self-reference). `set null` needs an optional reference (`?`) that carries no authority anywhere: no rule of any table reads it, no sync audience or horizon uses it, and it is not immutable, field-owned, a sequence parent or the root of a recorded or maintained path — clearing such a column would change who may read or write a row, which the deleter was never authorized to do. `keep` is refused on `read through` (a read-through child lives within its parent), and `cascade` on `read through` needs a child that never expires. Choose `cascade`, `restrict` or `keep` instead, as the message's rewrite shows. guide writes.
