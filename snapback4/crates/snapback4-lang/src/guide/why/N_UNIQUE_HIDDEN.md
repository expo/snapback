# N_UNIQUE_HIDDEN

N_UNIQUE_HIDDEN: A private unique conflict cannot disclose its named code.

A unique declaration's else CODE applies to readable conflicts. An unreadable conflict keeps generic E_RULE (or an undisclosed target miss), without a hidden key, row or hiding clause. This source-only note infers no stored conflict. Review a minimal readable key projection or operator-mediated claim when the product needs availability checks; do not widen private rows to get the named code. Guide rules.

A global unique over owner-scoped rows also asks whether another owner may infer a hidden claim. Include the owner in the unique key when uniqueness is per owner, or expose a minimal availability projection when the claim is deliberately global.
