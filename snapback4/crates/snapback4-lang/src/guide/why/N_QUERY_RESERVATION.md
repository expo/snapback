# N_QUERY_RESERVATION

N_QUERY_RESERVATION: A bounded query path cannot fit its physical authorization reservation.

Reduce real product caps or split independent reads. Mutually exclusive returning branches share their maximum; sequential work adds. Loops and expression blocks conservatively sum static occurrences, and an occurrence cannot spend its reservation twice. The unchanged physical allowance is 8,192; an oversized reservation gives empty partial pages, never proved absence. Guide reads.
