## Native crypto calibration

The worker used `node:crypto.scryptSync` with no third-party package and
explicit parameters `{ N: 16384, r: 8, p: 1, maxmem: 67108864 }`, a 16-byte
salt, and a 32-byte result. Five wall samples were:

`28.910708`, `26.409542`, `26.174584`, `25.802709`, and `25.881834` ms.

The median was **26.174584 ms**. Against LLP 0249 Motivation's cited (not
re-measured) embedded-engine range of 8,000–11,000 ms, that is
**305.640×–420.255×** faster. The digest prefix was
`d7590aca2c9801cf`.

There is a charter/RFC mismatch worth preserving: LLP 0227 pins argon2id v1
(`19,456 KiB`, 2 iterations, parallelism 1, 16-byte salt, 32-byte tag); it does
not pin a first-party scrypt hashing policy. The historical Clique-P2 gateway
at commit `b4d903612` called `scryptSync(password, salt, 32)`, which selects
Node's defaults. The spike made those historical defaults explicit rather than
mislabeling LLP 0227's accepted argon2 policy or its import ceilings as scrypt
parameters.

## Interpretation and remaining boundary

The results support proceeding with design work: a small real ESM package can
fit the proposed closure; native Node crypto removes the embedded-engine
performance pathology; registry/tar inspection can refuse a native package
before lifecycle execution; and the portable scanner boundary remains closed.

They do **not** support minting or advertising the capability. The required
launcher/channel attestation, versioned destination classifier and per-attempt
egress enforcement, OS sandbox battery, effects vocabulary, and worker custody
integration are the five concrete follow-ups filed with this spike.
