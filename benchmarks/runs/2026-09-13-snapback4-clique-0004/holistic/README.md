# Same-family holistic scores

Received scores: Astra 84, Grok 70, Muse 80; mean 78. All three score sessions
exited zero. These are own-family assessments of the builder narratives, not
independent assertion grades. The [complete score objects](./all.json) and
[category table](./summary.md) are verbatim frozen-aggregator outputs.

`COPIED_FILES.json` binds every copied artifact and the external raw transcripts
by SHA-256. Raw transcripts stay outside the repository. Astra and Muse final
answers are copied verbatim; Grok's received fenced answer is retained in
`all.json` and its external raw output, which was the aggregator's recorded
source. Integer scores, identities, all 13 entries, and evidence notes were
validated before aggregation. Muse's capture additionally proves that the
unique public terminal answer, rather than a prompt example, supplied its score.

The scorer prompts and commands were unchanged. Muse's builder had completed
public terminal answers but its background-session runtime kept issuing empty
continuations. Its actual builder exit130 is preserved; a separately retained,
hash-bound external predicate exception allowed the score to run. This does
not bless nonzero scorer or grader exits. The score's own actual exit is zero.

Technical attribution remains separate from the score. Astra charged pending
metadata behavior that the root pending-shape audit identifies as a documented
raw-row boundary. Grok charged Hermes failure that the root client audit traces
statically to the app's Expo/Babel mismatch. Received scores are not rewritten.

The earlier Snapback2 own-family scores were 64, 57, 78 (mean 66.33). The new
mean 78 is an N=1 calibration comparison, not a controlled improvement estimate:
the instrument revisions and lane outcomes differ, and those earlier scorers
considered three backends per family rather than one Snapback4 lane.
