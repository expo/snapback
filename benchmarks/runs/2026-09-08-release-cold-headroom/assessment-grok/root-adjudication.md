confirms these distinctions:

  ranking/zero-day/age checks do not substantiate their mapped clauses. The

  frozen group-receipt check nor the supplemental assessor drives. Keep the

  server expiry only. It performs no offline transition or post-expiry search;
  its claimed phrase-search refusal is not demonstrated in that script/output.

  fresh fallback is needed; the original `completed` field only describes the

  scrolling or absence of gaps.
