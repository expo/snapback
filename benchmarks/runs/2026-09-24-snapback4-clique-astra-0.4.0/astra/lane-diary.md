### Post-green scale correction

The original full replay matched its invariant manifest, but the extra probes exposed work that the small fixtures hid: inbox unread counting exceeded the examined-row meter in the busy chat, and materialized Following made sequential celebrity acknowledgements fail the 3× rule. I replaced unread scanning with a maintained receipt count, marking tombstoned receipts invisible. Expiry remains owned by the reference/expiry machinery and is being independently rechecked. I chose read-time Following filtering to remove write fan-out. The compiler warned honestly about sparse filtering; the full-scale probes now explicitly require a full 20-row page for the named readers.

A populated timeline could not be removed in place: `E_TIMELINE_REDEFINE`. Rather than reinterpret that as inability to express the query, I preserved both previous stores, rebuilt fresh starter state, and repeated the entire scale replay plus the ordered regression checks. This is meaningful late work and is reported separately from historical first-green timestamps. It made the comparison less uniformly positive: the convenient materialized feed path did not automatically meet the workload's acknowledgement ratio, and the scale-suitable choice involved a different query plan and a fresh-store transition.

The wide-row read-time feed then hit a heap cut at the 50% checkpoint. A compact public event projection, maintained atomically by post/repost mutations and backfilled with the published bounded-job mechanism, returned full pages without that cut. The expired-message unread proof also exposed stale receipt contributions; receipts now carry the message deadline and expire independently. These were corrected and independently rechecked. The frozen replay journal resumed past its completed checkpoint without replaying settled actions; failure and correction time remains visible in its clock. Offline reactions now predict inserts because their uniqueness and cap keys carry the membership scope. A test-selector error and a repeated same-DM verification overlap are preserved as agent errors, not platform defects.
