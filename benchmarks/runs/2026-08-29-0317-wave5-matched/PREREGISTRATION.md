## Required retained evidence

Each cell retains `run-log.md`, `diary.md`, `essay.md`, `final-report.md`,
`correction-loops.json`, `obligations.json`, exact environment/package/runtime
identities, proof receipts, and the application source. For every exercised
LLP 0317 obligation, `obligations.json` records:

1. whether the bad path was impossible, refused before costly work, merely
   documented, still possible, or not exercised;
2. whether the served Guide supplied the repair without package-source
   inspection;
3. correction-loop count and elapsed time;
4. whether application trust, visibility, or consistency changed; and
5. exact published package, runtime, and template identities.

The final synthesis publishes a dated per-obligation existence table split by
profile. A cell that lacks enough evidence is `inapplicable` or `unknown`, never
estimated. Repro-gated rows and Wave 4 commissioned primitives keep their
existing disposition unless the matched cell directly exercises them; the
rerun does not self-accept or implement those commissions.
