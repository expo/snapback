**Finding 1 — Medium, introduced: direct-child reaping does not establish log completeness.**

At [candidate-helper.py:212](/Users/ccheever/clique-improvement/reviews/default-install-timeout-race-r1/astra/input/author-evidence/candidate-helper.py:212), `logComplete` is copied directly from `actualProcessWaitCompleted`. The latter correctly describes the single `Popen` child, but the log opened at lines 188–190 can also be written through stdout/stderr inherited by that child's descendants. Trigger: the direct child exits while a descendant retains either descriptor and can still write. This can occur on the ordinary exit path, or when timeout cleanup refuses an identity and the direct child subsequently exits. The helper then hashes the file, asserts `logComplete=true`, and removes the running receipt at lines 209–215 without establishing completion of those writers. Even successful group signaling followed by waiting for only its leader is not itself a wait for every writer.

The distinction is within this delta: parent lines 199–202 already hashed the log and removed the running marker after a direct-child wait, and the identity/group lifetime limitations are inherited. The explicit claim that the log is complete is newly introduced. Do not expand this repair into a general process framework: remove or qualify that assertion, or make completeness conservative unless writer completion is actually established. Keep `actualProcessWaitCompleted` specific to the owned `Popen` child.

This is a conditional static correctness finding, not an observed descendant leak or changing supposedly complete log in this packet. [controlled-child.py:6](/Users/ccheever/clique-improvement/reviews/default-install-timeout-race-r1/astra/input/author-evidence/controlled-child.py:6) creates no descendants; all six controls have one log writer. A descendant-writer witness would require new execution and is **untested here**. The recorded passes therefore neither reproduce nor disprove this condition.
