The digest runs under each recipient's authority, scans only its period, emits with a durable user/period key, and uses the same logged provider seam. Real-time testing with every client closed proved the first digest's counts, hidden-chat and tombstone exclusion, inactive-user silence, an empty subsequent period, and restart idempotency. Suite, required choreography, and Hermes export pass. A supplemental browser proof demonstrates durable offline writes across page closure, ordered flush, read receipts, and same-account edit/reaction convergence; its first receipt wait timed out and an unchanged-code retry passed, so that timing observation remains ambiguous rather than being attributed to the platform. The acceptance suite now starts its own fresh verification server, seeds via normal auth/writes, and tests old-post exclusion on that separate clock. Maintained counts and schedule-as-recipient were substantially less glue than SQL triggers, cron, and storage policy coordination. Convex-style application functions would be more familiar to author, but here the engine owns more of the cross-device semantics. The compiler explicitly removed message prediction when receipt-derived unread maintenance was introduced; the M6 durable intent presentation handled that without pretending an intent was a delivered row. No feature was struck. Scale execution continues on its separate M6-schema instance and clock; final invariant verification follows that execution.
