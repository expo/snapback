## Table counts: names and roles, not three automatically removed mechanisms

Counts are top-level `table` / `maintain` declarations across each app's flat `snapback/*.q`, including distinct domain/read-model tables but excluding generated API, nested replay/proof copies, runtime data and M7. Source totals: **A 20 tables / 12 maintains; B 17 / 13; C 17 / 13**. A has one effect; B one; C two. All have zero actions. Physical line counts are not compared as an efficiency measure: B's worker/UI are compact, while C was formatted.

| Comparison | Table names absent from the later app | Table names introduced by the later app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A→B, 20→17** | `memberships`, `reactionTotals`, `digestCounts`, `trending`, `repostEdges`, `arrival`, `timeline`, `feedCatalog`, `feedReady` | `members`, `receipts`, `digests`, `rankings`, `reposts`, `views` |
| **A→C, 20→17** | `memberships`, `reactionTotals`, `digestCounts`, `trending`, `entries`, `repostEdges`, `arrival`, `timeline`, `feedCatalog`, `feedReady` | `members`, `readStates`, `digestStats`, `postRanks`, `timelines`, `opened`, `messageClocks` |

Several changes preserve roles under new names: membership (`memberships`→`members`), digest aggregate (`digestCounts`→`digests`/`digestStats`), ranking (`trending`→`rankings`/`postRanks`), and C's arrival sequencing (`arrival`→`messageClocks`). Read/receipt watermarks move from A's membership structure to separate B/C tables. View-once tracking also changes representation. The three A tables `timeline`, `feedCatalog`, `feedReady` implement its materialized reader-feed/pending-feed design; both later apps omit that design and use other authored feed models. C's `timelines` is its original/repost entry model, not evidence that A's singular materialized `timeline` survived unchanged; C Following directly merges at most 1,000 followed lanes. Explicit `reactionTotals` is also absent later. These are source-shape differences; the net three-table change cannot all be credited to the language or treated as removal of reporting work tables.
