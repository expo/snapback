**Two interpretations and an added prerequisite should be distinguished.**

1. **Broad residual-cost reading:** the post-§5 reporting authoring corrections above satisfy the observational content of “reporting still costs loops,” especially the ranking/sync and daily-counter authority work. The RFC explicitly allows familiarity to be the remaining case. This reading supports considering the specified SQL hatch even without a confirmed compiler/runtime defect. It does not make every app error eligible.
2. **Strict Snapback-attribution reading:** import the ceremony bar's explicit attribution standard into the full-cell trigger. The closest direct reporting corrections retain agent-error labels; the strongest confirmed current loop is presence integration/docs, not reporting SQL. On this reading the record should say evidence is qualified or attribution remains for decision, rather than declaring a positive reporting trigger solely from source size or M8 failures. The RFC does not give an explicit full-cell numeric threshold or a rule that resolves every mixed-attribution event, so this is an interpretation to disclose.
3. **SQL-specific causal-fix reading:** require a controlled demonstration that SQL eliminates a retained loop, or proof it is the only way to express the report. **That is an added requirement**, not the words of §9.1/§10.3. Such a prototype could inform the author's cost/benefit judgment, but cannot be represented as an already predeclared prerequisite or used silently to change the bar after seeing results. Likewise, requiring successful use/exhaustion of `group` before any loop counts is not stated in the trigger; absent use limits the claim that Quarry cannot express the shape, not the observed authoring cost.

The interim finding is factual: **post-§5 reporting-authoring corrections remain, predominantly with agent-error attributions; confirmed presence/docs integration cost also remains.** This neither rules SQL in nor out. Retain the clean ceremony bar, full-cell coverage/custody deviations and pending grades/M7. The author should receive completed evidence with its interpretation explicit, without a retroactive SQL-repair bar.
