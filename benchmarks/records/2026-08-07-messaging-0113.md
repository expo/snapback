## Basis

This run took place on 2026-08-07. Every lane received the lane-prompt block
from `../briefs/messaging.md` verbatim, the same brief text used by diaries
0065 and 0066; the native-carrier template applied its documented
on-simulator proof clause. The Snapback lanes used published
`@expo/snapback-*` 0.1.13 packages from npm `latest` and were fenced to

split. The four prompts were the proven instances of the templates in

8081/8082/8095/8097 and backend ports
4287/4290/4293/8788/8789/3210/3211/3212/3230 may be occupied — pick other
ports."

## Fairness notes

This is a published-consumer measurement: Snapback used npm 0.1.13 and
published surfaces only. It is not directly comparable to diary 0058's
in-checkout clock, which excluded Snapback-heavy orientation. All four lanes
used the same model, machine, serial execution, and cold-context discipline.
The Convex package version was not recorded.

Proof depth is asymmetric and is credited in the scoring rubric, not the
ratchet. Snapback lanes prove platform retention and register real verify
profiles. Convex hand-rolls its app-owned cache, which does not preserve
offline writes. The offline claims are therefore labeled platform contract
versus app-built rather than treated as equivalent.

Environment attributions are excluded from framework counts. Metro's file
watcher was blind under `/private/tmp`, and `CI=1` disabled watching. The iOS
26.3 simulator had an astral-emoji rendering bug; iOS 26.5 rendered the emoji
correctly. Neither is a Snapback defect.

This record has N=1 per configuration. Under LLP 0186 OQ2 and
`../protocol.md`, it is existence evidence only; it supports no magnitude or
ratchet claim by itself. These notes are mandatory whenever the numbers are
quoted or published; the full disclosure standard remains OPEN under LLP 0186
OQ5.
