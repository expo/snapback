# LLP 0198 claim rung matrix

P5 publishes only from this matrix. This matrix is part of every claim's
qualification digest.

The matrix carries LLP 0198 II.1's claim ladder and requalification rules.
Every claim-scoped qualification digest covers the run records and verify
receipts it reads from, this matrix, the protocol, brief texts, lane prompts,
and scoring definitions at their qualification commit. Unresolved choices are
not filled from observed results; they are marked `preregistered at brief
freeze`.
