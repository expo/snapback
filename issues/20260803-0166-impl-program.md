## Rules of the program

- **The Spec is law.** LLP 0166 v1.0 is Accepted; lanes implement it,
  they do not reinterpret it. A discovered spec defect is a dated
  amendment proposal to 0166, never a silent divergence.
- **Capability honesty.** No capability advances on any lane landing.
  The store's public claims stay at their current level until the
  §Executable acceptance oracles (S1–S8) and the LLP 0157-consumed
  Move-2 carrier exit pass on the claiming carrier; evaluation (L8)
  additionally gates on the dual-runtime conformance corpus and
  per-plan owner reclassification. Implementation presence ≠ claim.
- **Byte-exact grandfathering is a standing gate.** S4 (the unmodified
  `rqs-resume-suite.mjs` against the successor format, shipped legacy
  comparator/budget, no cross-section competition) must be green from
  the first format-touching landing and stay green at every landing.
- **Owner machinery is consumed, never reimplemented.** LLP 0043 outbox
  and lease lifecycles, LLP 0053 dispositions/fences/barriers, and the
  LLP 0090 admission ladder are called, not forked. Every destructive
  transition routes through the owner's operation.
- **Provisional constants** (0166 OQ4) are code constants surfaced in
  `StorePolicyInspectionV1`; do not tune them ad hoc — revision goes
  through OQ4's measured freeze.
- Wave-3 house rules apply (own-correctness green per landing; fresh
  worktrees; re-fetch before landing; drift batched to closing passes).
  Prefer one lane per session; L1 is the only hard prerequisite of the
  chain.
