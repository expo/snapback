# 0044 L1-05: TUS 1.0.0 resumable lane

**Status:** OPEN. **Authority:** LLP 0044 §Byte stores and resumable
transfer; https://tus.io/protocols/resumable-upload (1.0.0).

Implement TUS 1.0.0 core + creation, expiration, checksum, termination
extensions over restricted staging; no creation-defer-length. Snapback
auth/admission wraps the TUS resource; TUS is not the authority model.
Generated clients checksum every PATCH (conformance requirement);
checksum-less foreign clients are admitted and finalize at the
received-bytes-canonical grade (L1-06 owns grading). Resume binds
auth-session lineage (0093/0095/0096), not the transport connection;
resume-after-restart from last acknowledged offset.

Done when: the transfer acceptance rows pass, including interrupted-
resume, offset conflict, expiry, per-chunk checksum failure, and
deferred-length refusal.
