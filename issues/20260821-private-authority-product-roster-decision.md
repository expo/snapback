# Decide private authority relation and product roster composition

**Status:** Open — decision packet; implementation follows the ruling

## Problem

Private authority rows correctly prevent roster enumeration, while ordinary
products still need display names, join times, send-time recipient sets,
receipt aggregates, roles, and leave/rejoin history. Current apps maintain a
second product-visible projection and discover its synchronization and
bootstrap rules by refusal. Making authority rows public would erase the
security property; leaving every app to invent mirror maintenance preserves a
large and error-prone integration tax.

## Decision packet

Compare the three LLP 0295 options with executable sketches:

1. app-authored projection behind a generated synchronized command facade;
2. projection fields declared on the authority profile and generated by the
   platform; or
3. an authorized bounded/redacted roster query over the private relation.

For each, evaluate private-row non-enumerability, transition atomicity,
send-time recipient semantics, absent/idempotent branches, leave/rejoin
incarnations, bounded reads, role changes, receipt membership, offline history,
and Explicit/Familiar authoring cost. Name which facts are authority and which
are product data; do not silently make one representation serve both roles.

## Exit

The author selects a composition and the ruling lands in LLP 0240/0278 with a
negative-security model, transaction boundaries, generated/public surface,
migration posture, and Guide route. File independently closable implementation
tickets for the chosen mechanism and its vertical packed fixture, then close
this decision ticket. The existing Guide ticket may teach today's sanctioned
pattern before this decision closes but must not imply that it is the final
generated design.
