# Frozen probe policy (verbatim from LLP 0137)

This file freezes the Phase 1 competitor-probe policy. The normative text
below is copied verbatim from
`llp/0137-react-web-backend-championship.plan.md` (2026-07-31 v3), section
"Competitor references and probes", plus the two verbatim excerpts that bind
probe scheduling (Stage 3) and the mobile reference probe (Track B). If the
Plan is amended, this freeze is rewritten only through a dated prospective
amendment and a re-freeze.

## Competitor references and probes (verbatim)

> The default competitor reference is frozen evidence: the LLP 0102 diaries
> and reports, the LLP 0115 rerun evidence, and current official provider
> documentation. That evidence already documents Convex, Supabase, and
> Firebase outcomes on the four lineage shapes.
>
> A **fresh competitor probe** — a provider-native implementation of one
> contract, or a bounded slice of one — is justified only when:
>
> 1. the finding would change what Snapback builds, and frozen evidence cannot
>    settle what "good" looks like on the product fact in question;
> 2. no frozen evidence covers the dimension (chiefly A1's local-first floor
>    and A6's sequence/claims shape, plus current-generation vector and file
>    pipelines on A5); or
> 3. the provider's recommended path has materially changed since 0102 froze.
>
> Expected strongest-fit probes, pre-declared as guidance rather than
> commitments: Firebase on A1 (retained offline), Convex on A5 and A6
> (functions, scheduling, vector), Supabase on A5 (storage, pgvector, search),
> InstantDB on A1/A2 (compact React offline/room ergonomics), SpacetimeDB on
> A6 (transactional reducers). Probes are timeboxed at the deep-arm ceiling,
> use official provider paths, receive the same diary and cleanup discipline,
> and produce reference observations — never ranking claims, and never
> reusable as Phase 2 championship arms.
