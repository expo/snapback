## Summary

Snapback 4 has a coherent architecture and a credible path to an excellent
product. It does not yet have evidence of consistently excellent ergonomics,
performance and operation across complete applications.

Membership facts govern authorization and distribution. Authored mutations
serve authoritative execution and local prediction. Assets, expiry, maintained
values and background work use the same system. One Rust interpreter runs on
server and device hosts. Those choices remove substantial application work.

The five product outcomes below remain the agenda. Most mechanisms proposed
in this family's first draft already shipped through LLP 3000.007. The panel
therefore recommends a bounded completion program: truthful replay results,
verified acquisition and persistence boundaries, and focused application
measurements. Charlie's subsequent instruction authorizes selecting and
implementing the directionally right work; §8 records that selection. It does
not authorize a promotion claim or an unlimited new platform.

## 1. What excellence means

| Quality | Observable product outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Beautiful and elegant | A product rule composes with reads, writes, sync and presentation without being restated in application infrastructure. |
| Ergonomic | Ordinary work is expressible and discoverable through the package, generated types and diagnostics. |
| Performant | Useful screens, durable writes and offline readiness have affordable measured costs on realistic data and devices. |
| Dependable | Reconnects, changes and failures preserve the stated durability boundary and recover through supported behavior. |
| Holistically excellent | These properties hold together in substantial applications and their next change. |

A documented workaround can still be an ergonomic cost. A typed refusal can
be excellent behavior when the alternative is a false or unauthorized answer.
The strongest established fit is interactive shared data with live state and
intermittent connectivity; broader backend suitability remains a question.

## 4. Constraints that the work preserves

Follow [LLP 3000 §7](./3000-snapback4.explainer.md#7-decisions-during-execution),
[the scope fence](../snapback4/NOT-DOING.md) and [loop rules](../rules/RULES.md).
Preserve server authority, one interpreter, truthful completeness, runtime
metering, declared retention and the accepted historical-result withholding.
Missing local knowledge is not authoritative validation failure.

No new blocking gate, registry, hosted target, result-cache protocol or
performance ratchet is added. Slow measurements stay outside the affected
landing loop. A moving main alone does not invalidate unchanged semantic
checks. Public contract corrections land with their types, guide and examples.

The user's explicit panel and Astra implementation request overrides the
slice-one no-panel and lead-only coding restrictions **for this program**.
Astra lanes have disjoint ownership; the lead integrates and reviews. This is
not a standing relaxation of the scope fence.

## 5. Proposed sequence

The panel is complete. Reconcile the living documents, reproduce each newly
identified boundary defect, and implement the reproduced corrections in
isolated Astra lanes. Preserve baseline counterevidence when a hypothesis is
wrong. The existing LLP 3000.007 §19 presence/helper/`==` program keeps its
owner; consume its reviewed landing rather than duplicate its implementation.

Integrate the selected fixes, review their combined behavior independently,
and verify the affected real clients. Extend existing witnesses for the
missing timing/failure boundary and retain measurements with their artifacts.
Publish meaningful surface changes through the existing qualified path.

## 6. Completion and falsification

Completion of **this accepted increment** means each child's selected
acceptance has evidence or a precise non-reproduction disposition, the
changes are delivered, and remaining broader work is identified. It does not
mean every original aspirational acceptance item has been achieved.

Reconsider architecture or scope if ordinary features repeatedly require a
second application data plane, faithful partitions remain unaffordable, or
the same specialized coordination persists after the repairs. Keep native,
M7, generality and longer operational evidence separate from this increment.

## 7. Open decisions

No additional syntax, residency spelling, predicted-result channel or offline
variant is commissioned. Those choices are settled in LLP 3000.007.

A concrete second-application feature, recovery objectives for production,
physical-device qualification and promotion remain future decisions. Do not
call the existing Interview sketch a completed independent application.

## 9. Implementation and current gap

The [execution record](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-14-snapback4-excellence/README.md)
records the reproduced replay, acquisition, browser persistence/runtime and
stopped-restore defects, their corrections and the original red results. A
measured native replay delay selected one additional small correction:
`TCP_NODELAY` reduced the same 273-write drain from 20.25 s to 4.61 s while
keeping the existing 15 s deadline and all receipt/queue assertions.

Fresh [Sol](./reviews/3000.008-holistic-product-excellence-implementation.gpt.md)
and [Grok](./reviews/3000.008-holistic-product-excellence-implementation.grok.md)
code reviews returned READY without must-fix correctness findings. The
[author pass](./reviews/3000.008-holistic-product-excellence-implementation.author.md)
is separate. The execution record resolves documentation findings and binds
source/host/artifact evidence. The combined 0.2.14 sync encoder also received
fresh [Sol](./reviews/3000.008-holistic-product-excellence-sync-integration.gpt.md)
and [Grok](./reviews/3000.008-holistic-product-excellence-sync-integration.grok.md)
READY verdicts and actual-client requalification.

The current production Linux/browser observation acquires a 20,000-message
fixture and answers its first useful thread read in 11.28 s; a fresh offline
reopen takes 675 ms. This is an instrumented, descriptive client measurement,
not the full Clique floor or a render measurement. Cold acquisition remains
a material gap, along with the existing suite budget, physical/native and M7
application evidence, production recovery reconciliation and a concrete second
application. The separately owned LLP 3000.007 program retains its remaining
work; this increment does not claim its unlanded fixes.
