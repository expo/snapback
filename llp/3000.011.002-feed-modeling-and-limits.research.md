# LLP 3000.011.002: Feed modeling and delivery limits

**Type:** Research
**Status:** Active
**Systems:** Snapback 4 Following recipe, typed references, ranking, fanout, replica residency
**Author:** Codex / GPT-6 (for Charlie Cheever)
**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Revised:** 2026-09-22 (reviewed evidence and scoped remedies)
**Related:** [Parent](./3000.011-recent-builder-friction.research.md), [Improvement proposal](./3000.011.002.000-simpler-feed-integration.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.004](./3000.004-feeds-and-the-interview-reference-app.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.007.001](./3000.007.001-maintained-columns.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.007.002](./3000.007.002-common-application-shapes.rfc.md)

## 1. Problem

The feed recipe saves invention, but integrating it still introduces a second
set of feed identities, delivery tables, follow bridges and backfill behavior.
It also has limits that become product behavior, including a maximum of 32
shared-author pull lanes for one reader. Ranking has a separate derivation
and ordering problem; it should not be confused with Following delivery.

## 2. Evidence

The [0.2.30 M2 diary](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-18-snapback4-clique-astra-0.2.30/astra/lane-diary.md)
calls the recipe substantial, flags broad public replication and explicitly
names the 32 pulled-author cap. Its M8 needed a ranked table, precise score/time
order and a populated-store backfill. The
[0.2.31 diary](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-20-snapback4-clique-astra-0.2.31/astra/lane-diary.md)
records repeated corrections while bridging application post ids and recipe
event references; the recipe nevertheless supplied a working delivery design.

The [current feeds card](../snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/feeds.md)
makes the consequences concrete. Promotion needs a pull slot for every follower.
If another follower has exhausted its slots, the triggering follow and
promotion refuse atomically; a newcomer cannot free that other user's slot.
The author can remain at the promotion boundary while a dormant saturated
follower prevents progress. This is documented source behavior, not a failure
reproduced by the latest Clique run, whose celebrity fixture used one such lane.

The cap is not a maximum of 32 follows and is unrelated to word-prefix search's
former 32-word limit. Other recipe caps and the runtime meters still apply.

## 3. What is already solved

`init --recipe feeds --prefix feed` already checks collisions, compiles the
combined program, generates helpers, sizes global caps and prints a residency
measurement command. The card documents atomic bridging and media staying
in its original home row. None should be re-proposed as absent.

[LLP 3000.007 §21.3](./3000.007-relaxed-quarry-and-explicit-residency.rfc.md#213-the-six-properties)
records why a merge across all follows did not replace the recipe: the
measured read cost grew substantially at 500 follows. A simpler-looking query
does not establish a better serving design.

The accepted [maintained-column RFC](./3000.007.001-maintained-columns.rfc.md)
owns removing manual aggregate copies for qualifying parent-row counts.
The latest 0.2.31 record says that mechanism was absent from its package pin.
This finding must not start a competing implementation or extend the first
scope to predicting those fields or indexing expiring contributions.

## 4. Interpretation and confidence

There are two costs: integrating an explicit materialized delivery model, and
accepting the finite domain it supports. Better adapters can reduce the first
without changing the second. Solving slot saturation requires a serving
alternative and evidence about its write, read and offline behavior.

Broad public replication is a declared cost to measure. The latest lane's
server-query probes did not measure its native cold acquisition or memory;
this record does not infer a current native performance failure from them.

## 5. Desired outcome

A builder should connect application posts and follow intent through typed,
atomic helpers without hand-maintaining cross-table identities. The product
should make a saturated route diagnosable and offer a qualified alternative
when more pulled authors are required. Ranking should consume the existing
maintained-column work once available, with its own source-lifetime limits.

The [child RFC](./3000.011.002.000-simpler-feed-integration.rfc.md) proposes
an integration improvement first and a bounded serving experiment second.
It deliberately separates an authoring win from an unproven removal of the cap.
