## Packet 1 — the diagnosis (Summary, §§1–5)

**READY WITH CHANGES**

The qualitative core holds: pairwise run-to-run comparison can hide a slow using-cost slide; the quoted notes are about orientation and feed machinery, not a documented factual-mislead finding; the unused second shape belongs off the default path; a database/feeds/actions split is the wrong cut. The apparatus around that core overclaims. C1 is treated as a cause of the score series on evidence that does not show it. Several “falling” verdicts should not have been called.

### Critical

**§3 + §5 — C1 is a packaging fact presented as a cause of the Clique series.**  
The quoted scorer charges name README, guide cards, keyword cards, generated API/types, worker/wire, and the feeds recipe. They do not name OIDC, HTTP routes, steps, or a second server runtime. Across the retained diaries `oidc` / `service seat` / `search_all` appear **0** times. That is evidence the Clique app does not use 3000.009. It is also evidence against the claim that builders *paid Clique points for that surface*: non-mention is compatible with “never on the path they read.” Clique, by construction, cannot score 3000.009’s benefit. At most it can show **leakage onto a chat builder’s first read**, which is C2. A product fence may still be the right *fix*, but it is not licensed by this measurement as a *cause* of 90→88. The 2026-09-15 date is handled with a causation disclaimer and then spent as “where the series peaks” and “the fork.” 0.2.16 is **90**; the series also hits **91**. That is insinuation. Collapse C1-as-cause into C2 unless you can show those codes/cards were actually in the start path the scorer charged.

### High

**§1 — The estimator and bar do not support the table as printed. The table is not wholly under-powered.**  
N=1 per version and a stated adjacent-run move of **±3 with no surface change** are the noise model. OLS total change over 10 equally spaced points has SE ≈ residual σ. With σ≈3, fitted drifts of ~3 are ~1 SE; ~6 is marginal; only the large ones are even in range of a real slope.

The bar you used (“drift exceeds **one SD of its own series**”) is not that test, and it is not conservative. It is **anti-conservative on low-variance series**: `media_assets` 97→96 (−1.6, −0.8 without 0.2.28) will beat “1 series SD” because the series barely moves. Calling that falling is false precision. `operations` (−3.4/−2.9) and `data_modeling` without 0.2.28 (−2.3) sit inside the instrument. `setup_and_docs` (−5.1/−4.7) is weak. `scale_and_limits` (−13.6 → **−4.8** without 0.2.28) is sold as a surviving six-category finding after you correctly discarded overall for depending on that run; the same run is doing a large fraction of that category’s slope. Inconsistent.

What the series *can* support: `queries_and_live` (−8.8/−8.3) is large enough and stable to LOO on 0.2.28 that a directional claim is defensible **if** residual noise is really ~3 and the scorer is stable. `authorization` rising is in the same magnitude band as the weaker declines; treat it with the same caution you want for those declines. Headline “every category that measures the cost of *using* the surface declines” is an un-pre-registered grouping that tracks the cells you labeled falling. If “using-cost” is defined after seeing the slopes, the claim is circular. Leave-one-out is advertised as drop-*each*-run; only drop-0.2.28 is shown. **UNVERIFIED:** actual per-category series, series SDs, residual SDs, and the other nine LOO columns.

**§2 — “Charges volume and exonerates quality” is a selected reading, not a result.**  
Five volume/orientation snippets plus two “no misleading documentation was established” lines do not establish a ten-run law. A contrary reading of the *same* quotes: the scorer is charging **scatter / information architecture** (README + cards + generated API + worker/wire), which is a docs failure even when no sentence is false; “comprehensive but orientation-heavy” is not an exonerate-quality verdict; “never misled” is the instrument’s high bar for factual error, not a quality bill of health. A second contrary reading: the published rubric charges defects, missing capability, and *misleading* docs — **not volume**. If scores move on byte count anyway, that is a **validity** problem in the tool, not a clean causal arrow to surface volume. You are the session that built the tool; this section needs that tension stated, not quotes that fit C2/C3. `queries_and_live` notes (bounded read models, `N_WIDE_MERGE`, two-cursor walk) are engine-recipe cost. They support C3. They do not support “the scorer charges volume” as a single mechanism covering six falling cells.

**§3 — 48% is a prefix story, and “the benchmark forbids” overreaches.**  
29/60 = 48% is arithmetic. Classification by `E_OIDC_*` / `E_ROUTE_*` / `E_STEP_*` / `E_SERVER_*` serving 3000.009 is **UNVERIFIED** and load-bearing. If `E_SERVER_*` or `E_ROUTE_*` includes local-first replica/server or client routing, the 48% figure is inflated and C1’s quantitative hook shrinks. Clique’s brief puts OAuth/routes/services *out of scope for the chat app*. That is not “the benchmark forbids those codes from existing in the package.” Cost of being on the default read ≠ indictment of the accepted RFC. Guide growth 138 KB → 339 KB is not decomposed; code prefixes cannot attribute the 200 KB, so this table cannot size C1 vs C2 vs C3 (you already put 0.6 on relative sizes — the 48% headline fights that).

**§5 — The four causes are not four distinct causes; C4 is the weak one; a fifth is missing.**  
C1 and C2 are the same mechanism (extra surface on first read) with two fixes. Distinct owners do not make distinct causes. C3 is distinct *if* even a perfect start packet still leaves a 60 KB recipe because the engine requires it — that is the good version. C4 is not established by this packet: the probe is “last four lanes,” not ten calibrations; `diagnostics_dev_loop` is **flat**, which is where a check-note problem should show unless you show the charges landed in `scale_and_limits`; and that category’s slope is the one that collapses toward noise without 0.2.28’s loaded-host cold acquire. “Only C2 is a documentation change” is false on your own C4 (“say less”).

Missed fifth: **in-scope growth and correctness ceremony** (the 39% declarative codes, horizon/merge-position P1s, retained history, residency explicitness). You cite those as reasons not to revert, which implies they added builder-visible obligation. That is a cause of using-cost, and Clique would charge it. Second missed fifth, given authorship: **instrument drift / extra-legal volume charging**. Adjacent ±3 already says the tool moves without surface change; you used that to save overall and then trusted category OLS.

## Packet 2 — the proposal (§§6–11, Non-claims)

**NOT READY**

The product direction (fence the unused deployment shape, budget reads, retire the recipe in the engine, do not revert the engine) can follow from the stated diagnosis. The proposal as written does not, because its fence is stronger than its acceptance checks, its self-test cannot falsify what it claims, and it denies ruling the things it rules.

### Critical

**§9 — P1 is not distinguishable from noise; P1 and P2 are not mutually exclusive on this instrument.** The expected P1 move is three points (`93 → 96`) on an N=1-per-version scorer the RFC itself says moves ±3 between adjacent runs. One calibration, and even two, cannot tell recovery from the same adjacent jitter that made twelve waves look flat. “Toward” makes P1 one-sided besides: any uptick counts as success, any move inside ±3 counts as P2’s null. P2 therefore cannot retire §6.2. Worse, P2 *repeats the error this RFC exists to correct*: it treats one adjacent non-move as a theory-killer after a ten-run fit was required to see the decline. The sentence “each is falsifiable by one run of the existing instrument” is false for P1 and P2. P3/P4 are only slightly better: “recovers” / “both move” have no magnitude, so they also fit inside ±3 unless the −8.3 is treated as the effect size and stated as a threshold. This is the load-bearing epistemology of the proposal, not a wording nit.

**§10 — Calibrations after B and after D do not make P1–P4 attributable.** P1/P2 require C1 *and* C2; the first calibration is after B (C1 only). C2 lands in step C with no calibration. The second calibration is after D, so C3 is already in, and a `setup_and_docs` move cannot be charged to the fence/read-budget rather than the recipe. P3 needs a calibration after A (C4 alone) before B moves the map; there isn’t one. P4 needs a post-C baseline (C1+C2, no C3) and a post-D contrast; it has only the latter. The sequence as drawn cannot do the job §9 and §10 claim.

### High

**§6.1 — The fence is not coherent with an Accepted same-package 3000.009, and the RFC does not admit what that costs.** “Never learns these exist” is stricter than optionality. 3000.009 is Accepted and ships in the published package. Mechanism (package vs `init` flag vs served index) can belong to 3000.009; the *constraint* cannot. Leaving “how the fence is drawn” to 3000.009 is a reasonable split of mechanism and an evasion of ratification: this RFC never says “if 3000.009’s Accepted text requires the general-purpose surface to be visible from the default entry, 3000.009 is reopened to that extent.” Without that sentence, C1 is an unagreed amendment of another RFC, and sequence B is blocked on a ruling this document pretends not to need. UNVERIFIED: whether 3000.009’s Accepted text already allows a hidden default.

**§6.1 vs §8.2 — Acceptance contradicts the fence.** §6.1 forbids the declarative builder learning the second shape exists. §8.2 requires that a typed Tier 2 word print how to opt into that shape. That *is* learning it exists. Either 6.1 is rhetoric or 8.2 fails 6.1. “Absent from the default listing” is the checkable part; “never learns these exist” is not what §8 tests.

**§6.1 — Worker actions break the stated split axis.** The RFC says this is the only split and the axis is deployment shape, not capability, then recommends fencing `E_ACTION_*` with the 3000.009 cluster because twelve Clique runs did not use them, while citing 3000.007 §9.2 that they belong to the declarative product. That is a capability split justified by the benchmark the author built. Clique-out-of-scope is a fair reason to fence routes/OIDC/services/steps. Unused-in-Clique is not a fair reason to hide in-scope declarative surface.

**§6.4 — The rule is not safe.** “A default-path note must name a measured consequence, not a possible one” will demote real engine limits Clique’s chat app never hits (the `lanes > 50` example is the tell). “Measured” is unspecified (Clique? fixture? production?). Preventive cost cliffs and correctness ceilings are not “possible” merely because this instrument did not reach them. Moving “relatives” to `check --cost` is an unbounded set. P3 then treats score recovery as proof the notes were announcement; it cannot see a builder who needed the warning. Q4 in §11 reopens the same rule, so §6.4 is not actually decided.

**§7 vs §11 / Non-claims — The RFC claims not to rule what it rules.** Non-claims: nothing is ruled; no new rule; no disclosure boundary. C1 is a disclosure boundary. C4 is a new rule for what `check` may assert. §8 items 1–3 are product rules. §11 Q1–Q4 are the actual decisions of C1, C2, and C4 still open, including Q2 reopening a recommendation §6.1 already made. A proposal whose levers are still questions is not ready for the acceptance list it writes.

### Medium

**§6.2 — The 20 KB Tier 0 target is not earned from this series.** Tiers follow from unused vs used vs start-packet. ≤20 KB does not. The diagnosis gives 138→339 KB and a 60 KB recipe, not a measured M1 delivered-byte count that 20 KB would recover. “LLP 0259 carries over unchanged and is not re-derived here” is an import, not a derivation. UNVERIFIED: whether 0259 ever specified 20 KB. Q3 (per-template vs shared) means the packet is not designed. Acceptance item 1 does not even mention 20 KB, so the number is ornamental.

**§6.2 vs §6.3 — Tier 1 for feeds is known to be fiction.** 12/12 builders read a recipe that is 17.7% of the guide. Putting it in “on demand” does not change delivered bytes for anyone finishing the brief. §6.3 is honest that reading order cannot fix this; C2’s budget is then not a theory of the used surface. That would be acceptable if P2/P4 could detect it. They cannot (see Critical).

**§6.3 — Right owner, thin obligation.** Engine work already commissioned is the right owner of a 60 KB paved workaround. A reporting obligation on bytes removed is coherent and small. It does not follow that this RFC should sequence D as if C3 were its landing; the reporting rule can ship with A or C. Acceptance item 4 is broader than recipe-removal reporting (all declarative-path guide bytes). Align those.

**§8 — Acceptance is only partly checkable and misses C4.** Items 2–3 and 5 are checkable. Item 1 depends on “the fixture” and “bytes delivered,” neither specified here (UNVERIFIED that the fixture exists). Item 4 is checkable as a process rule. C4 has *no* acceptance item, despite being first in sequence and the only lever for P3. Item 1 tests compiling schema off Tier 0, not Clique M2–M8, so C2 can “accept” while every using-the-surface category stays down.

**§7 — Refusals are argued, unevenly.** Not reverting 0.2.16 is argued: authorization 95→88, horizon/`complete: true`, merge-position, retained history; surface declined, engine landings are not the cost. Good, assuming the diagnosis split. Refusing a database/feeds/actions split is only half from this series (used vs unused surface). The rest is imported (authorization rising; Convex scrypt; Supabase seams; “the integration is the product”). Bind that to 3000.007 or re-argue it; do not treat Clique authorization as proof against a feeds extract. Refusing a “fifth documentation wave” while C2 *is* a documentation-structure wave is slick (the sentence names C1, C3, C4 and omits C2).

### Low

**§9 P2’s gloss overclaims even a true null.** A still `setup_and_docs` would not prove “C3 is the whole story and §6.2 was wasted.” C2 could be necessary-not-sufficient, or could move other categories. Q5 is the right question and should be answered before §9 defines P2’s meaning.

**§9 P3/P4 wording.** Unquantified “recovers” / “move” plus N=1 narrative scoring (scorer reads the builder’s writeup) is not a causal design. Non-claims already admit the series is shape, not magnitude; §9 then sells magnitude tests. Pick one.

**Non-claims — Cause and coincidence are honest; scope is not.** N=1 as shape, 3000.009 date as coincidence not cause, no claim that volume *produced* the decline — those are honest. They are then spent on a §9 that cannot do the job. The 48% / brief-forbids / scorer-charges-volume lines are taken as given from the diagnosis.
