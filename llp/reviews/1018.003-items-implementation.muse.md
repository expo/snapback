### Material findings

1. Paged-out committed creates resurrect as drafts.

   [client-items.ts](/tmp/snapback-client-items-implementation-review/muse/snapback2/packages/snapback2/src/client-items.ts:163): retirement requires `(item || observation?.absent)`. If page excludes the row, `item` is undefined and present `through` does not retire; [client-items.ts](/tmp/snapback-client-items-implementation-review/muse/snapback2/packages/snapback2/src/client-items.ts:165) then creates an intent-only tail. Head page (`c:null` owns tails) will accumulate every proven create that scrolls off its bounded page. Test at `tests/client-items.test.ts:60` codifies page-exclusion, but that plus unbounded terminal retention means unbounded UI growth.

   Fix: retire a `create` on fresh positive proof even when `item` is undefined, or keep a per-query proven-intent set separate from current page membership. Only show tail when no fresh proof exists anywhere.

2. `key === undefined` creates never retire and break key stability.

   [client-items.ts](/tmp/snapback-client-items-implementation-review/muse/snapback2/packages/snapback2/src/client-items.ts:159): `key===undefined` forces `observation=undefined`, so `observed()` is always false. When `ids[0]` later becomes defined, the key changes and the outer keyed element remounts.

   Fix: stabilize to intent-key until explicit handoff, or document that `key` must be total for seamless handoff and never transition from undefined.

   [client-items.ts](/tmp/snapback-client-items-implementation-review/muse/snapback2/packages/snapback2/src/client-items.ts:124) vs [query-items.ts](/tmp/snapback-client-items-implementation-review/muse/snapback2/packages/snapback2/src/query-items.ts:63): `held.order` (answer order) vs `entry.order` (outbox FIFO).
