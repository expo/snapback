Concrete family: an N-character operation name, N sibling `if flag: emit notify {}` statements, and one qualifying absence guard. Source and IR size are Θ(N), but every successful guard lookup must compare an N-byte common prefix: **Ω(N²) byte-comparison work inside discovery itself**, independently of summary merging. Identifier parsing imposes no length bound ([parser.rs:629](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/parser.rs:629)). The benchmark substitutes short statement-address strings ([setups.rs:26](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/tests/presence/setups.rs:26)), hiding this cost.

Smallest resolution: explicitly accept a bound including total generated-site bytes—expected O((S + P) log S), with P defined accordingly—or eliminate discovery/marking’s string-key lookups using statement-ID metadata.
