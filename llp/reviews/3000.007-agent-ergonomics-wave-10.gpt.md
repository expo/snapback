**Finding 1 — Medium: an independent missing job body is classified as a cascade.**

Location: [snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/parser/programs.rs:103](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/parser/programs.rs:103).

Concrete counterexample:

```quarry
job empty():
  x = 1 == 1
```

The expression parser records the mechanical `==` diagnostic while successfully retaining the binding statement. `parse_chunks` puts that statement in the **prelude**, leaving the chunk body empty. The new code then marks “job empty has an empty body” as caused by the equality diagnostic, and default `check` hides it.

Changing `==` to `=` still leaves a job with no chunk body. The hidden diagnostic therefore carries an independent repair. Adding another prelude statement or nesting the equality inside an `if` preserves the problem. This also contradicts the new published claim “Independent errors stay visible.”

The smallest fix is to exclude jobs from this empty-operation cascade marking. Job chunk failures already have their own error path; a successfully parsed prelude does not explain missing chunks. Add the counterexample and its corrected-equality control.

**Finding 2 — Medium: the horizon-silence explanation reverses the reader dependency.**

Locations: [snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide.rs:706](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide.rs:706), [guide/codes.rs:133](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/codes.rs:133), and [packages/snapback4/README.md:1087](snapback4/packages/snapback4/README.md:1087).

The added sentence says:

> Silence means "no reader in this program forced the check"

The wave’s own [regression fixture](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/tests/residency_notes.rs:19) supplies the counterexample: an uncapped public table **without a reader emits the note**; adding an eligible mutation that point-reads it **suppresses the note**. The implementation explicitly suppresses advice when that reader needs whole-partition coverage. A cap, existing horizon or maintained table can also explain silence.

The smallest fix is to replace the sentence consistently with:

“Eligible mutations that require whole-partition coverage can suppress this advice. An absent note does not certify bounded acquisition or a safe cold-start size.”

Keep the existing explanation of bounded groups and update the exact-text assertion. The current test locks in the false sentence while separately proving behavior that contradicts it.
