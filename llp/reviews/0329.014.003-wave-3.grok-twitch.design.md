### 1. Product contract

Headwater is the chat, moderation, and in-stream engagement layer of a live video platform. Video ingest, transcode, HLS, and DVR live in an external media plane. Headwater stores when a broadcast started, the frozen HLS delay, and every chat event, and it is the system viewers, moderators, and the broadcaster actually touch.

A **channel** is owned by one **broadcaster**. Anyone may watch. Sending chat requires an account. A **stream** is one live broadcast. Chat exists while the channel is offline; those messages never appear on a VOD. **Moderators**, **VIPs**, and **editors** are channel-scoped grants with time intervals. **Subscribers** hold paid periods. **Followers** have a follow interval. **Staff** are platform operators. **Anonymous devices** may watch and receive chat; they cannot send, redeem, or bet.

**User-visible promises**

1. One channel may have **100,000 concurrent unique viewers** and **~1,000 accepted public messages per second** at peak. The product does not degrade into “this channel is full” as a substitute for that scale.
2. Live viewers share **one ordered public log** per channel, keyed by monotonic `seq`. Everyone in the same delivery class sees the same messages in the same order. A viewer client renders a tail of **150** messages and may **skip** under load (a `jump` to `headSeq-150`). **Moderators and the broadcaster must not skip** inside the last **2,000** seqs (about two seconds at peak, much longer on a quiet channel).
3. A live reconnect catches up **at most 500** seqs for viewers and **2,000** for moderators. Older live backscroll is not promised. The **VOD** for that stream still has every public message.
4. **VOD chat** is the same durable log, addressed by **video time** (`streamOffsetMs`), not by wall clock. Alignment promise: within **2 seconds** of what HLS live viewers saw. Offline chat (`streamId` null) is absent from every VOD and clip.
5. A **clip** is a 30-second DVR excerpt. Its picture is an immutable media asset. Its chat overlay is a **live query** of the durable log over that offset range and therefore honors later tombstones.
6. **Sub-only, follower-only, emote-only, verified-only, unique-chat, slow mode, and the 20/30s spam window** are server-enforced. A second device, a second tab, or a modified client cannot bypass them. Slow mode is **0 or 1–120 seconds**. Spam is **20** accepted-or-held-or-denied messages per **30 seconds** per user (moderators **100**, broadcaster unlimited).
7. **Chat delay** is **0, 2, 4, or 6 seconds**. Broadcaster, editors, moderators, and staff see a message as soon as it is accepted. Everyone else sees it only after the delay **and only if it has not been tombstoned**. Delay exists so a moderator can delete a message before the public sees it.
8. **Timeouts and bans** take effect for **new** sends in the same commit as the sentence. A timeout is `[startsAt, endsAt)` with `endsAt` at most **14 days** out. A ban is open-ended until unban. Unban does **not** restore purged messages. Timeout without purge does **not** hide already-public messages. Ban-with-purge hides that user’s public messages from the current stream (or, if offline, the last 24 hours) in live tails, VOD, and clip overlays.
9. Badges, name color, and which emote tokens rendered are **what was true at send time**, including on VOD a year later after the sender unsubscribed, was unmodded, or was banned.
10. **Raids** move A’s viewers onto B’s video **and B’s chat**. **Hosts** point A’s video at B; A’s viewers stay in **A’s** chat. A raid is not a link in a system message.
11. **Channel points** accrue only for live presence (10 per completed 5-minute slice; subscribers 12). They spend on rewards and predictions. Balances never go negative. Individual prediction bets are private; outcome totals are public.
12. The **viewer count** is concurrent unique users (plus anonymous devices) with a heartbeat in the last 45 seconds, published at most 5 seconds stale, allowed error **±5%**. It is not “how many rows this caller can read.” Above **200** unique viewers, nobody gets a chatter roster; they get the count. At or below 200, moderators may list up to 200 handles.
13. Writes require a live connection. There is **no offline send queue**, no offline timeout queue, and no merge of two devices’ unsent drafts. Reconnect is catch-up from `lastSeq` plus a presence heartbeat. The first accepted `clientNonce` wins; a second send is a new nonce and is subject to slow mode.
14. Held automod messages are visible to the **sender** (as held) and to **realtime moderators**. They are invisible to everyone else, do not occupy a public `seq`, and if approved become a public message **at approval time** (new `seq`, new `streamOffsetMs`). Denied holds never become public. Held, denied, and accepted messages all consume the sender’s rate budget.
15. Refusals to the sender are explicit (`slow_mode` with `retryAfterMs`, `sub_only`, `timed_out` with `endsAt`, `banned`, `spam`, `duplicate`, `cannot_chat`). Other viewers never see a refused body. The API does not answer “am I banned?” for a third party by a distinctive error on *their* send.
16. Two devices of one user share rate limits, sentences, subscriptions, and point balances. Presence is per device. Optimistic “pending” chat on device A does not appear on device B until the server accepts it.
17. Retention: public message **bodies** are served for **60 days** after `streams.endedAt` (or 60 days after `createdAt` if never attached to a stream). Tombstoned bodies remain available to **staff** for **90 days** for abuse review, then are physically dropped. Live clients do not read that staff store.
18. Out of scope, so later implementers must not invent them: whispers, bits, Hype Trains, reply threads, extensions, discover, and search over chat.

**Concurrency, offline, multi-device.** Concurrent actors on one hot channel: up to 1,000 senders/s, 100,000 delayed subscribers, ~20 realtime moderators, the broadcaster’s dashboard, the delay pipeline, presence GC, watch-point accrual, prediction lock, automod, the clip transcoder, and a raid redirect. Offline play is refused. Multi-device is first-accept plus shared per-user serialisation, not CRDT.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

1. **[others]** Public chat for a channel is one totally ordered log: each public message has a `seq` unique in that channel, assigned at the instant it becomes public, never reused, never reordered.
2. **[time]** A user may send in channel C at time T only if no moderation sentence covers T, and the chat-mode rules in force at T are satisfied by the grants, follow, and subscription periods that cover T. “Covers T” means `startsAt ≤ T < endsAt` and `revokedAt` is null or `> T`.
3. **[time]** Slow mode, unique-chat, and the spam window are constraints on **this user’s recent accepted-or-held-or-denied sends in this channel**, evaluated server-side under a per-user lock, not on the client clock and not per device.
4. **[time]** Mode exemptions (slow / follower / sub / emote / unique / verified) apply to the broadcaster, active moderators, active editors, and active VIPs **at T**. Realtime delivery (no delay, hold queue) applies to the broadcaster, active moderators, active editors, and staff **at T**. VIP is not realtime.
5. **[time][others]** Badges, color, and emote-set ids on a message are a write-once snapshot of entitlements at send (or at hold-approval). Replaying a VOD must not recompute them from current grants.
6. **[time][others]** A message’s `body` is immutable. The only legal mutation is a tombstone (`tombstoneAt`, `tombstoneBy`, `tombstoneReason`). Unban, role revoke, and subscription expiry must not alter body or tombstone.
7. **[others][time]** Timeout without `removeMode=user_in_stream` leaves public messages in the log. Ban or timeout **with** that remove mode tombstones that user’s public messages in the current stream (offline: `createdAt > now-24h`). Unban does not clear tombstones.
8. **[time]** Chat delay hides a message from the delayed audience until `createdAt + chatDelaySeconds`. If `tombstoneAt` is set before that instant, the delayed audience must never receive the body.
9. **[time]** `streamOffsetMs = max(0, createdAtMs - stream.startedAtMs - stream.hlsDelayMs)` using the stream’s **frozen** delay. VOD and clips address this field. Wall-clock `createdAt` must not be used as video time.
10. **[secrecy]** Automod scores, hold queue, and `modNotes` on sentences are visible to realtime moderators, editors, the broadcaster, and staff of that channel, not to ordinary viewers, not to the target (except a `publicReason` and timeout `endsAt`).
11. **[secrecy][others]** Individual prediction bets and channel-point balances are visible to the owner, the channel broadcaster (balances of **their** points, not bets of other channels), and staff. Other viewers see only per-outcome totals and the caller’s own bet.
12. **[money]** Channel-point balances change only by appending a ledger row and applying that delta in the same commit. Accrual mints. Redemption burns (or refunds). A prediction bet moves points from `balance` to `inPlay`. Resolve pays `floor(bet.points * pool / winningPool)` to winners and **burns** the floor remainder; cancel refunds `inPlay` exactly. No handler patches `balance` without a ledger row.
13. **[money]** A user has at most one bet row per prediction; additional points may only add to the **same** outcome. Switching sides is refused.
14. **[others]** Viewer count and prediction pools are facts about the channel, counted over the unfiltered domain, never over “rows this caller can read.”
15. **[others]** A block is a filter on the **blocker’s** client. It does not remove messages from the shared log, does not change other people’s views, and does not change counts.
16. **[time]** Presence is live iff `lastSeenAt > now-45s`. There is no stored `isOnline` that a late job is required to flip for the count to be correct.
17. **[others]** Mods cannot timeout or ban the broadcaster, staff, or a user with an active moderator grant; only the broadcaster or staff can sentence a moderator. Nobody can sentence themselves.
18. **[time]** A grant, follow, sub period, or sentence that has ended is history. Current-role enums are not the source of truth.
19. Public `seq` is allocated only for allowed user messages, approved holds, and system messages. Denies and open holds do not punch holes in `seq`.
20. **[others]** Raid redirect is authoritative: after `ExecuteRaid` commits, heartbeats to A are answered with `redirectChannelId=B`. Host never redirects chat.

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1 — Permission and badges are intervals at a time, not `users.role`. (temporal; the coterie analogue)**

- **Wrong:** `users.role ∈ {broadcaster, mod, vip, sub, viewer}` and `users.banned: bool`, updated in place. Send checks the enum. VOD renders `sender.role` now. Timeout sets `banned=true`; a job sets it false when the clock passes.
- **Why it is wrong:** Load-bearing instants differ. **May-send** is covering intervals at T_now (sentence, sub, follow age, VIP grant). **Badge on a message** is covering intervals at T_send. **May-moderate** is a covering mod grant at T_action. A subscriber who chats, then lets the period lapse, must still show the sub badge on the VOD. A user timed out from 14:00–14:10 must chat at 14:11 without a job having flipped a flag; if the job is late, they are illegally silenced, and if you filter reads with `banned=false`, their pre-timeout messages vanish and reappear. A moderator of channel A is a stranger in B. `roles: ['mod','former']` is not an interval.
- **Observation:** Message M at T0 has a sub badge. At T1 the sender’s period ends. VOD of M shows no badge, or a live send during a timeout is accepted because `users.banned` is still false until the sweep. Or unmodding Alice makes her old VOD messages lose the sword.

**Trap 2 — Hiding chat by the sender’s current ban is not retention. (temporal × ephemeral vs durable)**

- **Wrong:** Live and VOD queries add `JOIN users WHERE NOT banned`. Delete-message sets `visible=false` on one id. Ban is the same join. Unban makes everything return.
- **Why it is wrong:** Headwater’s retention rules are **per action**, not per identity. Timeout without purge: messages stay. Ban with purge: tombstone a **range** of that user’s messages in this stream; they stay gone after unban. Ban without purge: future sends fail, history stays. Live skip (ephemeral tail) must not delete the durable row. A clip already created must lose purged overlay lines without re-encoding video. Staff still need the body for 90 days.
- **Observation:** Timeout Alice; her last joke disappears for everyone, then returns when the timeout ends. Or ban-and-purge Alice, unban her, and the VOD shows the slurs again. Or a viewer who jumped over 800 firehose messages during a raid has those messages missing from the VOD as well.

**Trap 3 — 100k viewers are not 100k inboxes and not 100k personalized queries. (fan-out × visibility)**

- **Wrong:** Insert a message row. Each viewer has a live query `SELECT * FROM messages WHERE channelId=C AND seq > :cursor AND senderId NOT IN (my_blocks) AND (sender.role is visible to me)`. One insert invalidates 100,000 queries. Or write 100,000 per-recipient copies. Or server-filter blocks in the hot path.
- **Why it is wrong:** At 1,000 msg/s that is 100 million query re-evaluations per second, or 100 million row writes. The product log is **uniform per delivery class**. There are two public classes (`realtime`, `delayed`) plus a tiny `holds` class. Blocks are client-local (invariant 15). If you personalize the hot range, you have multiplied the axis by every block list and failed the 100k promise. If you use per-recipient inboxes, raids and slow-mode exemptions become inbox-rewrite jobs.
- **Observation:** CPU and fan-out grow linearly with viewer count on an idle copypasta line; adding a block list changes bytes on the wire for everyone; a raid from a 50k channel to a 10k channel cannot complete because it tries to insert 50k inbox rows in the request.

**Trap 4 — Slow mode as a client cooldown, or as a lock on the whole channel. (concurrency × write-rate rules)**

- **Wrong:** Disable the send button for N seconds in the first-party UI. Or `UPDATE channels SET lastMessageAt=now` once per message and reject if too soon **for anyone**. Or check rate state without serialising two sends from the same user.
- **Why it is wrong:** Two devices, two tabs, or a bot skip the button. A channel-wide last-write makes 1,000 distinct humans into 1 msg/s. Two concurrent `SendMessage` calls that both read `lastPublicOrHeldAt` in the past both pass slow mode and the spam window (“check-then-act”). Rate limits that do not count **held and denied** automod results are a firehose into the hold queue.
- **Observation:** Phone and laptop each send one identical line 200 ms apart in 30-second slow mode: both appear. Or the whole channel can only talk once per second. Or a user denied 20 times in 30 seconds still lands a 21st public message.

**Trap 5 — Chat delay as a delayed insert, or as a client sleep. (visibility × time)**

- **Wrong:** `INSERT` the public row at `createdAt + delay`. Or send immediately to everyone and ask the viewer client to buffer. Or put delay into the read ACL as `now() >= createdAt + delay` without a tombstone check on the delayed path.
- **Why it is wrong:** Mods must see the body at T0 to delete it. If the row does not exist until T0+4s, they cannot. If the client is trusted to wait, a modified client is the public. If you insert at T0 and delay-deliver without re-checking tombstone, the delayed audience receives a message a mod already deleted — the feature’s entire purpose is gone. Clock-in-the-read-rule also races replica time: a replica whose `now()` is 3s fast leaks.
- **Observation:** A slur appears in the delayed audience at T+4s after a mod deleted it at T+1s. Or the mod dashboard is empty until T+4s. Or curl on the subscribe endpoint returns the body at T+0 with `chatDelaySeconds=4`.

**Trap 6 — Counts, ranks, and two devices. (visibility × merge × money)**

- **Wrong:** `COUNT(*)` viewers the caller can read (blocks hide people; anonymous sees 1). Chatter list truncated to 50 **without saying so**. Prediction “percent on Blue” from bets the caller owns. Watch-point accrual once per device. Optimistic debit of channel points on both devices. Last-write-wins on `channel_settings` with both dashboards showing success.
- **Why it is wrong:** Viewer count and pools are **structural**. A visibility-filtered count is a confidently wrong integer (100k watching looks like 1). Two devices both accruing a slice double-mint. Two devices both betting the balance once each double-spend without a serial debit. Two `UpdateChatMode` with the same `settingsVersion` must not both return OK.
- **Observation:** Blocked 10,000 bots, dashboard shows 90k, public shows 100k — or the opposite. `PlacePredictionBet` twice, 10 points each, starting balance 10: both succeed. Accrual ledger has two `watch_accrual` rows for one `(userId, sliceId)`.

### 4. Data model

Types: `id` (128-bit opaque), `i16`, `i32`, `i64`, `text`, `bool`, `timestamptz`, `bytes`, `jsonb`. Enums listed. **Derived** fields are never client-written. Intervals are half-open `[startsAt, endsAt)` with `endsAt = timestamptz '+infinity'` when open.

**users.** `userId` PK; `handle` unique text; `displayName` text; `createdAt`; `phoneVerified` bool; `platformRole` `none|staff`; `chatColor` text nullable.

**channels.** `channelId` PK; `broadcasterId` unique FK users; `handle` unique; `createdAt`; `chatSuspended` bool default false.

**channel_settings.** `channelId` PK/FK; `settingsVersion` i32; `slowModeSeconds` i32 `0..120`; `followerModeMinutes` i32 nullable (`null` off, `0` any follower); `subOnly` bool; `emoteOnly` bool; `uniqueChat` bool; `verifiedOnly` bool; `chatDelaySeconds` i16 `0|2|4|6`; `holdFirstTimeChatters` bool; `updatedAt`; `updatedBy` FK.

**chat_mode_changes.** append-only audit: `changeId` PK; `channelId`; `at`; `byUserId`; `fromVersion`; `toVersion`; `patch` jsonb. Not the live source of truth.

**streams.** `streamId` PK; `channelId`; `startedAt`; `endedAt` nullable; `title` text; `category` text; `hlsDelayMs` i32 **immutable after insert**, default 8000; `vodAvailableUntil` timestamptz nullable **derived** `endedAt+60d`; `peakUniqueViewers` i32 default 0. At most one row per channel with `endedAt` null.

**channel_heads.** `channelId` PK; `publicSeq` i64 default 0; `hotWindowStartSeq` i64 **derived** `max(0, publicSeq-50000)`; `liveStreamId` nullable FK; `uniqueViewers` i32; `viewerSampledAt` timestamptz; `raidTargetChannelId` nullable; `hostTargetChannelId` nullable; `settingsVersion` copy. **Hot row.** `publicSeq` only increments in SendMessage / DecideHeldMessage(approve) / system-message inserts.

**channel_role_grants.** `grantId` PK; `channelId`; `subjectUserId`; `role` `moderator|vip|editor`; `startsAt`; `endsAt` default `+infinity`; `revokedAt` nullable; `revokedBy` nullable; `grantedBy`; `createdAt`. **Insert-only except `revokedAt`/`revokedBy`/`endsAt` (extend only: new `endsAt` ≥ old).** Active at T: `startsAt ≤ T < endsAt AND (revokedAt IS NULL OR revokedAt > T)`. Overlapping active intervals for the same `(channelId, subjectUserId, role)` are forbidden by Grant, not by a total unique.

**subscription_periods.** `periodId` PK; `channelId`; `subscriberUserId`; `tier` `1|2|3`; `startsAt`; `endsAt`; `origin` `purchase|resub|gift|prime`; `gifterUserId` nullable; `providerRef` text unique. **Insert-only.** Written by the billing webhook (external; not a Headwater user op). Active sub at T: `startsAt ≤ T < endsAt`.

**follows.** PK `(channelId, followerUserId)`; `followedAt`; `unfollowedAt` nullable. Re-follow after unfollow **resets** `followedAt` (follower-mode clock restarts) and appends **follow_events** (`eventId`, `channelId`, `followerUserId`, `kind` `follow|unfollow`, `at`) for audit. Active follow at T: `followedAt ≤ T AND (unfollowedAt IS NULL OR unfollowedAt > T)`.

**founder_badges.** PK `(channelId, userId)`; `awardedAt`. Insert-only, first 10 distinct subscribers the channel ever had. Cosmetic; survives lapse.

**moderation_sentences.** `sentenceId` PK; `channelId`; `targetUserId`; `kind` `timeout|ban`; `startsAt`; `endsAt` (`timeout`: `startsAt + durationSec`, duration `1..1209600`; `ban`: `+infinity`); `revokedAt` nullable; `revokedBy` nullable; `issuedBy`; `issuedByKind` `moderator|broadcaster|staff|automod`; `removeMode` `none|user_in_stream`; `publicReason` text; `modNotes` text; `purgeFromSeq` i64 nullable; `purgeToSeq` i64 nullable. Covering sentence at T: same interval predicate as grants. **Insert-only except revoke and purge seq fill.**

**messages.** `messageId` PK; `channelId`; `streamId` nullable; `seq` i64; `kind` `user|system`; `systemKind` nullable `raid_inbound|raid_outbound|host|sub|follow|prediction|mode_change`; `senderUserId` nullable; `body` text; `createdAt`; `streamOffsetMs` i32 nullable; `highlighted` bool; `entitlementSnapshot` jsonb `{badgeKeys: text[], emoteSetIds: id[], color: text|null, tier: i16|null}`; `tombstoneAt` nullable; `tombstoneBy` nullable; `tombstoneReason` nullable `mod_delete|ban_purge|staff`; `clientNonce` bytes nullable; `redemptionId` nullable. **Body append-only.** Unique `(channelId, seq)`. Unique `(channelId, senderUserId, clientNonce)` where nonce not null. Access: `(channelId, seq)`; `(streamId, streamOffsetMs, seq)` where `streamId` not null; `(channelId, senderUserId, seq)` for purge.

**held_messages.** `holdId` PK; `channelId`; `senderUserId`; `body`; `createdAt`; `clientNonce`; `reason` `automod|first_time|timeout_classifier`; `status` `held|approved|denied`; `decidedBy` nullable; `decidedAt` nullable; `publicMessageId` nullable; `scores` jsonb staff-only. Unique `(channelId, senderUserId, clientNonce)`.

**chat_rate_state.** PK `(channelId, userId)`; `lastPublicOrHeldAt` timestamptz nullable; `lastNormHash` bytes nullable; `windowStartedAt` timestamptz nullable; `acceptedInWindow` i32; `updatedAt`. Hot per active chatter, not per viewer.

**channel_user_stats.** PK `(channelId, userId)`; `firstPublicSeq` i64 nullable; `publicCount` i64.

**stream_user_stats.** PK `(streamId, userId)`; `firstSeq` i64 nullable; `messagesAccepted` i32; `joinedViaRaidId` nullable; per-reward counts jsonb.

**presence.** PK `(channelId, userId, deviceId)` with `userId` nullable for anonymous (`anonDeviceId` in `deviceId`, `userId` null); `joinedAt`; `lastSeenAt`; `joinedViaRaidId` nullable. Time-bounded by heartbeat: stale if `lastSeenAt ≤ now-45s`.

**viewer_count_samples.** append-only: `channelId`, `sampledAt`, `uniqueViewers` i32, `connections` i32.

**blocks.** PK `(blockerUserId, blockedUserId)`; `createdAt`. Not read on the send or fan-out path.

**emote_sets.** `setId` PK; `scope` `global|channel_sub|channel_bits`; `channelId` nullable; `tierMin` i16 default 1.

**emotes.** `emoteId` PK; `setId`; `token` text; `assetUrl` text. Unique `(setId, token)`.

**channel_point_balances.** PK `(channelId, userId)`; `balance` i32; `inPlay` i32; `lifetimeEarned` i32; `balanceVersion` i32; `lastAccrualSliceId` i64 nullable.

**channel_point_ledger.** append-only: `entryId` PK; `channelId`; `userId`; `delta` i32; `inPlayDelta` i32; `reason` `watch_accrual|redemption|redemption_refund|prediction_bet|prediction_payout|prediction_refund|adjustment`; `refId` text; `sliceId` i64 nullable; `createdAt`. Unique `(channelId, userId, reason, refId)` for idempotency (`watch_accrual` refId = `sliceId`).

**rewards.** `rewardId` PK; `channelId`; `kind` `highlight_message|queued_action`; `title`; `cost` i32; `enabled` bool; `maxPerUserPerStream` i32 nullable; `skipQueue` bool.

**redemptions.** `redemptionId` PK; `channelId`; `rewardId`; `userId`; `streamId` nullable; `status` `unfulfilled|fulfilled|cancelled`; `createdAt`; `cost` i32 copied.

**predictions.** `predictionId` PK; `channelId`; `streamId`; `title`; `outcomes` jsonb `[{outcomeId, label}]` length 2; `status` `open|locked|resolving|resolved|canceled`; `opensAt`; `locksAt`; `resolvedAt` nullable; `winningOutcomeId` nullable; `pool` i32; `countByOutcome` jsonb; `createdBy`. At most one of `open|locked|resolving` per channel (partial unique).

**prediction_bets.** PK `(predictionId, userId)`; `outcomeId`; `points` i32; `createdAt`; `updatedAt`; `payout` i32 nullable; `refunded` bool default false.

**clips.** `clipId` PK; `channelId`; `streamId`; `createdBy`; `startOffsetMs` i32; `durationMs` i32; `status` `pending|ready|failed`; `assetUrl` text nullable; `externalJobId` text nullable; `createdAt`.

**raids.** `raidId` PK; `fromChannelId`; `toChannelId`; `startedAt`; `viewerCountSnapshot` i32; `canceledAt` nullable.

**Relationships.** Channel 1—1 settings, 1—1 heads, 1—* streams, grants, periods, follows, sentences, messages, presence. Stream 0—1 live on heads; 1—* messages with that `streamId`; 1—* clips. Prediction 1—* bets. Message 0—1 redemption.

**Time-bounded.** Grants, periods, follows, sentences, presence (45s), chat delay, VOD `vodAvailableUntil`, clip overlay (inherits tombstones), timeout `endsAt`, raid redirect until A’s presence expires.

**Derived.** `hotWindowStartSeq`, `vodAvailableUntil`, `uniqueViewers` (from samples), badge snapshot (from intervals at send), `streamOffsetMs` (from frozen delay), prediction `pool` = sum of bet points (maintained).

### 5. The hard operations

Helpers used below:

- `coveringSentence(C,U,T)` — any sentence with interval covering T.
- `ents(C,U,T)` — flags `isBroadcaster`, `isEditor`, `isMod` (grant or broadcaster or editor or staff), `isVip`, `isSub`, `followAgeSec`, `isVerified`, `modeExempt = isBroadcaster∨isMod∨isVip`, `realtime = isBroadcaster∨isMod∨staff`.
- `norm(body)` — NFKC, lower case, collapse whitespace, strip trailing punctuation; then SHA-256.
- Per-user lock key `(channelId, userId)` — held by SendMessage, BanOrTimeoutUser (on the **target**), DecideHeldMessage.

---

**Op 1 — SendMessage** (write; calls automod)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `body`, `clientNonce` (16–64 hex chars), `deviceId`, optional `redemptionId`.
- **Auth:** caller is user U, signed in. Anonymous → `sign_in_to_chat`.
- **Steps:** (1) If `body` length 0 or >500 Unicode scalars → `too_long`. (2) Load `channel_heads`, `channel_settings`. If `chatSuspended` → `chat_unavailable`. (3) If a `messages` row exists for `(C,U,nonce)`, return it (idempotent). If a `held_messages` row exists for that nonce: return `{status:held|denied|approved,…}` without a second send. (4) Acquire lock `(C,U)`. (5) If `coveringSentence(C,U,now)`: `timed_out` with `endsAt` or `banned`. (6) Compute `ents(C,U,now)`. (7) If `subOnly` and not modeExempt and not isSub → `sub_only`. (8) If `followerModeMinutes` not null and not modeExempt: missing follow or `followAgeSec < minutes*60` → `follower_only`. (9) If `verifiedOnly` and not modeExempt and not phoneVerified → `verified_only`. (10) If `emoteOnly` and not modeExempt: every non-whitespace token must be an emote `token` in sets U is entitled to at now (global ∪ this channel’s sub set if isSub ∪ other channels’ sub sets for U’s covering periods) → else `emote_only`. (11) Load/create `chat_rate_state`. If not isBroadcaster: if `windowStartedAt` is null or `< now-30s`, reset window to now and `acceptedInWindow=0`. Cap = isMod? 100:20. If `acceptedInWindow ≥ cap` → `spam`. (12) If `slowModeSeconds>0` and not modeExempt and `lastPublicOrHeldAt` is not null and `now < lastPublicOrHeldAt + slowModeSeconds` → `slow_mode` with `retryAfterMs`. (13) If `uniqueChat` and not modeExempt and `lastNormHash = norm(body)` and `lastPublicOrHeldAt > now-30s` → `duplicate`. (14) `forceHold = holdFirstTimeChatters ∧ channel_user_stats.firstPublicSeq IS NULL ∧ not isBroadcaster`. (15) **External:** `AutomodClassify({userId, channelId, body, firstTime})` deadline 150ms; timeout ⇒ `hold`. If `forceHold` and result `allow`, result = `hold`. (16) Increment `acceptedInWindow`, set `lastPublicOrHeldAt=now`, `lastNormHash=norm(body)` for allow, hold, **and** deny. (17) **deny:** insert `held_messages` status `denied` (or a decision row); return `{status:denied}` to sender only; no public seq; no fan-out. (18) **hold:** insert `held_messages` status `held`; publish to topic `holds:{channelId}` (realtime mods); return `{status:held, holdId}`. (19) **allow:** `streamId = liveStreamId`. `streamOffsetMs` as invariant 9, or null. Snapshot entitlements (badgeKeys include `broadcaster|moderator|vip|subscriber|founder|staff|raider` as applicable; `raider` if `stream_user_stats.joinedViaRaidId` not null). If `redemptionId` present: load redemption `unfulfilled`, `userId=U`, `kind=highlight_message`, same channel; CAS to `fulfilled`; set `highlighted=true`; ledger already burned at redeem time (op not listed separately: treat RedeemHighlight as a prefix of this step or refuse if not pre-redeemed). **Product:** highlight requires a `fulfilled` redemption created in the last 30s. (20) `seq = ++channel_heads.publicSeq`. Insert `messages`. Set `firstPublicSeq` if null. Bump `stream_user_stats.messagesAccepted`. (21) Publish projection to `chat:{C}:realtime`. If `chatDelaySeconds=0`, also to `chat:{C}:delayed`; else enqueue delay-pipeline with `deliverAt=createdAt+delay`. (22) Release lock.
- **Atomic:** lock, sentence/mode/rate checks, rate_state write, seq increment, message or hold insert, optional redemption CAS. Automod I/O is inside the **user** lock, never a channel-wide lock.
- **Eventual:** delayed topic; dashboard chat-rate sample.
- **Optimistic UI:** append a pending bubble keyed by `clientNonce` on **this device only**. On `slow_mode`/`spam`/… remove it and show the error. On `held`, convert to “held for review” (sender’s devices, via user-topic). On allow, replace with `seq` and server `createdAt`. Do not paint the message onto other viewers.
- **Conflict:** second in-flight send waits on the user lock, then typically `slow_mode` or `spam`. Duplicate nonce returns the first. Ban that wins the same user lock first causes `banned`/`timed_out`. Settings CAS is not required here: SendMessage reads a snapshot; at most the in-flight set can sneak through a just-enabled sub-only mode.

**Op 2 — CatchUpAndSubscribeLiveChat** (read + subscribe; heartbeat)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `lastSeq` i64 (`0` if none), `deviceId`.
- **Auth:** signed-in or anonymous with a stable `deviceId`. If `chatSuspended` and caller is not staff/broadcaster/mod → `chat_unavailable`.
- **Steps:** (1) Classify audience with `ents` at now (`realtime` vs `delayed`). Anonymous is delayed. (2) `head = publicSeq`. Viewer catch-up start = `max(lastSeq, head-500, hotWindowStartSeq)`. Mod/realtime start = `max(lastSeq, head-2000, hotWindowStartSeq)`. If `lastSeq` was behind that floor, `gap=true`. (3) Read `messages` where `channelId=C AND seq > start AND seq ≤ head` order by `seq` asc, limit 500 (viewers) or 2000 (realtime). (4) Project. Delayed: **omit** `tombstoneAt IS NOT NULL`; **omit** `createdAt + chatDelaySeconds > now`; omit `kind=system`? **No** — system messages are included, and they are also published with delay 0 (delay pipeline delivers system immediately). Realtime: include tombstoned rows as `{seq, messageId, tombstoneAt, tombstoneReason, senderUserId, body?}`; include `body` of tombstoned user messages only if `tombstoneAt > now-1h`. (5) Register the connection on `chat:{C}:{audience}`. Realtime also registers on `holds:{C}`. (6) Upsert `presence` (`lastSeenAt=now`). If `channel_heads.raidTargetChannelId` is set, return `redirectChannelId` and do not stay on A’s topics. (7) Return `{gap, audience, headSeq, uniqueViewers, chatDelaySeconds, messages[]}`. (8) Heartbeat: client repeats a cheap `Heartbeat(channelId, deviceId)` every 20s which only upserts `lastSeenAt` and returns redirect/count. Missing 45s ⇒ presence stale (op 10’s counter will drop them).
- **Atomic:** none required across 100k connections. Catch-up read is a bounded range.
- **Eventual:** subsequent seqs via the topic. Viewer count on a 5s sample.
- **Optimistic UI:** show catch-up, then append. On `jump` payload `{toSeq}`, drop local pending tail and request catch-up from `toSeq-150`.
- **Conflict:** none on subscribe. Slow consumer: **delayed** topic may emit `jump` if that connection’s backlog exceeds 200 messages. Realtime must not jump below a 2,000 backlog.
- **Fan-out rule (server, on each public insert):** evaluate **once** per delivery class, deliver the **same bytes** to all connections in that class. Do not re-run CatchUp per subscriber.

**Op 3 — DeleteMessage** (write)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `messageId`.
- **Auth:** caller’s `ents` at now has `isMod` on C, or staff.
- **Steps:** (1) Load message; wrong channel → `not_found` (do not distinguish missing vs other-channel). (2) If already tombstoned, return success with existing tombstone (idempotent). (3) Set `tombstoneAt=now`, `tombstoneBy=caller`, `tombstoneReason=mod_delete`. (4) Publish `{type:tombstone, seq, messageId}` to **both** realtime and delayed topics immediately (delayed viewers who already received the body must hide it; those who have not must never show it).
- **Atomic:** the tombstone patch.
- **Eventual:** VOD/clip reads see it on next fetch; no rewrite of clip assets.
- **Optimistic UI:** mod’s dashboard hides the body immediately.
- **Conflict:** two mods: first writer wins; second is idempotent success.

**Op 4 — BanOrTimeoutUser** (write; bulk purge)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `targetUserId`, `kind` `timeout|ban`, `durationSec` (required for timeout), `removeMode` `none|user_in_stream`, `publicReason`, `modNotes`.
- **Auth:** caller `isMod` on C. Then invariant 17: refuse if target is broadcaster, staff, self, or (active mod and caller is not broadcaster/staff).
- **Steps:** (1) Lock `(C, target)`. (2) Insert `moderation_sentences` with `startsAt=now`, `endsAt` as specified. (3) If `removeMode=user_in_stream`: let `streamId = liveStreamId`. If streamId present, tombstone all `messages` with that `streamId`, `senderUserId=target`, `kind=user`, `tombstoneAt IS NULL` (a **predicate update**, potentially hundreds of rows; cap synchronous at 1,000 rows then continue in the same lock via chunks of 1,000 until done — still before lock release). If no live stream, same predicate on `channelId` and `createdAt > now-24h`. Set `purgeFromSeq`/`purgeToSeq` to min/max seq touched (or head if none). (4) Publish **one** `{type:purge, targetUserId, fromSeq, toSeq, sentenceId}` to both topics. Clients drop matching sender in that seq range. (5) Release lock. Do not disconnect by deleting presence; SendMessage will fail; UI learns via the user’s next send or a user-topic `{cannot_chat}`.
- **Atomic:** sentence insert + user lock vs SendMessage. Purge of existing rows in the same lock so a message cannot be accepted after `startsAt` and remain unpurged.
- **Eventual:** VOD indexes; staff audit store already is the same row.
- **Optimistic UI:** mod hides the user’s tail immediately.
- **Conflict:** overlapping timeout: insert a **new** sentence; covering-at-T uses the union (any covering sentence blocks). Two mods timeout at once: both rows exist; later `endsAt` can only extend silence, never shorten, unless a revoke is issued. **Unban/untimeout:** `UnbanUser` (same auth as ban-on-mods rule) sets `revokedAt` on all covering sentences for that target; does not clear tombstones. Treat Unban as part of this family; implement as **Op 4b** with the same lock.

**Op 5 — UpdateChatMode** (write)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `settingsVersion`, patch of the settings fields in §4.
- **Auth:** broadcaster, editor, or staff. Moderators may patch `slowModeSeconds`, `subOnly`, `emoteOnly`, `followerModeMinutes`, `uniqueChat`, `chatDelaySeconds` but **not** `holdFirstTimeChatters` (broadcaster/staff only).
- **Steps:** (1) Validate enums/ranges. (2) `UPDATE channel_settings SET …, settingsVersion=v+1 WHERE channelId=C AND settingsVersion=v`. 0 rows → `settings_conflict` with current row. (3) Copy version onto `channel_heads`. (4) Insert `chat_mode_changes`. (5) Insert a `kind=system` `mode_change` message (seq++) so the log records the mode as of that seq. (6) If `chatDelaySeconds` changed, delay-pipeline uses the **new** value for messages with `createdAt` after this commit; already-queued delay items keep their `deliverAt`.
- **Atomic:** CAS version + system message + heads copy.
- **Eventual:** subscribers see the system line; clients flip local composer rules when they receive it (server still enforces).
- **Optimistic UI:** dashboard toggle; revert on `settings_conflict`.
- **Conflict:** CAS. Two devices of the broadcaster: one wins.

**Op 6 — GrantOrRevokeRole** (write)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`, `subjectUserId`, `role`, `op` `grant|revoke|extend`, optional `endsAt`.
- **Auth:** grant/revoke **moderator**: broadcaster or staff. **editor**: broadcaster or staff. **vip**: broadcaster, editor, or staff. Cannot grant to staff or to the broadcaster. Cannot self-grant.
- **Steps:** (1) `grant`: refuse if an active covering grant exists for that triple; insert a new interval `[now, endsAt or +infinity)`. If this is VIP and subject is an active mod, still allowed. (2) `extend`: load the unique active row; refuse if none; set `endsAt = max(old, new)`. (3) `revoke`: set `revokedAt=now` on all covering rows of that triple. (4) No change to historical `entitlementSnapshot`. (5) If revoking moderator, do not auto-unmod their past deletes.
- **Atomic:** overlap check + insert or revoke patch.
- **Eventual:** next SendMessage / CatchUp classification.
- **Optimistic UI:** roster.
- **Conflict:** two grants of the same VIP: second hits overlap → `already_granted`. Revoke vs SendMessage: a send in flight may still snapshot `moderator` if it computed `ents` before revoke; acceptable (in-flight set).

**Op 7 — ReplayVodChat** (read)

- **Inputs:** `streamId`, `fromOffsetMs`, `toOffsetMs`, `limit` default 150 max 500, `afterSeq` optional.
- **Auth:** if `now ≤ vodAvailableUntil` (or stream still live and VOD preview on): anyone, including anonymous. Else `vod_expired`. Staff may pass `includeTombstoned=true`.
- **Steps:** (1) Refuse if `toOffsetMs < fromOffsetMs` or range > 120,000ms. (2) Read `messages` where `streamId=S AND streamOffsetMs ≥ from AND streamOffsetMs < to AND seq > afterSeq` and (unless staff flag) `tombstoneAt IS NULL` and `kind in (user,system)`, order by `streamOffsetMs, seq`, limit. (3) Return snapshot fields as stored. **Do not** join current grants. **Do not** join `users.banned`.
- **Atomic:** n/a (read).
- **Eventual:** tombstones committed milliseconds ago must appear within the same 2s alignment budget.
- **Optimistic UI:** player may prefetch the next 30s window.
- **Conflict:** none. Live stream: offsets still work for the DVR; messages with `streamOffsetMs` in the future of the player head are not returned (`toOffsetMs` should be ≤ current media time).

**Op 8 — CreateClip** (write; **external** DVR/transcode)

- **Inputs:** `channelId`.
- **Auth:** signed-in user. Channel not suspended.
- **Steps:** (1) Load live stream; if none → `not_live`. (2) `nowOff = max(0, nowMs - startedAtMs - hlsDelayMs)`. `startOffsetMs = max(0, nowOff-30000)`. `durationMs = nowOff - startOffsetMs` (may be <30s at stream start). (3) Insert `clips` `pending`. (4) Call media plane `ExtractDvr({streamId, startOffsetMs, durationMs})` with idempotency key `clipId`. (5) Return `{clipId, status:pending, startOffsetMs, durationMs}`. **Op 8b — ClipTranscodeCallback** (external inbound, media plane): if signature valid, set `ready` + `assetUrl` or `failed`. Chat overlay is never copied; players call op 7 on `[startOffsetMs, startOffsetMs+durationMs)`.
- **Atomic:** clip row insert. Media extract is **not** in the same transaction; `pending` is the honest state.
- **Eventual:** `ready`. If callback never arrives, a 15-minute sweeper marks `failed`.
- **Optimistic UI:** “creating clip” with local start offset; do not show other users until `ready`.
- **Conflict:** 10 clips/hour/user/channel; excess → `clip_rate`. Two clicks: two clips (no nonce); that is allowed.

**Op 9 — ExecuteRaid** (write; subscription storm)

- **Inputs:** `fromChannelId`, `toChannelId`.
- **Auth:** caller is broadcaster of A (or staff).
- **Steps:** (1) A has `liveStreamId`, B has `liveStreamId`, `A≠B`, A’s `raidTarget` is null. (2) Insert `raids` with `viewerCountSnapshot = channel_heads.uniqueViewers` of A. (3) Set A’s stream `endedAt=now`, `channel_heads.liveStreamId=null`, `raidTargetChannelId=B`. (4) Insert system message on **A** `raid_outbound` (seq++ on A) and on **B** `raid_inbound` with the snapshot count (seq++ on B). (5) Publish on A’s topics `{type:redirect, toChannelId:B, raidId}`. **Do not** update 100k presence rows in this transaction. (6) Clients call op 2 on B; Heartbeat to A returns redirect. Set `joinedViaRaidId` on new B presence.
- **Atomic:** raid row + A end + redirect pointer + two system messages.
- **Eventual:** B’s `uniqueViewers` ramps over ≤45s as heartbeats land. That lag is allowed; a transactional 100k move is not required.
- **Optimistic UI:** A’s player switches to B after redirect. B sees the raid banner when the system seq arrives.
- **Conflict:** A already ending, or B not live → `cannot_raid`. Two raid clicks: CAS `raidTargetChannelId` from null.

**Op 10 — AccrueWatchPoints** (scheduled bulk, every 5 minutes) **and** **SampleViewerCounts** (every 5s)

Accrue:

- **Inputs:** `sliceId = floor((now-5min)/5min)` (accrue the slice that just closed).
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:** (1) For each channel with a stream whose `[startedAt, coalesce(endedAt, now))` overlaps the slice. (2) In chunks of 1,000 `userId`s: select distinct **non-null** `userId` from `presence` where `channelId=C` and `lastSeenAt` in the slice window **or** `joinedAt` in window and `lastSeenAt ≥ sliceStart` (a heartbeat that landed in the slice). Anonymous does not accrue. (3) For each user, `delta = ents(C,U,sliceEnd).isSub ? 12 : 10`. Insert ledger `watch_accrual` with `refId=sliceId` (unique). If unique conflict, skip (idempotent). Apply `balance+=delta`, `lifetimeEarned+=delta`, `balanceVersion+=1`. (4) Chunks are separate transactions. Restartable: unique ledger key.
- **Atomic:** per user: ledger insert + balance apply. Not one 100k-row transaction.
- **Eventual:** balances visible on next dashboard/heartbeat.
- **Optimistic UI:** none (a +10 animation may run on a client timer but the server is authoritative).
- **Conflict:** overlapping workers: unique `(channelId,userId,watch_accrual,sliceId)` wins.

SampleViewerCounts (same op family, every 5s): count distinct `userId` (coalesce to `deviceId` if null) from presence where `lastSeenAt > now-45s`; write `viewer_count_samples`; set `channel_heads.uniqueViewers`; if live, `peakUniqueViewers = max(peak, sample)`. **Do not** `COUNT` through visibility. **Do not** increment per heartbeat without GC; this resample is the source of the public number.

**Op 11 — PlacePredictionBet** (write)

- **Inputs:** `predictionId`, `outcomeId`, `points` i32 ≥ 10.
- **Auth:** caller U. Channel’s live stream must match `predictions.streamId`.
- **Steps:** (1) Load prediction `status=open` and `now < locksAt`; else `not_open`. (2) `UPDATE channel_point_balances SET balance=balance-points, inPlay=inPlay+points, balanceVersion=balanceVersion+1 WHERE channelId=C AND userId=U AND balance>=points`. 0 rows → `insufficient_points`. (3) Insert ledger `prediction_bet`. (4) If no bet row: insert. If bet exists with **same** outcome: `points +=`. If different outcome → **rollback** this transaction → `cannot_switch`. (5) `pool += points`; increment `countByOutcome[outcomeId]`.
- **Atomic:** debit + ledger + bet upsert + pool. Serialise on the balance row.
- **Eventual:** public totals (may be the maintained columns, not a live SUM).
- **Optimistic UI:** local balance decrease; **must revert** on `insufficient_points` / `not_open`. Never show someone else’s bet.
- **Conflict:** two devices: one debit wins, the other `insufficient_points` or adds to the same outcome if funds remain. Lock vs bet: if lock commits first, `not_open`.

**Op 12 — LockExpiredPredictions** (scheduled, every 1s)

- **Inputs:** none.
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:** `UPDATE predictions SET status='locked' WHERE status='open' AND locksAt≤now`. For each row locked, system-message on the channel. Do **not** use `now < locksAt` in the PlacePredictionBet **read ACL** as the only fence; the status word is the fence. PlacePredictionBet still checks both.
- **Atomic:** per-row status CAS `open→locked`.
- **Eventual:** clients hide the bet form when the system seq arrives; a bet in flight that passed the open check but commits after lock must lose on `status=open` predicate (include `AND status='open'` in the prediction update inside op 11, or re-read in the same txn).
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** overlapping lock workers: 0-row updates are no-ops.

**Op 13 — ResolvePrediction** (write; bulk payout)

- **Inputs:** `predictionId`, `winningOutcomeId` or `cancel=true`.
- **Auth:** broadcaster of C or staff. Status must be `locked` (or `open` if cancel before lock).
- **Steps:** (1) CAS `locked|open → resolving` (cancel: `→canceled` then refund path). 0 rows → `not_resolvable`. (2) Freeze `winningOutcomeId`. (3) **Cancel:** chunk bets of 1,000: `inPlay-=points`, `balance+=points`, ledger `prediction_refund`, `refunded=true`, `pool` down. Then `status=canceled`. (4) **Resolve:** `winningPool = countByOutcome[win]`. If `winningPool=0`, treat as cancel (refund all). Else for each winning bet chunk: `payout=floor(points * pool / winningPool)`; `inPlay-=points`; `balance+=payout`; ledger `prediction_payout`; remainder `pool - sum(payouts)` is burned (no ledger to the broadcaster). Losing bets: `inPlay-=points`, `payout=0`. (5) `status=resolved`, `resolvedAt=now`. System message.
- **Atomic:** the CAS to `resolving` in the caller’s request. **Payout is not** one transaction at 50k bets; chunks of 1,000 with idempotent `payout IS NULL` predicate. After all chunks, status `resolved`.
- **Eventual:** up to 30s to finish chunks. UI shows “paying out.”
- **Optimistic UI:** none on other people’s balances.
- **Conflict:** second resolve loses CAS. Chunk workers: `WHERE payout IS NULL` prevents double pay.

**Op 14 — DecideHeldMessage** (write)

- **Inputs:** `holdId`, `decision` `approve|deny`.
- **Auth:** realtime `isMod` on that channel.
- **Steps:** (1) Load hold `status=held`. (2) Lock `(C, senderUserId)`. (3) If deny: `status=denied`, `decidedBy`, `decidedAt`. Notify sender-topic. (4) If approve: **re-check covering sentence at now** (a ban while held means deny instead). Assign `seq=++publicSeq`, `createdAt=now` (approval time), `streamOffsetMs` from **now**, snapshot `ents` at **now** (not original type time), insert `messages`, link `publicMessageId`, `status=approved`. Fan-out as in op 1 step 21. Rate window was already charged at hold time; do not charge again.
- **Atomic:** hold CAS `held→approved|denied` + possible public insert.
- **Eventual:** delayed delivery.
- **Optimistic UI:** mod removes from queue.
- **Conflict:** two mods: `held→…` CAS, loser `already_decided`. Sender banned during hold: approve path converts to deny.

### 6. Scale and cost

Successful deployment, not a toy:

| Thing | Size / rate |
|---|---|
| Accounts | 40 million |
| Channels | 8 million |
| Concurrent live streams | 80,000 |
| Global concurrent viewers | ~6 million |
| **Hot channel unique viewers** | **100,000** |
| **Hot channel accepted public msg/s** | **1,000 peak**, 50–200 typical during the broadcast |
| Moderators on a hot channel | 5–30 realtime connections |
| Presence heartbeats, hot channel | 100,000 / 20s ≈ **5,000 upserts/s** |
| Viewer-count samples | 1 per channel / 5s; hot count scans 100k presence keys / 5s |
| Delayed topic connections, one key | **100,000** |
| Realtime topic connections, one key | ~30 |
| Fan-out bytes at peak | 1,000 msg/s × ~400 B × 100,000 ≈ **40 GB/s** if unicast; multicast/bus required |
| Catch-up reads at raid | 50,000 new subscribers × 1 range read in a few seconds |
| Messages on a 4h hot stream at 200/s avg | ~2.9 million durable rows |
| Messages on a 4h hot stream at 1,000/s | ~14 million |
| Global public messages/day | ~1e9 order of magnitude |
| Watch-accrual, hot channel / 5 min | **100,000** ledger+balance writes |
| Prediction bets, hot channel | 5,000–50,000 rows, one hot `predictions` row |
| VOD replay | 1 player = 1 range of ≤500 rows / ~5s of seeking |
| Clips/day/channel (hot) | hundreds pending against one DVR |

**What gets expensive**

- **Op 2 fan-out** is the axis: one `seq` must reach 100k delayed connections. Per-subscriber query re-execution is infeasible. Per-connection backlog and `jump` for viewers is mandatory; mods sit on a different topic so their 2,000-seq promise does not ride the firehose policy.
- **`channel_heads.publicSeq`** is a single-row increment at 1,000/s. That is the write hot spot. Rate state is **per user** and is fine at 1,000 distinct senders; it is not fine if implemented as one channel-wide timestamp (trap 4).
- **Op 1 automod** at 1,000/s is 1,000 outbound RPCs/s on one channel; budget 150ms and a bulk classifier, not a per-row human queue.
- **Op 4 purge** is a predicate update on `(streamId, senderUserId)`. At spam-cap (20/30s) a 4h stream is at most ~9,600 messages per user; still a set write, not 9,600 client deletes.
- **Op 9 raid:** 50k–100k **new subscribers** on B’s delayed topic in seconds, plus 50k CatchUp range reads. Cost is subscribe-join, not a 100k-row SQL update.
- **Op 10 accrual** is a 100k-row burst every 5 minutes **per hot channel**. Must be chunked and idempotent. A naive `INSERT SELECT` of 100k in the web request is a failure.
- **Op 13 payout** same shape on bets.
- **ReplayVodChat** is cheap per player if indexed on `(streamId, streamOffsetMs)` and not implemented as “replay live seq from 0.”
- **Presence GC / count:** scanning 100k keys every 5s is the price of invariant 16; a stored `isOnline` flag without a sample still needs the same scan or will drift (booking’s corpse-hold problem).

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**1. Uniform-audience subscription to one hot range.** Ordinary “live queries over rows” re-evaluate a query per subscriber. Here one channel has **100,000** subscribers to `messages where channelId=C AND seq > :head`, the rule is the same for all delayed viewers, and the product requires **one evaluation, N deliveries**. Two additional classes (`realtime`, `holds`) are tiny. *Concession:* cap a channel at a few thousand viewers, or replace live tail with 2s polling of a precomputed 150-row snapshot (polling 100k × 2s is 50k reads/s on one range — only honest if you also drop the 500ms-feel promise). *Cheat:* claim 100k concurrent live chat and implement per-viewer live queries or 100k inbox rows; or “it worked in the first-party client because we didn’t open that many sockets.”

**2. Write-rate limits as serial constraints over recent history.** Ordinary auth is role checks on a user row. Slow mode, unique-chat, and spam are **predicates on the user’s recent write log**, under a per-user lock, counting holds and denies, with exemptions from **intervals at T**. *Concession:* slow mode as a channel-wide 1 msg/s firehose limiter, advertised as such. *Cheat:* keep per-user slow mode, unique-chat, and “second device cannot bypass,” then enforce only in the UI or with a racy read-then-insert.

**3. Interval membership at T, plus write-once snapshots.** Ordinary `role` enums and `banned` booleans cannot express send-at-T, badge-at-T_send, and timeout-until. The clock must not be a hidden writer that a sweep job is required to make true (timeouts expire by interval; presence is live by `lastSeenAt`; delay is a delivery class). *Concession:* no VOD badges, no VOD at all, and timeouts that only round to 1-minute flag flips, advertised as approximate. *Cheat:* ship VOD replay and “timeout 10 seconds” while filtering `WHERE NOT users.banned` at read time (trap 1–2).

**4. Predicate tombstone plus a range invalidation event.** Ban-with-purge is `tombstone WHERE streamId=S AND sender=U`, then **one** `purge` event so 100k clients do not wait for 500 single-id deletes. Ordinary CRUD deletes by primary key; ordinary live queries would re-run 100k times on each of 500 patches. *Concession:* purge only the last 20 messages by id, advertised as “recent messages.” *Cheat:* claim retroactive removal across the stream and VOD while only hiding in the live 150-message client cache.

**5. Two clocks, two delivery classes, scheduled bulk that must not become 1,000 cron rows/s.** Public `seq` (live order) and `streamOffsetMs` (video order) are both stored. Delay is a **pipeline** (or a delayed topic) that re-checks tombstone, not 1,000 `scheduled_jobs` inserts/s and not `now()` in the read ACL. Watch-point accrual and prediction payout are **chunked, idempotent, system-authorized** bulk writes, 1e5 rows, not a user mutation that touches every viewer. *Concession:* no chat delay; no channel points; VOD chat aligned to wall clock (“close enough”). *Cheat:* keep delay, points, and VOD-sync marketing; implement delay in the client, accrual as 100k online `UPDATE`s inside a click, and VOD as `ORDER BY createdAt`.

**Softening that is a cheat, restated.** If you still claim (i) 100k concurrent viewers on one channel, (ii) server-enforced slow/sub/follower/spam rules across devices, (iii) mods can intercept via delay, (iv) VOD/clip chat stays in sync and honors ban-purge after the fact, and (v) badges/roles as of send, then uniform multicast, per-user serial rate state, interval grants/sentences, durable tombstones with range purge, and the two-clock log are the product. Capping the hot channel, dropping VOD, or moving enforcement to the client while keeping those claims is cheating the axis.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice:** a single ordered chat log with a monotonic sequence; pub/sub fan-out rather than per-user inboxes; IRC/Twitch-style mode exemptions; automod hold queue; timeout/ban as moderation sentences; tombstone rather than physical delete; clip as a DVR cut with chat overlaid from comments; raid as a viewer redirect; channel points as a ledger; prediction pools with floor remainder burned; presence via heartbeats; approximate viewer counts; HLS latency as a frozen offset; reconnect as cursor catch-up; idempotent client nonces; CAS on settings versions and prediction status.

**Standard genre rules, specified tightly rather than invented:** Twitch-like slow mode, follower-only with a minutes threshold, sub-only, emote-only, unique-chat, 20/30s spam, 0/2/4/6s mod delay, VIP exemptions without realtime, mods cannot mod other mods, purge as an optional modifier on the sentence, unban does not untombstone, held messages publish at approval time, VOD comments keyed by content offset, hosts do not merge chat, raids do.

**Invented for the exercise (to keep the axis load-bearing):** the explicit **two-topic** delivery class (`realtime` vs `delayed`) as a capability boundary rather than a client sleep; **`jump`** as a first-class viewer promise (mods must not jump); purge as a **range event** with `fromSeq`/`toSeq`; founder badges as insert-only cosmetics; `joinedViaRaidId` as a stream-scoped badge; watch accrual at **100k rows / 5 min** as a required bulk job; prediction payout chunking with a `resolving` status; hot window **50,000** seqs with catch-up **500/2,000**; automod timeout ⇒ hold; spam window counting **denies and holds**.

**Not invented, but skipped in shallow chat apps and here mandatory:** roles/subs/timeouts as **intervals**; **snapshots** on messages; durable vs live tail as two access paths; structural viewer/pool counts; per-user **serial** rate limits.

I have not load-tested 100k-subscriber multicast or 40 GB/s unicast-equivalent, and I have not run the delay pipeline against a 1,000/s tombstone race. The traps are checkable by the observations in §3; those do not depend on production traffic.
