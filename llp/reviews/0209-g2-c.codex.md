## Weakest seam

The weakest seam is corpus-to-runner identity. The scheduler test creates the appearance of corpus execution by loading a row and comparing its authored ordinals, while substituting an unrelated graph, fixture, mutations, and authorization identities. This allows structural corpus checks and behavioral scheduler checks to pass independently even when their semantic carriers diverge. A single runner that consumes the frozen case end-to-end would remove both material overclaims.
