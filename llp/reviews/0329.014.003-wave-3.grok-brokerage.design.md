### 1. Product contract

**Fathom** is a US-retail brokerage for NMS equities and ETFs (no options, crypto, futures, or shorts). Users are the primary holder, a joint holder, a time-bounded limited power of attorney, licensed support, and compliance. An **account** is the books; a **user** acts only through a currently active `account_parties` grant. Cash and margin accounts are both first-class.

Money is integer **USD micros** (`1 USD = 1_000_000`). Quantity is integer **share nanos** (`1 share = 1_000_000_000`), always a multiple of 10 (eight displayed decimals). Binary floating point is never used for money or quantity.

Fathom makes these promises:

1. For every tradable instrument the app shows bid, bid size, ask, ask size, last sale, last size, and session (pre-market, regular, after-hours, closed), labeled with the feed. A user with `quote_tier = realtime_sip` sees the SIP tape coalesced to a **100 ms** latest snapshot. A user with `quote_tier = delayed` sees that same snapshot **emitted 15 minutes later**, never the live tape. Risk, buying power, and liquidation always use the live risk mark, which the delayed customer is not shown.
2. A quote snapshot for one `(instrument, session_class, feed)` is the same sequence number for every entitled watcher. The product does not deliver every SIP tick to every watcher; it delivers a latest-value stream with a lag bound of 100 ms while the book is moving, and it delivers a last-sale snapshot immediately when the coalescer has not flushed in that window.
3. Portfolio **equity** = cash + dividend receivables + Σ `qty_remaining × customer_visible_mark`, with every term taken from one `(mark_seq, adjustment_generation)` pair. **Today’s P&L** is `equity_now − equity_at_prior_regular_close_adjusted − net_external_cash_today + 0`, and a split overnight does not invent a −75% day. A cash dividend is accrued on the ex-date so the ex-date print drop is not a fake loss.
4. **Total return** vs tax lots (cost basis) is a different number from today’s P&L and is labeled as such.
5. A tap on Buy/Sell is not a fill. The server first **accepts** (buying power or shares reserved, `client_order_id` recorded) and then **routes**. The UI may show `accepted` immediately after the server receipt; it may not show `filled` until a venue execution has been booked. Partial fills are first-class: remaining quantity stays live, each booked fill moves cash, lots, and reservations together.
6. Regular session (09:30:00.000–16:00:00.000 America/New_York, including early closes) accepts market and limit, whole and fractional, `day` or `gtc` (GTC lasts 90 calendar days). Extended hours (04:00–09:30 and 16:00–20:00) accept **limit only**. Market orders and extended-hours orders on a halted instrument are rejected. All open orders on an instrument are cancelled, not price-adjusted, when a corporate action applies.
7. Whole shares route to an external venue. The fractional remainder of a mixed order is filled by Fathom as principal. The customer sees **one** fill at the weighted average; internally there may be two executions. Minimum buy: `0.00000001` shares and `$1.00` notional.
8. Sells allocate tax lots at **accept** time: `fifo` (default) or `specific` with lot ids and as-adjusted quantities tagged with `adjustment_generation`. That designation is what the 1099 uses. Average-cost display is not the tax method.
9. A margin account with `equity_at_prior_regular_close < $25,000` is allowed **three** day trades in any window of five New York equity **session dates** (today plus the prior four sessions, including early closes, excluding holidays). The fourth closing day-trade is rejected. At or above `$25,000` the account may be flagged PDT and receives day-trade buying power equal to `4 × excess_maintenance_at_prior_close`. A PDT flag is sticky for 90 calendar days. The `$25,000` test uses prior regular-close equity plus settled cash since that close, **not** live unrealized P&L.
10. Corporate actions (forward/reverse splits, cash dividends, symbol changes) change **current** economic state (lots, positions, receivables, quote factors, previous close) as of the effective session. They do not rewrite as-executed fills, mailed statements, or filed 1099s.
11. Monthly statements freeze at T+2 after month-end and are bit-identical on re-download. Annual 1099-B freezes when generated in January and is the tax document even if a later split changes in-app history.
12. Watchlists sync across devices. Public **watch count** on an instrument is an exact integer Fathom publishes; it is not inferred by counting rows the caller can see.
13. Two devices share one buying-power fence. If both try to spend the same cash or the same shares, the server’s accept order decides; the loser gets a durable rejection visible on both devices.
14. Offline, a device may show last quotes and last portfolio **labeled with `mark_seq` and a staleness clock**, edit watchlists, and queue **drafts**. A draft is not accepted, reserved, or filled. Reconnect replays drafts by `client_order_id`; authorization and PDT/buying power are re-checked then.
15. Authority is evaluated at **server accept**, not at draft creation. A revoked POA, a closed account, or a newly PDT-restricted account rejects the queued draft even if it looked valid when written.
16. Customers never see another account’s orders, fills, positions, day-trade count, or buying power. Refusals use generic reasons (`not tradable`, `insufficient buying power`, `order rejected`) and do not disclose halts-vs-entitlement, PDT-vs-cash, or a counterparty’s restrictions. Support sees PII only under a dual-controlled break-glass grant that is itself time-bounded and logged.
17. Every accepted order, fill, cash entry, and lot event is retrievable for seven years. Replaying fills and lot events through a given `adjustment_generation` reproduces positions at that generation.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

1. **[Money]** Amounts are `usd_micros` integers and quantities are `share_nanos` integers (multiple of 10); no API, sum, or mark uses binary floating point.
2. **[Money]** Cash of an account equals the sum of its `cash_entries`; position quantity equals the sum of remaining lot quantity at the instrument’s current `adjustment_generation`; both are reconstructible from append-only events.
3. **[Money]** `equity = cash_balance + dividend_receivable − pending_withdrawal + Σ remaining_qty × risk_mark`, computed at one `(mark_seq, adjustment_generation)`.
4. **[Money, Time]** Today’s P&L uses `equity_at_prior_regular_close` restated by every corporate action whose effective session is today or later, plus net external cash today, and never uses a raw pre-split close against post-split quantity.
5. **[Money]** Buying power and share availability are checked against reservations plus booked fills, never against UI marks alone; available shares = position remaining − open sell reservations.
6. **[Money, Time]** An order reserves cash or shares at accept and releases only on fill, cancel, expire, or reject; reservation + filled notional never exceeds the accepted reservation.
7. **[Money, Time]** A fill is booked at most once per `venue_exec_id` (and at most once per `internal_exec_id` for principal fractional legs); retries and duplicate reports do not duplicate lots or cash.
8. **[Money]** As-executed fills are immutable. Corrections are new reversing fills linked to the original, not edits.
9. **[Money, Time]** Lot remaining quantity and remaining basis at generation *g* equal the original fill after applying every corporate-action factor with `effective_generation ≤ g` minus closes booked at generations ≤ *g*.
10. **[Money, Time]** A sell’s lot allocation is frozen on the accepted order; later FIFO state, later specific-lot wishes, and later splits cannot reassign a fill already booked.
11. **[Money, Time]** Wash-sale disallowed loss is added to the replacement lot’s basis; the 30 calendar-day window is before and after the sale date, counted on NY dates, and is not `now() − 30 days` in UTC.
12. **[Money, Time]** On a split’s effective session, `qty × mark` using mixed generations is forbidden: a reader sees either all pre-generation or all post-generation facts for that instrument.
13. **[Money, Time]** Issued statements and 1099s are frozen `as_stated` snapshots; later generations do not change their bytes or totals.
14. **[Money, Time]** A day trade is a **closing order** (all fills of that `order_id` on that session date, not each print) that reduces quantity opened **the same session** on the same account and instrument. Overnight inventory is closed first and does not count.
15. **[Money, Time]** Day-trade count for the PDT test is the sum, over the current session date plus the previous four **session dates** on the NYSE calendar, of per-symbol-day counts. The per-symbol-day count is `max(opening_orders_at_least_partially_closed_same_session, closing_orders_that_closed_any_same_session_qty)` using the pairing in operation 6. Calendar days, UTC rolling 120 hours, and `COUNT(fills)` are not this number.
16. **[Money, Time]** The PDT equity test uses `equity_at_prior_regular_close + settled_cash_delta_since_that_close`. Live marks cannot open or close the `$25,000` gate.
17. **[Money, Time]** Closing fills that can change the PDT count take the account’s `pdt_fence`; two closing orders cannot both observe count 3 and both become the fourth.
18. **[Time]** Session membership of an event is the NYSE calendar in `America/New_York`, including early closes. An order at 16:00:00.000 is not in the regular session; 15:59:59.999 is.
19. **[Secrecy, Other people’s data]** Customer-visible marks for a delayed account are the delayed feed. Live risk marks do not leave the risk path onto any websocket, portfolio field, or error the delayed user can read.
20. **[Secrecy, Other people’s data]** Watch count is a maintained public counter. Counts of orders, day trades, holders, or watchers computed from caller-visible rows are not customer answers.
21. **[Secrecy, Time, Other people’s data]** A user may trade only if an `account_parties` row covers them with `trade` at the accept instant (`valid_from ≤ accept_time < valid_until` and `revoked_at` null). Historical membership is not current authority.
22. **[Secrecy]** Quote entitlements, PDT status, and restriction codes are not disclosed by distinct error strings.
23. **[Money, Time]** `client_order_id` reused with the same account and same payload digest returns the original accept; a different payload is rejected.
24. **[Money, Time]** Every venue request is preceded by a durable outbox row with a stable venue idempotency key and frozen payload; an unknown timeout is not converted to failure with a new key.
25. **[Time]** Quote sequence numbers for a given `(instrument, session_class, feed)` are monotonic. A client may skip snapshots but may not apply an older `mark_seq` over a newer one.

### 3. The traps, ranked

**T1 — Temporal: three histories of the same lot (the membership-at-a-time trap).**  
**Wrong version:** `UPDATE fills SET qty = qty*4, price = price/4; UPDATE positions SET qty = qty*4;` after a 4-for-1 split, and generate statements by querying fills.  
**Why wrong:** A fill is three facts. **As-executed** is what the venue did (10 shares at `$400` on 15 Aug, symbol `ABC`). **As-adjusted** is the economic equivalent now (40 shares at `$100` after the 2 Sep split). **As-stated** is what was frozen on the August statement and what the IRS was told. Collapsing them rewrites a mailed PDF, changes a filed 1099, and makes “what did I own on 15 Aug?” unanswerable. Day P&L is a fourth clock: `equity_at_prior_close` must be restated so 40 × `$100` close equals 10 × `$400` close. Using post-split quantity against the unadjusted close reports a −75% day that did not happen. Dividends add a receivable clock: the print drops on the ex-date, cash arrives on the payable date; without an accrued receivable, ex-date P&L is a fake loss. Symbol change (`FB` → `META`) is an interval on the identifier, not a rename in place: statements keep `FB`, the live ticker is `META`, the `instrument_id` is stable.  
**Observation:** Issue the August statement, apply a 4-for-1 before the next open, open the app at 04:01. Naive: today −75%, August PDF now shows 40 shares, 1099-B proceeds drift. Correct: today ≈ $0 before the open, August PDF unchanged, live lots 40, fill row still 10 @ `$400`.

**T2 — Visibility + fan-out: a quote is not a cell on the instrument row.**  
**Wrong version:** `instruments.last_price`, everyone live-queries it; portfolio value uses that cell; delayed users get the same cell.  
**Why wrong:** Last sale ≠ NBBO mid ≠ official close ≠ extended last; pre-market last is not the mark for overnight equity; a halted instrument still has a last. Entitlement is a visibility rule on a **public** stream: leaking the live mark through portfolio ticks, through “order rejected because the ask moved,” or through a real-time websocket the delayed user can open, *is* giving them the SIP. Fan-out is the other half: AAPL at peak is tens of thousands of SIP quote updates per second and millions of watchers. Per-watcher rows, or one row plus a naive live query to two million clients, either explode write amplification or miss the 100 ms bound. Coalescing to latest-value snapshots is the product, not a degradation, but coalescing must still produce one `mark_seq` per feed so two devices do not show different “now.” Public watch count is a structural count: `COUNT(*)` under caller visibility returns 0 or 1 for a user who can see only their watchlist rows.  
**Observation:** A delayed account that holds only AAPL and has an empty watchlist still sees equity tick every 100 ms in regular hours — they have the live tape. A popularity badge implemented as a filtered count shows “1” on AAPL. Two devices of a real-time user diverge by dollars because they applied different last prints as “the” mark.

**T3 — Temporal + regulatory count: PDT is pairing over session dates, not `COUNT(*)` of today’s sells.**  
**Wrong version:** `day_trades += 1` on each sell; reset at midnight UTC; unlock PDT when live equity ≥ `$25,000`.  
**Why wrong:** (a) A day trade exists only when a **closing order** eats quantity **opened that session**; selling overnight inventory is not a day trade. (b) Partial prints of one order are one transaction. (c) `buy, buy, sell-all` is two day trades; `buy, sell, sell` of the same opening is two; `buy 100, sell 100` in two fills is one. (d) The window is five **session dates** on the NYSE calendar, including early-close days, not 120 hours and not the user’s timezone. A Monday count includes Friday–Monday plus the two prior sessions; a Wednesday holiday disappears from the window. (e) Live AAPL spike cannot mint `$25,000` of PDT equity; the test is prior close plus settled cash. (f) Two concurrent closing orders that each see count = 3 would both become the fourth unless they share a fence. (g) The count is about the **account**, not the user; a joint holder’s trade counts.  
**Observation:** Buy 100 Mon, sell 100 Tue: naive counts 1, FINRA 0. Buy 100 and sell 100 in pre-market Monday with UTC reset on Sunday 20:00 ET: naive may drop Friday’s trades early. Two devices each place a closing sell with count = 3: naive books four day trades on a sub-$25k margin account. Equity prints through `$25,000` at 10:02 and a fourth day trade is accepted; at 10:03 the print is `$24,800`.

**T4 — Concurrency + external saga: the fill is not the click, and cancel races the venue.**  
**Wrong version:** On tap, debit cash, credit shares, `status=filled`. On timeout, retry with a new venue id. Cancel sets `cancelled` locally.  
**Why wrong:** The venue may partial-fill, reject, replace, or fill after a cancel request. A timeout after the venue committed, retried under a new key, duplicates the position. A cancel-ack and a fill can arrive in either order; both “winning” creates shares without cash or cash without shares. Fractional legs split: 10.25 shares is 10 at the venue and 0.25 principal; one customer lot, two executions, two idempotency keys. Buying power is reserved at accept, consumed by fills, released by the remainder; treating accept as a fill overstates equity and PDT.  
**Observation:** Kill the worker after the venue fill and before the local book; retry. Naive: 2× shares, 2× cash debit. Send cancel, then a late partial: naive shows cancelled and still books the fill against a released reservation, overdrawing cash.

**T5 — Concurrency + merge: one buying-power fence, two devices, drafts that are not money.**  
**Wrong version:** Each device subtracts from cached BP, labels the order sent, last-write-wins on the order row; watchlist merge is used for orders.  
**Why wrong:** Cached `$1,000` on two phones is not `$2,000`. Device clocks do not order money. An offline “success” that another person (or the same person’s other device) can already see as a fill cannot be unwound by CRDT merge. Drafts may merge by `client_order_id`; accepts may not. Watchlists *can* union by instrument. Quotes may not be averaged or last-write-wins by device time; newer `mark_seq` wins.  
**Observation:** Two devices each send a `$800` market buy against `$1,000`. Naive accepts both. Correct: one accept, one `insufficient buying power` on both screens, one outbox message.

**T6 — Authority + lots: who may act, and which lot is sold, is an instant plus a generation.**  
**Wrong version:** The user who opened the account can always trade; specific-lot ids are current `lot_id`s with live remaining qty; FIFO is computed at fill time from then-open lots.  
**Why wrong:** Joint/POA membership is an interval. A revoked POA’s offline draft must fail at accept. FIFO computed at fill time (minutes later) can pick lots opened by a later order on the other device, so the 1099 does not match the ticket the customer confirmed. Specific-lot quantities are as-adjusted at a generation; after a split the same `lot_id` has a new remaining quantity — which is why open orders are cancelled on CA rather than reinterpreted. Support break-glass is not the holder and must not mint orders without an auditable grant.  
**Observation:** Designate lot L (6 shares) for a 6-share sell; before fill, the other device sells 2 of L FIFO on another order; naive fill oversells L or silently substitutes. Revoke POA, reconnect that phone: naive routes the draft.

### 4. Data model

All timestamps are UTC instants. Session dates are dates in `America/New_York` that appear in `market_sessions` with `is_open = true`. Identifiers are 128-bit opaques.

**Identity and authority (time-bounded grants)**

- `users(user_id PK, status enum[active,disabled], created_at)`
- `devices(device_id PK, user_id FK, public_key, status, last_cursor, last_seen_at)`
- `accounts(account_id PK, kind enum[cash,margin], status enum[open,closing_only,frozen,closed], quote_tier enum[delayed,realtime_sip], pdt_flagged_at nullable, pdt_flagged_until nullable, bp_version int64, pdt_version int64, created_at)`
- `account_parties(party_id PK, account_id FK, user_id FK, role enum[primary,joint,poa,support_break_glass], perms set[view,trade,withdraw,statements], valid_from, valid_until nullable, revoked_at nullable, grant_reason)`. Historical rows remain. **Active** = `valid_from ≤ t AND (valid_until is null OR t < valid_until) AND revoked_at is null`.
- `agreements(agreement_id PK, user_id FK, kind enum[customer,margin,sip_nonpro], accepted_at, blob_ref)`

**Calendar and instruments (time-bounded identifiers)**

- `market_sessions(session_date PK, regular_open_utc, regular_close_utc, ext_open_utc, ext_close_utc, is_open, is_early_close, early_close_utc nullable)`
- `instruments(instrument_id PK, cusip, primary_exchange, status enum[tradable,halted,auction,delisted], fractionable bool, tick_size_micros, lot_size_nanos, trading_state, cumulative_split_num int64, cumulative_split_den int64, adjustment_generation int64, watch_count int64, created_at)`
- `instrument_identifiers(instrument_id FK, id_type enum[symbol,old_symbol], value, valid_from, valid_until nullable, PK(instrument_id,id_type,valid_from))`. Current symbol = the row covering now.
- `quote_entitlements(user_id FK, feed enum[sip_realtime,sip_delayed], valid_from, valid_until nullable, PK(user_id,feed,valid_from))`

**Quotes (hot latest-value; not per watcher)**

- `quote_snapshots(instrument_id FK, session_class enum[pre,regular,post,closed], feed enum[sip_realtime,sip_delayed,risk], mark_seq int64, bid_micros, bid_size_nanos, ask_micros, ask_size_nanos, last_micros, last_size_nanos, last_sale_time, official_close_micros nullable, official_close_session_date nullable, adjustment_generation, updated_at, PK(instrument_id,session_class,feed))`. Overwritten in place by the coalescer. **Derived from** the tape; source of customer marks.
- `official_closes(instrument_id FK, session_date, close_micros, adjustment_generation_at_close, PK(instrument_id,session_date))`. Append-only.
- `quote_subscriptions` are **not** durable rows. The fan-out plane holds `(device_id, instrument_id, feed, last_ack_seq)` in memory and may spill a resume cursor on `devices`.

**Watchlists**

- `watchlists(watchlist_id PK, account_id FK, name, sort_key, version)`
- `watchlist_items(watchlist_id FK, instrument_id FK, added_at, added_by_user_id, PK(watchlist_id,instrument_id))`
- `watch_count_shards(instrument_id FK, shard_no 0..63, count int64, PK(instrument_id,shard_no))`. `instruments.watch_count` is the sum, maintained in the same transaction as item insert/delete (shard = `hash(account_id) mod 64`).

**Cash, lots, positions**

- `cash_entries(entry_id PK, account_id FK, delta_micros, reason enum[deposit,withdrawal,buy_debit,sell_credit,dividend,cash_in_lieu,fee,reversal], ref_type, ref_id, session_date, settled_session_date, created_at)`, unique `(ref_type,ref_id,reason)` where applicable. **Append-only.**

### 5. The hard operations

**1. SubscribeQuotes** (read, fan-out)  
**Inputs:** device, list of `instrument_id` (max 200), requested feed.  
**Authorization:** active `view` on some account; feed is `sip_realtime` only if `quote_tier = realtime_sip` and a live `quote_entitlements` row covers now; otherwise forced to `sip_delayed`.  
**Steps:** (1) Resolve current symbol and `adjustment_generation`. (2) Attach the device to the fan-out group `(instrument, session_class_now, feed)`. (3) Send the current `quote_snapshots` row as snapshot 0. (4) Stream later rows with `mark_seq > last_ack`. (5) On session-class change (09:30, 16:00), send an explicit class switch; do not reuse last extended print as regular mark.  
**Atomic:** entitlement check vs first snapshot.  
**Eventually consistent:** attach/detach of the device in the fan-out plane (bound 1 s).  
**Optimistic UI:** last cached snapshot with `stale=true` until snapshot 0.  
**Conflict:** a tier downgrade drops the realtime group and the next message is delayed or a `feed_changed` notice; the client must not keep the old socket.

**2. ReadPortfolioAndDayPnl** (read)  
**Inputs:** account_id, optional `as_of_mark_seq` per instrument (else latest entitled).  
**Authorization:** active `view`.  
**Steps:** (1) Take `cash_balances`, open `dividend_receivables`, and `positions` at each instrument’s current generation. (2) For each instrument, load `quote_snapshots` for the **customer feed** and the session class required by the product: while regular is open, mark = regular snapshot; overnight, mark = official close of last regular session, split-adjusted. (3) Reject a row whose `adjustment_generation` ≠ position generation (retry after CA flip). (4) `mv = Σ qty_nanos * mark_micros / 1e9`. (5) Load `account_session_marks` for prior session date; restate `equity_at_prior_regular_close` by the product of split ratios effective after that close. (6) `net_external_cash_today` = sum of cash_entries today with reason in `{deposit,withdrawal}`. (7) Return equity, day P&L, total return vs lot basis, `mark_seq` vector, generations.  
**Atomic:** the returned vector of marks, cash, qty, and generation is one consistent cut.  
**Eventually consistent:** websocket push of step 7 as quotes move (coalesced to 100 ms per account, not per tick).  
**Optimistic UI:** cached cut labeled stale; never a locally computed fill.  
**Conflict:** a CA generation flip invalidates the cut; client must re-read.

**3. PlaceOrder** (write)  
**Inputs:** account_id, `client_order_id`, instrument, side, type, tif, session_window, qty_nanos, limit_micros?, lot_method, specific `lot_allocations`?, payload digest.  
**Authorization:** active `trade` party at **now**; account not `frozen`/`closed`; `closing_only` allows sells that do not open a day trade.  
**Steps:** (1) Idempotency: same digest → return stored accept; different digest → reject. (2) Load instrument, calendar, now → session_class. Reject market in extended, reject if halted for market, reject qty not multiple of 10, reject buy notional < `$1`. (3) Lock `accounts.bp_version` (the buying-power fence). (4) Load cash, positions, reservations, `pdt_window_cache`, `account_session_marks` prior, `symbol_day_trades` for this instrument today. (5) If buy: `reserve_cash = type==limit ? limit*qty/1e9 : ceil(risk_ask * qty / 1e9 * 1.03)` (3% market collar). Reject if `cash − other cash reservations < reserve_cash` (cash account) or if margin BP (`overnight_bp` or `dtbp` per PDT flag and tif/session) is insufficient. (6) If sell: `reserve_qty = qty`; reject if `position.qty − other share reservations < qty`. (7) If sell, run operation 6’s **prediction** on this order as a closing order; if it would raise window count to 4 and prior-close PDT equity < `$25,000` and account is margin, reject. (8) If `fifo`, snapshot open lots oldest `acquired_session_date`, then `opened_fill_id`, assigning qty until filled; if `specific`, verify each lot remaining at current generation and that the sums equal qty. Write `lot_allocations`. (9) Insert `orders` `accepted`, `reservations`, `order_events`, `venue_outbox` `new` with stable key; increment `bp_version`. (10) Return accept receipt.  
**Atomic:** 3–9. The venue call is **not** in this transaction.  
**Eventually consistent:** outbox dispatch (operation 13).  
**Optimistic UI:** `submitting` until accept receipt; then `accepted`. Never `filled`. Offline: `draft` only.  
**Conflict:** concurrent PlaceOrder/Cancel on the same account serialize on `bp_version`; one may reject for BP or shares.

**4. ApplyVenueReport** (write, external inbound)  
**Inputs:** verified provider message (`provider`, `provider_msg_id`, kind, `venue_exec_id`, qty, price, venue_time).  
**Authorization:** venue inbox only (mTLS + signed payload), not customers.  
**Steps:** (1) Insert `venue_reports`; duplicate `provider_msg_id` → return prior result. (2) Lock the order. (3) `ack`: set `routed`, store `venue_order_id`. (4) `reject`/`expire`/`cancel_ack`: if `remaining > 0` and no in-flight fill with higher venue_time, release remaining reservation, set terminal status, cash/shares returned. If a fill with later processing arrives after cancel_ack, step 5 still books it **if** `venue_exec_id` is new and the venue declares the fill valid; reservation is allowed to go temporarily over-released and is repaired by a signed reversing cash/qty pair — **preferred product:** treat fill-after-cancel as a new `accepted` child order booked against BP; if BP insufficient, break-glass receivable and `accounts.status = closing_only`. Specify the preferred path: **fill-after-cancel books**, reservation may go negative on that order only, account BP fence is re-checked, shortfall becomes a `cash_entries` debit that may take cash negative and freeze withdrawals (not a silent skip). (5) `partial`/`fill`: unique `venue_exec_id`; insert `fills` as-executed; `filled_qty += qty`; `remaining -= qty`; consume reservation (`cash` for buys = `qty*price/1e9`, shares for sells = qty); insert `cash_entries`; for buy, insert `tax_lot_events open` and `tax_lots` at current generation; for sell, apply frozen `lot_allocations` in order, inserting `close` events (if a later CA cancelled the order this fill cannot exist). (6) If this fill is on a sell, lock `pdt_version` and run operation 6 **commit** for this order. (7) If remaining = 0, status `filled` and zero reservation. (8) Principal fractional: a sibling `internal_exec_id` fill may be produced by Fathom’s internalization worker under the same order; it follows the same booking with `liquidity=principal_fraction`. Customer display averages the two.  
**Atomic:** per report: inbox row, order, fill uniqueness, cash, lots, reservation, PDT. The network is not atomic with this.  
**Eventually consistent:** UI push, 1099 is not live.  
**Optimistic UI:** none on this path.  
**Conflict:** cancel vs fill: both reports apply; fill uniqueness and remaining ≥ 0 (except the documented fill-after-cancel shortfall) are the guards. Duplicate exec ids replay.

**5. CancelOrder** (write)  
**Inputs:** order_id.  
**Authorization:** active `trade` on the account, or system expiry.  
**Steps:** (1) Lock order. Reject if terminal. (2) If never routed, release reservation, `cancelled`, no outbox. (3) If routed, insert `venue_outbox` `cancel` with stable key derived from `order_id`; status stays `partial`/`routed` until cancel_ack or fill-to-zero.  
**Atomic:** local decision + outbox insert.  
**Eventually consistent:** venue cancel.  
**Optimistic UI:** `cancel pending`, remaining qty still live.  
**Conflict:** a fill that commits first reduces remaining; cancel_ack cancels only what is left.

**6. EvaluateDayTrades** (read used as a write helper; the rolling-window count)  
**Inputs:** account_id, instrument_id, session_date, proposed or committed closing `order_id`.  
**Authorization:** same as PlaceOrder / ApplyVenueReport.  
**Steps:** (1) `prior_qty` = position at start of `session_date` (lots open before first fill of that date, at the generation in force **that morning**). (2) Walk today’s fills for this instrument ordered by `(venue_time, fill_id)`, aggregated **by order_id**. Maintain `prior_left = prior_qty` and a FIFO queue of today’s buy orders with `unclosed_qty`. (3) A buy order appends to the queue. (4) A sell order first reduces `prior_left` (not a day trade). Residual reduces today’s buy queue FIFO. If it reduced any queue qty, this sell is a **closing-against-same-day** order. Each buy order whose `unclosed_qty` fell is an **opening-order-closed**. (5) `symbol_day = max(count of such buys, count of such sells)`. (6) Window = this `session_date` plus the four prior rows in `market_sessions` with `is_open`. `window_count = sum(symbol_day_trades.day_trade_count)` over that set. (7) PDT equity = `account_session_marks[prior].equity + sum(cash_entries settled since that close with reason deposit/withdrawal)`. (8) Gate: margin and not flagged and window_count would be ≥ 4 and PDT equity < `25_000_000_000` micros → deny.  
**Atomic with the fill that commits it:** update `symbol_day_trades` and `pdt_window_cache` under `pdt_version`.  
**Eventually consistent:** the customer’s PDT badge (≤ 1 s).  
**Optimistic UI:** a warning if `window_count == 3` and this sell would pair; not a local increment.  
**Conflict:** two sells serialize on `pdt_version`; the second re-runs the walk.

**7. ApplyCorporateAction** (bulk, scheduled)  
**Inputs:** `ca_id`; scheduler identity; next `effective_session_date`.  
**Authorization:** corporate-action scheduler only; not users. Runs after 20:15 ET, must finish before 04:00 ET.  
**Steps:** (1) Lock CA `announced → cancelling_orders`. (2) Page open orders on the instrument 1,000 at a time; each page is operation 5 (system cancel) + reservation release. (3) `status = staging`; `target_generation = current+1`. (4) Page open lots 5,000 per transaction: **insert** `tax_lots` rows at `target_generation` (do not update old generation). Split: `qty *= ratio_num/ratio_den`, `basis` unchanged in total, per-share basis scaled; leftover nanos on reverse split that are not a multiple of 1 share after the ratio become `cash_in_lieu` (close leftover, `cash_entries` at the CA’s cash-in-lieu print). (5) Page positions the same way. (6) Cash dividend: at ex-date, page holders at previous close qty, insert `dividend_receivables`; on payable date a later job posts cash and marks `paid`. (7) Symbol change: close the old `instrument_identifiers` interval at `effective` open, insert the new symbol interval; `instrument_id` unchanged. (8) Insert restated `official_closes` factor for P&L: prior close micros `*= ratio_den/ratio_num` at `target_generation`. (9) **Single atomic flip:** `instruments.adjustment_generation = target_generation`, update `cumulative_split_*`, `ca.status = applied`. Readers of generation `g` never see mixed lots. (10) Re-enable routing.  
**Atomic:** each chunk; the flip in (9); never a mixed-generation read.  
**Eventually consistent:** chart stores, search of old symbol.  
**Optimistic UI:** instrument shows `corporate action applying` from staging until flip.  
**Conflict:** a fill after cancelling_orders is a T4 fill-after-cancel against the old generation and must be booked **before** the flip or parked in manual_review; it must not apply to `target_generation` qty.

**8. RollRegularSession** (scheduled)  
**Inputs:** `session_date` that just closed (`regular_close_utc + 5 minutes`, wait until official closes exist for all tradable instruments or 16:45 ET, whichever first).  
**Authorization:** session scheduler.  
**Steps:** (1) Persist `official_closes` from the listing-exchange close feed (not last extended print). (2) Expire `tif=day` orders still live (operation 5 system). (3) Page accounts in shards of 2,000: `mv = Σ position.qty * official_close / 1e9`; write `account_session_marks` (equity, excess maintenance = equity − 0.25×long_mv, `dtbp = 4 * max(excess,0)` if PDT flagged). (4) Recompute `pdt_window_cache` for the new current session (drop the session that aged out of the five). (5) If PDT flagged and this close equity < `$25,000`, set `accounts.status = closing_only` until a close ≥ `$25,000`.  
**Atomic:** per account mark row; official close per instrument; expiry per order. The whole roll is not one transaction.  
**Eventually consistent:** next session’s DTBP display.  
**Optimistic UI:** “market closed”; day P&L holds at the close mark until 04:00.  
**Conflict:** a 15:59:59.999 fill is in today’s marks; 16:00:00.000 is extended and not in official close. Duplicate schedulers use `session_date` uniqueness.

**9. SyncDevice** (write/read)  
**Inputs:** device, last_cursor, watchlist mutations, draft orders with `client_order_id`.  
**Authorization:** device’s user; each draft re-checks `account_parties` at accept time.  
**Steps:** (1) Apply watchlist adds/removes (operation 12) in the batch; last add wins on the same instrument. (2) For each draft in device order, call PlaceOrder; stored accepts return; BP failures are durable events. (3) Return all account events after `last_cursor`.  
**Atomic:** per PlaceOrder / per watchlist item, not the batch.  
**Eventually consistent:** push to other devices.  
**Optimistic UI:** drafts = `queued on this device — not sent`.  
**Conflict:** T5; watchlist union is the only automatic merge.

**10. FreezeMonthlyStatement** (periodic bulk)  
**Inputs:** account_id, calendar month. Scheduler at month-end T+2 02:00 ET.  
**Authorization:** statement scheduler; customer `statements` perm to read.  
**Steps:** (1) Copy cash, positions at **then-current** generation, and the month’s `cash_entries`/`fills` into `statement_lines` using **as-stated** symbol and as-executed qty/price for activity, and as-adjusted qty only for the end-of-month **position** line, with the generation recorded in the digest. (2) Hash, store blob, insert `statements`. Re-run with same inputs must yield the same sha256; if a CA applied after the freeze, this row is left alone.  
**Atomic:** insert of statement + lines + hash.  
**Eventually consistent:** PDF render.  
**Optimistic UI:** “preparing.”  
**Conflict:** unique `(account_id, period_end)`; retries return the frozen row.

**11. Generate1099B** (periodic bulk)  
**Inputs:** tax_year. Scheduler in January.  
**Authorization:** tax scheduler; customer may download.  
**Steps:** For each account, every **sell fill** with `session_date` in the tax year produces lines from `tax_lot_events kind=close` joined to the **opening as-executed** dates and the **basis remaining at the generation of the close**, including wash adjustments already booked. Do not apply CAs from a later year. Freeze `tax_form_lines` + sha256.  
**Atomic:** per account form.  
**Eventually consistent:** IRS file.  
**Optimistic UI:** none.  
**Conflict:** unique `(account_id,tax_year,kind)`; a re-run is a new **versioned** form only under compliance dual control, never a silent replace.

**12. AddOrRemoveWatchlistItem** (write)  
**Inputs:** watchlist_id, instrument_id, add|remove.  
**Authorization:** `view` on the watchlist’s account.  
**Steps:** (1) Insert or delete `watchlist_items`. (2) `watch_count_shards[hash(account) mod 64] += ±1`; `instruments.watch_count += ±1`.  
**Atomic:** item + both counters.  
**Eventually consistent:** other devices’ lists.  
**Optimistic UI:** item appears; count may lag 1 s on the public badge.  
**Conflict:** duplicate add is a no-op; two devices adding the same instrument: one insert.

**13. DispatchVenueOutbox** (background, external)  
**Inputs:** leased `venue_outbox` row.  
**Authorization:** outbox worker.  
**Steps:** (1) Lease with owner + `lease_until`. (2) Send **the frozen payload** with **the same** `idempotency_key`. (3) On ack, `acked`. (4) On definitive reject, `failed_final` and enqueue a local ApplyVenueReport `reject`. (5) On timeout, `sent_unknown`; **do not** new-key retry; poll venue by key / wait for report. (6) `sent_unknown` older than 30 s is polled; older than 24 h → `manual_review`.  
**Atomic:** lease; status transitions; never the HTTP call with the DB.  
**Eventually consistent:** venue.  
**Optimistic UI:** order stays `accepted`/`cancel pending`.  
**Conflict:** two workers: lease wins; venue dedupes by key.

### 6. Scale and cost

Target: 15 million accounts, 8 million funded, 5 million DAU, 8,000 NMS instruments.

**Quotes.** Aggregate SIP quotes 1–5 million/s at peak; AAPL 20,000–80,000 quote updates/s and 200–2,000 last sales/s. Simultaneous watchers: AAPL 2 million, top 20 symbols 8 million attachments, tail symbols tens to thousands. Coalesced emit: 10 snapshots/s/instrument/feed. Naive fan-out `2e6 × 5e4 = 1e11` messages/s on AAPL; coalesced still `2e6 × 10 = 2e7` messages/s on AAPL alone — requires a tree/multicast fan-out plane, not per-row live queries. Delayed feed is the same coalesced stream shifted 15 minutes (materialized as a second group, not a `NOW()−15min` query).

**Orders.** 3 million accepts/day, 8 million venue reports/day, 2,000 accepts/s peak, 20,000 reports/s burst on an open. One IPO: 100,000 open orders on one instrument, all cancelled in a CA window.

**Books.** Average 8 open lots per funded account → 64 million open lot versions; seven-year fills → ~20 billion fill rows. A popular split (2 million holders, 3 open lots) rewrites 6 million lot versions and 2 million positions in < 8 hours, then one generation flip.

**PDT.** Every sell fill on a margin account walks that symbol-day’s orders (usually < 20) and updates the five-session cache. 1 million margin sellers/day. The fence is per account, not global.

**Marks.** Session roll: 8 million funded × 8 positions = 64 million close multiplies in < 40 minutes. Live portfolio push is coalesced per account, not per quote: a user holding 20 names gets ≤ 10 portfolio messages/s.

**Expensive operations:** AAPL snapshot fan-out; `instruments.watch_count` hot row (hence 64 shards); per-account `bp_version` under bursty trading; CA staging of millions of versioned lots; session-roll marks; 1099 scan of a year’s sells; seven-year statement replay. Uniform traffic hides all of these.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

| Requirement | Why ordinary queries + mutations over rows fail | Honest concession | Cheat boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Latest-value broadcast of public ticks to millions, split by entitlement, with a 100 ms bound | Updating one instrument row and notifying N watchers is O(N) per tick; N×tick_rate is physically too large; a delayed user reading the same row gets the live tape; portfolio fields become a side channel | Coalesce to ≤ 10 snapshots/s, hierarchical fan-out, separate `feed` groups, risk marks never on the customer socket | Dropping streaming quotes, polling at seconds, using one `last_price` for delayed and realtime, or marking delayed portfolios on the live tick |
| PDT pairing over a five-**session** window | `COUNT(fills WHERE created_at > now()-5d)` is the wrong number (fills vs orders, overnight vs same-day, UTC vs NYSE calendar); live equity around `$25k` is the wrong gate; visibility-filtered counts of “my rows” cannot implement a **structural** pairing; two closing sells need a fence | Maintain `symbol_day_trades` + `pdt_window_cache` in the fill transaction; calendar table; prior-close equity | Claiming PDT compliance with a 120-hour counter, counting prints, or skipping the `$25k` rule |
| Bulk retroactive rewrite that must not mix generations and must not touch frozen documents | In-place `UPDATE` of fills/positions is the T1 bug; a long transaction over 6 million lots blocks trading; readers during the job see mixed qty×price; statements generated from live queries move after mailing | Version lots/positions by `adjustment_generation`, stage then atomic flip, copy statements/1099s into frozen lines | Not applying splits to lots (sell 10 after a 4-for-1), or regenerating mailed statements from live data |
| External execution saga with cancel/fill and unknown timeouts | A local transaction cannot include the venue; retries with new keys duplicate; last-write-wins on `orders.status` loses a fill or a cancel | Durable outbox + inbox, stable keys, unique `venue_exec_id`, explicit `sent_unknown`, fill-after-cancel rule | Mocking the venue as a local insert, treating timeout as failure, or showing `filled` at tap |
| Join of private positions to a public tick stream for millions of equities 10 times/s | `SELECT positions JOIN quotes` per user per tick does not fit; stale generation × new price overvalues by the split ratio | Per-account coalesced portfolio messages from a mark service that holds positions in memory and consumes snapshot seqs, fenced by generation | Computing day P&L from tax-lot average cost, or a 5-second poll presented as streaming equity |

A product may refuse extended hours, refuse fractionals, or refuse specific-lot (FIFO only). Those shrink surface. They are cheats if the app still claims extended-hours trading, fractionals, or 1099 lot relief. A product may delay statements to T+5; it may not make the statement a live query. A product may cap watchers shown a live tape (e.g. 50 symbols per device); it may not put the live tape in a delayed user’s equity.

### 8. Confidence

High (standard brokerage practice): SIP real-time vs 15-minute delayed; official close vs last print; as-executed vs split-adjusted vs statement-frozen facts; FINRA pattern-day-trader **four** day trades in **five** business days and the `$25,000` equity test; day-trade pairing at **order** granularity with overnight inventory excluded; Reg-T-ish overnight vs day BP; FIFO vs specific identification frozen at trade time; wash-sale 30 calendar days; outbox/inbox idempotency for venue; cancel/fill races; principal fill of fractional remainders; NYSE session calendar including early closes.

Medium: the exact 3% market-order collar, 100 ms coalesce, 90-day GTC, `$1` minimum, 64 watch-count shards, fill-after-cancel booking into a possible cash shortfall, DTBP as `4 × excess maintenance at prior close`, and PDT equity ignoring live marks (stricter than some retail brokers who lift the gate on same-day deposits plus some of day’s P&L). These are coherent locked choices for the later implementation, not universal law.

Invented for the exercise: the name Fathom; long-only NMS-only scope; `share_nanos` / `usd_micros` scaling; 8-hour CA completion SLA; 2 million AAPL watchers as the fan-out sizing case (order of magnitude is real at a successful retail broker; the exact integer is a target).

The axis is not optional in ordinary use: every open app is a quote fan-out subscriber; every sell on margin is a PDT window walk; every popular split is a bulk generation rewrite; every market order is a venue saga. Softening any of those to “poll prices, count today’s sells, update qty in place, insert a fill row locally” produces an app that looks like a brokerage and is wrong on the first split, the first partial, and the first delayed user.
