NOT READY

§8 resolves the mixed-chain and mutable-lifetime blockers. The narrowed online resolver is precise enough to implement. The sync algorithm still has these blockers:

1. **High — authority consistency does not establish data consistency (§§8.3–8.4).** Child inserts, updates and deletes need not change `A`. One child scope can finish acquisition, change while other scopes are acquired, then publish an obsolete image or horizon under the same `(G,A)`. Specify a shared starting data watermark, bounded log catch-up for every staged scope, and a terminal publication watermark. Existing snapshots explicitly require this catch-up before publication: [partition.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w14-rules4/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/partition.rs:605), [device.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w14-rules4/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/src/device.rs:880). “Ordinary acquisition” needs an explicit composition algorithm for the new manifest.

2. **High — bounded enumeration lacks a guaranteed progress algorithm (§8.3).** Membership audience indexes enumerate groups, then parents within groups; they do not directly provide global parent-id order. The referenced path currently materializes the group population: [partition.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w14-rules4/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/partition.rs:526). Specify the bounded traversal, ordering and continuation state. In particular, after exhausting the allowance on inaccessible candidates, withholding a hidden continuation can leave acquisition permanently unable to finish—even with no concurrent writes. Define how it resumes without disclosing hidden identities, and distinguish budget exhaustion from enumeration completion.

3. **High — total staging storage is not bounded by the stated acquisition limits (§§8.3–8.4).** `last N` bounds children per parent, but every authorized parent requires a descriptor and potentially empty-scope coverage. Arbitrarily many parents with zero children still grow the manifest. Keeping Active and Staging also retains overlapping populations. Specify finite accounting for descriptors, coverage, rows, indexes and concurrent active/staged storage, with reservation before persistence and an atomic refusal/cancellation path. Referencing “existing account admission limits” does not define how these new objects consume that allowance.

4. **Medium — global restart starvation is too broad for the proposed sync scope (§8.4).** Any relevant roster write or parent creation can restart every viewer’s manifest, including viewers unaffected by that edit. A writer confined to an unrelated department can therefore prevent others from completing acquisition indefinitely. The RFC acknowledges starvation, but the blast radius makes it an ordinary operational concern. Narrow invalidation/restart domains, provide a convergence algorithm, or defer live sync from this revision.

These are missing algorithmic contracts, not findings about absent implementation. Under the stated second-round rule, keep the RFC in Review and take the recipe fallback.
