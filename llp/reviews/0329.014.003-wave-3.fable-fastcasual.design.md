# Fast-casual ordering—a trap-first design

### 1. Product contract

**What it is.** A Sweetgreen/Chipotle-style order-ahead app for a 600-store chain. Customers build a cart alone or as a group, pick a pickup slot (or ASAP), pay, and collect in-store or curbside. Kitchens work orders on station displays (KDS). The store's point-of-sale (POS) stays the system of record for prices, inventory counts, and the day's sales; the app and the POS *both* control the "86 list" (items sold out for the day). Instants are UTC; "local" means the store's time zone.

**Who uses it.** *Customers* (account holders) and *guests* (join a group cart by link, no account); *store staff*—cooks on shared station tablets (grill, bowl line, salad, expo) identified per shift by a PIN, a *store manager*, a curbside *runner*; *brand ops* (menus, schedules, promotions, earn rates); and *systems*—the POS vendor, the payment processor, push notifications.

**Promises.**
1. The menu you see is the menu for the store *and the pickup time you chose*; anything unavailable then is greyed with the reason ("breakfast ends 10:30", "sold out until 2:00 pm").
2. If something in your cart becomes unavailable, every device viewing the cart is told within 5 s and offered substitutes. Nothing is ever silently removed from a cart.
3. If an item in a paid order sells out before the kitchen starts it, you choose (substitute / refund the line / cancel) before a deadline, else your saved preference applies. You are never charged for a line that wasn't made.
4. A slot you can select has room for your order; once you pay, it is yours—even if a manager later shrinks or closes the slot.
5. Group carts: everyone with the link adds and edits their own items; the host can edit anything, choose the slot, and check out. Others' changes appear live. Offline edits are merged on reconnect—unless the host already placed the order, in which case you are told exactly which edits did not make it.
6. You pay the prices shown when you confirmed; if a price or promotion changed in between, you see the difference before any charge.
7. You are charged exactly once per order; an abandoned checkout charges nothing and frees the slot within 5 minutes.
8. Order status is live: received → in kitchen → ready → picked up. Exactly one "ready" notification per readiness (a pull-back yields one "delayed" and one new "ready").
9. Curbside: tap "I'm here" and a runner brings it out; in-store: shelf pickup by name.
10. Points accrue on every captured payment at the displayed rate; redeem at checkout; every movement is itemized. In a split-pay group order each payer earns on what they paid.
11. Promotions apply automatically when eligible; limits are exact ("first 500 orders" never becomes 501; "once per customer per week" never twice).
12. Refunds return to the original payment method; remakes cost nothing and jump the kitchen queue.
13. Kitchen: every order reaches the right station at the right time—fired at *promised − prep lead*, never hours early, never lost, never twice; one cook per item at a time; a crashed tablet never strands an item for more than 60 s.
14. Staff can 86 an item from any station tablet, the manager app, or the POS register; the systems agree within 5 s; new orders for it are refused until it is un-86'd or the store's day rolls over.
15. Every app order is in the POS for the store's books exactly once, even through POS outages.
16. Multi-device: carts, group carts, and orders are identical on every signed-in device.
17. Offline: you can browse a cached menu and edit carts, not check out or join a group. Station tablets keep working through a network blip; their claims are honored for 60 s and reconciled on reconnect.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

Tags: **[M]** money, **[S]** secrecy, **[T]** time, **[O]** other people's data.

1. **[T]** Availability is a fact about (store, item, *instant*). A cart line is evaluated at the cart's pickup instant (slot start, or now + prep lead for ASAP), never at the wall clock alone, and re-evaluated whenever that instant or the store's availability changes.
2. **[T][O]** An 86 at store S for item I reaches every open cart at S containing I whose pickup instant falls inside the 86 window, and every paid-but-unfired order at S containing I, by *marking*, never by deleting, in the same transaction that records the 86.
3. **[T][M]** A placed order's line names, prices, modifiers, and totals are snapshots taken at confirmation and never change; corrections are new rows (voids, substitutions, refunds).
4. **[M]** A slot's *committed units + live held units* never exceeds its capacity at the moment a hold or commitment is made. Lowering capacity later never cancels a paid order.
5. **[M]** Slot commitment, promotion consumption, points redemption, and order creation succeed or fail together. Capture follows placement; a failed capture cancels the order and releases everything it consumed.
6. **[O][S]** A group-cart line is editable only by its owner or the host. Participants never see each other's payment methods, loyalty balances, or contact details.
7. **[T]** Once checkout begins the cart is frozen: no edit is accepted, whatever the device's clock says about when it was made, and the device is told which edits were refused.
8. A ticket is *ready* exactly when every line is ready or voided; readiness is computed inside the transaction that changes a line, never stored independently.
9. A line is claimed by at most one station device at a time; a claim dies 60 s after its device was last heard from; a dead claim returns the line to the queue with its progress note.
10. **[T]** A ticket fires at max(now, promisedAt − prepLead). ASAP tickets fire at placement. A remake fires immediately.
11. Every app order and every refund is posted to the POS exactly once (idempotent by our id); POS acknowledgements are stored.
12. **[T]** For a fact both systems write (86 state), the change with the later *origin time* wins; a snapshot never overwrites a later change.
13. **[M]** Points are earned on capture at the rate in effect at capture time; every movement is a ledger entry; balance is the sum of entries and goes negative only through refund clawback.
14. **[M]** A promotion with a global limit N is consumed by at most N orders ever; per-customer limits hold per customer; both are enforced in the placement transaction.
15. **[M]** A refund never exceeds captured minus prior refunds on that payment, and claws back earned points in proportion.
16. **[M]** A remake creates a new ticket linked to the original, charges nothing, earns nothing, and bypasses slot capacity.
17. **[S]** Customers see their own orders and the group carts they belong to; staff see their store; a participant who leaves a group cart stops seeing it but keeps receipts for lines they paid.
18. **[O]** "Spots left" for a slot and "redemptions left" for a promotion are computed over *all* commitments at the store/brand, never over the rows the viewer may see.
19. Exactly one *ready* notification per (order, readiness generation).
20. **[T]** Menu and price changes affect new quotes immediately and existing quotes never; an open cart is re-quoted at checkout and the customer sees the diff.
21. **[T]** An 86 without an explicit end expires at the store's close of business; nothing 86'd today is 86'd tomorrow unless someone says so.

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1—"Available" is not a property of an item; it is a property of (store, item, instant), three different instants are in play, and it has reverse dependencies.** *(temporal; reverse dependency)*

*Wrong version:* `menuItem.available: boolean`, checked when a line is added to the cart, plus a breakfast/lunch switch keyed to the client clock.

*Why wrong:* (a) The instant that matters is the pickup instant, not now—a breakfast burrito added at 10:20 for a 10:45 slot is *not* available; a guac bowl for the 15:00 slot *is* available at 11:40 when avocado is 86'd "until 14:00". (b) The fact changes after the check; validating once trusts a stale boolean and the kitchen discovers the shortage at make time. (c) One 86 must reach every open cart and every paid-but-unfired order at the store, each with a different consequence (mark the line; raise a deadline-bound exception; badge the fired ticket). (d) The 86 is itself an interval (until close of business by default), so "unavailable" is a predicate over a window, not a flag.

*Exposing observation:* At 11:40 the grill 86s avocado "until 14:00". Correct: the 12:15 cart with a guac bowl is marked within 5 s and offered a substitute; the 15:00 cart is untouched; the paid 12:00 order raises an exception with a 11:50 deadline; the ticket already on the grill shows an "86'd item" badge and keeps cooking; at 14:00 nothing needs to happen. A naive app fails at least three of those five.

**Trap 2—Slot capacity is a structural count over units and live holds, gated inside the commit; a count over the viewer's visible rows is confidently wrong.** *(concurrency; visibility through counts; time)*

*Wrong versions:* (i) `count(orders where slot=S)<capacity` counts orders, not kitchen units (a group order of 8 bowls is 8 units), and ignores customers at the payment step, so two checkouts both pass and both pay. (ii) The count is computed over rows the requester may read (their own orders): it returns 0 or 1 and every slot looks open. (iii) Holds without expiry starve the lunch rush. (iv) Treating `committed≤capacity` as an invariant over existing rows, so lowering capacity cancels paid orders.

*Why wrong:* The gating number must include in-flight intent (holds) and be evaluated under the same lock as the commit, or overselling is a matter of when. Capacity gates *new* commitments; it does not constrain old ones (invariant 4).

*Exposing observation:* Two phones confirm 2-unit orders for the last 2 units of the 12:15 slot within 100 ms—exactly one succeeds; the other sees "slot full" with alternatives *before* any charge. Then the manager cuts the slot from 24 to 10 with 14 committed: nothing is cancelled, nothing new is accepted, spots-left shows 0, not −4.

**Trap 3—A group cart is not a document; it is a set of owned lines merged at line and modifier-element granularity, with a freeze fence that rejects by cart state, not by time.** *(merge; authority; concurrency)*

*Wrong versions:* whole-cart last-writer-wins (two offline participants add items; one add vanishes); per-line LWW registers (A sets qty 2→3, B sets 2→1; whoever synced last survives); modifier sets replaced wholesale (A adds extra guac, B removes onions; onions come back); a remove that loses to a later-arriving edit; accepting late offline edits into a placed order (the kitchen already has the ticket) or discarding them silently.

*Why wrong:* Lines are owned (invariant 6), so with client-generated `lineId`s most edits never conflict; the residual conflicts (host vs owner on one line, one person on two devices) need a stated rule, and modifiers need element-wise merge with a dietary tie-break: a concurrent *remove* beats a concurrent *add* of the same option, because the person removing peanuts must win. The freeze is not "edits after 12:01:03 are refused"—device clocks are meaningless—but "the cart is `checkingOut` or `placed`, so this op is refused and the device is told".

*Exposing observation:* Two participants edit offline; the host checks out; both reconnect. Correct: their edits are refused with a per-edit notice; the order is unchanged. Also: A removes onions while B adds onions on the same line → no onions, and B's device shows "removed by A".

**Trap 4—Kitchen tickets are time-fired, claims are leases derived from device liveness, and ticket state is a computed fact under a per-ticket serialization.** *(time; concurrency; authority)*

*Wrong versions:* a ticket appears when the order is placed (placed at 09:00 for 12:15, fired at 09:00, made cold); `claimedBy` as a plain field (a tablet dies mid-claim and the item is stuck until a manager notices); `ticket.state` as a stored field each station's bump updates (two stations bump their last items at once, each reads "the other isn't done", nobody marks ready—or both do and the customer gets two pushes); cooks identified only by the tablet (no audit).

*Why wrong:* Fire time is a scheduled transition, not a consequence of placement. A claim must expire without the claimant's cooperation; deriving liveness from the device heartbeat costs no per-claim writes. Readiness is a conjunction over lines, so it must be computed where the lines change, in the same transaction, serialized on the ticket, with an idempotent notification key.

*Exposing observation:* Kill a station tablet 10 s into a claim → another tablet can take the item within 60 s, note intact. Bump the last two items on two stations at once → ready exactly once, one push. Place at 09:00 for 12:15 with a 12-minute prep lead → fires at 12:03.

**Trap 5—The POS is a second writer for the same facts, on a different clock, over an unreliable link.** *(external sync; time; idempotency)*

*Wrong versions:* periodic state copy ("every 60 s overwrite our 86 list with the POS's")—an app-side 86 at 11:40:05 is undone at 11:40:30 by a batch carrying the POS's 11:39 snapshot; order posting retried without an idempotency key (a 20-minute POS outage becomes 40 double-posted sales); auto-86 from the raw POS count (it lags by every app order the POS has not ingested).

*Why wrong:* Convergence needs *changes with origin times*, not snapshots (invariant 12); posting needs at-least-once delivery plus dedupe on our id (invariant 11); the inventory to act on is *projected* (POS count minus what the POS has not yet counted).

*Exposing observation:* 86 an item on the register 10 s before the app's next sync—the app refuses orders for it within 5 s of the POS emitting the event, and the register's 11:39 snapshot never un-86s an app-side 11:40 86. Take the POS down for 20 minutes—afterwards it has each order once.

**Trap 6—Loyalty and promotions are money with a clock: earn at capture at that instant's rate; global limits are structural counters consumed in the placement transaction; refunds claw back proportionally; split pay decides who earns.** *(money; time; visibility)*

*Wrong versions:* earn at placement (a cancelled order leaves orphan points); `count(redemptions)<500` as a read followed by an insert (the 500th and 501st race); clawback capped at the current balance and silently forgiven (a $12 refund after the 120 points were spent costs the brand $1.20 with no record); points to the host of a group order regardless of who paid; the group cart showing "host used 800 points" to participants (reveals the host's balance floor—show only the discount).

*Exposing observation:* the 501st redemption at 12:00:00.05 nationwide is refused; a refund after a redemption leaves balance −20 with a clawback entry; a split-pay order shows each payer exactly their share in their own ledger.

### 4. Data model

Types: `id`, `ts` (UTC instant), `localTime`, `int`, `cents`, `bool`, `enum`, `set<>`, `list<>`, `json`. ★ = append-only. ◷ = time-bounded. "Materialized" = derived and stored in the same transaction as its inputs.

**store** PK `storeId`: `name`, `tz`, `prepLeadMinutes int` (12), `slotMinutes int` (15), `openLocal`, `closeLocal`, `curbsideEnabled bool`, `posVendor enum`, `posStoreRef`, `defaultSlotCapacityUnits int` (24).

**menuItem** PK `itemId`: `name`, `description`, `unitLoad int` (kitchen units: 1 per bowl, 0 per drink), `stationId`, `dietaryFlags set<enum>`, `modifierGroupIds list<id>`, `substituteItemId?`, `ingredientUsage list<{ingredientId, qty}>`.
**storeItem** PK (`storeId`,`itemId`): `priceCents` (POS-owned), `active bool`, `priceVersion int`.
**modifierGroup** PK `groupId`: `name`, `minSelect int`, `maxSelect int`. **modifierOption** PK `optionId`: `groupId`, `name`, `priceDeltaCents`, `unitLoadDelta int`, `ingredientId?`.
**menuSchedule** PK `scheduleId`: `storeId`, `name`, `daysOfWeek set<int>`, `startLocal`, `endLocal`, `itemIds set<id>` ◷.

**availabilityEvent★** PK `eventId`: `storeId`, `targetKind enum(item|option)`, `targetId`, `state enum(eightySixed|available)`, `effectiveFrom ts`, `effectiveUntil ts` (default: close of business) ◷, `originSystem enum(app|pos|inventory)`, `originAt ts`, `originSeq?`, `recordedAt ts`, `actorRef`, `reason?`, `superseded bool`.
**availabilityState** (materialized from events by max `originAt`) PK (`storeId`,`targetKind`,`targetId`): `eightySixed bool`, `until ts?` ◷, `lastOriginAt ts`, `lastEventId`, `auto bool` (set when origin = inventory).
Predicate **availableAt(storeId, itemId, modifiers, t)** ≡ `storeItem.active` ∧ ∃ schedule at the store containing the item with `dayOfWeek(local(t))∈daysOfWeek∧startLocal≤local(t)<endLocal` ∧ ¬(`availabilityState.eightySixed` ∧ (`until` is null ∨ `until>t`)) ∧ the same 86 test for each selected option.

**ingredient** PK `ingredientId`. **inventoryLevel** PK (`storeId`,`ingredientId`): `posCount int`, `posCountAt ts`, `projectedCount int` (derived: `posCount` − usage of app orders the POS had not ingested at `posCountAt`), `autoEightySixBelow int`.

**customer** PK `customerId`: `displayName`, `phone`, `email`, `substitutionPolicy enum(contactMe|autoSubstitute|refundLine|cancelOrder)`, `pushToken?`. **guest** PK `guestId`: `displayName`, `createdAt`.

**pickupSlot** PK (`storeId`,`slotStart ts`): `capacityUnits int`, `committedUnits int` (materialized: Σ `units` of non-cancelled orders in the slot), `heldUnits int` (materialized: Σ `units` of holds in state `held`), `state enum(open|closed)`, `version int`. Derived: `spotsLeftUnits=max(0, capacityUnits−committedUnits−heldUnits)`.
**slotHold** PK `holdId`; UK (`cartId`) where `state='held'`: `storeId`, `slotStart`, `cartId`, `units int`, `expiresAt ts` ◷, `state enum(held|committed|released|expired)`.

**cart** PK `cartId`: `storeId`, `kind enum(solo|group)`, `hostParticipantId`, `joinCode` UK (group only), `targetSlotStart ts?` (null = ASAP), `fulfillment enum(inStore|curbside)`, `state enum(open|checkingOut|placed|abandoned)`, `version int` (+1 per applied op), `frozenAtVersion int?`, `activeQuoteId?`, `orderId?`, `createdAt`, `lastActivityAt`.
**cartParticipant** PK (`cartId`,`participantId`): `role enum(host|member)`, `payMode enum(hostPays|paysOwn)`, `joinedAt`, `leftAt?` ◷.
**cartLine** PK (`cartId`,`lineId`—client-generated UUID): `ownerParticipantId`, `itemId`, `quantity int`, `modifiers set<optionId>`, `note`, `state enum(ok|unavailable|removed)`, `unavailableEventId?`, `lastEditVersion int`, `lastEditorParticipantId`, `lastEditAt ts` (client-reported; used only for same-actor tie-breaks).
**cartOp★** PK `opId`; UK (`deviceId`,`deviceSeq`): `cartId`, `actorParticipantId`, `baseCartVersion int`, `kind enum(addLine|setQty|setModifiers|setNote|removeLine|substituteLine|setSlot|setFulfillment|join|leave|systemUnavailable|systemAvailable|systemUnfrozen|systemSlotClosed)`, `payload json`, `clientAt ts`, `result enum(applied|rejected|superseded)`, `reason?`, `resultVersion int`, `appliedAt ts`.

**quote★** PK `quoteId`: `cartId`, `cartVersion int`, `lines list<{lineId, itemId, itemName, unitPriceCents, modifiers list<{optionId, name, priceDeltaCents}>, quantity, unitLoad, payerParticipantId}>`, `promotions list<{promoId, discountCents}>`, `pointsToRedeem int`, `perPayer list<{participantId, subtotal, discount, tax, tip, total}>`, `slotStart ts`, `holdId`, `expiresAt ts` ◷.

**order** PK `orderId`: `storeId`, `cartId`, `hostCustomerId`, `state enum(placed|exception|scheduled|fired|inProgress|ready|handedOff|cancelled)`, `promisedAt ts`, `fireAt ts`, `firedAt?`, `readyAt?`, `readyGeneration int`, `fulfillment`, `curbside {carDescription?, arrivedAt?}`, `totals {subtotal, discount, tax, tip, total}`, `units int`, `slotStart ts`, `isRemake bool`, `parentOrderId?`, `posTicketRef?`, `posPostState enum(pending|posted|parked)`, `posAckedAt?`, `placedAt`, `version int`.
**orderLine** PK (`orderId`,`lineId`)—snapshot fields ★: `ownerParticipantId`, `payerParticipantId`, `itemId`, `itemName`, `unitPriceCents`, `modifiers list<…>`, `quantity`, `unitLoad`, `lineTotalCents`, `stationId`, `replacesLineId?`; mutable: `state enum(queued|claimed|ready|voided|remade)`, `voidReason?`, `readyAt?`, `progressNote?`.
**claim** PK `claimId`; UK (`orderId`,`lineId`) where `state='active'`: `stationDeviceId`, `cookPin`, `claimedAt`, `state enum(active|completed|released|expired)`. Liveness is derived: live ⇔ `state='active'` ∧ `stationDevice.lastSeenAt≥now−60 s`.
**station** PK (`storeId`,`stationId`): `name`, `displayLimit int` (8). **stationDevice** PK `deviceId`: `storeId`, `stationId`, `currentCookPin?`, `lastSeenAt ts`.

**exception** PK `exceptionId`: `orderId`, `lineId`, `kind enum(itemUnavailable|paymentFailed)`, `raisedAt`, `deadlineAt ts` ◷, `offeredSubstitutes list<itemId>`, `choice?`, `chosenBy?`, `resolvedAt?`, `resolution?`.
**payment** PK `paymentId`: `orderId`, `payerParticipantId`, `processorIntentRef` UK, `amountCents`, `state enum(created|authorized|captured|failed|voided)`, `capturedAt?`. **refund★** PK `refundId`: `paymentId`, `amountCents`, `reason`, `state enum(requested|succeeded|failed)`, `actorRef`, `createdAt`.
**notification★** PK `notificationId`; UK `dedupeKey`: `recipientRef`, `kind`, `payload`, `createdAt`, `sentAt?`.

**earnRate** PK `rateId`: `pointsPerDollar int`, `effectiveFrom ts`, `effectiveUntil ts?` ◷. **loyaltyEntry★** PK `entryId`: `customerId`, `deltaPoints int`, `kind enum(earn|redeem|clawback|adjust|expire)`, `orderId?`, `refundId?`, `rateId?`, `createdAt`. **loyaltyBalance** (materialized) PK `customerId`: `points int`, `lastEntryId`.
**promotion** PK `promoId`: `name`, `rule json` (e.g. `{type:'percentOff', pct:20, minSubtotalCents:1500}`), `startsAt`, `endsAt` ◷, `globalLimit int?`, `perCustomerLimit int?`, `perCustomerWindowDays int?`, `stackable bool`. **promotionCounter** PK `promoId`: `consumed int`. **promotionRedemption★** PK (`promoId`,`orderId`): `customerId`, `redeemedAt`.

**posOutbox** PK `outboxId` (monotonic): `storeId`, `kind enum(orderPost|refundPost|availability)`, `refId`, `idempotencyKey` UK, `payload`, `attempts int`, `nextAttemptAt ts`, `state enum(pending|acked|parked)`, `lastError?`. **posInbox★** PK (`storeId`,`posSeq`): `kind`, `payload`, `receivedAt`, `processedAt?`, `outcome enum(applied|superseded|failed)?`. **posCursor** PK `storeId`: `lastAppliedSeq`.
**auditLog★**: `at`, `actorRef`, `storeId?`, `action`, `subjectRef`, `before?`, `after?`.

Relationships: cart 1—* cartLine / cartParticipant / cartOp; cart 0..1 live slotHold; cart 0..1 order; order 1—* orderLine / payment / exception; orderLine 0..1 live claim; order 0..* child orders (remakes). Row visibility derives from `cartParticipant` (carts), `order.hostCustomerId` and `orderLine.payerParticipantId` (orders), and `stationDevice.storeId` or manager role (staff); KDS views show first name and last initial only.

### 5. The hard operations

Notation: `lock(x)` takes a row lock for the transaction; steps inside **TX** are one atomic transaction; EC = eventually consistent; UI = optimistic client behavior; Conflict = what concurrent actors see. `evalTime(cart)=cart.targetSlotStart??ceilToSlot(now+store.prepLeadMinutes)`.

**Op 1—Read: menu for (store, pickup instant).** Inputs: `storeId`, `targetSlotStart?`, `dietaryFilter?`. Auth: anyone. Steps: (1) `t=targetSlotStart??now+prepLeadMinutes`; (2) for each active storeItem and each of its options compute `availableAt(store, item, {}, t)` and, when false, a reason: `scheduleEndsAt`/`scheduleStartsAt` from the schedule intervals bracketing `t`, or `soldOutUntil=availabilityState.until` ("today" when null); (3) return items, reasons, `menuVersion=max(priceVersion, lastOriginAt)`. Live: the client subscribes to the store's availability changes and re-runs step 2 locally. EC: the view may lag by push delay; Op 5/6 are authoritative.

**Op 2—Write: join / leave a group cart.** Inputs: `joinCode`, `participantId` (customer or freshly minted guest), `displayName`. Auth: anyone holding the code. **TX:** lock the cart by code; require `state='open'` (else `rejected/cartClosed`—revealing only that the code is spent); upsert `cartParticipant(role=member)`; `version+=1`; append `cartOp(join)`. Leave: set `leftAt`; the leaver's lines stay, editable only by the host.

**Op 3—Write: cart edit (the merge operation).** Inputs: a batch of ops `{opId, deviceId, deviceSeq, cartId, actorParticipantId, baseCartVersion, kind, payload, clientAt}` in `deviceSeq` order. Auth: actor is a participant with `leftAt` null; line ops only by the line's owner or the host; `setSlot`/`setFulfillment` host only. **TX per cart:**
1. `lock(cart)`. If `state≠'open'` → every op `rejected/cartFrozen`; return.
2. Per op: if (`deviceId`,`deviceSeq`) already exists → return the stored result (idempotent replay). A gap in a device's sequence blocks its later ops until the client resends.
3. `addLine{lineId, itemId, quantity, modifiers, note}`: if `lineId` exists → `applied` (duplicate delivery). Validate group min/max. Require `availableAt(store, item, modifiers, evalTime)` else `rejected/unavailable`. Insert with `state='ok'`.
4. `setQty{lineId, quantity}`: line `removed` → `rejected/lineRemoved`. If `line.lastEditVersion>baseCartVersion` and `lastEditor≠actor`: owner beats host—host's op → `superseded/ownerEdited`; owner's op applies. Same actor from another device: apply iff `clientAt>line.lastEditAt`, else `superseded`. `quantity≤0` → treated as `removeLine`.
5. `setModifiers{lineId, add[], remove[]}`: line `removed` → reject. `next=(current∪add)−remove`. If an option in `add` was removed by any op applied since `baseCartVersion` → drop it from `add` (remove-wins, the dietary rule) and mark `superseded/removedConcurrently`. If `next` violates a group's `maxSelect` → `rejected/modifierConflict` (earlier-applied wins). Else apply.
6. `removeLine`: idempotent; `state='removed'`; always applies for owner or host.
7. `substituteLine{lineId, newItemId, modifiers}`: owner or host; require `availableAt(new, evalTime)`; set item/modifiers, `state='ok'`, clear `unavailableEventId`.
8. `setSlot{targetSlotStart}`: host only; require the slot exists, `state='open'`, `slotStart≥now+prepLead`; set; then recompute every non-removed line's `state` at the new `evalTime`, appending `systemUnavailable`/`systemAvailable` ops for each flip.
9. After each applied or superseded op: `cart.version+=1`; `line.lastEditVersion=version`, `lastEditor`, `lastEditAt=clientAt`; insert the `cartOp` row with `result`, `reason`, `resultVersion`.
10. Return per-op results plus the cart snapshot at the final version.
Atomic: the batch, per cart. UI: the device applies ops to its cached cart immediately and keeps a persistent outbound queue; on response it adopts the server snapshot and replays still-unacknowledged ops on top; `rejected`/`superseded` ops leave the queue with a toast naming the line and reason. Conflict: batches serialize on the cart lock; outcomes are deterministic given arrival order.

**Op 4—Read: slot picker with spots left.** Inputs: `storeId`, `date`, `cartUnits` (advisory). Auth: anyone. Steps: (1) slots for the store/day with `state='open'` and `slotStart≥now+prepLead`; (2) `spotsLeftUnits=max(0, capacityUnits−committedUnits−heldUnits)` from the materialized counters—over *all* orders and holds at the store (invariant 18); (3) return `{slotStart, spotsLeftUnits, fits: spotsLeftUnits≥cartUnits}`. Live: subscribe to the day's slot rows. EC: advisory; only Op 5 gates.

**Op 5—Write: begin checkout (freeze, revalidate, hold, quote).** Inputs: `cartId`, `pointsToRedeem`, `tipCents`, `promoCodes[]`. Auth: host. **TX:**
1. `lock(cart)`; require `state='open'`; set `state='checkingOut'`, `frozenAtVersion=version` (Op 3 now refuses everything).
2. For each line with `state='ok'`: recompute `availableAt` at `evalTime` from `availabilityState`—the source, not the line mark. Any failure → mark those lines `unavailable`, set `state='open'`, `frozenAtVersion=null`, `version+=1`, return `rejected/linesUnavailable{lineIds}`.
3. Require ≥ 1 line, all non-removed lines `ok`; `units=Σ quantity×(item.unitLoad+Σ option.unitLoadDelta)`.
4. `slotStart=evalTime` (ASAP is simply the next slot). `lock(pickupSlot)`; require `state='open'`; `avail=capacityUnits−committedUnits−heldUnits`; if `units>avail` → unfreeze as in step 2 and return `rejected/slotFull{avail, alternatives: next 3 slots with spotsLeft≥units}`; else insert `slotHold(units, expiresAt=now+5 min, state='held')`, `heldUnits+=units`.
5. Price: `unitPrice=storeItem.priceCents+Σ priceDeltaCents` at current versions; promotions: window contains now, rule satisfied, `perCustomerLimit` not exceeded (count `promotionRedemption` for the host within `perCustomerWindowDays`), `promotionCounter.consumed<globalLimit` (advisory here; enforced in Op 6); best-of among non-stackable; tax by store; split `perPayer` by `payerParticipantId` (the host pays for `hostPays` participants).
6. `lock(loyaltyBalance(host))`; require `points≥pointsToRedeem`; redemption is valued at 1¢/point against the host's share only.
7. Insert `quote(expiresAt=hold.expiresAt)`; set `cart.activeQuoteId`.
After TX: create one processor payment intent per payer (idempotency key `quoteId:payerId`); insert `payment(created)` rows. Atomic: 1–7. EC: intent creation (retryable). UI: show the quote at once. Conflict: two carts on the last units serialize on the slot lock; the loser gets alternatives before paying.

**Op 6—Write: place order (confirm).** Inputs: `quoteId`; processor confirmations (`authorized` per intent via client callback or webhook). Auth: host; system for webhooks. **TX:**
1. `lock(cart)`, `lock(quote)`, `lock(slotHold)`, `lock(pickupSlot)`; require `cart.state='checkingOut'`, `cart.version=quote.cartVersion`, `quote.expiresAt>now`, hold `held` and unexpired, every `payment` for the quote `authorized` with amount = `perPayer.total`. A missing hold → `rejected/holdExpired` (the sweeper won).
2. Re-run `availableAt` for every quoted line at `quote.slotStart` against the source table. Failure → after TX void authorizations; in TX release the hold (`heldUnits−=units`, hold `released`), cart → `open`, return `rejected/linesUnavailable`.
3. For each quoted promotion with `globalLimit`: `lock(promotionCounter)`; if `consumed≥globalLimit` → `rejected/promoExhausted` (cart stays `checkingOut`; client re-quotes via Op 5 without it); else `consumed+=1`. Recount the per-customer window in the TX; insert `promotionRedemption`.
4. `lock(loyaltyBalance(host))`; require `points≥pointsToRedeem`; insert `loyaltyEntry(−points, redeem)`; balance −= points.
5. Insert `order` from the quote: `promisedAt=slotStart`, `fireAt=max(now, promisedAt−prepLead)`, `state=fireAt≤now?'fired':'scheduled'` (`firedAt=now` if fired), `units`, totals; insert `orderLine`s (`state='queued'`, `stationId` from the item).
6. Hold → `committed`; `heldUnits−=units`; `committedUnits+=units`.
7. `cart.state='placed'`, `cart.orderId`.
8. Insert `posOutbox(orderPost, idempotencyKey=orderId)`; `notification(dedupeKey=orderId:placed)`.
After TX: capture each payment (idempotent by intent). On capture confirmation, **TX:** `payment.state='captured'`, `capturedAt`; `rate=earnRate` effective at `capturedAt`; insert `loyaltyEntry(+floor(paidCents/100×pointsPerDollar), earn, rateId)` for the payer's customer (guests earn nothing); balance += . On capture failure: `exception(paymentFailed)` → Op 12 `cancelOrder`. Atomic: 1–8. EC: capture, earn, POS post, notifications. UI: "Order placed" only after the TX responds. Conflict: Op 6 vs the hold sweeper is decided on the hold row lock; no half state.

**Op 7—Write: 86 / un-86 (the reverse-dependency fan-out).** Inputs: `storeId`, `targetKind`, `targetId`, `state`, `until?` (default: close of business), `origin∈{app(actorRef), pos(posSeq, originAt), inventory}`, `reason?`. Auth: any station device or manager at the store; the POS integration for that store; the inventory job. **TX:**
1. `lock(availabilityState(store, target))` (upsert). `originAt=origin is pos?payload.originAt:now`.
2. If `lastOriginAt≥originAt` → insert `availabilityEvent(superseded=true)` and return (invariant 12). Ties: `eightySixed` wins. An `available` from the inventory job is ignored when the current state has `auto=false` (a manual 86 is never auto-cleared).
3. Insert `availabilityEvent`; update `availabilityState{eightySixed, until, lastOriginAt, lastEventId, auto}`.
4. **Carts:** for every `cartLine` joined to `cart` where `cart.storeId=S`, `cart.state∈{open, checkingOut}`, `line.state='ok'`, (`line.itemId=target` or `target∈line.modifiers`), and `¬availableAt(line, evalTime(cart))` (a 15:00 cart is untouched by an 86 until 14:00): `line.state='unavailable'`, `unavailableEventId`; per affected cart `version+=1` and append `cartOp(systemUnavailable, {lineId, reason, until, substitutes: [item.substituteItemId] filtered by availableAt})`. For `available`: lines `unavailable` via this target that now pass → `ok` plus `systemAvailable`. A `checkingOut` cart is not unfrozen here; Op 6 step 2 refuses and unfreezes it.
5. **Orders:** for every `orderLine` at S matching the target with `line.state='queued'`, `order.state∈{placed, scheduled}`, and `¬availableAt(line, order.fireAt)`: insert `exception(itemUnavailable, deadlineAt=min(order.fireAt−2 min, now+10 min), offeredSubstitutes)`; `order.state='exception'`; `notification(dedupeKey=exceptionId)`. Fired tickets are not modified—the KDS derives an "86'd item" badge at read time and a cook uses Op 10 `cantMake`.
6. If origin is `app` or `inventory`: `posOutbox(availability)`. `auditLog`.
Atomic: 1–6 (bounded by per-store fan-out; a few hundred rows). EC: push delivery. UI: the KDS marks the item at once; the manager app shows affected counts from the TX result. Conflict: two staff toggling the same item within a second serialize on the state row; later origin time wins; both events are logged.

**Op 8—Background: checkout sweeper (holds, quotes, abandoned carts).** Every 5 s per store shard. **TX (batch ≤ 200):** select `slotHold` with `state='held'`, `expiresAt≤now`, *for update, skip locked*; for each: `lock(pickupSlot)`, `heldUnits−=units`, hold `expired`; `lock(cart)`: if `state='checkingOut'` and `activeQuoteId` is this hold's quote → `state='open'`, `frozenAtVersion=null`, `version+=1`, append `cartOp(systemUnfrozen, holdExpired)`, payments → `voided` (void with the processor after TX, idempotent). Hourly: carts `open` with `lastActivityAt<now−7 d` → `abandoned`.

**Op 9—Scheduled: kitchen clock (exception deadlines, fire, claim expiry).** Every 5 s per store shard. **TX (batch):**
1. Exceptions with `resolvedAt` null and `deadlineAt≤now` (*skip locked*) → apply the host's `substitutionPolicy` through Op 12: `autoSubstitute` → first offered substitute still `availableAt(fireAt)`, else `refundLine`; `contactMe` → `refundLine` with a "we couldn't reach you" notice; `refundLine`; `cancelOrder`.
2. Fire: orders `scheduled` with `fireAt≤now` → `fired`, `firedAt=now`, `notification(orderId:inKitchen)`. Orders in `exception` never fire; step 1 runs first so a just-resolved order fires in the same tick.
3. Claims `active` whose device has `lastSeenAt<now−60 s` → `expired`; line → `queued` (note kept); if the order has no other claimed or ready lines → `order.state='fired'`.
Every predicate is re-checked under lock, so a duplicate scheduler is harmless. Conflict: a customer resolving at the deadline instant—both Op 12 and this job lock the order; the second sees `resolvedAt` and stops.

**Op 10—Write: station claim / bump / can't-make / handoff (KDS).** Inputs: authenticated `deviceId`, `cookPin`, `orderId`, `lineId`, `action∈{claim, bump, unbump, cantMake, handoff}`. Auth: the device's `stationId` must equal the line's for claim/bump; expo devices may bump/unbump any line and hand off; managers may force-release claims; `cookPin` must be the device's current shift PIN and is stored on the claim for audit. Read side: station queue = `orderLine` at (store, station) with `state∈{queued, claimed}` joined to `order.state∈{fired, inProgress}`, ordered by `order.isRemake desc, order.promisedAt asc, order.firedAt asc`, limit `displayLimit`, plus `overflowCount` and an "upcoming" count of `scheduled` orders with `fireAt<now+15 min`. **TX:**
- `claim`: `lock(orderLine)`; require `queued`, or `claimed` with a dead claim (expire it) or with `claim.deviceId=this device` (re-claim after reconnect); insert `claim(active)`; line `claimed`; `lock(order)`: `fired` → `inProgress`.
- `bump`: `lock(order)` first (the ticket's serialization point), then `lock(orderLine)`; require the live claim belongs to this device (or expo/manager); line `ready`, `readyAt`; claim `completed`; `allDone=∀ lines: state∈{ready, voided}` (lines with a pending exception count as not done); if `allDone` → `order.state='ready'`, `readyAt`, `readyGeneration+=1`, `notification(dedupeKey=orderId:ready:readyGeneration)`; if curbside and `arrivedAt` set → runner task.
- `unbump` (expo): `lock(order)`; line `queued`; if order was `ready` → `inProgress`, `notification(orderId:delayed:readyGeneration)`.
- `cantMake`: `lock(order)`; insert `exception(itemUnavailable, deadlineAt=now+3 min)`; the ticket continues on its other lines; order stays `inProgress`.
- `handoff`: `lock(order)`; require `ready`; → `handedOff`; `notification(orderId:handedOff)`.
Atomic: each action. UI: the tablet greys the item immediately; offline it queues actions for ≤ 60 s and replays; a replayed bump on an expired claim is `rejected/claimLost`. Conflict: two stations bumping their last lines serialize on the order lock; exactly one computes `allDone`; the unique `dedupeKey` is the second guard. Heartbeat: every 20 s the device writes `stationDevice.lastSeenAt`—one row per device, never per claim.

**Op 11—External: POS sync (outbox drain + inbox apply).** Auth: system, per-store integration credentials.
*Outbox drain*, one worker per store, every 2 s or on insert: select `posOutbox` for the store with `state='pending'`, `nextAttemptAt≤now`, order by `outboxId`, limit 50, *skip locked*; call the POS with `idempotencyKey`; on ack **TX:** `acked`; for `orderPost` set `order.posTicketRef`, `posPostState='posted'`, `posAckedAt=now`. HTTP 409 (duplicate key) → fetch the existing POS ticket, treat as acked. 5xx/timeout → `attempts+=1`, `nextAttemptAt=now+min(2^attempts, 300) s`; after 10 failures `parked` + alert and the worker continues past it (a poison row must not block the store's queue).
*Inbox apply*, per store, on webhook or 60 s poll: insert `posInbox` (dedupe on (`storeId`,`posSeq`)); **TX:** `lock(posCursor)`; apply rows with `posSeq>lastAppliedSeq` in order:
- `itemAvailability{itemRef, state, originAt}` → Op 7 with `origin=pos`.
- `priceUpdate{itemRef, priceCents}` → `storeItem.priceCents`, `priceVersion+=1` (open carts unaffected until Op 5; existing quotes never—invariant 20).
- `inventoryCount{ingredientRef, count, countAt}` → `posCount`, `posCountAt`; `projectedCount=posCount−Σ(ingredientUsage×quantity)` over app orders at the store not yet `posted` at `countAt` (`posAckedAt` null or `> countAt`); if `projectedCount<autoEightySixBelow` for an item using the ingredient and the item is not 86'd → Op 7 `origin=inventory`; if a restock lifts it and the current 86 is `auto=true` → Op 7 `available`.
- `posOrderCreated` (walk-in sale) → reduce `projectedCount` by its usage until the next count.
- `refundIssued` for our order with no matching `refund` → mirror as `refund(succeeded, actor=pos)` and claw back points.
Advance `lastAppliedSeq`. Atomic: cursor + each event's effects. EC: 60 s by poll, ~2 s by webhook. Conflict: POS snapshot vs app change → origin-time rule; POS outage → retry with dedupe.

**Op 12—Write: resolve an exception.** Inputs: `exceptionId`, `choice∈{substitute(newItemId, modifiers), refundLine, cancelOrder}`, `actorRef`. Auth: the order's host; the line's payer when `paysOwn`; a store manager acting for the customer (recorded in `chosenBy`); the kitchen clock. **TX:**
1. `lock(order)`, `lock(exception)`; require `resolvedAt` null, else return `alreadyResolved{resolution}`.
2. `substitute`: require `newItemId∈offeredSubstitutes` (a manager may pick any item priced ≤ the original) and `availableAt(new, max(now, order.fireAt))`; insert `orderLine(replacesLineId=old, unitPriceCents=min(new, old), state='queued')`; old line `voided`, `voidReason='unavailable'`; if cheaper, refund the difference as in step 3. Capacity is not re-checked (the store absorbs it).
3. `refundLine`: old line `voided`; `amount=lineTotal+tax share−discount share`; insert `refund(requested)` against the payer's payment; after TX call the processor (idempotent by `refundId`); on success insert `loyaltyEntry(−round(earnedOnPayment×amount / captured), clawback)`—the balance may go negative—and `posOutbox(refundPost)`. If every line is now voided → `cancelOrder`.
4. `cancelOrder`: all lines `voided`; full refund per payer; `lock(pickupSlot)`: `committedUnits−=order.units`; `order.state='cancelled'`; redeemed points returned (`loyaltyEntry(+points, adjust)`); promotion consumption is *not* returned to the pool (prevents count gaming; per-customer counts also stand).
5. Set `resolvedAt`, `resolution`, `chosenBy`. If no pending exceptions remain: `state=fireAt≤now?'fired':'scheduled'` (or stays `inProgress` if raised by `cantMake`). `notification(exceptionId:resolved)`.
Atomic: 1–5. EC: processor refund, POS post. UI: the choice is reflected immediately; if the deadline job won, the client receives `alreadyResolved`.

**Op 13—Write: refund (manager/support) and remake (expo/manager).** *Refund* inputs: `orderId`, `lineIds?` (null = full), `reason`. Auth: manager at the order's store or brand support. **TX:** `lock(order)`, `lock(payments)`; `refundable=captured−Σ refunds(succeeded|requested)` per payment; require requested ≤ refundable per payer; insert `refund` rows; clawback entries as in Op 12 step 3; `auditLog`. After TX: processor calls; `posOutbox(refundPost)`.
*Remake* inputs: `orderId`, `lineIds`, `reason`. Auth: expo device or manager at the store. **TX:** `lock(order)`; require `order.state∈{ready, handedOff}` and lines not already `remade`; insert child `order(isRemake=true, parentOrderId, state='fired', fireAt=now, promisedAt=now, totals=0, units, slotStart=parent's)` with copied line snapshots (`lineTotalCents=0`); parent lines → `remade`; no hold, capacity check, payment, loyalty, or promotion (invariant 16); `posOutbox(orderPost, isRemake)` so the POS books $0; `notification(childOrderId:placed)`. The child's readiness (Op 10) notifies `remakeReady`. Conflict: a second expo remaking the same line finds `remade` and is refused.

**Op 14—Bulk/periodic: capacity change, slot seeding, points expiry.**
*Capacity change* inputs: `storeId`, `fromSlot`, `toSlot` (≤ 56 slots), `capacityUnits?` or `close`. Auth: store manager or brand ops. **TX:** for each slot: `lock`; set `capacityUnits` or `state='closed'`; **never touch `committedUnits`**; `version+=1`. For carts at the store targeting a now-closed slot without a live hold: append `cartOp(systemSlotClosed)` (the host must re-pick; Op 5 step 4 refuses). Carts *with* a live hold keep it until it commits or expires (granted under the old capacity). `auditLog`.
*Slot seeding*, daily at 00:05 local: insert `pickupSlot` rows for day+14 across `openLocal..closeLocal` at `defaultSlotCapacityUnits`; idempotent by PK.
*Points expiry*, nightly per customer shard: FIFO lot accounting of `earn` against `redeem|clawback|expire`; lots older than 365 days with a remainder → `loyaltyEntry(−remaining, expire)`; 1 000 customers per TX; idempotent by (`customerId`, expiry date).

### 6. Scale and cost

Successful deployment: 600 stores, 5 M customers.

| Quantity | Size / rate |
|---|---|
| Digital orders | 600/store/day → 360 k/day; lunch peak 90/store/h → 15 orders/s chain-wide, bursts 40/s |
| Order lines | 2.6/order → 940 k/day, 340 M/year (append-only; partition by month) |
| Carts | 3× orders → 1.1 M/day; 12 ops/cart → 13 M ops/day, 150/s average, 600/s peak; group carts 8 % of orders, 4 participants, ~3 live devices each |
| Slots | 56/store/day, seeded 14 days ahead → 470 k live rows; hot rows see ~2 hold/commit writes per second per store at peak |
| 86 events | 5/store/day → 3 k/day; per-event fan-out at peak ≈ 40 open carts + 15 unfired orders at that store |
| KDS | 4 stations/store → 2 400 devices; heartbeat every 20 s → 120 writes/s; ~1 M claims/day; 2 400 live queue subscriptions |
| Live subscriptions | ~120 k customers watching order status at peak; ~6 k live group carts; 600 manager dashboards |
| Loyalty | 400 M entries/year; balance materialized (80+ rows per customer per year otherwise summed on every read) |
| Promotions | a national promo puts every order's write on one counter row at 15–40/s |
| POS | 600 outbox workers; 360 k order posts + 5 k refunds + 3 k availability per day; inbox 600 polls/min or ~10 webhooks/s; ~1.5 M push notifications/day |

Where it gets expensive: (1) **Op 7's fan-out** is bounded per store but runs inside the 86 transaction—cheap in aggregate, spiky per store, and it must not be dropped. (2) **Slot counters** are hot per-store rows; the risk is correctness under the hold/commit/expire triad, not throughput. (3) The **promotion counter** at 40 writes/s on one row is fine for a lock but is a chain-wide serialization point; the standard relief is per-store token batches drawn from the counter 5 at a time. (4) **Loyalty balance** must be materialized; the nightly expiry job is the largest batch (5 M customers, ~50 M entry reads). (5) **Station queue** ordering needs an index on (`storeId`, `stationId`, `state`, `promisedAt`); customer order history is unbounded and paginates by `placedAt`.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**U1—A predicate-selected fan-out write in the same transaction as its cause, with per-row derived values.** Op 7 must find every open cart line at a store matching an item *and* failing a time-parametrized predicate at each cart's own pickup instant, mark them, bump each cart's version, append a per-cart event, and raise per-order exceptions with computed deadlines—atomically with the availability change—so no customer observes "available" after staff observe "86'd". A one-row-at-a-time write API makes this a client-side loop with neither atomicity nor a guarantee of completion. *Concession:* fan out asynchronously within 5 s through a durable queue, **provided** the commit path (Op 5 step 2, Op 6 step 2) re-checks the *source* table and never the line marks. *Cheat boundary:* any order placeable for an 86'd item during the propagation window, or a propagation that can be lost. The marks are a projection; the commit must not trust a projection.

**U2—Structural counts over rows the viewer cannot read, maintained in the transactions that change them, and used to gate writes.** Spots left (Op 4/5), promotion consumption (Op 6), "+12 more" on a station queue, "2/4 items ready" on a ticket. A backend that filters every read by the caller's visibility returns a wrong integer for spots left—0 or 1—and *admits* it. *Concession:* denormalized counters (`committedUnits`, `heldUnits`, `consumed`) updated in the same transaction as every hold, commit, expiry, and cancel. *Cheat boundary:* a counter reconciled by a background job that also gates writes (it oversells between reconciliations), or any count over the caller's visible domain.

**U3—Operation-log merge with server-serialized application, deterministic conflict rules, and a state fence that reports per-op outcomes.** Op 3 needs idempotent per-device sequences, base-version conflict detection at line granularity, element-wise set merge with an asymmetric tie-break, refusal of everything after the freeze, and a per-op result the device can act on. A "set field = value" backend gives whole-row LWW and no outcome channel. *Concession:* owner-only line editing (the host may remove but not edit others' lines), which eliminates the host-vs-owner case. *Cheat boundary:* whole-cart LWW; silently dropping post-freeze ops; a post-freeze op reaching a placed order; or a modifier merge in which a concurrent *remove* can be undone by a concurrent *add*.

**U4—Time-driven transitions with exactly-once effects under system authority, fenced against concurrent user actions.** Fire at `fireAt`, not at creation; expire holds, quotes, and claims at their instants; apply an exception default at its deadline; expire points nightly. Each is a write nobody requested, under an authority no user has, that must re-check its predicate under lock and coexist with a user doing the same thing in the same instant (Op 9 vs Op 12). *Concession:* a 5-second polling sweeper with skip-locked batches. *Cheat boundary:* running the transition on a client when it happens to open the app; a sweeper that reads then writes without re-locking; or exposing the sweeper's authority through a user-callable endpoint.

**U5—Bidirectional sync of a shared fact with two clocks, plus at-least-once outbound delivery with dedupe, plus inbound events routed through the same fan-out as native writes.** Op 11's inbound 86 must be ordered by origin time against app-side events and then trigger U1; its outbound orders must survive a 20-minute outage without duplication. *Concession:* make the app the sole writer of the 86 list and have the register's 86 button call the app—U5 then reduces to the outbox. *Cheat boundary:* ignoring POS-originated 86s (staff use the register; the app keeps selling), or accepting "the sync eventually overwrites us" for a fact the app just changed.

**U6 (minor)—A read parametrized by an instant other than now.** Op 1's menu at 15:00 evaluates schedule intervals and 86 windows at a future instant; an "is this item currently available" call cannot answer it, and a boolean availability column cannot express the interval at all (invariant 21). No concession is needed; it is a pure predicate that has to be *expressible*.

### 8. Confidence

*Standard practice (high confidence):* snapshotting order lines at confirmation; hold-then-commit slot reservation with expiring holds; immutable quotes binding a checkout; authorize before placing, capture after committing; idempotency keys and a transactional outbox toward the POS; skip-locked sweeper batches; lease-style claims with expiry; an append-only points ledger with a materialized balance; per-fact origin-time ordering for two-writer sync; dedupe keys for exactly-once notification.

*Product decisions made for this exercise (medium confidence, defensible):* kitchen units rather than order counts as slot capacity (real chains throttle by throughput per 15-minute window); ASAP treated as the next slot; the 5-minute hold and 5-second sweeper cadence; 60-second device-derived claim liveness; 86 defaulting to close of business; owner-over-host and remove-over-add merge rules; refusing post-freeze edits rather than queuing a follow-on order; capacity bypass for remakes; substitutions capped at the paid price; promotion consumption not refunded on cancel; clawback allowed to drive a balance negative; points to the payer in split pay.

*Invented / least certain:* the exact inventory-projection formula (POS vendors differ on whether counts include pending tickets); putting all five consequences of an 86 in one transaction rather than a durable queue (U1's concession is the more common industrial shape); the 3-minute `cantMake` deadline. None of these change the traps; they change the numbers.
