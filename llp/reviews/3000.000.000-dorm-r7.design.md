Targeted final design clarification to the two-READY DORM §19 amendment:

The paired rename form uses the same closed scalar/lifetime boundary as computed down: references, assets, collections and engine-owned/maintained columns reject during compilation, before either direction can activate. This initial surface never accepts a forward-only protected rename.

Identical-type scalar renames within the same row/table remain supported. Fully structural type equality includes bounds/nullability/enums. Existing reference/asset/capture/expiry lifetime laws are unchanged. The implementation audit found that paired lowering had allowed forward protected migrations to be compiled even though reverse admission would refuse; this clarification requires refusal before any activation, preventing a one-way advertised rename. No new value conversion or authority is added. All other r6 design semantics remain unchanged.
