What I checked and found sound:

- Per-arm partition logging deduplicates principal recipients. The changed-row accounting handles transient updates, births, deletion, and deletion followed by reinsertion: surviving events account for final recipients while preserving revocations owed to initial recipients. Finding 1 is a separate membership-boundary gap.
- Ordinary puts, R1 conversions, snapshots, and join expansion check current readability before sending images. R1 uses the viewer projection and an independent unbounded ledger.
- Scope augmentation covers logged, expiry, and predecessor scopes. Both application paths validate scope width and apply column-arm sparing. Prediction restoration precedes ordinary and deferred application; staged snapshots inspect staged committed images. The trailing-principal trust argument is sound.
- The put-before-scope disposition is sound for the described leave commits. Group events at the leave sequence are excluded; principal events settle ownership in either write order.
- Per-arm marker typing, boundary lookup, floor admission, and authorization-absence coverage implement the intended conservative coverage rules. The added simulator worlds improve coverage, but do not reach findings 1–3.
