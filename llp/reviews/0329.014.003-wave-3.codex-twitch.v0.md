### 2. Schema in v0

An exact transliteration is impossible: v0 has no optional/nullable type, enums/check constraints, bytes, partial unique indexes, field ACLs, or i64. Below is the complete declaration-equivalent rewrite.

Notation:

- `T(n) = text({max:n})`, `N=int()`, `B=bool()`, `At=time()`, `J(n)=json({maxBytes:n})`.
- Enums are `T(n)` plus algebraic allowlists conjoined to every insert/update rule.
- `x?` is not invented syntax: it expands to a unique auxiliary `{ownerId,value}` table with the same four rules.
- `R(r,i,u,d)` expands to all four required rules.
- `P=public(reason)`, `S=principal.kind==='system'`, `F=staff(viewer)`, `X(u)=u===viewer`, `O(c)=channel owner`.
- `RT!(c)` means exact current broadcaster/editor/moderator/staff access; `I!(op)` means write authority restricted to a named operation. The `!` marks a required predicate v0 cannot express, so these declarations remain refused.
- Unspecified text/JSON maxima are **[inferred]**.
- `U`, `A`, `Im`, `Ex`, and `Cap` below mean `unique`, `appendOnly`, `immutable`, `exclusion`, and `cap`.

### 7. Product changes

| Product change | What it dodges | When it is acceptable | When it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cap hot channels at a few thousand or poll every 2s | Shared multicast/QoS | Explicitly smaller community product | Keeping the 100k/live-feel promise |

Schema/logic corrections are not concessions: preserve seq delivery order, reauthorize sockets after role changes, use a rolling spam window, store frozen `deliverAt`, and distinguish outcome point totals from bettor counts.

### 8. Where the design is wrong

1. **Delayed delivery contradicts the ordered log.** System messages are published with delay zero, so seq 101 can overtake delayed user seq 100. Confirm by sending seq 100 under six-second delay, then committing a mode-change seq 101 one second later; a delayed client sees 101 before 100.

2. **Moderator revocation does not revoke an existing subscription.** Catch-up classifies once; GrantOrRevokeRole only promises the next send/catch-up sees the change. Confirm by revoking a moderator without disconnecting their socket: it continues receiving `holds` and realtime bodies.

3. **Watch accrual does not prove a completed five-minute slice.** Its predicate awards anyone with a join/heartbeat in the window. Confirm by joining at 4:59.9, sending one heartbeat, and receiving the full 10/12 points—or by leaving after 45 seconds and still receiving the slice.
