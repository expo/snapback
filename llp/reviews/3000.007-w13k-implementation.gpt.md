1. **High — A renewal can erase the acquisition obligation.**  
   [partition.rs:691](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-w13-review-f/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/partition.rs:691), with [interval construction at 583](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-w13-review-f/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/partition.rs:583) and [renewal skip at 707](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-w13-review-f/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/partition.rs:707).

   `intervals` closes the preceding span on **every** join, including a live renewal. The new `span.to.is_none()` condition consequently skips the original grant; the final join is then skipped as continuous.

   Concrete scenario: existing posts precede Bob’s watermark; Bob joins at sequence 2 with expiry 1000, renews at time 500/sequence 3 to expiry 2000, then catches up. Sequence 2 fails `open`; sequence 3 satisfies `continuous`. Neither supplies posts or acquisition markers, and the watermark advances past both. Horizonless local reads can consequently report an empty complete result.

   The same composition after an expired predecessor also skips the scope attached to the replacement join, potentially preserving rows deleted during the membership gap. The existing renewal test first acquires a snapshot, so it misses this case ([w13g_probe.rs:311](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-w13-review-f/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/tests/w13g_probe.rs:311)).

   **Smallest correct change:** distinguish expiry-tracking spans from uninterrupted membership authority. Preserve the original acquiring join through continuous renewals, including its predecessor scope, while keeping genuinely revoked intervals closed. Add grant→renew→catch-up and expiry→replacement→renew→catch-up regressions, including pagination, both device stores and wasm.
