NOT READY.

SEC13 intends the right class — default witnessed questions, no admission/gate-registry change — but as written it leaves material correctness, evaluability, and budget gaps.

Governing contracts: [LLP 3000.036.004.000 §13](llp/3000.036.004.000-structural-write-authority.rfc.md), [§12 work/reservation rules](llp/3000.036.004.000-structural-write-authority.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.036.004 §19](llp/3000.036.004-security-invariants-and-exposure.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.037.000 §1](llp/3000.037.000-wave-30-default-verdict-and-password-starters.rfc.md), and SEC11 item 1 in [LLP 3000.036.004.001](llp/3000.036.004.001-witnessed-security-decisions.rfc.md).

Concrete issues:

1. Default-visible unwitnessed questions. SEC13 says time questions "retain source-bound rationale when no replay is available and print compact source-fact question by default". Governing rule is witnessed-only by default; unwitnessed advice stays behind `--review`. Either this creates default-visible unwitnessed items, breaking the median `<=5` output target and the DX13 verdict contract, or "by default" means card style, in which case reword.
2. Unacknowledgeable time questions. Media/time ack is defined as requiring "current concrete replay evidence" plus the SEC10 digest/`permit OPERATION as CALLER`/`audience:`/`intended effect:` clause. A no-replay source-fact question therefore has no defined disposition. It becomes permanent default pending noise. Define: unwitnessed stays behind `--review`, and state its ack path or explicit coverage-only status.
3. No work-reservation separation or per-candidate cap. SEC11 item 1 states question evidence must not compete with the 3,072 gate reservation, keeps the 8,192 advisory limit, and caps repeat replay at two calls per candidate with deterministic `Context::with_ids` IDs and no entropy fallback. SEC13 says only "no new campaign budget". Name the consumed reservation, restate gate non-competition, per-candidate call cap, deterministic IDs, row-write ceiling, and no unbounded second search. "Every edit that can move that time" is otherwise an unbounded enumeration inside a fixed budget.
4. Media-witness precision missing. SEC11 absence required single-write operation, no maintains/branching/loops, dedicated `$derived/sec11-absence/` site, final-store survival at effective clock, commit-end expiry exclusion, and isolation from authored invariants. SEC13 says "committed surviving parent" and "atomic repair is not absence" but drops the operation limits, site prefix, live-at-clock/expiry rule, and committed-row predicate evaluation. Risk of witnessing a repaired intermediate state as absence.
5. Suppression conditions undefined. "Safe required argument/guard" and "atomic child creation supplies no witness" have no predicate. "Immutable anchors" and "exact value-preserving writes are quiet" need the existing immutability/discriminator definitions. Without them the quiet/suppress paths are unevaluable.
6. Wrong-anchor equivalence undefined. "An equivalent check on that event operand answers the candidate, a bound against now alone does not" needs an equivalence test: same window and offset, same operation scope, and handling of a constraint that already exists in another guard. Otherwise any check on the event operand clears the question, or an already-constrained schema keeps asking.
7. Redaction and identity not restated. Media witnesses carry images/assets; SEC10 §13 and §19 require `<redacted>` opaque values, alpha-normalized IDs, canonical evidence-plus-code identity, and live-only raw exclusion. SEC13 reuses "the existing question card" but never retains redaction, normalization, or witness-renewal on evidence change. State these, plus no new gate codes and no new top-level output section, to hold the no-lane-merge scope. Presentation ownership should name DX14 as SEC11 does.
