## Material finding: the `!=` carve-out in `old_restricted` compares the wrong side

[transition_questions.rs:53](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-b31-sec11/snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/bind/security/derived/transition_questions.rs:53) exempts a `!=` guard only when the *focused* side print-equals the caller value:

```rust
op=="!=" && ((focused(left) && print(left)==print(value))
           || (focused(right) && print(right)==print(value)))
```

A focused side is always a qualified member (`b.stage`, `.stage`). `value` is always the bare caller arg (`stage`). They can never print-equal, so the exception is dead code.
