Concrete scenario: `query any(): return exists tasks where .owner = viewer` over a parties-only rule with no `where`-`or` keeps the single-index plan (true-or-unavailable), while §8 promises `exists(b1) or exists(b2)` answering `false`; no test covers that shape (the existing `exists` test and `exists-parties-false` example are both `where`-`or`/case-1). Behavior is sound and engine/model agree — only the words are wrong. Smallest fix: reword §8, §9, and `OPEN.md` C8 to "case 1 only", matching §13; or implement the grant-free case-2 `exists` merge in both binder and model.
