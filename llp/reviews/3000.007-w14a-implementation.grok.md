## Findings

### Medium — reads card compact spelling still states the 32-word-per-scope rule

- **Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/reads.rs:341–352`
- **What:** The prose at 216–225 matches the corrected table (resumable read-meter drain cuts, heap-null even during drain). The compact **Reads** spelling still says a uniform lazy drain cut “carries `next` after its last emitted row” and that matching merges have “at most 32 words per scope”. That is the only remaining product sentence of that form (`at most 32 words per scope` appears nowhere else under `snapback4/` except this line). After W14b, this card *is* the README's search/merge reference.
- **Scenario:** A builder (or `guide reads`) copies the compact grammar, still plans a 32-word cap for a uniform chat search, and is told an unqualified drain `next`.
- **Smallest fix:** Replace those two clauses with the same qualified sentences already used at 216–225 and in `search.rs` 101–109 (meter-bounded uniform expansion; 32 only for admitted non-uniform; resumable drain cuts with the heap exception).

### Low — `N_WIDE_MERGE` still overpromises drain continuation

- **Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/codes.rs:160`
- **What:** `why` still ends “a uniform lazy drain cut carries `next` after its last emitted row” with no heap/final-answer/shape qualifications. Serialized `meter`/`words` are unchanged; this is builder text only.
- **Scenario:** `check` emits `N_WIDE_MERGE` on a matching merge; `why` contradicts the search card.
- **Smallest fix:** Same qualified drain/heap sentence as the search card.

### Low — range/list descriptor heap is no longer the old `charge_lane` approximation

- **Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:1046–1054` and `word_merge.rs:116–126`; pin at `tests/trending/lanes.rs:192–194`
- **What:** `charge_lane` still charges only the range/list **step**. Descriptor **heap** for those lanes now uses `vec_growth` + `word_heap::push` (36,720 → 27,472). The R0 brief said to leave that path untouched. Default heap is 16 MiB, so a production range/list merge is unlikely to change cut/no-cut; the ranked-key test would now pass a merge that used to sit between those two heap numbers.
- **Scenario:** A W15c-style trending merge under a test heap ceiling in (27,472, 36,720] completes instead of heap-cutting.
- **Smallest fix:** Keep the exact formula for word descriptors; restore the previous range/list descriptor charge (or document this as an intentional exact-accounting fix and leave the pin).

### Medium — witness observations could change; not compared to base

- **Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/src/device.rs:245–251`; `read.rs:672–680` with discovery retained at 432–439; report §2 witness / app-diagnostic mismatch
