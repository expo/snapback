### 1. Product contract

**Handnote** is city-scale USD bearer cash issued by **Harbor Mint**, a licensed e-money institution. People carry notes on a phone or watch and pay stalls, buses, and neighbors for days with no network. The mint is the only issuer, redeemer, and double-spend adjudicator. It is **not** the live authority over a note that has already been loaded into a pocket. Ordinary use is offline device-to-device payment; online settlement is bookkeeping, not the purchase.

**Actors.** *Holder:* a KYC-tiered person with at most one **active pocket** device at a time. *Merchant:* a registered till that accepts notes and refunds them. *Transit gate:* a merchant till with a 300 ms fare path. *Mint operator / compliance:* Harbor staff who issue, freeze, extend offline windows in disasters, and pay guarantee claims. *Bank partner:* the external RTP/ACH rail that funds minting and pays redemptions.

**What a note is.** A note is a mint-signed serial of a fixed denomination in integer USD cents. Legal denominations: `1, 5, 25, 100, 500, 2000, 10000` (1¢ through $100). Payments move **whole notes**. Making $3.50 from a $20 is a local **canonical split**, then a one-way handoff of the exact set. The serial survives splits only as ancestry: children are new serials; the parent serial is dead.

Harbor Mint promises:

1. A holder can pay any in-limit amount to any in-range device (NFC to start, BLE for the payload, QR only as a session-hash bootstrap) with **zero mint connectivity**, and the payee can **re-spend those notes immediately** to a third device that is also offline.
2. After the payee’s device has verified the chain and stored the notes, the payment is **accepted cash**, not a pending intent. The UI’s primary balance is accepted cash. A secondary badge reads `unsettled` until the mint has seen the chain. Labeling accepted cash “pending” is a broken product.
3. The same notes cannot settle as live money to two recipients. If a sender copies a pocket and pays two disconnected payees, **at most one chain settles**. The other chain is void from the branch. Double-spend is a crime the mint detects with certainty at settlement and punishes (freeze, legal identity, clawback from online account). It is not a sync conflict to merge.
4. A verified merchant who accepted in policy (valid signatures, hop count, offline expiry, revocation bundle age, amount limits) and then holds a voided chain-tip is **made whole from the mint’s guarantee fund** at the note’s denomination. Intermediaries who already passed the note on are even and are not paid twice. A consumer-to-consumer victim is covered up to $100 per incident under the same policy tests.
5. Signing a handoff **kills the notes for the signer even if the radio dies before an ack**. Lost-bill behavior: retry the **same** `sessionId`; do not resurrect notes locally. Unclaimed signed handoffs reclaim to the signer only after 7 days **and** only if the payee has not settled them.
6. A person may own many devices and may back them up. **Only one device is the pocket.** A backup restore of live notes is an `untrusted_copy`, not cash. Moving the pocket from phone to watch is an explicit all-notes handoff between two device certificates of the same `personId`.
7. Offline life of a note is **72 hours from its last mint settlement**, not from the last hop. Hopping does not refresh the timer. A mint-signed `EmergencyExtension` (disaster) may extend that window up to 14 extra days and raise hop caps; it spreads by gossip, not by hoping towers recover.
8. A payee may refuse a note that is expired, over hop cap (32 for merchants, 8 for transit gates, 128 under a live emergency extension), on the local revocation list, from a revoked or expired device certificate, or that would break the payee’s pocket cap. Refusals are typed, because a cashier has to know what to do.
9. Pocket caps, honest-device enforced and mint-audited: unverified receive-only, $100 pocket, 24 h to settle; KYC-basic $500 pocket, $200 per handoff, $2,000/day mint+redeem; KYC-full $2,000 / $500 / $10,000; merchant till $10,000 / $2,000 incoming / $50,000/day; transit fare box $25,000, fares $100–$1500.
10. **Refunds** are new handoffs from merchant to holder, linked to a receipt, allowed until `refundUntil` (merchant policy, default 24 h, max 14 days). Partial refunds are allowed. Cumulative refunds cannot exceed the receipt’s `amountCents`. A mint operator cannot “edit the payment back.”
11. **Mint and redeem** against a Harbor account funded by the bank partner. RTP credit is spendable-as-notes only after the partner’s definitive success. The holder can mint a requested mix of denominations, or `optimized`, onto the active pocket, and redeem live notes back to the account.
12. Harbor’s books stay solvent: sum of account balances + face value of outstanding live notes = customer claims. Staff cannot inflate serials. A serial is never reused.
13. Harbor does not tell a payee the payer’s pocket total, KYC legal name, or other notes. The payee sees offered notes, a display name, a role (`consumer|merchant|transit`), a KYC tier, and a device certificate. Mapping `devicePub` → legal identity is mint-only. Chain device pubs are visible to anyone who holds the note (offline cash cannot be Chaumian-blind and passable).
14. Two overlapping spends of one live note on one device: one session locks it, the other gets `note_busy`. Two tills are two pockets; they do not share a drawer without an explicit handoff.
15. Stolen-device report from a second device or the web appends a revocation. Notes the thief already passed to in-policy merchants follow promise 4. Notes still on the stolen device will not settle. Harbor will not pretend it can remote-wipe an offline pocket. Cash already handed over before gossip arrived is treated like a stolen wallet, unless merchant guarantee applies.
16. Settlement uploads the append-only chain and splits; the mint verifies signatures, conservation, uniqueness of `(noteId, fromVersion)`, limits, and revocation. Success refreshes `expireOfflineAt` to `mintNow + 72h` (plus any live emergency extension).
17. A transit tap completes in 300 ms: exact fare by local split on the passenger device, one-way handoff, gate beep. Gates do not give change.
18. Fees, disclosed before mint/redeem/merchant settlement: P2P and transit taps 0%; merchant incoming 0.3% of settled face value at settlement; mint/redeem 0 under $500/day per person, then 0.5%. Fees debit the account, never by silently shrinking a note’s face value.
19. Multi-device history (receipts, settled chains) may appear on a holder’s other devices after settlement. **Live notes never appear on two devices as spendable.**
20. Clock skew: a payee rejects a handoff whose `payerTime` differs from local now by more than 1 hour, or whose `expireOfflineAt` is already past local now. Final time is mint time at settlement.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

1. **[Money]** Every amount is a positive integer number of USD cents; face values are only the seven legal denominations; floating point is never used for money.
2. **[Money]** A `noteId` is assigned once by the mint, never reused, and at most one **live** bearer exists for that serial.
3. **[Money]** For every minted serial, at every mint-admitted instant, the sum of live descendant face values equals the original denomination, or the value has been redeemed exactly once, never both, never neither (except during a lock that settlement will resolve).
4. **[Money]** Consuming a pair `(noteId, fromVersion)` a second time is not an update, a merge, or a retry; it is a double-spend case.
5. **[Money]** After a device emits a valid signature on a handoff or split of a note it holds, that device’s possession of that `(noteId, fromVersion)` is dead, including when the other radio never acks.
6. **[Money, Time]** `notes.settledBearerDeviceId` is who last **settled**, not who currently **holds**. Spending authority while disconnected is exclusive possession of the chain tip, not a server field.
7. **[Money, Time]** A device is the live bearer of a note only on the half-open interval `[receivedAt, spentAt)`. `spent` / `live` as a timeless enum on a replicated row cannot express this.
8. **[Money]** Whole-note conservation: a handoff moves one serial unchanged; a split consumes one parent and produces two to sixteen children whose denominations sum to the parent and each appear in the canonical break table.
9. **[Money]** Canonical breaks are the only legal splits: `10000→5×2000`, `2000→4×500`, `500→5×100`, `100→4×25`, `25→5×5`, `5→5×1`. Arbitrary partitions are rejected at settlement.
10. **[Money]** A pocket’s live face value never exceeds the holder’s cap, and live note **count** never exceeds 200, on an honest device; the mint rechecks both against the settled picture and freezes overflows.
11. **[Money, Time]** `expireOfflineAt` is set at mint or at successful settlement to `mintNow + 72h` and is **not** moved by a hop. An `EmergencyExtension` is the only other writer, and only inside its `[validFrom, validUntil)`.
12. **[Money, Time]** Hop count is the number of handoffs since last settlement. Over-cap acceptances are either refused or, if a malicious payee accepted anyway, rejected at settlement and not covered by guarantee.
13. **[Money]** Two-way change (principal one direction, change the other) commits both directions on a device or leaves that device’s notes locked to the `sessionId`; it never applies one direction.
14. **[Money, Time]** A refund is a new session with `purpose=refund` and `receiptId` set. It does not delete, invert, or rewrite the original handoffs. Sum of refunds for a receipt ≤ `receipts.amountCents`, and each refund occurs at a device time ≤ `refundUntil` (mint time used if later disputed).
15. **[Money]** First valid settlement of a `(noteId, fromVersion)` wins. The other chain is void from that version. Void notes cannot redeem. The current in-policy holder of a void tip may claim guarantee; intermediaries who passed it on cannot.
16. **[Money]** Harbor solvency: `sum(accounts.balanceCents) + sum(live notes face) + guarantee_fund + mint_float = corresponding asset accounts` at each settlement watermark; serials cannot be minted without a matching account debit or float debit.
17. **[Money, Time]** Mint and redeem are idempotent on `fundingIntentId` / `redeemIntentId`. A partner timeout is not a failure that authorizes a new partner key.
18. **[Money, Time]** Daily mint/redeem/incoming velocity is counted in `America/New_York` on mint-admitted events, not on device clocks.
19. **[Secrecy, Other people’s data]** A payee learns only the notes offered, the counterparty device certificate’s displayable fields, and typed refusals. Pocket totals, account balances, legal names, and unrelated serials are not in the protocol.
20. **[Secrecy, Other people’s data]** The mint stores the map `deviceId → personId`. Participants see `devicePub` values on chains they hold. Publishing that map, or a count that reveals another person’s pocket, is forbidden.
21. **[Time]** Acceptance checks use the payee’s last `trustBundle` (mint key, revocation watermark, optional emergency extension). Guarantee coverage uses that bundle’s age at `acceptedAt`: ≤24 h full; 24–72 h half; >72 h none.
22. **[Time]** Device certificates expire (`expiresAt`). An expired cert cannot originate a handoff that settlement will honor.
23. **[Money, Other people’s data]** Mint support, refunds staff, and compliance cannot spend, move, or resurrect notes they do not physically hold, except by freeze-at-settlement and court-ordered reissue of **unspent-at-mint** serials.
24. **[Money]** `untrusted_copy` notes are not spendable until the mint attests that those serials are still outstanding to that device; if they already settled elsewhere, the copies become `void_double_spend`.
25. **[Money]** Fees never change a serial’s `denominationCents`. Face value is immutable after mint.

### 3. The traps, ranked

1. **Authority as a replicated `holderId` (authority + merge).**  
   **Wrong version:** One `notes` table syncs to every device. Paying copies `holderId=payee` locally; last-write-wins or CRDT merge later.  
   **Why it is wrong:** A replica fence **copies**. Bearer cash **moves**. While disconnected, the mint’s row is stale by construction (I6). Two disconnected replicas of the same live row are two spending powers. Merging them “into one holder” either steals from an honest payee or magics money. This is the coterie trap for cash: live bearer versus historical holder is an interval `[receivedAt, spentAt)`, and the mint’s `settledBearerDeviceId` is a **different time slice** than the pocket’s chain tip. A status enum `live|spent` on a synced row is `member|former` — not an interval, and not exclusive.  
   **Observation:** Alice’s phone loads a $20. Her iPad still has the synced row. Both, offline, pay different stalls. Both stalls re-spend. Both stalls later settle. Any merge that keeps a single `holderId` has destroyed one merchant’s cash without recording a crime.

2. **Treating a missed ack as an abort (concurrency + time).**  
   **Wrong version:** Payer signs, radio drops, 120 s timer fires, payer sets notes back to `live` and pays someone else.  
   **Why it is wrong:** The signature **is** the transfer (promise 5). The payee may already have verified and re-spent. Resurrecting notes is a built-in double-spend with a UX excuse.  
   **Observation:** Kill BLE after the payee has applied and before the payer’s ack. Payer’s UI “fails” and retries a **new** session to a second payee. Both chains are validly signed by Alice. The mint can punish Alice; it cannot make the protocol honest if resurrection is allowed.

3. **Backup restore and the second device as a second pocket (merge + authority).**  
   **Wrong version:** Restore last night’s snapshot onto a new phone; both snapshots contain the same `pocketNotes`; both spend. Or watch and phone both sync the wallet.  
   **Why it is wrong:** Users want backups and a watch. Photocopying bills is not a backup of cash. Multi-device replica of live notes **is** trap 1 with a legitimate-looking cause.  
   **Observation:** Restore a backup taken before a $60 market day; spend the restored $60 while the original phone’s notes are already with merchants. Settlement of the restored serials is double-spend against the owner’s own KYC identity.

4. **Refreshing `expireOfflineAt` on each hop (time).**  
   **Wrong version:** Each accept sets `expireOfflineAt = now + 72h`, so circulating cash never goes stale.  
   **Why it is wrong:** Two colluding devices hop a note between themselves forever, delay settlement forever, and delay double-spend detection forever. The offline window is a **fraud-detection deadline**, not a freshness hint. Disaster longevity is a mint-signed extension, not a hop timer.  
   **Observation:** A fraudster pays Bob, who is offline for 10 days in a real outage. If hop-refresh were the rule, the fraudster’s clone spend to Carol also looks fresh. If settlement-only refresh is the rule, Bob may refuse stale notes unless an `EmergencyExtension` gossipped through the market — which is the product users in a hurricane actually need.

5. **Refund or change as editing the original payment (authority + time).**  
   **Wrong version:** Delete or invert the original handoff rows; or patch `denominationCents`; or the mint “puts the serial back” in the payer’s pocket while the merchant is still offline holding it.  
   **Why it is wrong:** The merchant may have already paid their wholesaler with those serials. Clawing them back from the mint’s lagged registry is taking money from whoever holds the tip now. Face-value mutation breaks I25 and every downstream conservation check.  
   **Observation:** Coffee $7, merchant refunds $7 by deleting the session, customer’s phone shows $7 live again, merchant’s till still has the notes and pays the baker. Two live $7s.

6. **Visibility-filtered uniqueness, caps, and refusals (visibility).**  
   **Wrong version:** “How many live copies of serial S?” counted inside the caller’s readable rows; pocket-cap check against the local replica; `insufficient_funds` vs `revoked_device` vs `expired_note` collapsed to a generic `no`.  
   **Why it is wrong:** Each dishonest holder sees **one** live row, so the count is 1 everywhere and never 2. A structural uniqueness question about the serial is not a per-user policy count. Caps that exist only locally are theater: a patched client accepts $50,000 offline and the mint discovers it only if settlement rechecks a **global** outstanding set. Generic refusal hides the cashier’s next action and, conversely, a typed refusal leaks (revoked ≈ stolen, expired ≈ 72 h offline) — that leak is **accepted**, but then it must be the same for every honest till, not a function of what rows the till can read.  
   **Observation:** Two pockets each report `count(noteId=S, live)=1`. A mint dashboard that uses the same visibility-filtered count reports circulation of one $20 when two merchants both hold it. Guarantee payouts and AML velocity are then computed on a lie.

### 4. Data model

Types: `uuid`, `text`, `int` (cents or counts), `bytes`, `bool`, `time` (UTC instant). Enums are `text` with the listed values. Money fields are `int` cents.

**Derived, never stored as authority:** `pocketBalanceCents = sum(pocketNotes.denominationCents where possessionState='live')`; `unsettledCents` same with `settledInBatchId` null; `account.balanceCents` = sum of `accountEntries.signedCents`; `hopCount` = handoffs since the note’s last `settlementBatchId`; mint `outstandingCents` = sum of `notes` that are not `redeemed`/`void` at current version.

**Time-bounded:** `deviceCerts.expiresAt`; `notes.expireOfflineAt`; `sessions.radioExpiresAt`; `sessions.reclaimAfter`; `receipts.refundUntil`; `limitWindows` (one NY calendar day); `emergencyExtensions.validFrom/validUntil`; `trustBundles.createdAt` (age drives guarantee); `fundingIntents` pending until partner terminal state or 7-day ops kill.

---

**people** — versioned. PK `personId`.  
`legalName text`, `kycTier text` (`unverified|basic|full`), `kycExpiresAt time`, `status text` (`active|frozen|closed`), `displayName text`, `createdAt time`.  
Frozen people cannot mint, redeem, or settle incoming except guarantee payouts.

**deviceCerts** — versioned, mint-signed. PK `deviceId`. Unique `devicePub`.  
`personId → people`, `devicePub bytes`, `role text` (`consumer|merchant|transit|mint_ops`), `displayName text`, `isActivePocket bool`, `issuedAt time`, `expiresAt time` (default +365 d), `mintSig bytes`, `status text` (`active|revoked|superseded`).  
At most one `isActivePocket=true` per `personId` among `status=active` (mint-enforced at certification and at `MovePocket` settlement; devices also track this locally).

**merchants** — versioned. PK `merchantId`. Unique `personId`.  
`category text` (`street|retail|transit|wholesale`), `defaultRefundHours int` (1–336), `pocketCapCents int`, `perHandoffInCents int`, `feeBps int` (30 for street/retail, 0 for transit).

**accounts** — versioned projection. PK `accountId`. Unique `personId`.  
`balanceCents int` (derived, stored as a cache), `status text`.

**accountEntries** — **append-only**. PK `entryId`. Index `(accountId, createdAt)`.  
`accountId`, `signedCents int` (positive credit), `reason text` (`mint_debit|redeem_credit|fee|guarantee_credit|clawback_debit|partner_funding|partner_payout`), `intentId uuid`, `batchId uuid|null`, `createdAt time`.

**fundingIntents** — versioned. PK `fundingIntentId`. Unique `(personId, clientOpId)`.  
`personId`, `deviceId`, `direction text` (`mint|redeem`), `amountCents int`, `mixJson text` (denomination counts or `optimized`), `partner text`, `partnerKey text` (idempotency to the bank), `partnerPayloadDigest bytes`, `state text` (`created|sent|succeeded|failed|indeterminate`), `createdAt time`, `terminalAt time`.  
Spans the bank partner.

**notes** — mint registry, **versioned, not a writable replica on devices**. PK `noteId`. Unique `(noteId, currentVersion)` as current tip. Index `(settledBearerDeviceId, expireOfflineAt)`, index `ancestryRootId`.  
`ancestryRootId uuid` (original minted serial), `parentNoteId uuid|null`, `denominationCents int` (immutable), `currentVersion int`, `state text` (`outstanding|redeemed|frozen|void`), `settledBearerDeviceId uuid|null`, `settledPersonId uuid|null`, `issuedAt time`, `expireOfflineAt time`, `lastSettledAt time|null`, `issueSig bytes` (mint signature over `noteId, denominationCents, issuedAt, ancestryRootId`).  
This row **lags**. It is updated only by mint, redeem, settlement, freeze, void.

**pocketNotes** — **device-local exclusive possession; must not replicate as live to any other device**. PK `(deviceId, noteId)`.  
`deviceId`, `noteId`, `denominationCents int`, `version int` (chain tip this device holds), `possessionState text` (`live|locked|spent|split_parent|untrusted_copy|void_double_spend|redeemed`), `sessionId uuid|null`, `expireOfflineAt time`, `receivedAt time`, `spentAt time|null`, `chainBlob bytes` (the verified handoff+split chain since last settlement, plus mint `issueSig`), `settledInBatchId uuid|null`.  
`live` rows on two devices for one `noteId` is already a protocol breach; the backend must not cause it by sync.

**handoffs** — **append-only**. PK `handoffId`. Unique `(noteId, fromVersion)` **globally at the mint**; locally unique per device. Index `(sessionId)`, `(toDevicePub, createdAt)`.  
`sessionId`, `noteId`, `fromVersion int`, `toVersion int` (`fromVersion+1`), `fromDeviceId`, `toDeviceId`, `fromDevicePub bytes`, `toDevicePub bytes`, `denominationCents int`, `payerTime time`, `purpose text` (`purchase|refund|p2p|fare|pocket_move|change`), `sig bytes` (Ed25519 of deterministic CBOR), `receivedAt time` (payee local), `settledInBatchId uuid|null`.  
Insert-only. Never update except the mint stamping `settledInBatchId`.

**splits** — **append-only**. PK `splitId`. Unique `(parentNoteId, parentVersion)` at the mint.  
`parentNoteId`, `parentVersion int`, `deviceId`, `childJson` (list of `{noteId, denominationCents}`, 2–16 children, canonical break only), `sig bytes`, `createdAt time`, `settledInBatchId uuid|null`.

**sessions** — versioned, local then uploaded. PK `sessionId`.  
`payerDeviceId`, `payeeDeviceId`, `amountCents int`, `purpose text`, `receiptId uuid|null`, `state text` (`open|locked|signed|applied|abandoned`), `radioExpiresAt time` (created+120 s), `reclaimAfter time` (created+7 d), `createdAt time`.

**receipts** — **append-only**. PK `receiptId`. Index `(payerPersonId, createdAt)`, `(payeeMerchantId, createdAt)`.  
`sessionId`, `payerDeviceId`, `payeeDeviceId`, `payeeMerchantId uuid|null`, `amountCents int`, `refundedCents int` (cache of sum of later refund sessions; reconstructible), `refundUntil time`, `noteIdsJson text`, `createdAt time`.  
Replicated as **history** to the payer’s other devices after settlement. Not spendable.

**revocationEvents** — **append-only**. PK `revocationId`. Index `(deviceId, revokedAt)`.  
`deviceId`, `personId`, `reason text` (`stolen|frozen|superseded|ops`), `revokedAt time`, `mintSig bytes`.

**emergencyExtensions** — mint-signed, time-bounded. PK `extensionId`.  
`validFrom time`, `validUntil time`, `extraHours int` (≤336), `hopCapMerchant int`, `hopCapTransit int`, `region text`, `mintSig bytes`.

**trustBundles** — signed snapshots devices gossip. PK `bundleId`.  
`mintKeyId text`, `revocationWatermark time` (all `revocationEvents.revokedAt` ≤ watermark are included or hashed), `revocationBloom bytes`, `extensionId uuid|null`, `createdAt time`, `mintSig bytes`.  
Devices accept a bundle only with a valid mint signature and `createdAt` not in the future by >1 h.

**settlementBatches** — **append-only**. PK `batchId`. Index `(deviceId, createdAt)`.  
`deviceId`, `personId`, `handoffIds`, `splitIds`, `mintNow time`, `result text` (`accepted|partial|rejected`), `doubleSpendCaseIds`, `createdAt time`.

**doubleSpendCases** — append-only evidence plus versioned investigation. PK `caseId`. Unique competing pair.  
`noteId`, `fromVersion int`, `firstHandoffId`, `secondHandoffId`, `firstSettledBatchId`, `detectedAt time`, `status text` (`open|frozen|guarantee_paid|closed`), `spenderPersonId`, `winnerDeviceId`, `loserDeviceId`.

**guaranteeClaims** — versioned. PK `claimId`. Unique `(caseId, claimantDeviceId)`.  
`caseId`, `claimantPersonId`, `claimantDeviceId`, `amountCents int`, `bundleAgeHours int`, `policyResult text` (`full|half|none|pending`), `payoutEntryId uuid|null`.

**limitWindows** — versioned. PK `(personId, yyyymmdd, kind)` with `kind` in `mint|redeem|incoming_settle`.  
`usedCents int`. Mint-only. Not on devices except as a cached advisory.

### 5. The hard operations

Signature encoding for all signed objects: deterministic CBOR, Ed25519, mint or device key as specified. Transport: NFC or QR carries `sessionId`+endpoint; payload on BLE, max 64 KB, 120 s radio timeout.

---

**5.1 ReadPocket** (read, local)

**Inputs:** `deviceId`.  
**Auth:** the calling app is that device; no other person reads this pocket.  
**Steps:** (1) Load `pocketNotes` for `deviceId` with `possessionState in (live, locked, spent)`. (2) `liveCents = sum(live)`, `lockedCents = sum(locked)`, `unsettledCents = sum(live|locked|spent where settledInBatchId is null)`. (3) Flag any live note with `expireOfflineAt ≤ now` as `stale`. (4) Return mix by denomination, stale flags, `isActivePocket` from local `deviceCerts` copy, last `trustBundle.createdAt`.  
**Atomic:** read snapshot of local pocket.  
**Eventual:** mint registry may disagree; UI must not show mint holder as truth.  
**UI:** show `liveCents` as cash; badge unsettled and stale. Not optimistic.  
**Conflict:** none.

---

**5.2 MintNotes** (write, online, external bank)

**Inputs:** `personId`, `deviceId`, `clientOpId`, `amountCents`, `mix` (`optimized` or map denom→count), `direction=mint`.  
**Auth:** active person, active pocket device certificate, KYC sufficient for `amountCents` plus today’s `limitWindows(mint)`.  
**Steps:** (1) Reject if `deviceId` is not `isActivePocket`. (2) Insert `fundingIntents` in `created` with partner idempotency key = `fundingIntentId` (reuse `clientOpId` if present: return original). (3) Debit-check: `accounts.balanceCents` ≥ amount, else require partner pull of the shortfall. (4) Send partner RTP/ACH **with that same partner key**; set state `sent`. (5) On definitive success: insert `accountEntries` (`partner_funding` if pulled, then `mint_debit` −amount); pick serials; insert `notes` (`outstanding`, `currentVersion=0`, `expireOfflineAt=mintNow+72h`, `issueSig`); insert matching `pocketNotes` `live` version 0 onto `deviceId` only; bump `limitWindows`. (6) On definitive failure: `failed`, no notes. (7) On timeout: `indeterminate`; **do not** mint; **do not** new partner key; poll/reconcile.  
**Atomic:** intent row before partner call; notes+accountEntries+pocketNotes in one mint transaction after success.  
**Eventual:** partner.  
**UI:** `minting…` until success; never show notes before step 5.  
**Conflict:** two mint requests: both may proceed up to caps; same `clientOpId` collapses. Insufficient balance: refuse.

---

**5.3 RedeemNotes** (write, online)

**Inputs:** `deviceId`, `noteIds[]`, `clientOpId`.  
**Auth:** those notes are `live` on this active pocket; person not frozen.  
**Steps:** (1) Verify each note’s chain locally. (2) Create `fundingIntents` `direction=redeem`. (3) Run **SettlePocket** for those notes (5.8) inside the same mint transaction. (4) If settlement accepts them as `outstanding` to this device: set `notes.state=redeemed`, `pocketNotes.possessionState=redeemed`, `accountEntries` `redeem_credit` +face, partner payout if requested. (5) Fees per promise 18.  
**Atomic:** settle+redeem+accountEntries.  
**Eventual:** partner payout.  
**UI:** notes leave the pocket only after mint ack.  
**Conflict:** note already settled to someone else → double-spend path, redeem aborted for that serial.

---

**5.4 SplitNote** (write, local, offline)

**Inputs:** `deviceId`, `parentNoteId`, (always canonical break of that denomination).  
**Auth:** parent is `live` on this device.  
**Steps:** (1) Refuse if `live count + childCount - 1 > 200`. (2) Look up canonical children amounts. (3) Allocate new child `noteId`s (UUID v4, device-generated; mint will accept if unique). (4) Insert `splits` row, sign. (5) Set parent `split_parent`, `spentAt=now`. (6) Insert child `pocketNotes` `live`, `version=0` relative to split (chainBlob = parent chain + this split). Children inherit `expireOfflineAt` of the parent (**not** now+72h).  
**Atomic:** parent death + children birth + split row on this device.  
**Eventual:** mint sees the split at settlement; conservation checked then.  
**UI:** mix changes, total cents unchanged.  
**Conflict:** parent `locked` or not `live` → refuse. Two concurrent splits of the same parent on one device: device mutex, one wins.

**5.5 PayByHandoff** (write, two devices, offline) — ordinary path, including transit

**Inputs:** `sessionId` (payer-generated UUID), `payerDeviceId`, `payeeDeviceId`, `amountCents`, `purpose` (`purchase|p2p|fare|change|refund`), optional `receiptId`.  
**Auth:** payer device cert valid and unrevoked in **payee’s** last `trustBundle`; payer signs notes it holds `live`; payee accepts only if each note passes I10–I12 and I21–I22 against **payee** local state. Neither device asks the mint.

**Exact-pay steps (normal and transit):**  
(1) Exchange certs and last `trustBundle` (newer mint-signed `createdAt` wins and is stored).  
(2) Payee advertises `amountCents` (till or fare beacon). Payer confirms.  
(3) Payer `SelectNotes`: greedy largest denoms ≤ remaining; if remainder, `SplitNote` canonical on the smallest live note larger than remainder, repeat, until an exact set exists or fail `insufficient_notes`. Transit: this is on-device, no extra radio.  
(4) Payer sets those notes `locked` with this `sessionId`. If lock fails (`note_busy`), abort session, unlock nothing else.  
(5) Payer builds one `handoffs` row per note: `toDeviceId=payee`, `fromVersion=pocketNotes.version`, `toVersion=from+1`, `payerTime=now`, `purpose`, `sig`. **After this signature exists, those `(noteId, fromVersion)` can never return to `live` on the payer (I5).**  
(6) Payer sends `{session, cert, handoffs, chainBlobs}`.  
(7) Payee verifies, per note: mint `issueSig`; every split conservation and canonical break; every handoff signature and version chain; last `toDevicePub` equals payer; new handoff; `denominationCents` match; `expireOfflineAt` > now; hop count +1 ≤ cap; serial not in revocation bloom; payer cert not revoked; resulting `liveCents` ≤ payee cap; live count ≤ 200. Any failure: typed refuse, do not store live notes.  
(8) Payee applies: insert/update `pocketNotes` `live` at `toVersion`, store chainBlob, `receivedAt=now`; persist handoffs; insert `sessions` `applied`; if `purpose=purchase|fare` insert `receipts` with `refundUntil=now+merchant.defaultRefundHours`.  
(9) Payee acks `applied`. Payer sets notes `spent`, `spentAt=now`. If ack never arrives: payer still sets `spent` once it has sent signatures (or on send-complete); UI “Payment sent — notes locked to session”. Retry repeats **the same signed handoffs**, never new versions.  
(10) Payee may immediately use the notes in another PayByHandoff (promise 1).

**Change path (optional second phase, never for transit):** if the till cannot require exact pay, run a second session `purpose=change` with roles reversed, **after** step 8. Each device applies change only if it already applied/holds the principal outcome (I13). Prefer exact pay so this path is rare.

**Atomic:** on each device, the set of notes in the session flips together (`locked`→`spent` on payer; insert live on payee). There is **no** atomicity across the two devices; I5 + retry-same-session replaces 2PC.  
**Eventual:** mint uniqueness at settlement.  
**UI:** payee balance **jumps by face value at step 8** — this is not an optimistic guess. Payer balance drops when signatures are emitted. Show `unsettled`.  
**Conflict:** (a) same note two sessions on one device: `note_busy`. (b) copied pocket, two payees: both may succeed through step 8; settlement voids one (trap 1). (c) payee cap would break: refuse, payer notes stay `spent`/`locked` to session until retry to this payee or 7-day reclaim if payee never applied.

**Fare specialization:** hop cap 8, no change, 300 ms budget, gate does not lock its own notes (one-way incoming).

**5.6 RefundPurchase** (write, two devices, typically offline)

**Inputs:** `receiptId`, `amountCents` ≤ remaining refundable, new `sessionId`.  
**Auth:** payee of the receipt (merchant device) holds live notes summing to `amountCents`; `now ≤ refundUntil`; person not frozen. Original payer device is the payee of this session.  
**Steps:** (1) Load receipt; `remaining = amountCents - refundedCents`. (2) Refuse if requested > remaining or window closed. (3) `PayByHandoff` with `purpose=refund`, `receiptId` set, roles reversed. (4) On merchant apply of the outbound handoffs, add to a local refund tally; at settlement mint sets `receipts.refundedCents` to the sum of settled refund sessions for that id and refuses a refund that would exceed.  
**Atomic:** the inner handoff on each device; the remaining-refund check is authoritative **at mint settlement**, locally enforced on honest tills.  
**Eventual:** two offline refunds of the same receipt totaling more than remaining: mint accepts in settlement order, rejects the excess; extra notes stay with the customer if the merchant already handed them over — merchant ate a till error, not a mint rewrite.  
**UI:** merchant till shows remaining refundable; customer sees incoming cash at accept.  
**Conflict:** concurrent refunds: mint serializes on receipt remaining. Refund after customer is gone: cannot mint-push notes onto a device that is not present (I23).

---

**5.7 MovePocket** (write, two devices of one person)

**Inputs:** `fromDeviceId`, `toDeviceId`.  
**Auth:** both certs `personId` equal, both unrevoked, `from` currently `isActivePocket` locally.  
**Steps:** (1) `PayByHandoff` all `live` notes, `purpose=pocket_move`, amount = full live sum. (2) On success, `from.isActivePocket=false`, `to.isActivePocket=true` locally; `from` refuses further spends. (3) Settlement updates `deviceCerts` accordingly; if both devices claim active, mint freezes both until ops review.  
**Atomic:** per-device with the inner handoff.  
**Eventual:** mint’s one-active-pocket constraint.  
**UI:** watch becomes the cash; phone shows “pocket moved”.  
**Conflict:** both devices spend before move settles → double-spend against self (trap 3).

---

**5.8 SettlePocket** (write, online)

**Inputs:** `deviceId`, optional `noteIds` (default all `spent|live|locked|split_parent` unsettled).  
**Auth:** that device’s cert; mint endpoint.  
**Steps:** (1) Upload `handoffs`, `splits`, `sessions`, `receipts` not yet in a batch, plus each note’s `chainBlob`. (2) Mint verifies I1–I12, I17–I18, I22 per item. (3) For each `(noteId, fromVersion)` insert into a unique consumption index; on conflict create `doubleSpendCases`, mark this chain `void` from there, leave the **first** inserted consumer as winner (the first **committed settlement**, not the earlier `payerTime`). (4) Update `notes.settledBearerDeviceId/PersonId/currentVersion/expireOfflineAt/lastSettledAt`. (5) Stamp `settledInBatchId`. (6) Recheck pocket cap and daily `incoming_settle` for the winner; overflow → `people.status=frozen`, notes `frozen`. (7) Take merchant fee on newly settled incoming purchase/fare face value. (8) Return per-note `accepted|void|frozen` so the device can flip `void_double_spend` and refresh expiry on accepted live notes.  
**Atomic:** one batch: uniqueness inserts, note registry updates, account fees, case rows.  
**Eventual:** other devices’ receipts sync as history.  
**UI:** `unsettled` badge clears per accepted note; void notes vanish from live and open a claim if eligible.  
**Conflict:** concurrent SettlePocket of two chains of the same consumption pair: one transaction wins the unique index, the other takes the double-spend branch. No merge.

---

**5.9 GossipTrustBundle** (read/write, D2D or online)

**Inputs:** a `trustBundles` row.  
**Auth:** valid `mintSig`; any device may store and forward.  
**Steps:** (1) Verify mint signature and key id. (2) If `createdAt` newer than local, replace local bundle, replace revocation bloom/watermark, store `extensionId` if present. (3) Offer local bundle to peers during every PayByHandoff step 1. Online devices also fetch from mint.  
**Atomic:** local replace.  
**Eventual:** gossip. Stolen-device revocation can lag; that lag is exactly the guarantee-age function (I21).  
**UI:** “protection current as of {createdAt}”.  
**Conflict:** two bundles, both signed: higher `createdAt` wins. Never merge blooms ad hoc.

---

**5.10 ReportDeviceStolen** (write, online if at all possible)

**Inputs:** `deviceId` to revoke, reporter `personId` (same person or `mint_ops`).  
**Auth:** same `personId` as the device, or mint ops.  
**Steps:** (1) Append `revocationEvents`, sign. (2) Set `deviceCerts.status=revoked`, `isActivePocket=false`. (3) Publish a new `trustBundles` watermark. (4) Freeze unsettled `notes` whose `settledBearerDeviceId` is the stolen device **only if they have not already settled to someone else**. Do not void serials already accepted by third parties. (5) If the person has another device, they must `MintNotes` or receive a `MovePocket` of whatever the mint still shows outstanding to them — which may be zero.  
**Atomic:** revocation event + cert + bundle publish.  
**Eventual:** gossip to offline tills.  
**UI:** “device revoked; cash already handed over follows stolen-wallet rules.”  
**Conflict:** thief settles concurrently: uniqueness still first-settlement-wins; then guarantee may pay the merchant (5.13).

**5.11 SweepDoubleSpendsAndReclaimLocks** (scheduled, mint, every 30 s / daily)

**Inputs:** none (system).  
**Auth:** mint system principal only.  
**Steps:**  
A. Every 30 s: scan new uniqueness conflicts not yet in `doubleSpendCases`; freeze `spenderPersonId`; freeze their devices; enqueue guarantee eligibility for loser tip holders who have settled.  
B. Daily: for `sessions` with `state in (signed, locked)` and `reclaimAfter < mintNow`, if the signed handoffs’ `(noteId, fromVersion)` have **not** been consumed by any payee settlement, mint records a reclaim: those serials remain `outstanding` to the **payer** device (still I5 on the device until it pulls this result). If a payee later presents the same signatures, payee **wins** (the signature was a completed transfer); payer reclaim is reversed into a double-spend case against the payer who spent again after reclaim. Therefore reclaim is **refused** if the payer has already settled **other** spends of those pairs.  
**Atomic:** per case / per session.  
**Eventual:** devices learn on next SettlePocket or an explicit reclaim pull.  
**UI:** none at sweep time; payer sees “unclaimed payment returned” only after mint attestation.  
**Conflict:** reclaim vs late payee settlement: payee wins (step B).

---

**5.12 CloseMerchantDay** (bulk, online, end of business day)

**Inputs:** `merchantId`, `deviceId` (till), `remintMix`.  
**Auth:** that till’s merchant cert.  
**Steps:** (1) SettlePocket for the entire till (often 2 k–10 k notes, tens of thousands of handoffs). (2) Optionally RedeemNotes of settled live notes down to a `workingFloatCents`. (3) MintNotes `optimized` or `remintMix` for the working float so the next morning has a useful denomination mix with fresh `expireOfflineAt`. (4) Materialize a day sheet: incoming face, fees, refunds, voids, guarantee claims, closing mix. (5) Restartable: keyed by `(deviceId, yyyymmdd)`; a second call returns the same sheet if the watermark is unchanged.  
**Atomic:** settle is already atomic in batches of ≤5,000 consumptions; the day close iterates batches in `noteId` order until the till’s unsettled set is empty, then redeem/remint.  
**Eventual:** partner payout of redeemed surplus.  
**UI:** “day closed, float $X”.  
**Conflict:** clerks still tapping during close: those sessions settle in the next batch or next day; they must not be dropped. Close does not take the till offline.

---

**5.13 FileGuaranteeClaim** (write, online; spans investigation)

**Inputs:** `caseId` or voided `noteId`+`version`, `claimantDeviceId`.  
**Auth:** claimant settled as holder of a void tip, or mint ops filing on their behalf.  
**Steps:** (1) Load `doubleSpendCases`. (2) Compute `bundleAgeHours` from the claimant’s uploaded accept-time bundle (must have been stored at PayByHandoff step 8). (3) Policy: in-cap, hop, expiry, revocation, role → `full|half|none` (I21). (4) If `full` or `half`, `accountEntries.guarantee_credit` of face or floor(face/2); mark case `guarantee_paid`. (5) Clawback: `clawback_debit` on spender’s account if funds; else debt flag + freeze (already frozen).  
**Atomic:** case transition + entries.  
**Eventual:** collections.  
**UI:** merchant “voided $20, Harbor credited $20”.  
**Conflict:** two claimants for the same void tip: only the settled current tip holder is eligible (I15). Duplicate claim unique-key no-ops.

---

**5.14 PublishEmergencyExtension** (write, mint ops; background distribution)

**Inputs:** `validFrom`, `validUntil`, `extraHours`, hop caps, `region`.  
**Auth:** `mint_ops` dual-control (two distinct ops personIds; the implementation must record both, even if the second is a logged approval row).  
**Steps:** (1) Insert `emergencyExtensions`, mint-sign. (2) Publish a new `trustBundles` containing it. (3) Online devices fetch; offline devices receive via GossipTrustBundle. (4) Accept path uses `expireOfflineAt + extraHours` and raised hop caps **only** while `validFrom ≤ now ≤ validUntil` on the payee clock, rechecked at settlement on mint clock.  
**Atomic:** extension + bundle.  
**Eventual:** gossip; a till that never hears of it will keep refusing stale notes — correct, conservative.  
**UI:** “disaster mode until {validUntil}”.  
**Conflict:** overlapping extensions: accept the one in the newest valid bundle; settlement honors any extension valid at **accept time** on the stored bundle, not a later revocation of the disaster (extensions are not silently shortened for already-accepted notes).

### 6. Scale and cost

Harbor’s successful city: **2.0 M** people, **500 k** weekly active holders, **50 k** merchants, **800** transit gates. **~2.2 M** notes outstanding (holders average 3 live notes; tills average 25; gates average 200 in the fare box). **~4 M payments/day** (≈2 M fares + 2 M commerce/P2P). Average **3.2 notes** per payment → **~13 M handoffs/day**, plus **~2 M splits/day**. Peak: stadium exit **50 k** people, then a **Wi-Fi slam** of 50 k SettlePocket calls in 20 minutes.

Rows after one year: handoffs **~5 B**; splits **~0.7 B**; receipts **~1.4 B**; notes (including dead children) **~80 M**; accountEntries **~400 M**; revocationEvents **< 2 M**. Handoffs and splits are the volume. They are append-only and must be partitioned by `noteId` (settlement and uniqueness) and by `deviceId+time` (upload).

**Expensive operations.**  
- **SettlePocket** after a 72 h (or 17 d disaster) market: a single $5 may carry a 32-hop chain × several notes; verification is CPU (Ed25519 verify × hops × notes) and is the mint’s hot path. Batch 5,000 consumptions, but a till close of 10 k notes × average 6 hops is **60 k verifications** plus unique-index inserts.  
- **Sweep uniqueness** is an index on `(noteId, fromVersion)` at 13 M inserts/day — the cost of I4.  
- **GossipTrustBundle**: a revocation bloom for 1 M stolen/superseded devices is still small; re-signing bundles every 10 minutes is cheap; **fan-out** to 800 gates that have no backhaul depends on passenger gossip, not mint CPU.  
- **ReadPocket** is local O(notes in pocket) ≤ 200.  
- **CloseMerchantDay** is the cost of SettlePocket plus remint; do not run it as one unbounded transaction.  
- **FileGuaranteeClaim** is rare (target < 50/day) but each case must keep **both** competing chains, which is why handoffs are never deleted.  
- Fan-out: one popular stall, 400 taps/hour, one pocket. Serialization of **locks on that till’s notes** is the throughput limit, not the mint. Two radios on one till still one pocket mutex (promise 14).

Storage: chainBlob on the device is bounded by hop cap (32 × ~250 B × notes in pocket) — a few hundred KB, fine. Mint stores chains at settlement; after redeem+N years, archive cold.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

1. **Move, don’t replicate, inside a store-wide offline fence.** Live `pocketNotes` are exclusive possession. Receipts and settled history replicate. One fence that copies every table turns ordinary sync into double-spend (trap 1). An ordinary “rows + offline replica + merge” backend will implement Handnote as a balance integer. The concession “notes live only in a secure element, the database holds history” **removes the axis from the store** — a cheat **for this design’s purpose**, even though it is how some CBDCs are built. The concession “everything is a replica, payments are queued until the server says so” is a cheat against promises 1–2.

2. **Spend authority is not a server check.** While disconnected, the mint cannot permit or deny. Authorization is “I hold the chain tip and a live device key.” Ordinary backends evaluate writes with server-side row ACL at commit time. Here the commit that matters happens on a stall’s phone. Concession: online authorization per spend — cheat. Concession: detect-and-punish at settlement rather than prevent — **not** a cheat; it is the physics of a copyable store (the brief allows it). Claiming **impossibility** while `pocketNotes` are ordinary replicated rows is a lie.

3. **A signed handoff is irreversible without a coordinator.** Ordinary two-phase commit aborts if the ack is lost. I5 forbids abort-after-sign. The backend needs an object whose insertion on device A is a durable capability on device B, with retry-idempotency on `handoffId` / `(noteId, fromVersion, toDevicePub)`, not last-write-wins. Concession: 120 s auto-unlock — cheat (trap 2). Concession: exact-pay only (no two-way change) — **not** a cheat if SplitNote exists.

4. **Conflict is evidence.** Two writes of the same `(noteId, fromVersion)` must both persist as `handoffs` plus a `doubleSpendCases` row. Ordinary unique indexes **drop** the second writer. Here the second writer is the crime and the guarantee input. Concession: unique-reject the second and forget it — a cheat against promise 3’s *detectably* and against promise 4.

5. **Structural uniqueness and conservation are global, unfiltered, and binding on every writer including “handlers.”** `sum(children)=parent`, canonical breaks, at-most-one live bearer, solvency, hop/expiry. A visibility-filtered count of live serials is the wrong number (trap 6). If a privileged path can patch `denominationCents` or `possessionState` back to `live`, I5–I9 are conventions. Concession: drop conservation and use a single mutable `balanceCents` — cheat against bearer notes and against double-spend of *the same value* as identifiable objects.

**Not cheats:** 72 h (not 30 d) offline; hop caps; one active pocket plus MovePocket; last-holder guarantee instead of cryptographic impossibility; mint-time (not hop-time) expiry refresh; typed refusals that leak stolen/stale.

**Cheat line:** if the product still claims days-long offline **immediate acceptance** and **re-spend**, the mutations that move live notes cannot be “whatever the server later decides, shown as pending until then.” That product is a ledger with an offline draft folder, not cash.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice:** denomination mix and canonical break (physical till); device-bound keys and mint-signed device certificates; UTXO-style unique consumption of `(serial, version)`; first-settled-wins plus criminal freeze; merchant guarantee / insurance for in-policy acceptors; stolen-wallet treatment for pre-revocation spends; RTP/ACH idempotency keys; daily merchant close and working float; transit tap as one-way exact debit; gossip of revocation lists.

**Standard-but-contested:** offline CBDC papers often put the note in a secure element so copying the OS store does not copy spend power. This design **keeps live notes in `pocketNotes`** because the axis under test is one server plus a store-wide offline fence. The SE holds the signing key, which is normal, not a relocation of the coin.

**Invented for the exercise:** Harbor’s exact caps, 7-day reclaim-with-payee-priority, 24 h / 50% guarantee ladder, hop caps 8/32/128, 200-note pocket, 5,000-consumption settlement batches, `untrusted_copy` as a first-class possession state, dual-control `PublishEmergencyExtension`.

**Physics, not invention:** a store that copies rows cannot make double-spend **impossible**; it can make it **detectable**. A shallow app that stores `wallets.balance` and syncs it never even surfaces the live-versus-settled interval. That interval is this archetype’s equivalent of active-versus-historical membership, and it sits on the ordinary path: every stall tap.
