## Overall assessment

The codec implementation appears byte- and behavior-preserving by static inspection. The same `serde_json` text serializer remains authoritative; valid page commands are decoded and validated from the same immutable input; all three hosts use that route; output metering retains its allocation-free writer; and borrowed programs cannot outlive or race backend adoption.

The change is not ready to land because the plan’s mandatory Chromium/IndexedDB acquisition and fresh-realm reopen evidence is absent, and the blocking suite is still red. I also found a newly introduced provenance error: the final wasm measurement is attributed to the baseline commit.

Explicitly:

- **Did the change introduce a new problem?** Yes: the metrics record assigns the optimized artifact to `70a663c22`, the baseline whose measured size was 520,557 gzip bytes. I found no demonstrated wire, durable-store, meter, or runtime semantic regression.
- **Would regression tests pass without the optimization they claim to cover?** Yes. Most codec tests are compatibility tests and would remain green if production call sites reverted to the old families or the page fast path reverted to tree dispatch.

## Findings

### High — S1 is declared implemented without its mandatory acquisition evidence

**File and line:** [JOURNAL.md:29012](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/JOURNAL.md:29012), [JOURNAL.md:29016](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/JOURNAL.md:29016)

**Scenario:** The exact risk motivating this plan is a previous codec diet that passed narrower checks but regressed acquisition. At IMPL_COMMIT, the blocking suite exits 1, the React Chromium check still SIGABRTs, and neither `witness/idb.mjs` nor the required cold-acquisition/fresh-realm reopen was run. Nevertheless, the entry concludes “S0/S1 are implemented.” That is not the acceptance state defined by LLP 3000.002.

**Smallest fix:** Treat this as an S1 candidate, run the exact shipped wasm through Chromium IndexedDB acquisition, offline fresh-realm reopen, and the paired scale acquisition on the exact artifact, then record a green result. Do not claim the rung landed before that evidence exists.

### Medium — the final measurement is attributed to the baseline commit

**File and line:** [metrics/wasm.jsonl:5](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/metrics/wasm.jsonl:5)

**Scenario:** A consumer grouping metrics by `sha` reads `70a663c22 → 462,005 B gzip`. But `70a663c22` is BASELINE, whose recorded measurement in the plan is 520,557 B. The optimized measurement came from a dirty tree containing the implementation, so the record assigns two incompatible artifacts to the baseline source identity.

**Smallest fix:** Append a measurement tied to a source revision containing the implementation, or extend the record with `base`, `dirty: true`, and a tree/diff digest. Do not use `git rev-parse HEAD` alone as artifact provenance for dirty-tree measurements.

### Medium — the added regression tests do not bind the production optimization

**File and line:** [codec.rs:9](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/tests/codec.rs:9), [codec.rs:163](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/tests/codec.rs:163), [diet_timing.rs:8](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/tests/diet_timing.rs:8), [codec.test.mjs:11](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-dm-review/D1-r1/repo/snapback4/packages/snapback4/test/codec.test.mjs:11)

**Scenario:** Keep `json_codec` and the tests, but restore production call sites to direct `serde_json::{from_value,to_value}`, or implement `call_text` by parsing a `Value` and invoking the owned dispatcher. The following tests remain green while the 58,552-byte saving and no-tree page path disappear; the budget merely reports `OVER` and exits successfully:

- `reference_backends_and_loadable_corpus_keep_their_derive`
- `defaults_custom_formats_variants_and_tagged_values`
- `owned_argument_errors_do_not_claim_positions_in_synthetic_text`
- `text_page_and_owned_dispatch_agree_including_duplicate_keys_and_refusals`
- `page_fast_path_keeps_json_validation_inside_unknown_fields`
- the shipped-wasm `codec.test.mjs`
- `counting_writer_keeps_exact_meter_bytes` if the runner retains its old inline counter
- ignored `diet_hot_paths`, which prints timings but asserts no comparison or bound

`old_store_opens_without_rewriting_rows_or_schema_identity` and `cursor_tokens_keep_the_old_bytes_in_both_directions` also pass at BASELINE by design; they are useful compatibility guards but not evidence that S1 is wired.

**Smallest fix:** Add an artifact-level family check with an explicit allowlist, make the fast-path route observable under `cfg(test)` and exercise it on MemoryStore plus SQLite/process, test the real runner at output ceiling and ceiling-minus-one, and use frozen BASELINE fixtures plus an old-runtime reverse-open test. The timing test should be driven by a checked paired comparator rather than counted as a passing regression test.
