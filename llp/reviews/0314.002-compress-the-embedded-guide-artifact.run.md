### Cross-family convergence

Both families independently reported:

1. **LLP 0314.000 Phase 2 is not "indifferent" to compression.** The
   frozen plan pins the existing `embedded-guide.v1.bin` format at a
   named member path and requires one deterministic encoder shared by
   two callers with a compiled-in `artifactSha256`. Compression rotates
   `artifactSha256` and changes record layout.
2. **`\x01` is the version byte, not decoration.** §3.1's "keeps its
   magic and gains a format version" is self-contradictory; a breaking
   container bump is required.
3. **The encoder must be required to be bit-stable.** `gzip(1)` stamps
   MTIME; `flate2`'s `GzBuilder` is bit-stable by default (mtime 0, OS
   255). The RFC measured with the CLI and implied the crate, and
   requires neither.
4. **gzip is not dependency-free.** `flate2`/`miniz_oxide` are in
   `Cargo.lock` only transitively; no workspace `Cargo.toml` names
   either and neither appears in `crates/allowed-dependencies.v1.json`.
5. **OQ2's size comparison is invalid** (top-five per-record against
   whole-corpus).
6. **§6's "no new failure mode" is false** — decompression bombs on the
   Phase 2 external-sidecar path.
7. **The §3.5 LLP 0061 paraphrase is wrong**: the manifest records each
   flat's digest; the container stores path, length, and bytes with no
   per-record digest.
8. **"Never considered" overreaches** — LLP 0314.001 §2c considered
   encoding compression and dismissed it as marginal.

Both also confirmed the two things the RFC most needed to get right:
**`contentHash` is genuinely unaffected**, and the **~1:1 binary saving
premise is sound** (codex found the artifact once in `__TEXT,__const`
at 20,666,784 bytes for 20,658,338 payload bytes).

### Orchestrator verification

Every load-bearing claim was checked before acceptance; all confirmed:

- `ARTIFACT_MAGIC = b"SNAPBACK_EMBEDDED_GUIDE\0\x01"` is consumed as one
  unit labelled `"magic/version"` (`crates/snapback-server/src/guide.rs`).
- `flate2` appears in no workspace `Cargo.toml` and is absent from
  `crates/allowed-dependencies.v1.json`.
- LLP 0284 requires "UTF-8 for every Markdown/JSON record" **before
  exposing any record**, so first Guide access decompresses the whole
  text corpus regardless of per-record storage — defeating OQ2's
  laziness rationale as written.
- The §7 provenance quotation **is** present in the cited review file
  (grok could not verify it under the blindness ban; codex flagged the
  0322-named path as an auditability weakness rather than an error).
- **Codex's matched 2×2 re-measurement reproduces exactly** over the same
  187 files / 20,658,338 payload bytes:

  | Encoding | Whole stream | Per-record |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | `gzip -9 -n` | 3,452,614 | 3,608,417 |
  | `zstd -19` | 1,128,188 | 2,838,299 |

  This falsifies two RFC claims. Under the recommended per-record
  design zstd saves ~0.77 MB over gzip, not the ~2.2 MB §4 OQ1 asserts.
  And gzip's whole-versus-per-record penalty is only ~0.16 MB, so
  OQ2's "cross-record redundancy is large" is false for gzip — though it
  is emphatically true for zstd (~1.71 MB). Encoding and granularity are
  **coupled** and cannot be decided in separate open questions, which is
  precisely codex's suggested fix.

### The one IN-DELTA MATERIAL, and its fold

Codex: revision (b) left granularity "simultaneously open and fixed."
§3.1 normatively required every record to carry an encoding tag and
decoded length — a per-record container — while the Summary and OQ1
still described encoding/granularity as an unsettled coupled decision.
Whole-stream compression needs a different outer envelope, so two
implementers could have produced incompatible `\x02` formats.

This defect was introduced by the round-1 fold, not present in (a). It
is exactly the failure mode the delta round exists to catch.

**Revision (c)** resolves it by settling the decision: per-record `gzip`
via `flate2` is now the proposal in the Summary, and OQ1 becomes a
settled choice with three explicit revisit triggers (unacceptable
per-record decode cost at OQ3; the ~0.77 MB zstd advantage becoming
worth a new package; OQ2 selecting eager-retain, which weakens
whole-stream's loader objection and makes zstd's ~2.32 MB advantage
worth re-costing). Any trigger reopens §3.1's container shape by
revision, not by implementer choice.

Codex's IN-DELTA minor is also folded: the §1 byte counts inherited from
(a) were default `gzip(1)` output mislabelled `-9 -n`. Orchestrator
re-measurement confirms codex exactly — `registry.json` is 395,831 under
`-n`, not 395,845, a 14-byte filename/mtime header, and the same applies
to the other four rows. §3.2's claim that the measurements stamp MTIME
is corrected, and the ratio prose no longer groups
`reference/diagnostics.md` (7.5:1) with the two flat projections
(4.3:1 and 6.7:1).
