READY

No material design defects found in the four axes. Basis, per axis:

- Privacy: the hidden-target immutable exception is unchanged at runtime (`ignore_hidden_immutable_patch`), and the new static `E_QUARRY_IMMUTABLE` refusal is per-program at bind time, so it cannot become a per-row occupancy oracle. Bounded and vacancy lanes reuse the per-candidate `authorized_candidate` path: hidden candidates never reach payload, and allowance cuts set `value_source_incomplete` rather than skipping. The upfront physical draw is occupancy-independent by construction.
- Boundedness: the `(cap + 1) × (1 + rule probes)` price is sound against the exclusions as implemented. Reference-hop terms are declined, and every remaining `Exists` probe costs at most one physical row (`live_lookup` point path; lifetime-sensitive, read-through, and maintained targets are all excluded, and `Created` probes are in-memory). Disjunction/conjunction probe counts sum worst-case, short-circuit only reduces cost, and execution is gated by `branch_merge_lazy`, so the `reference_prepaid` window opens solely for admitted lanes with no double-spend and no draw refund.
- Proof invalidation: `resolve` stops at any intervening write (all write statements covered by `Effects::writes_in`), at both branch/loop forms (`If`/`For` are the only ones), and at volatile `NewId`; source locations are stripped before identity comparison, and spreads are expanded before the check. Rebinding clears field facts, and writes clear point/authorization/count facts while correctly retaining per-snapshot non-null facts.
- Compatibility: `branch-pages-v5` is set exactly for bounded lanes and non-legacy vacancy lanes; legacy `not exists t[.id]` shapes stay v4-only, and `membership_v1` is untouched. The planner re-validates speculative bounded lanes through engine `bounded_branch` with fallback, so planner/engine strictness differences fail safe.

No required corrections.
