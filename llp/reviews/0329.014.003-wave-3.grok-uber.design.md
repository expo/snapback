### 1. Product contract

Hail is a ride-hailing app for one metro (“Metro”, ~1,200 km² service polygon). Two paying roles: **riders** and **drivers**. A person may hold both roles on one account but not in the same open trip. Support can read an active trip during an SOS; support cannot dispatch, accept, or set surge by hand.

Hail promises the following, and nothing quieter:

1. A rider can request an economy, comfort, or XL car from a pickup pin to a dropoff pin and receive a **fare quote that expires in 120 seconds** (min/max, line items, locked surge multiplier).
2. Confirming a still-valid quote creates a trip and **card-preauthorizes the quote’s max**. No driver is assigned at confirm time. The rider sees “Finding your driver,” not a name, plate, or car.
3. The system, not the rider, **offers the trip to one idle driver at a time for 15 seconds**. Decline, miss, or expiry sends the same trip to the next eligible driver. The rider does not see identities during this search.
4. After a driver accepts, the rider sees **first name, photo, rating, vehicle make/model/color/plate, and a live position ~1 Hz** until the trip closes. The driver sees exact pickup, dropoff, rider first name and rating.
5. Before a request, the map shows a **pickup ETA (minutes) and a surge multiplier** for the rider’s hex. It does **not** show real driver identities or a live fleet. Optional “ghost cars” if present are delayed ≥ 20 s, jittered ±80 m, and are not the matching set.
6. A driver going online publishes position **4 Hz when speed > 1 m/s, 1 Hz when stopped**. Positions older than **10 seconds** do not receive offers.
7. Trip states a user can see: `requested` → `offering` → `matched` → `arriving` → `in_trip` → `completed`, or `cancelled`. `offering` means an exclusive offer is outstanding or about to be; it is not a match.
8. Rider cancel is free in `requested`/`offering`; after match it may incur a fee from a published schedule. Driver cancel before `in_trip` **re-dispatches the same trip** (same fare lock) to someone else. Five minutes with no accept: system cancel, no fee, preauth released.
9. Final fare = `max(minimumFare, base + perKm×mappedKm + perMin×elapsedMin) × lockedSurge + bookingFee + tolls + waitFee + tax`, plus optional tip. Surge on the trip is the multiplier **locked at request**, not the live map. Tip is 100% driver.
10. After `completed`, each side may submit **one** 1–5 rating of the counterpart who finished the trip. Ratings are hidden from the counterpart’s identity screen for 24 hours, then fold into their public average.
11. A rider may mint a **live share link** (read-only map of that trip’s rider+driver positions) that dies at trip close, 60 minutes, or revoke, whichever is first.
12. A driver has one **active device session**. A second online attempt either takes over (old session becomes `kicked`) or is refused; both cannot feed matching.
13. Driver earnings show per-trip gross, commission, tolls pass-through, tip, net, online minutes, and a daily rollup. Instant payout is a separate, optional drain against settled net.
14. In-app SOS from an active trip notifies support with the last 5 minutes of path and the counterpart’s identity.
15. Rider and driver can each have the trip open on multiple devices as **watchers**. Only the driver’s **active session** may go online, publish matchable locations, or accept. Only one open rider request at a time per account.

**Concurrency.** Two drivers cannot hold a pending offer for the same trip. A driver cannot hold two pending offers. Accept and timeout are a single winner. Rider cancel versus accept: whichever commit lands first; the loser is told. Quote confirmation is serialized per rider so two devices cannot open two trips.

**Offline.** Request, accept, cancel, arrive, start, complete, and pay require a server round-trip. The driver app may **queue up to 30 seconds of GPS** and flush; samples with `recordedAt` older than 10 s are stored on the trip path if in-trip, but **never** enter matching. If the driver accepts while the radio is down, the UI waits; it does not claim success. After 15 s the offer is gone whether the tap happened on-device or not.

**Multi-device.** Rider state is account-scoped: any logged-in device sees the same open trip. Driver location for matching is session-scoped. A stale phone that keeps sending GPS after being kicked is ignored.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

Marking: **[money]**, **[time]**, **[secrecy]**, **[others’ data]**.

1. A rider has at most one trip in `{requested, offering, matched, arriving, in_trip}`. **[time]**
2. A driver has at most one trip in `{matched, arriving, in_trip}`. **[time]**
3. At most one `offers.state = pending` row exists per trip. **[time]**
4. At most one `offers.state = pending` row exists per driver. **[time]**
5. `trips.currentDriverId` is null unless state ∈ `{matched, arriving, in_trip, completed}` (completed keeps the finishing driver). It is never set by `RequestRide` or by creating an offer. **[time]**
6. Creating a pending offer does not match the trip. Match happens only when an accept CAS succeeds. **[time]**
7. A pending offer grants that driver an exclusive right to accept that trip until `exclusiveUntil`. No other driver is offered that trip in that interval. **[time]**
8. Accept succeeds only if `state = pending` AND `now < exclusiveUntil` AND the trip is still `offering` AND the rider has not cancelled. **[time]**
9. System timeout succeeds only if `state = pending` AND `now ≥ exclusiveUntil`. **[time]**
10. A driver already present on any `offers` row for this `tripId` is not offered this trip again. **[time]**
11. A driver is **idle at time T** iff all of these hold at T: an open session, no trip in `{matched, arriving, in_trip}`, no pending offer with `exclusiveUntil > T`, profile `active`, vehicle type eligible, and a location sample with `recordedAt ≥ T − 10s`. `isIdle` if stored is a cache of this, not a source of truth. **[time]**
12. Matching may consider only locations from the driver’s **active session**, with `recordedAt ≥ now − 10s`. **[time] [others’ data]**
13. Dispatch, offer timeout, surge/ETA publication, and payment capture run under **system** authority. Riders and drivers have no API that assigns `currentDriverId`, writes surge, or captures a charge. **[money]**
14. Preauthorization of `quote.maxTotalCents` must succeed before the trip is eligible for dispatch. **[money]**
15. The trip’s `surgeMultiplierLocked` and `surgeSnapshotId` are copied from the published surface at request time and do not change if live surge moves. **[money] [time]**
16. Final fare uses locked surge, map-matched path distance, elapsed `in_trip` time, wait fee from the fee schedule, recorded tolls, and tax. It does not re-read live surge. **[money]**
17. Cancellation fee is computed from **state and timestamps at the cancel commit**, using the schedule in §5 `CancelTrip`. **[money] [time]**
18. Commission is 25% of (fare + wait fee − tolls). Tolls and tips are 100% driver. **[money]**
19. Unmatched riders cannot read driver ids, plates, exact positions, idle lists, or per-hex idle/open counts. **[secrecy] [others’ data]**
20. The public marketplace surface may expose `surgeMultiplier` and `pickupEtaSeconds` per hex, not `idleDriverCount` or `openRequestCount`. Those counts are internal. **[secrecy] [others’ data]**
21. Surge and pickup ETA are computed from the **unfiltered** idle/request domain, then published. They are not counts of rows the caller can read. **[secrecy]**
22. During `offering`, the offered driver may read that trip’s pickup/dropoff and rider first name/rating; they receive **no live rider GPS**. After the offer closes without accept, the server sends that driver no further facts about that trip. **[secrecy] [others’ data]**
23. During `{matched, arriving, in_trip}`, the rider, the current driver, and unrevoked unexpired share-token viewers may read **this trip’s** current driver position (and rider position for share/SOS). Nobody else may. **[secrecy] [others’ data]**
24. A declined or timed-out driver is not a subscriber to that trip’s position channel, even though they once saw the pickup pin. **[secrecy]**
25. `offers`, `tripStateEvents`, `driverLocationSamples`, `tripPathPoints`, `hexInternalSnapshots`, and `earningEntries` are **append-only** (state columns on `offers` move only forward: pending → one terminal state). **[time]**
26. Ratings: only `completed` trips; at most one row per `(tripId, fromUserId)`; the rider rates `currentDriverId` at completion; the driver rates the rider. **[others’ data]**
27. Share tokens authorize **that trip’s** positions until `min(expiresAt, trip closed, revokedAt)`. They cannot list other trips or the fleet. **[secrecy] [time]**
28. A kicked or ended session’s location writes are stored at most as forensic samples; they do not update `driverLocationCurrent` or the idle index. **[time] [others’ data]**

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1 — Idle-at-T is an interval intersection, not a boolean.**

- **Wrong:** `drivers.isIdle = true/false`, flipped in `SetDriverOnlineState`, and matching is `WHERE isIdle`.
- **Why:** Idle ends when an exclusive offer is planted, when another trip matches, when GPS goes stale, when the session is kicked, and when a 15 s offer expires. Those are overlapping intervals. A flag written by “go online” is already wrong two seconds later, and a flag written by dispatch is wrong if timeout is a different worker that forgets to flip it back. This is the membership-at-a-time bug: “idle” is a fact **about a time**, not about a person.
- **Observation:** Nearest driver sits on a 15 s offer and ignores it. Timeout fires. Re-dispatch without restoring idle → city has nobody. Or the flag is left true during the offer → the same driver is offered two trips at once, violating invariants 3–4. Querying `isIdle` at 12:00:20 cannot answer “were they idle at 12:00:07, when the search snapshot was taken?”

**Trap 2 — Offering is not matching, and the 15 s exclusive is a lease with two closers.**

- **Wrong:** Dispatch writes `trips.driverId` and `state = matched`, then pushes the driver. Timeout is a job that unsets `driverId` if they have not tapped. Accept is an update `where driverId = me`.
- **Why:** Match is a **driver** speech act inside a **time window** created by **system** work. Putting `driverId` on at offer time makes the rider UI legally entitled to a person who has not agreed, and makes “the driver of this trip” true for a person who will 40% of the time never drive it. Accept and timeout both close the lease; without compare-and-swap, both succeed: rider sees driver A, driver B is also told they won, or A accepts at t=15.01 and still wins.
- **Observation:** Offer expires at `12:00:15.000`. Accept packet arrives `12:00:15.040` with on-device tap time `12:00:14.910`. If the server uses device time, the timeout job already gave the trip to B. If it uses neither CAS nor `exclusiveUntil`, A and B both match. The rider’s receipt names one driver; payroll names another.

**Trap 3 — Marketplace numbers computed from visible rows are silently 1.0× / “no cars.”**

- **Wrong:** Rider query: `surge = f(count(drivers I can read in this hex), count(open trips I can read))`. Nearby ETA = min distance over `driverLocationCurrent` rows the rider is allowed to see.
- **Why:** By invariant 19 the rider can read **zero** idle drivers. A visibility-respecting count returns 0 supply and (if they can only see their own request) 0 or 1 demand. Surge displays **1.0× in a shortage**. ETA displays **unavailable** while 30 idle cars sit in the ring. The number is authorized and **wrong**. Raw counts, if published, also leak fleet size to a scraper.
- **Observation:** Seed a hex with 0 rider-visible drivers, 40 idle drivers, 20 open requests. Correct surface: surge 2.4×, ETA 3 min. Naive filtered query: 1.0×, no ETA. Same bug as “how many people are ahead of me” when you can only see your own row.

**Trap 4 — Nearest is as-of a sample, over objects that move several times a second.**

- **Wrong:** `ORDER BY haversine(now_location, pickup) LIMIT 1` as if `driverLocationCurrent` were the driver’s body.
- **Why:** At 14 m/s a 4 Hz sample is already 3.5 m stale; a 10 s freshness window allows 140 m. The driver who wins k-NN may have entered an offer for someone else, entered a tunnel (sample frozen), or driven past the pickup in the 80 ms between search and insert. Without freezing `(lat, lng, recordedAt, sessionId)` on the offer and without excluding pending-offer drivers, you re-offer a phantom.
- **Observation:** Driver D is 200 m away at `recordedAt = now − 9s` heading outbound at 16 m/s (actually ~350 m past pickup). Driver E is 280 m away with a 1 s sample, inbound. Naive nearest is D. D times out (they are now 1.5 km away). E was the right offer. Audit must show the **search snapshot**, not the current map.

**Trap 5 — `currentDriverId` is present tense; several drivers can be historical on one trip.**

- **Wrong:** One `trips.driverId`, overwritten on re-dispatch; ratings, pay, SOS, and “who saw this pickup” use that column.
- **Why:** Driver-cancel in `matched` re-dispatches the **same** trip id (fare already locked, preauth already held). Driver A is historical: they saw the pickup, they may owe a cancel strike, they must **not** receive further GPS, they must **not** be paid the fare, they must **not** be the ratee. Driver B completes. Shallow overwrite loses A; shallow “all drivers on the trip” pays both.
- **Observation:** A cancels 90 s after match. B completes. A’s earnings dashboard shows the fare (wrong). Rider is asked to rate A because `driverId` was first-write-wins (wrong). B’s location is still being sent to A’s kicked UI because the pubsub group was “trip id” not “current driver id + generation.”

**Trap 6 — Two devices are not two locations; `recordedAt` is not a trustworthy clock.**

- **Wrong:** Last-write-wins on `driverLocationCurrent` by `recordedAt` from the device.
- **Why:** A pocketed old phone with a skewed clock, or a rider-role iPad running a forgotten driver build, will either (a) keep the driver matchable from a parking lot they left hours ago, or (b) overwrite a good 4 Hz stream with a 200 m-wrong point whose timestamp is *ahead*. Matching then offers a driver who is not there. Merge is **session ownership**, not CRDT of points.
- **Observation:** Active phone at the airport, kicked phone at home with clock +45 s. LWW-by-`recordedAt` matches the driver to a downtown pickup they cannot cover. Accept succeeds; the arriving state never happens; rider cancel-with-fee fights the driver. The forensic sample table shows two streams; the current-row table showed only home.

### 4. Data model

Types: `uuid`, `text`, `int`, `bigint` (cents), `float64`, `numeric(10,6)`, `timestamptz`, `enum`, `bool`. Coordinates: WGS84. Money: integer cents, `USD`. H3: resolution 9 string (`h3r9`), ~2,400 cells in the service polygon; resolution 8 (`h3r8`) for coarse rollups.

**`users`** — `id`, `phoneE164`, `email`, `createdAt`, `deletedAt`.  
**`riderProfiles`** — `userId` PK, `defaultPaymentMethodId`, `ratingSum`, `ratingCount`, `completedTripCount`.  
**`driverProfiles`** — `userId` PK, `status enum(pending,active,suspended)`, `licenseExpiresOn`, `ratingSum`, `ratingCount`, `completedTripCount`, `cancelStrikeCount`, `connectedAccountId`, `homeH3r9`.  
**`vehicles`** — `id`, `driverId`, `plate`, `make`, `model`, `color`, `year`, `type enum(economy,comfort,xl)`, `inspectExpiresOn`. One active vehicle per online session.

**`driverSessions`** — `id`, `driverId`, `deviceId`, `vehicleId`, `startedAt`, `endedAt null`, `endReason enum(logout,kicked,stale_heartbeat,suspend)`, `lastHeartbeatAt`. **Time-bounded:** open session is `endedAt IS NULL`. Partial unique: one open session per `driverId`.

**`driverLocationCurrent`** — LWW **current** row, PK `driverId`. `sessionId`, `lat`, `lng`, `headingDeg`, `speedMps`, `accuracyM`, `h3r9`, `h3r8`, `recordedAt`, `receivedAt`, `seq int` (monotonic per session). Derived: `h3r9` from lat/lng. **Not** the path; not rider-readable.

**`driverLocationSamples`** — **append-only**. Same payload plus `id`. Persist 1 Hz (downsample the 4 Hz stream). Retention: 48 h rolling, longer if attached to a trip.

**`hexIdleDrivers`** — derived index, PK `(h3r9, driverId)`, `sinceAt`, `vehicleType`, `sessionId`. Presence means “idle **now**” as cached by the last mutation that knew it. Rebuilt from invariants if dirty. Not readable by riders/drivers.

**`hexInternalSnapshots`** — **append-only** (one row per hex per compute tick). `id`, `h3r9`, `computedAt`, `validUntil`, `idleDriverCount int`, `openRequestCount int`, `offeringCount int`, `inTripCount int`, `demandSupplyRatio float64`, `surgeMultiplier numeric(3,2)`, `pickupEtaSeconds int`, `algoVersion text`. **[secrecy]** system/support only.

**`hexPublicSurface`** — versioned current, PK `h3r9`. `snapshotId` FK, `surgeMultiplier`, `pickupEtaSeconds`, `validFrom`, `validUntil`, `publishedAt`. This is the **only** marketplace aggregate riders and drivers may read. When a new snapshot publishes, the previous `validUntil` is closed. **Time-bounded.**

**`trips`** — `id`, `riderId`, `vehicleType`,  
pickup: `pickupLat`, `pickupLng`, `pickupH3r9`, `pickupAddress`,  
dropoff: `dropoffLat`, `dropoffLng`, `dropoffH3r9`, `dropoffAddress`,  
`state enum(requested,offering,matched,arriving,in_trip,completed,cancelled)`,  
`currentOfferId null`, `currentDriverId null`, `dispatchGeneration int default 0`,  
`requestedAt`, `requestExpiresAt` (= requestedAt+5min), `matchedAt`, `arrivingEnteredAt`, `driverArrivedAt`, `inTripStartedAt`, `completedAt`, `cancelledAt`,  
`cancelledBy enum(rider,driver,system,support)`, `cancelReason text`, `cancelFeeCents`,  
`quoteId`, `surgeSnapshotId`, `surgeMultiplierLocked numeric(3,2)`, `surgeH3r9`,  
`predictedDistanceM`, `predictedDurationS`,  
`actualDistanceM`, `actualDurationS`, `waitSeconds`,  
`paymentIntentId`, `currency 'USD'`,  
`driverLastLat/Lng/Heading/At` — denormalized **1 Hz** position for authorized readers,  
`lastDispatchAt`, `offerAttemptCount`.  
Derived: `pickupH3r9` from pickup. Partial uniques: one open trip per rider; one active-driving trip per `currentDriverId`.

**`tripStateEvents`** — **append-only**. `id`, `tripId`, `generation`, `fromState`, `toState`, `at`, `actorType enum(rider,driver,system)`, `actorId null`, `payload json`.

**`offers`** — **append-only rows, forward-only state**. `id`, `tripId`, `driverId`, `generation`,  
`state enum(pending,accepted,declined,timed_out,superseded,cancelled)`,  
`offeredAt`, `exclusiveUntil` (= offeredAt+15s), `respondedAt null`, `declineReason null`,  
frozen search: `driverLat`, `driverLng`, `driverRecordedAt`, `sessionId`, `straightLineM`, `etaSeconds`, `rankInSearch`, `searchSnapshotId`.  
Partial uniques: one pending per trip; one pending per driver. **Time-bounded lease:** pending ∧ now < exclusiveUntil.

**`dispatchSearchSnapshots`** — **append-only**. `id`, `tripId`, `generation`, `searchedAt`, `pickupLat/Lng`, `selectedDriverId null`, `candidateCount`, `idleInFirstRing`, `algoVersion`.  
**`dispatchSearchCandidates`** — **append-only**. `snapshotId`, `rank`, `driverId`, `lat`, `lng`, `recordedAt`, `straightLineM`, `etaSeconds`, `excludedReason null`. Top 20 plus the winner.

**`fareQuotes`** — `id`, `riderId`, `vehicleType`, pickup/dropoff fields, `predictedDistanceM`, `predictedDurationS`, `routeHash`, `surgeSnapshotId`, `surgeMultiplier`, `currency`, `lineItems json` (base, time, distance, surge, booking, estTolls, tax), `minTotalCents`, `maxTotalCents`, `quotedAt`, `expiresAt` (= quotedAt+120s), `consumedByTripId null`. **Time-bounded.**

**`invoices`** — 1:1 trip after settle. `tripId` PK, `subtotalCents`, `surgeAmountCents`, `waitFeeCents`, `tollsCents`, `bookingFeeCents`, `taxCents`, `tipCents`, `cancelFeeCents`, `totalChargedCents`, `driverGrossCents`, `commissionCents`, `driverNetCents`, `settledAt`. Line items also in `invoiceLines` (append-only).

**`paymentMethods`** — `id`, `userId`, `provider`, `providerRef`, `last4`, `brand`.  
**`paymentIntents`** — `id`, `tripId`, `userId`, `providerIntentId`, `preauthCents`, `captureCents null`, `status enum(requires_action,preauthed,captured,released,failed)`, `errorText`.  
**`payouts`** — `id`, `driverId`, `periodStart/End` or `tripId`, `amountCents`, `status`, `providerTransferId`.

**`earningEntries`** — **append-only**. `id`, `driverId`, `tripId null`, `type enum(fare,wait,toll,tip,commission,cancel_penalty,adjustment)`, `amountCents` (signed), `createdAt`.  
**`driverDailySummaries`** — derived, PK `(driverId, date)`. Online minutes, net cents, trip count. Recomputed from sessions + earningEntries.

**`ratings`** — `id`, `tripId`, `fromUserId`, `toUserId`, `stars int 1–5`, `tags text[]`, `createdAt`. Unique `(tripId, fromUserId)`.

**`tripShares`** — `id`, `tripId`, `createdByUserId`, `tokenHash`, `label`, `createdAt`, `expiresAt`, `revokedAt null`. **Time-bounded.**  
**`sosEvents`** — append-only. `id`, `tripId`, `userId`, `at`, `lat`, `lng`.  
**`tripPathPoints`** — append-only, 1 Hz during `{matched,arriving,in_trip}` from the current driver’s samples: `tripId`, `at`, `lat`, `lng`, `source enum(driver,rider)`.

**`dispatchWork`** — queue. `id`, `tripId`, `generation`, `reason enum(new_request,timeout,decline,driver_cancel,retry_empty)`, `notBefore`, `claimedBy null`, `claimedUntil null`, `doneAt null`.

**`dispatchWorkerLeases`** — `workerId`, `expiresAt`, `heartbeatAt`. System fencing.

Economy rates (comfort +40%, XL +80% on base/perKm/perMin, not booking): `baseCents=200`, `perKmCents=90`, `perMinCents=20`, `minimumFareCents=600`, `bookingFeeCents=250`. Surge is a multiplier in `{1.00, 1.20, 1.40, …, 4.00}` (steps of 0.20). Wait fee: $0.30/min after 120 s of `driverArrivedAt` before `inTripStartedAt`.

### 5. The hard operations

ETA used in matching and the public surface: `etaSeconds = round(haversineMeters / 8.333)` (30 km/h), `algoVersion = haversine_v1`. If a routing vendor is configured and returns within 150 ms, store that as `osrm_v1` and prefer it; on timeout/failure, `haversine_v1`. The rest of the design does not depend on the vendor.

---

**1. PublishDriverLocation** (write, 1–4 Hz)

- **Inputs:** `sessionId`, `lat`, `lng`, `headingDeg`, `speedMps`, `accuracyM`, `recordedAt`, `seq`.
- **Auth:** the caller is the driver who owns the **open** session `sessionId`. No other role.
- **Steps:**  
  1. Load session; reject if `endedAt` set or `driverId` mismatch.  
  2. Reject if `seq` ≤ current `seq` for that session (stale reorder).  
  3. Reject if `recordedAt > now + 5s` (clock ahead) or `recordedAt < now − 30s` (too old to flush).  
  4. `receivedAt = now`. Upsert `driverLocationCurrent`. Append a sample if last persisted sample for this driver is ≥ 1 s old.  
  5. Heartbeat: `driverSessions.lastHeartbeatAt = now`.  
  6. Recompute `h3r9`. If the driver is idle-at-now (invariant 11 evaluated in this transaction), upsert `hexIdleDrivers` for the new hex and delete the old hex row if the hex changed; if they are **not** idle, delete any `hexIdleDrivers` row.  
  7. If this driver is `currentDriverId` of a trip in `{matched, arriving, in_trip}` and `now − trip.driverLastAt ≥ 1s`: copy lat/lng/heading/at onto the trip row; append `tripPathPoints`.  
  8. If heartbeat older than 20 s on a **later** watchdog pass, the session is ended `stale_heartbeat` (operation 2’s inverse, system).
- **Atomic:** 1–7 for one sample.  
- **Eventual:** live-query push of the trip row to rider/share subscribers; sample archival.  
- **Optimistic UI:** driver map uses on-device GPS immediately.  
- **Conflict:** kicked session → reject, UI goes offline. Concurrent samples: highest `seq` wins. Never LWW by `recordedAt` across sessions.

---

**2. SetDriverOnlineState** (write)

- **Inputs:** `desired enum(online,offline)`, `deviceId`, `vehicleId`.
- **Auth:** caller is that driver; `driverProfiles.status = active`; vehicle belongs to them and `inspectExpiresOn > today`.
- **Steps (online):**  
  1. If an open session exists on another `deviceId`, set its `endedAt=now`, `endReason=kicked`. Delete `hexIdleDrivers` for this driver.  
  2. Insert `driverSessions` open row.  
  3. Do not insert idle-index row until a fresh location arrives (a driver without a point is not matchable).  
- **Steps (offline):** close session, delete idle-index row. If a **pending offer** exists, CAS it to `cancelled` (reason `driver_offline`) and enqueue `dispatchWork(reason=decline)` for that trip so search continues. If trip is `{matched, arriving}`, this is **not** a silent offline: treat as `CancelTrip` by driver (operation 9). If `in_trip`, refuse offline; driver must complete or SOS.
- **Atomic:** session close/open + offer cancel + idle-index.  
- **Eventual:** push “offer pulled” to the kicked device.  
- **Optimistic UI:** none for online (wait for server). Offline may show offline immediately; if in_trip, snap back.  
- **Conflict:** two devices go online together → one kicks the other; the kicked device’s subsequent location writes fail step 1 of operation 1.

---

**3. ReadMarketplaceSurface** (read)

- **Inputs:** `h3r9` (usually the rider’s current hex), `vehicleType`.
- **Auth:** any authenticated user. Returns **only** `hexPublicSurface` for that cell and k-ring 2 (19 cells): `surgeMultiplier`, `pickupEtaSeconds`, `validUntil`, `publishedAt`. No counts, no driver ids.
- **Steps:** point-read those 19 rows. If `validUntil < now`, still return them flagged `stale` (client may show last known; it may not recompute).
- **Atomic:** n/a.  
- **Eventual:** surface lags the 30 s publisher (operation 10).  
- **Optimistic UI:** last cached surface, labelled if older than 60 s.  
- **Conflict:** none. **Do not** substitute a client-side count of visible cars.

---

**4. CreateFareQuote** (write)

- **Inputs:** `pickupLat/Lng`, `dropoffLat/Lng`, `vehicleType`.
- **Auth:** caller is a rider with a payment method. No open trip (invariant 1) required for quoting.
- **Steps:**  
  1. Snap pickup/dropoff to addresses (geocoder; if fail, keep pins).  
  2. Predicted route: vendor or haversine×1.3 for distance and distance/8.333 for duration.  
  3. Read `hexPublicSurface[pickupH3r9]` (not internal counts).  
  4. Compute line items from §4 rates × `surgeMultiplier`; `minTotalCents` = computed; `maxTotalCents` = round(min×1.25) plus estimated tolls pad.  
  5. Insert `fareQuotes` with `expiresAt = now+120s`.
- **Atomic:** the insert.  
- **Eventual:** none.  
- **Optimistic UI:** show a spinner; do not locally compute surge.  
- **Conflict:** none. Quote is not a driver hold (a 3 min ETA on the quote can be 8 min at request).

---

**5. RequestRide** (write)

- **Inputs:** `quoteId`, `paymentMethodId`.
- **Auth:** caller is `fareQuotes.riderId`; no open trip; quote unexpired and unconsumed.
- **Steps:**  
  1. Re-read quote; abort if `now ≥ expiresAt` (client must call operation 4 again).  
  2. Insert `paymentIntents` and call the card provider **preauthorize `maxTotalCents`**. On decline, insert failed intent, abort, no trip.  
  3. Insert `trips`: `state=requested`, `currentDriverId=null`, `currentOfferId=null`, `dispatchGeneration=0`, copy pickup/dropoff/vehicleType/predictions, `surgeSnapshotId` and `surgeMultiplierLocked` **from the quote** (not from a fresh read — the rider accepted that number), `requestExpiresAt=now+5min`.  
  4. Mark quote `consumedByTripId`.  
  5. `tripStateEvents` requested.  
  6. Insert `dispatchWork(reason=new_request, notBefore=now, generation=0)`.
- **Atomic:** 3–6 plus the local paymentIntent row. The **provider preauth** is an external side effect that must succeed before commit; if commit fails after preauth, a reconcilation job (bundled with operation 11) releases it.  
- **Eventual:** dispatch worker wakeup.  
- **Optimistic UI:** “Finding your driver.” Never a name/ETA-from-a-specific-car.  
- **Conflict:** second device confirms at once → unique open-trip index fails; only one trip exists.

**6. DispatchTick** (scheduled/background, system) — the axis operation

- **Inputs:** none. A worker with a live `dispatchWorkerLeases` row wakes on `dispatchWork` where `doneAt is null AND notBefore ≤ now`, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, batches of 50, every 100 ms or on notify.
- **Auth:** system only. No user credential can insert offers or set `currentDriverId`.
- **Steps, per work row:**  
  1. Load trip. If state ∈ `{completed, cancelled}` or `generation` on the work row ≠ `trip.dispatchGeneration` (except `new_request` at 0), mark work done.  
  2. If `now ≥ requestExpiresAt` and state ∈ `{requested, offering}`: CAS trip to `cancelled` (`cancelledBy=system`, reason `unfilled`); CAS any pending offer to `cancelled`; restore that driver to idle index if still idle-at-now; enqueue payment release; mark done.  
  3. If a pending offer exists and `now < exclusiveUntil`: mark this wake spurious, done.  
  4. **Close expired lease:** if pending offer and `now ≥ exclusiveUntil`: `UPDATE offers SET state=timed_out, respondedAt=now WHERE id=? AND state='pending' AND exclusiveUntil≤now`. If rowcount=0, accept won the race; done. If rowcount=1: `dispatchGeneration += 1`, `currentOfferId=null`, `state=requested`, restore driver to `hexIdleDrivers` if idle-at-now, append state event. Continue in the **same transaction**.  
  5. **Search snapshot.** `exclude = {driverIds already on offers for this tripId} ∪ {driverIds with any pending offer} ∪ {currentDriverId of any trip in matched/arriving/in_trip}`. From pickup `h3r9`, take k-rings 0, 2, 5, 10, 15. At each ring, read `hexIdleDrivers ⋈ driverLocationCurrent ⋈ driverSessions ⋈ vehicles` where `vehicle.type` matches, `recordedAt ≥ now−10s`, `session.endedAt is null`, driver not in `exclude`, profile active. Compute `straightLineM` and `etaSeconds`. Stop expanding when at least one candidate has `straightLineM ≤ 8000`. Persist `dispatchSearchSnapshots` + up to 20 `dispatchSearchCandidates`. `idleInFirstRing` = unfiltered idle count in k-ring 0–2 (**structural count**, not rider-visible).  
  6. If no candidate: `lastDispatchAt=now`, enqueue `dispatchWork(reason=retry_empty, notBefore=now+3s, generation=current)`, done.  
  7. Winner = min `etaSeconds` (tie: min `straightLineM`, then `driverId`). Insert `offers` pending, `exclusiveUntil=now+15s`, freeze location fields, `generation=trip.dispatchGeneration+1`. Delete winner from `hexIdleDrivers`. Update trip: `state=offering`, `currentOfferId=new`, `dispatchGeneration+=1`, `offerAttemptCount+=1`, `currentDriverId` **still null**. Enqueue `dispatchWork(reason=timeout, notBefore=exclusiveUntil, generation=new)`. Push driver: offer payload (pickup, dropoff, eta, rider first name, rating, 15 s clock).  
  8. If `offerAttemptCount ≥ 20` after a timeout close: skip 7, go to system-cancel as in step 2.
- **Atomic:** steps 2–7 (or 2–4+8) for one trip in one transaction, including idle-index add/remove and work enqueue.  
- **Eventual:** push to driver; rider live-query still shows “Finding” (no identity).  
- **Optimistic UI:** none (system). Driver UI starts a 15 s countdown only after the push.  
- **Conflict:** accept vs step 4 CAS (one winner). Two workers: `SKIP LOCKED` + pending unique indexes; a duplicate offer insert aborts the transaction and retries. Winner goes offline between 5 and 7: unique/ session check in 7 fails, mark candidate excluded, take next in the same snapshot list without a new search if still fresh (<200 ms), else retry search.

**7. RespondToOffer** (write)

- **Inputs:** `offerId`, `decision enum(accept,decline)`, `declineReason optional`.
- **Auth:** caller is `offers.driverId`; session open.
- **Steps (decline):** CAS `pending → declined` where `id=offerId AND state=pending` (allowed even after `exclusiveUntil` for bookkeeping if still pending because timeout hasn’t run; if already `timed_out`, return already-closed). Restore idle index if idle-at-now. Enqueue `dispatchWork(reason=decline, generation=trip.dispatchGeneration)`. Trip `currentOfferId=null`, `state=requested`, generation++.  
- **Steps (accept):**  
  1. `UPDATE offers SET state=accepted, respondedAt=now WHERE id=? AND state='pending' AND exclusiveUntil > now`. If rowcount=0: return `offer_closed` with the row’s terminal state.  
  2. `UPDATE trips SET state=matched, currentDriverId=driverId, matchedAt=now WHERE id=? AND state='offering' AND currentOfferId=?`. If rowcount=0 (rider cancelled): reverse the offer to `cancelled` is too late — leave `accepted` on a cancelled trip is wrong; instead: if trip is `cancelled`, return `trip_cancelled` and do **not** set currentDriverId (the CAS in 1 still consumed the lease; they accepted a dead trip; show that).  
  3. Append state event. Delete idle-index row (already gone).  
  4. Push rider the match card (name, photo, plate, live position). Push loser-none (exclusive).  
- **Atomic:** 1–3.  
- **Eventual:** pushes.  
- **Optimistic UI:** **none.** Spinner until accept CAS returns. Showing “you got it” locally is how two drivers both drive to the pin.  
- **Conflict:** timeout vs accept (Trap 2). Decline vs timeout: both terminal; dispatch still runs once (`dispatchWork` unique per generation).

**8. ProgressTrip** (write: arrive / start / complete)

- **Inputs:** `tripId`, `action enum(mark_arriving, mark_arrived, start, complete)`, optional `tollsCents` on complete.
- **Auth:** caller is `currentDriverId`; trip state must be the predecessor: `matched`→arriving or arrived, `matched|arriving`→start, `in_trip`→complete. `mark_arriving` is also allowed to the **system** when haversine(driver, pickup) < 300 m (optional auto).
- **Steps:**  
  - `mark_arriving`: `matched → arriving`, set `arrivingEnteredAt`.  
  - `mark_arrived`: set `driverArrivedAt` (state stays `arriving`). Rider push: “Your driver is here.”  
  - `start`: require `driverArrivedAt` set OR haversine < 80 m. `→ in_trip`, `inTripStartedAt=now`. Start persisting `tripPathPoints` if not already.  
  - `complete`: require haversine to dropoff < 250 m OR rider confirms (rider input `riderConfirmDropoff=true`). `→ completed`, `completedAt=now`. `actualDurationS = completedAt − inTripStartedAt`. `actualDistanceM` = map-match of `tripPathPoints` (fallback: sum of consecutive haversines × 1.05). `waitSeconds = max(0, inTripStartedAt − driverArrivedAt)`. Enqueue `SettleTripPayment`. Restore idle index if session still open and location fresh.
- **Atomic:** the state CAS + timestamps + (on complete) enqueue settle. Distance write may use the path table as-of commit.  
- **Eventual:** fare UI waits on settle; idle index is in the transaction.  
- **Optimistic UI:** driver “complete” shows “ending trip…” not a dollar amount.  
- **Conflict:** double-complete: second CAS fails, returns the existing invoice pointer. Rider cancel vs start: see operation 9.

**9. CancelTrip** (write)

- **Inputs:** `tripId`, `reason`.
- **Auth:** rider of the trip, or current driver if state ∈ `{matched, arriving}`, or system (unfilled / safety). Not during `in_trip` except SOS+support (out of scope as a rider self-serve).
- **Fee schedule (rider cancel, computed at commit):**  

  | State at commit | Condition | `cancelFeeCents` |
  |---|---|---|
  | requested, offering | any | 0 |
  | matched, arriving | `now − matchedAt < 60s` and `driverArrivedAt` is null | 0 |
  | matched, arriving | `now − matchedAt ≥ 60s` and not arrived | 200 |
  | arriving | arrived and `now − driverArrivedAt < 120s` | 500 |
  | arriving | arrived and wait ≥ 120s | 800 |

- **Steps (rider):** CAS trip to `cancelled`, `cancelledBy=rider`, store fee. CAS pending offer (if offering) to `cancelled`; restore that driver’s idle index. If matched/arriving, clear the driver’s current trip, restore idle, **stop** position fan-out, push driver “trip cancelled.” Enqueue settle-for-fee-or-release.  
- **Steps (driver cancel in matched/arriving):** do **not** close the trip. Append event `driver_dropped`. Increment `cancelStrikeCount`. CAS current offer (already accepted — leave it). Set `currentDriverId=null`, `currentOfferId=null`, `state=requested`, `dispatchGeneration+=1`, `matchedAt=null` (keep `requestedAt` and surge lock). Exclude this driver via the existing `offers` history (they have an `accepted` row; invariant 10 uses any offer row). Enqueue `dispatchWork(reason=driver_cancel)`. Rider UI returns to “Finding your driver” **without** a new preauth.  
- **Atomic:** the CAS + offer close + idle-index + work enqueue.  
- **Eventual:** payment; push.  
- **Optimistic UI:** rider cancel may show cancelled immediately; if CAS fails because complete already committed, snap to completed.  
- **Conflict:** accept vs rider cancel — trip unique state CAS: if accept commits first, cancel uses the matched-row fee; if cancel first, accept hits step 2 failure in operation 7. Driver cancel vs timeout: irrelevant, offer already accepted.

**10. RecomputeMarketplaceSurface** (periodic bulk, system)

- **Inputs:** none. Runs every **30 s** (every **10 s** if citywide `openRequestCount > 3 × idleDriverCount`).
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:**  
  1. For each of the ~2,400 service hexes, in one pass over `hexIdleDrivers` and open trips:  
     - `idleDriverCount` = rows in that hex (unfiltered).  
     - `openRequestCount` = trips in `{requested, offering}` with `pickupH3r9` in that hex.  
     - `inTripCount` = trips in `{matched, arriving, in_trip}` whose **driver** current hex is this hex.  
  2. `demandSupplyRatio = (openRequestCount + 0.25×inTripCount) / max(idleDriverCount, 0.5)`.  
  3. `surgeMultiplier = clamp(round_to_0.2(1.0 + 0.4 × max(0, ratio − 1)), 1.00, 4.00)` but 1.00 whenever `openRequestCount = 0`.  
  4. `pickupEtaSeconds`: k-NN from hex **centroid** to idle drivers of type `economy` with the same search as DispatchTick step 5, rings up to 15; if none, `null` (UI: “No cars nearby”). This is a **structural nearest-neighbor**, not a rider-visible min.  
  5. Insert `hexInternalSnapshots` for all hexes (bulk).  
  6. Update `hexPublicSurface` with **only** `(surgeMultiplier, pickupEtaSeconds, snapshotId, validFrom=now, validUntil=now+45s)`. Never copy counts onto this table.
- **Atomic:** per-hex snapshot insert + public upsert can be one transaction per hex or one citywide transaction; citywide is preferred so a rider reading two adjacent hexes does not see mixed ticks. If the table is too large for one txn, fence with a `surfaceGeneration` integer: readers only see rows matching `marketplaceEpoch.current`.  
- **Eventual:** client live-queries of public surface.  
- **Optimistic UI:** n/a.  
- **Conflict:** overlap with DispatchTick mutating `hexIdleDrivers` — use a snapshot isolation read of the idle index as-of `computedAt`. Do **not** hold a citywide lock against location writes.

**11. SettleTripPayment** (background, spans the card network)

- **Inputs:** `tripId` (enqueued by complete, rider-cancel-with-fee, or system-cancel).
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:**  
  1. Load trip + path + quote + paymentIntent. Idempotent on `invoices` existing.  
  2. If completed: compute fare from §2.16 and §4 rates; wait fee = `max(0, waitSeconds − 120) × 5 cents/s` ($0.30/min); add `tollsCents`; tax 0% in this spec (field exists, always 0) so implementers don’t invent a jurisdiction. `totalChargedCents = min(computed, paymentIntent.preauthCents)` if computed ≤ preauth; if computed > preauth, capture preauth and create a **second** incremental charge for the remainder (if it fails, invoice `totalChargedCents = captured`, `shortfallCents` recorded, support).  
  3. If cancelled with fee: capture `cancelFeeCents` (0 → release preauth).  
  4. If system unfilled: release preauth.  
  5. Call provider `capture` / `release`. On provider timeout, leave intent `preauthed` and retry (at-least-once; capture must be idempotent on `providerIntentId`).  
  6. Insert `invoices` + `invoiceLines`. Insert `earningEntries`: +fare, +wait, +tolls, +tip (0 until rider tips), −commission.  
  7. Open a 30-minute window for `tipCents` patch (rider-only); a tip later inserts another earning entry and an incremental capture.
- **Atomic:** local invoice + earningEntries after **successful** provider response; the provider call is outside the DB transaction, fenced by intent status CAS (`preauthed → capturing → captured`).  
- **Eventual:** driver earnings read; receipt push.  
- **Optimistic UI:** rider sees “receipt pending” then the invoice.  
- **Conflict:** double settle — unique `invoices.tripId`. Capture vs incremental tip: serialize on the intent row.

**13. CreateOrRevokeTripShare** (write)

- **Inputs:** `tripId`, `action enum(create,revoke)`, on create `ttlSeconds ≤ 3600`.

### 6. Scale and cost

Successful large-city deployment (one metro, not global):

| Thing | Size / rate |
|---|---|
| Registered drivers | 40,000 |
| Online at peak | 12,000 |
| Idle at peak | ~3,000–4,000 |
| Service hexes (r9) | ~2,400 |
| Trips / day | 200,000 |
| Peak new requests | ~8 / s (≈ 30k / peak hour) |
| Offers / s at peak (≈ 2.5 offers/trip + retries) | ~25 / s |
| Concurrent trips `{matched…in_trip}` | ~8,000 |
| Location writes | 12,000 × ~3 Hz mean ≈ **36,000 / s** (peak 48,000) |
| Location samples persisted 1 Hz | 12,000 / s |
| Trip-row position fan-out 1 Hz | ~8,000 / s |
| Surface recompute | 2,400 hexes × 2 k-NN-ish passes / 30 s ≈ **80–200 small geo searches / s** extra |
| `hexIdleDrivers` rows | ~4,000 |
| `offers` rows / day | ~500,000 |
| `driverLocationSamples` / day | ~1×10⁹ before downsample; **persisted ~1×10⁹×(1/3) ≈ 3×10⁸ / day** at 1 Hz × 12k × 0.6 online-hours-factor — plan **~400 M rows/day** at busy, 48 h retention ≈ **800 M hot rows** |
| Card captures / day | ~200,000 + tips |

What gets expensive:

- **`PublishDriverLocation`** is the cost center. A row-store update-in-place on 12k hot rows at 4 Hz is survivable; indexing those points with a general B-tree on `(lat,lng)` is not. The idle index must be hex-bucketed so DispatchTick does not scan 12k drivers. Writing a sample row at 4 Hz without downsample will not hold.
- **DispatchTick k-NN** is cheap **if** it hits `hexIdleDrivers` by ring (tens to low hundreds of candidates). It becomes a city-scan if the idle index is missing or if exclusion joins (`pending offers`, `active trips`) are nested-looped on 8k trips per offer.
- **RecomputeMarketplaceSurface** is 2,400 nearest-neighbor queries every 30 s. Naive: 2,400 × scan of 4,000 idle = 10 M distance computations / 30 s, fine in-process; **not** fine as 2,400 independent SQL queries each doing `ORDER BY earth_distance LIMIT 1` over a heap. Must be one sweep: invert idle drivers into hex buckets, then walk rings in memory.
- **Broadcast:** 8,000 riders polling `driverLocationCurrent` globally is a visibility bug and a load bug. Authorized 1 Hz on the **trip row** is 8k writes/s extra — acceptable. Cross-product live queries (“all drivers near me”) will not survive.
- **`ReadDriverEarnings`** over unbounded `earningEntries` without daily summaries becomes a history scan; summaries are required at this volume.
- **Path map-match on complete** is bursty at 8 completes/s; keep it bounded to that trip’s points (~15–40 minutes × 1 Hz ≈ 2,400 points), never “all samples for this driver today.”

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**1. System-run exclusive leases with clock close and generation fencing.**  
Ordinary “user mutation updates a row” cannot be dispatch. DispatchTick must run as **system**, enqueue a durable timer (`notBefore = exclusiveUntil`), and **CAS** against driver accept. Generation on `dispatchWork` must no-op stale timeouts after an accept. A backend with only user-triggered writes, or with timers that are not transactional with the offer insert, will double-offer or stall.  

*Concession:* poll every 1 s from the driver (“got any offers?”) and skip exclusive leases.  
*Cheat boundary:* if you drop exclusivity or the 15 s timeout-as-a-first-class closer, you are no longer testing this archetype’s axis. Polling is allowed as a **transport** for the push; it is not allowed as the **arbiter** of who holds the trip.

**2. k-NN over a moving set with a freshness predicate, several times a second.**  
This is not `WHERE idle = true ORDER BY distance LIMIT 1` on a static table. The candidate set changes on every offer, accept, heartbeat, and hex crossing. The search must be **as-of a snapshot** that is persisted (`dispatchSearchSnapshots`) because the moving set will not be reconstructable later.  

*Concession:* match to a random idle driver in the pickup hex, ignore neighboring hexes and ETA.  
*Cheat boundary:* updating locations every 30 s, or matching on a boolean `isIdle` without the 10 s freshness window, removes the moving-object problem. Hex-only random match removes nearest-neighbor.

**3. Broadcast of position snapshots to a dynamic, tiny subscriber set.**  
The 36k/s write stream must not be a table anyone lists. After match, ~1 Hz snapshots go to {rider, current driver, live shares} for **that** trip and stop on generation change (Trap 5). Subscriber membership is a function of trip state **and** who the current driver is.  

*Concession:* denormalize `driverLastLat` onto the trip row at 1 Hz and let authorized clients poll/live-query **that row**. That still preserves visibility.  
*Cheat boundary:* letting unmatched riders (or any rider) read `driverLocationCurrent` / subscribe to a city feed, even “just for the map.” Ghost cars must be a **separate derived product**, delayed and jittered, not the idle index.

**4. Public values derived from counts and nearest-neighbor over a domain the reader cannot see.**  
Surge and pickup ETA are about the fleet. The fleet rows are secret. A generic “count/min where the caller can read the row” produces Trap 3’s silent 1.0×. The product needs an **elevated, periodic, unfiltered** compute that writes a **narrow public projection**. Internal counts stay off the rider-readable table because they leak supply.  

*Concession:* publish only a bucketed ETA (“2–5 min”) and a three-level surge (“none / busy / very busy”) to reduce leakiness — still computed unfiltered.  
*Cheat boundary:* author-set surge sliders, or surge computed from rider-visible rows, or exposing `idleDriverCount` as the “transparency” feature. If the number is not derived from supply/demand **counts of real idle drivers and open requests**, the axis is gone.

**5. Time-bounded uniqueness (leases), not just unique keys.**  
“One pending offer per driver” is a partial unique index **plus** a clock. The uniqueness must lift at timeout without a user write. Backends that can unique on `(driverId) WHERE state='pending'` still need a closer that is not the driver. The same shape appears on open sessions, open trips, and share tokens.  

*Concession:* 15 s exclusivity is “best effort”; two accepts, last write wins, customer support cleans up.  
*Cheat boundary:* that concession is a cheat the moment the product still claims exclusive offers and “your driver” as a fact. Money (who is paid) and safety (whose GPS you see) cannot be last-write-wins.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice (do not invent a new marketplace):** trip state machine with cancel fees; preauth-then-capture; driver Connect-style payouts; H3-style hex surge; GPS breadcrumbs; post-trip ratings; live share links; one active driver device; commission on fare not tip. Peak rates are in the right order of magnitude for a large Uber/Lyft city, not for global Uber.

**Deliberate simplifications (not cheats on the axis):** one metro, one currency, no airports/zones/scheduled rides/carpool/Wait-and-Save; haversine 30 km/h matching ETA unless a router is present; tax = 0; sequential **one-at-a-time** exclusive offers rather than production batch matching. Sequential offers are **on purpose**: they make the lease, the timeout closer, and re-dispatch load-bearing in the ordinary path (most trips will pass through 1–3 offers). Batch matching would hide the axis inside a solver.

**Invented for the exercise (but required by the traps):** freezing a search snapshot per offer; splitting `hexInternalSnapshots` from `hexPublicSurface` so counts cannot leak through a “harmless” column; forbidding re-offer of the same driver on the same trip (prevents timeout loops on the nearest sleeper); downsampling broadcast to 1 Hz on the trip row while matching still sees 4 Hz current points; treating idle as an interval intersection that the idle index only **caches**.

**Known production divergence:** Uber/Lyft use batch/upfront dispatch, a real map matcher, and richer surge (temporal smoothing, hex neighbors, driver-side guarantees). Replacing exclusive 15 s offers with a batch solver would make a better product and a **worse** test of this axis. Do not “improve” that away in implementation.

I am high-confidence on the traps, the idle-at-T definition, the accept/timeout CAS, and the silent-wrong surge/ETA if computed under row visibility. I am medium-confidence on the exact surge formula coefficients and on 400 M location samples/day (online-hours factor varies). I am low-confidence on map-matched distance without a specified matcher — implementors should treat path length × 1.05 as the spec’d fallback and a vendor match as an allowed replacement that must still be computed at complete, not at request.
