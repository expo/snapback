## 4. Suggestions

- **Claim OQ8's answer explicitly.** Stage 1-before-Stage 2 is the RFC's real answer to 0129 OQ8's substrate-attribution worry (a Candidate A failure over the new store/graph substrate is attributable to the IR rung; over today's substrate it is not). One sentence in §Sequencing saying "Stage 1 is the substrate-bounding that 0129 OQ8 requires before the lab can be scheduled" would convert an implicit virtue into a citable one and directly help the author close OQ8.
- **Say what closes the pre-intent restart gap.** The RFC lists the restart-renders-pre-intent-state limitation (lines 176-179) and Move 5's discriminator covers restart reconstruction, but the document never states that IR replay at boot over the Move 2 durable store — pure Candidate A machinery, unfrozen under 0129's carve-out — is expected to close this gap regardless of the Move 5 outcome. Making that explicit would show move-level payoff independent of the Option-C decision.
- **Inherit the delta-lane egress seam fail-closed.** `issues/20260801-live-delta-frames-bypass-result-contract-validation.md` (open) records that `try_live_delta_fanout` publishes rows without result-contract validation while the re-query path validates. Move 2 makes delta frames a *store ingestion* path, which raises that seam's stakes: the local materialization would durably retain unvalidated rows. A guardrail bullet in Move 2 ("store ingestion admits only contract-validated frames; the open delta-egress ticket is a Stage 1 prerequisite or explicit accepted risk") would tie the direction to a known live defect.
- **Consider two-tier acceptance for Move 1.** The fail-closed completeness half (refuse implicit prefixes, `unknown`/`complete`/`refused` states) is safety work already partially landed (`SNAPBACK_QUERY_IMPLICIT_LIMIT_REACHED`, `b52e3b9c`) and is expressible against the existing 0080 grammar; the composable graph is the long pole. An explicit interim milestone — completeness states shipped on the current grammar before the graph Spec lands — would deliver the Fleet lesson's protection earlier without changing the move.
- **Cross-reference LLP 0145.** The client-bundle architecture research is in Related and currently under active revision; Move 2's "one normalized local materialization" is the largest client-architecture commitment since that document. A sentence on how Move 2 lands within (or revises) 0145's bundle conclusions would prevent a fork.
