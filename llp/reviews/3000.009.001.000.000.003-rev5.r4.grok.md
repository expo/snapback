## Review (verbatim answer text)

I'll review LLP 3000.009.001.000.000.003 revision 5 at design altitude, starting with the review skill and the RFC plus its spike evidence.The three §21 changes hold, and they do not reopen the §19 or §20 fixes. No remaining material defect.

**Publication release sequence (§4.5, §5.2, witness 6).** Invalidate, then install immutable entries tagged with the prefix they were filled at, then release-store `W`, and only then send replies, continuations, and wakeups. A reader that still holds an older `W` cannot hit the new entry: the hit requires the entry’s `W` to equal the acquired `W`, and an install is rejected if `W` moved after the fill was read. That closes the in-place replacement where a lock-free reader observed a newer payload under an older `W`. Invalidations still happen-before the release-store, so a reader that acquires the new `W` cannot use a cache generation that `W` has already invalidated. Waiters re-acquire `W` after arming and before sleeping, and the wakeup is fired only after the release-store, so a wakeup can no longer be consumed while `W` is still the old value. Two-image reads, per-client minima, and prefix-ordered acknowledgements are unchanged.

**Failed post-grant qualification (§6.6).** A backend that fails the later read-committed check unlocks the session advisory lock or disconnects, and drops its prepared state, before any body runs. Takeover therefore cannot sit forever on a shared holder that never bound the new token. The successful path is unchanged: no body runs before qualification, token publication stays in the same transaction as the exclusive advisory lock, and a barging holder is still the watcher’s problem. Releasing a failed qualification does not open an old-token commit window.

**Stale restart (§5.1, witness 6).** A row outside `[owm, orev)` still aborts the whole snapshot. Each retry re-acquires `W` and opens a new snapshot at a fresh `V`, so it no longer repeats the same doomed view until `E_STORE`. Discarded partial rows, probes, and fills, plus “streams restart before emission or terminate retryably,” keep the retry from splicing two prefixes. On one owner `W` is monotone and `owm` is a dispatch watermark, so the new `V` is at least `owm` and the two-image case resolves. The per-client minimum is a floor; moving forward still satisfies it.

The §19 and §20 obligations still stand: the exclusive fence and the certificate re-read, retention at `T≥5s` with cursor pins, restored R0 on expo-build-450b, keyed and mix gates, qualification on a fresh read-committed snapshot, and invalidation before `W`.

Serial equivalence, holes and takeover, routing, and the budget do not show a new concrete failure. Readers bind `W` and then `V = GREATEST(W, H)` inside one snapshot; commits above `V` stay on `odata` because footprints are held through `W`. Acknowledgements wait for `W`, and the void or the same-key `_commits` record still decides unknown outcomes. The drain still finishes every open reservation before an ordered head CAS. §8 still treats 4,525/s as unsupported until a restored R0 mean is ≤6.0 ms on the real pool. Two-image writes, attempt records, prefix lag, and loan occupancy are inside that gate. The ≤6.4 ms product bar is measured later. Witness 6 now covers the cache install, the waiter, and the re-bind.
