## Overall assessment

The three themes are useful: remove the second-client escape hatch, ship
complete forms at the fixture scale, and make durable writes work through
ordinary browser lifecycle. The original mechanisms and evidence claims
needed revision before implementation. In particular, the proposals could
weaken offline truth and benchmark coverage while duplicating fixes already
on main.

## Strengths

- The parent groups repeated builder workarounds by user-visible outcome.
- The query child preserves the proven-empty count control and asks for
  the real Insights shape rather than a convenient one-count surrogate.
- The scale child favors executable repairs and sound bounds over a ceiling
  increase.
- The write child asks for real Chromium evidence and acknowledges that
  online first-write success does not close offline replay.

## Material concerns and dispositions

1. **High — duplicate implementation and stale diagnosis.** The query plan
   proposes the unheld-total repair now present in `twin.ts` and
   `real-owner-successor.test.ts`. The reproduced bug published a zero with
   a partial receipt, while an earlier control had a valid stale complete
   zero. The acquisition lane also repaired a foreground pre-custody wait.
   **Applied:** record those source landings and move already-fixed cases
   to qualification; retain exact timed/media/replay cases as separate work.

2. **High — presentation cannot manufacture authority.** Query Summary/C
   suggested suppressing numeric zero based on `live`, source or server
   sequence, and allowed `ready` with undefined data. That conflicts with
   the pure summary contract and would hide legitimate offline zeros.
   Unreadable maintained values also differ from physically absent groups.
   **Applied:** retain LLP 1012 §6 and LLP 1018.003 §2; classify coverage and
   screen freshness separately; compare matched operation/time/authority.

3. **High — offline once policy would be reversed.** Query D required a
   descriptor on cold durable reopen without distinguishing offline from
   fresh online delivery. `guide offline`, LLP 1012 §9 and
   `store/asset-policy.ts` deliberately null once media offline.
   **Applied:** test live unconsumed publication separately from offline
   redaction, consumption and mounted-instance lifetime. Any fix stays at
   the proven publication boundary; it cannot revive offline viewing.

4. **High — scale requirements were misidentified.** The frozen brief's
   named probe has 500 follows and 100 conversations; the generator also
   gives `u0001` 100 groups plus 900 DMs. A 1,000-follow query, 50-row
   composite or 100-row job chunk is not itself mandated by the brief.
   **Applied:** audit named fixtures and preserve their populations. No
   benchmark reduction is selected to accommodate implementation limits.

5. **High — smaller lane bounds lose data.** A first-100 membership query
   cannot answer a 1,000-chat search. A purported page/head-merge recipe
   needs legal syntax, cursor ownership, full reachability, ordering and
   revocation behavior. Static refusal sizes are schema-dependent; forcing
   larger cases to refuse would block a valid future optimization.
   **Applied:** accept only compiling, executed complete forms; test a match
   in the final slice and live/offline boundaries; preserve honest refusal
   without requiring specific useful programs to remain inadmissible.

6. **High — keepalive was treated as durability.** The write plan mixed
   route unmount and document unload, expected rejection to imply no
   durable row, and suggested keepalive could protect async admission.
   Durable mutations already enqueue before replay. After owner commit a
   lost response is an unknown transport outcome, not proof of no effect.
   **Applied:** define before/after-admission and after-owner-commit cuts,
   preserve result states and same-intent recovery, and keep transport
   optimization optional. The revised child cites Fetch/IndexedDB standards.

7. **Medium — qualitative success and experimental evidence were mixed.**
   “Same diaries,” independently green M1–M8 and an 82-point ceiling are
   stronger than the retained records establish. The later runs disclose
   shared load and cross-lane traffic; unlike isolated replications, their
   agreement cannot settle causal attribution.
   **Applied:** separate reports, source reproductions, graded outcomes and
   published qualification. Scores are secondary to semantic acceptance.

8. **High — current costs change what is feasible.** The analysis still
   targeted 30 seconds and treated multi-page transport as unselected.
   Bounded batching is implemented; the objective is now 2–3 seconds, with
   a 28.509-second final smoke and an unqualified multi-run cohort. The
   latest runtime is 89,999 bytes against 90,000.
   **Applied:** update G2/G3/G8/G10, preserve failed controls and first-view
   regression, require measured deletion to fund additions, and retain
   acquisition as its own substantial open problem.

9. **Medium — process criteria needed ownership and scope.** Generic lanes,
   “well under 15 seconds,” mandatory full browser landing gates and a
   Snapback 1 LLP 0189 citation did not establish an executable Snapback 2
   sequence. **Applied:** name proposed owner/date, deterministic oracles,
   explicit proposed timing targets, three-round stops, integration and
   existing scheduled qualification. These Drafts create no extra gate.

## Suggestions retained as conditional work

Try the current one-client freshness operations before designing a refresh
surface. Prefer a sound single bounded query before adding composition, but
judge a multi-page or job form by complete product semantics. Investigate
admission, replay and observation clocks separately so a UI delay is not
repaired by weakening durability. Keep small starter fixes independent of
these mechanism decisions.

## Open questions and next step

The exact timed Insights, online once publication and original offline-vote
replay still need targeted evidence. The 1,000-chat compiling form remains
to be demonstrated. A new refresh/admission API is conditional on a consumer
case the existing surface cannot express; it is not approved here.

Recommended next step: revise and remain Draft. The authorized revision is
applied to all five documents; the author can now assess the corrected
program. This review did not run new product tests or benchmark experiments.
