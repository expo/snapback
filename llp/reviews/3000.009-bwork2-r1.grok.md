## Received review

**VERDICT: NOT READY**

The slice matches the stated kernel, HTTP and authority shape: effect rows
reuse the shared step policy; new admissions bind the generic validator tag,
name, payload and full `WorkSpec`; NULL contracts remain the legacy bypass;
release is not the compatibility fence; and strict claim, settle and step
sample after `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`. The material issue is that generation parking
rewrites terminal outbox rows.

`park_generation` updated `_outbox` by id with no live-state predicate, and
settlement plus every step verb called it before the lease/state check.

Failing scenario:

1. A fenced worker claims `mail`, performs provider I/O and commits
   `POST /effects/<id>/done`; the HTTP response is lost.
2. The app adopts a changed declaration, such as `retry 2` to `retry 3`.
3. The at-least-once worker retries `POST /effects/<id>/done`.
4. `contract_stands` rewrites the successful `sent` row to `parked`, replaces
   its error and settlement evidence, and clears its authority fields.
5. An operator can mistake the overwritten evidence for work that needs a new
   intent and duplicate the external effect.

The initial test covered running rows with stale leases, not terminal rows.
The requested fix was to park only queued/running work and preserve sent,
satisfied, failed and cancelled evidence.

Advisories, not repair blockers for this slice, covered the legacy maintenance
expiry path, encoded cancel ids, pre-transaction step input validation,
presence-row cleanup, lease-header whitespace and inherited lease-boundary
equality. Typed context, status and PostgreSQL were correctly absent.
