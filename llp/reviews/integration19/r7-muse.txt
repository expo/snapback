Scalar-only outputs mean no `_dormant_refs` handling is needed. Absence vs `Null` vs default stays distinct, per §19.1.

Identical newer images can therefore get divergent old views (synthesized vs as-is) depending on dormancy at declaration time. Reachable but narrow; fail direction varies with the synthesis.
