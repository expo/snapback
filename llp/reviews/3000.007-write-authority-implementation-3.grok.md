## Overall assessment

The repair is the third-pass table, not a new contract. A uniformly decided page takes its verdict from the pinned prefix before any candidate is examined; a false verdict returns the empty complete page at no charge, even after 8,192 examined rows or probes. A rule probe is one unit across expired siblings, with extra equalities applied in memory after that one lookup. Target resolution checks a static worst case (rule-probe count from the rule trees, heap from the schema ceiling plus the supplied patch) before the lookup, so hidden-refused and absent either refuse `E_BOUND` together or do not abort in resolution. Aggregate overflow names the maintain and `value`, never a total. An explicit asset id on an unreadable target is validated as the writer’s upload even when it equals the retained placement. `Expr::Merge` is refused from structure, not `merge_site`. Every mutation page is a plain non-expiring prefix, predicting or not, looped or bound. The binder refuses `upsert … if` with one diagnostic. 17 (a) ignores immutable columns on an unreadable `update`/`upsert` hit behind `ignore_hidden_immutable_patch`; a readable mismatch still names the column. Historical receipts re-sanitize `why.message` on the way out. The shared helper carries the remaining paired cases, with a revocation step where the case is revocation.

This family’s third-pass “what would resolve it” list is met. Item 1 (binder-refuse immutables on upsert) was superseded by ruling 17 (a)’s note under §14; the ignoring form is what landed, and it does not reopen `NOT_FOUND` versus `sent` on upsert.

### 2. Channels the repairs could have opened

**Does the reservation refuse in a way that depends on hidden state?** No. `reserve_target_resolution` reads the schema, the caller’s patch, `upsert`, and `meter.used` from prior work in the same mutation (`write_target.rs` 18–28). It does not look up the target. `room` is `used + amount <= ceiling` (`meter.rs` 152–164). The 16-pair fixture warms the same prelude in both worlds, then both refuse `E_BOUND` with equal complete observations. If the reservation does not fit, the lookup never runs; if it fits, `lookup_live(..., false)` / `fetch` cannot abort on examined siblings. Constraint work after an admitted write remains the §15 residual.

**Does the pinned-prefix verdict evaluate anything that differs between an empty and a populated hidden prefix?** No. `probe_row` is built only from index components zipped with the prefix (`read.rs` 470). `scan_visibility` is uniform only when every deciding field is pinned (`rules.rs` 48–51), so the verdict cannot depend on an unpinned column of a candidate. False returns before `tx.scan` of the page (476–478). `exists` probes are point lookups of other keys (or a self-witness of a specific key), not a walk of the page’s hidden population. Empty vs 1 vs 10,000 hidden rows in that prefix therefore share the complete observation (`page_admission.rs` 73–85). A false exists-probe is not charged (478 vs 480), which is why a meter already at 8,192 no longer aborts on the first hidden candidate.

**Does ignoring immutable columns on an unreadable target change any committed value, maintain, or event that a writer could later read?** Not by ignoring those columns. The guard removes them from the patch without comparison (`write_target.rs` 110–118), so merge keeps `old` (`write_target.rs` 32–40). `w1_an_immutable_patch_on_a_hidden_row_ignores_matching_and_mismatching_guesses` (183–196) asserts `sent`, `result: null`, Alice still has the original `frozen`, and `changes == {}` for both guesses. Mutable fields still apply under the update rule; that is the granted write. A later read, or a maintain the writer can already read, can see those mutable deltas (`N_MAINTAIN_DELTA`). The ignored immutables do not move. The writer’s stream still does not gain a row they cannot read (`write_authority.rs` 233–248). Profile upsert’s readable `result` is from `owner: viewer` being mutable, which the test names as a grant, not from `frozen`.

**Does the merge of `rule_probe_exists` with the equality filter keep a rule probe at one unit and keep the equalities from disclosing through cost?** Yes. Lane V’s remaining equalities are applied after the single `lookup_live` (`read.rs` 87–88). `PrefixProbe` counts one `exists` call (764–767); `Runner::exists` charges one unit before the lookup (39–45). Extra equalities do not take a second index and do not add examined or probe units. `membership.rs` 198–206 keeps `rule_probes == 1` with `role = .role` on the total unique key. Match vs mismatch of those equalities is the same one unit; the boolean is the rule’s predicate, not a cost oracle.

Unmetered sibling walks (`lookup_live` with `charge: false`, and the live-maintain source scan at `read.rs` 62–84) are the cost closure the row asked for. Physical time to walk 9,000 expired siblings remains the timing residual.
