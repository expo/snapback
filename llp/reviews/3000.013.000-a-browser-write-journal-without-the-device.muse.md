7. MINOR (§1, §4.4). `start()` "replays in recorded order" overstates the existing concurrent `Promise.all`; strict seq-order recovery must be sequential. Clarify which the RFC requires.

8. MINOR (§5 residual). A server backup that lost receipts re-executes unless the head regresses below the dispatch floor ([client.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-ws-jr/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/client.ts:472)); calling it "the existing `E_STORE_RESTORE` case" is glib.
