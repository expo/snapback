## Required behavior

P1: expiration is a server-authored delete trigger. Every scheduled sweep, born-expired row and same-id expired replacement applies declared cascade, set-null and restrict actions atomically. A logically expired root is unreadable and is absent from update/delete target lookup even while physically retained. Future-but-unexpired write targets remain addressable. Expected restrict or physical read-through blockers are preflighted before closure writes. A read-through child outside a cascade closure may retain it; a read-through child inside the closure is deleted first and is not a blocker. Repeated bounded calls, including `limit = 1`, advance across rows and tables; continuation publishes only after commit. Unexpected late errors roll the transaction back. Multiple set-null clears accumulate from current images.

P2: maintained aggregate ids are lowercase 64-character SHA-256 hex over a versioned, domain-separated canonical logical group tuple at every check/read/write/rebuild site. Empty logical tuple `[]` is the identity input for ungrouped views while compiler-owned `$global = true` is only the physical discriminator. Existing SQLite and evaluation-PostgreSQL stores without the marker atomically rebuild ordinary aggregate targets, publish the marker and advance semantic generation under their writer transaction. A real store written by frozen 0.4.28 reopens with the same value under its digest id. Schemas require `maintained-id-sha256-v1`; an old device refuses before silently reading a legacy address. Legacy corpus adapter seeds and canonical expected IDs cross this representation seam explicitly without changing logical expected values.

Lean evidence must state logical lifetime, sweep action/progress, fixed aggregate-id bound and atomic adoption invariants without claiming to prove SHA-256. Its physical write model must refuse expired update/delete targets, treat expired upsert targets as absent, and model expired same-id retirement as an explicit referential-action transition before insertion/secondary uniqueness. Mutation authority remains a proved `Steps` chain. The differential generator must execute supported expiry cases; maintained-view evaluation stays explicitly outside the full Lean oracle and gets a separate generated invariant.
