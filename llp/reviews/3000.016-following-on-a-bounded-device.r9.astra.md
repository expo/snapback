2. **High — §2.1a: replacing a source image can lose the association needed to remove its previous event.**

   Repost IDs can be deleted and reused with different mapped fields. The [immutability check applies to updates](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-worktrees/review-3000.014-r2/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/write.rs:415), not subsequent insertions under the same ID.

   **Concrete probe:**

   - Pin snapshot watermark W.
   - Commit A inserts repost R for post P, creating event E. A later snapshot page stages R/P and E.

Capture that association before overwriting the source and retire its old event atomically.
