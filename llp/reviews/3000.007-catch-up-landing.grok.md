## Findings

### H1 — High — non-uniform matching lane expands hidden words and can starve later lanes

**Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:325` (sets `non_uniform`, does not return), `350–376` (word discovery), `663–684` (outer merge, no stop on `capped_at`); contrast uniform hoist at `330–346`.

**Scenario.**

```quarry
table messages:
  roomId: id
  authorId: principal
  body: text <=4000
  by byRoomWords: roomId, words(body), at, id
  read <- exists members[.roomId, viewer] and .authorId = viewer
query everywhere(q: text <=100, c: cursor ?):
  return merge messages matching q after c last 50 by byRoomWords:
    [m.roomId] for m in members[viewer] first 50 by byPrincipalRoom
```

Worlds share membership (alice in `r1` then `r2`) and alice’s own messages in `r2` (“coffee”). They differ only in **other people’s** `cof*` messages in `r1` (hidden by `.authorId = viewer`).

- World A: `r1` has no matching hidden rows. Page includes `r2`’s “coffee”.
- World B: `r1` has 32+ distinct `cof*` words and enough hidden rows to exhaust the 8,192 examined ceiling (non-uniform batch is at least 32, `read.rs:516`).

Observed: World B’s `r1` lane charges index entries and examined rows for hidden words (`350–376`, `524–527`). Once `capped_at` is set, later `r2` word discovery/`scan_prepared` charges fail immediately → **empty or shorter `data`**, different `next`. HTTP sends `data`/`complete`/`next` (`serve.rs:865`). `complete` is already false because `non_uniform`, but **rows on the wire** differ.

Required (§18.1 + Q2): a lane over a scope (or rows) the viewer cannot read contributes nothing; meter and completeness must not depend on hidden words, in `Cut::Cap`.

**Smallest fix:** Before word discovery, evaluate the **pinned-prefix** projection of the rule (the `exists members[.roomId, viewer]` conjunct is decided by `roomId`). If that projection is false, empty the lane at the uniform denied cost and do not discover words. If it is true and extra conjuncts remain, keep per-row checks **only on that admitted scope**, and do not let a hidden-word 32-cap/ceiling cut later lanes. Add a paired-world test: extra conjunct + 33 hidden words in room 1 vs 0, same visible room 2, compare `data`, `complete`, `next`, `capped_at`, every meter field, Cap and (if reachable) Abort.

The uniform hoist already does the right thing for Clique’s exact `exists members[.roomId, viewer]` rule (`search_merge.rs:68–72`).
