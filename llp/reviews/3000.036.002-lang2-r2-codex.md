Nested predicate authorization can therefore bypass the shared physical allowance without belonging to the draw. **Required correction:** explicitly restrict this fragment to total, row-read-free predicates and enforce that in core admission, or suspend prepayment around predicate reads so they consume their own shared allowance and preserve incomplete-candidate retry semantics.
