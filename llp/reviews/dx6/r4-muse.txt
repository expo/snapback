## Newest repair (LLP 3000.013 §6/§12.7 cutoff) — sound

- Scope-owned capture lease: [app.ts:363](/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/app.ts) (`cleanupScope`) acquires `holdClose(scope.client)` only when a `Client` exists, refcounts reentrant credential work (`depth`), and releases at zero. The `finally` awaits only `scope.logout` (a `removeSession` storage barrier) and the `persist` chain — both local storage-locked work (`app.ts` `removeSession`/`persist`), never auth, network, or the revocation queue (`revokeRetired` is fire-and-forget). This matches the proposal's "without waiting for auth/network/revocation queues."
- No wait cycle: `client.close()` resolves its `closing` promise after calling `finish()` without awaiting `done`, so `cleanupScope(scope, () => raw.close())` cannot deadlock against `closingResponses.done`. The local-mutate `await closingResponses.done` path ([local.ts:1311](/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/local.ts)) waits on inner settlement resolved by `resolveClosingWrites` during close, not on the caller's own promise in `active`. The online `act()` recovery callback is not a member of the `requests` set awaited by `close()`.
- No authority adoption: `onParsed` capture fires synchronously inside `readResponse` before caller continuations, gated by `phase !== "complete"` and `status < 500` ([closing-responses.ts](/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/closing-responses.ts)). 5xx envelopes and invalid bodies are consumed for validation but never captured (`http-response.ts` returns the denied envelope without calling `onParsed`). `begin()` is `open→closing` only, post-`complete` `hold()` is a no-op, and `complete()` runs once — a completed capture cannot reopen.
- No lost known outcomes: closed online `act()` recovers via `await done` then `response.take()`; closed online mutate maps pending outcomes through `entry.response.write(outcome)`; closed local mutate awaits `done` then `response.write(await result)`. Unknown/never-captured outcomes stay `pending`, never upgraded.
- Early local post-admission close uses the existing arbitration (`cleanupScope` around `raw.close()`/`raw.signOut()`, [app.ts:704](/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/app.ts)) and preserves the settlement entry; no outcome rewrite on that path.
