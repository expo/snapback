These failures accumulate against the viewer’s 128 MiB pinned-byte allowance indefinitely. Valid admission alone cannot eliminate the problem: a schema change between readiness and placement can introduce a terminal refusal.

That rule must preserve same-ID replay and cancellation ordering. This is a design dependency on unsupported ordinary-receipt behavior, rather than merely an unfinished test.
