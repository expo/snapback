### 1. Product contract

Matchlock runs competitive events (esports and in-person) from registration through payout. An **entry** is the competitor unit: a solo player or a team. Matches occupy entries, not raw user accounts.

**Actors.** Organizer (TO) on an organization; co-TOs granted per tournament; captains and members of entries; sponsors; spectators (logged-out allowed); a platform operator only for chargebacks and frozen payouts.

**Formats in ordinary use (not extras).** Single elimination, double elimination with optional grand-final reset, round robin, Swiss. A tournament is a sequence of **phases**. The common paid event is round-robin pools → cutoff → double-elim playoff. Swiss is used for 64–2048 entries where a full elim DAG is the wrong shape.

**The 16 promises**

1. A public tournament page shows name, game, rules, schedule, format, cap, check-in window, prize-pool figure, and (after the TO’s publish flags) the registrant list.
2. A user registers as a solo entry, or a captain registers a team entry with a roster and up to two named substitutes, until registration close or cap.
3. Check-in is a distinct act from registration, open only inside `check_in_opens_at` … `check_in_closes_at`. Missed check-in means the entry is not in the phase.
4. After check-in closes, the TO generates the current phase. Generation is all-or-nothing. Spectators then see a live bracket (or schedule, or Swiss round list) with placeholders (`Winner of WB-5`, `Seed 4`) until occupants are known.
5. Seeding is a snapshot taken at generation: manual order, rating-at-lock, or random with a stored seed. Re-seed means regenerate the whole phase, and only if no match in that phase has a locked occupant.
6. Byes exist as real slot occupants. A player-vs-BYE match auto-advances in the same transaction as generation (elim) or pairing (Swiss / odd round robin).
7. In elim phases, a match’s two players are whoever the DAG says they are: seeds, winners, losers, or the grand-final reset condition — not a TO typing two names.
8. Each match is best-of-N (`wins_needed`). **Both** entries must report the same score to confirm. Disagreeing reports open a dispute. A single report never crowns a winner.
9. After confirmation, the winner (and, in double elim, the loser) fill downstream slots immediately. Spectators update without refresh beyond the live channel. Unconfirmed reports are invisible to spectators.
10. A TO may force-confirm, forfeit, vacate, or set an official score. Force-confirm is auditable and names the losing consent (deadline, no-show, evidence).
11. Swiss rounds are **not** pre-generated. The TO (or a scheduled round timer) pairs the next round from current standings. Rematches are avoided; a bye goes to the lowest-ranked entry that has not yet had one.
12. Anyone can contribute cash to the prize pool during the open window. A sponsor can pledge “match *R*% of **net** community contributions, up to $X.” The page shows community net, matched, organizer-added, and total.
13. Payouts run only after placements are final. Money goes to each placing entry’s payout recipient (default: captain). The split among teammates is outside the product.
14. A captain may substitute a named substitute onto the active roster only while the phase’s roster is unlocked, and never so that one user is active on two entries in the same tournament.
15. Phases advance by cutoff: top *K* of each pool (or top *K* Swiss) become the seeded field of the next phase, generated atomically from standings.
16. A spectator opening the page on a phone, and a TO on a laptop, see the same confirmed graph within one second of commit under ordinary network conditions.

**Concurrency.** Last-write-wins is forbidden for scores, seeds, and money. Check-in is idempotent. Bracket generation, Swiss pairing, cutoff, pool lock, and payout are compare-and-set on a phase/tournament version. Two TOs cannot generate twice.

**Offline.** LAN wifi dies. A player may queue **one** report per match on-device and flush it later; the server treats it as that reporter’s current report, not as confirmation. A TO must not generate, pair, resolve disputes, or payout while offline; those actions refuse if the replica is stale. Contributions never credit from a client ack — only from the payment processor webhook.

**Multi-device.** The same user on two devices is one reporter. Two in-flight reports from that user collapse to one current report keyed by `(match_id, reporter_entry_id)`. Devices that showed “waiting for opponent” reconcile to confirmed, disputed, or still-waiting from the server. Bracket pixels are not a local merge; they are a projection of server-confirmed slots.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

Marking: **[MONEY]**, **[SECRET]**, **[TIME]**, **[OTHER]** (other people’s identity, roster, or competitive fate).

1. An entry plays in a phase only if it was **checked in at the moment that phase was generated** (or, for Swiss round *r*>1, still active and not disqualified). **[TIME]**
2. Registration without check-in is not a seed, not a match occupant, and not a placement. **[TIME]**
3. Check-in after `check_in_state = closed` is refused even if a client clock says the window is open. The durable state flag, not the wall clock, is authority. **[TIME]**
4. A phase has at most one **active** generation. Generating replaces nothing in place; it inserts a new generation and retires the previous only when the previous has zero locked matches. **[OTHER]**
5. The elim graph for *n* checked-in entries is the unique DAG produced by `generate(format, next_pow2(n), seed_order)` — including byes for missing seeds. Two runs with the same spec bytes produce identical match codes and slot pointers.
6. Every elim match has exactly two slots. A slot’s occupant, when known, is a function of its source (seed map / winner of *M* / loser of *M* / BYE / conditional reset), not an independent fact.
7. `slot.occupant_entry_id` is a cache of that function. If it disagrees with the function, the cache is wrong and must not be shown. **[OTHER]**
8. A BYE vs a real entry confirms as a walkover for the real entry in the same transaction that first makes that occupancy true (generation or cascade).
9. No round-1 elim match is BYE vs BYE.
10. A match does not become `confirmed` because one entry reported. Confirmation requires two agreeing current reports, or a TO official result, or a walkover/forfeit rule firing. **[OTHER]**
11. Spectators learn scores and occupants only from confirmed, admin-resolved, forfeit, or walkover results — never from a lone report. **[SECRET]**
12. An entry may see its own current report immediately; it may see the opponent’s report only after it has submitted one, or after `report_due_at`. **[SECRET]**
13. Disagreement of the two current reports moves the match to `disputed` and **does not fill downstream slots**. **[OTHER]**
14. When a result is applied, every downstream slot that **has not been occupancy-locked** is rewritten from the function in (6) in the same transaction. **[OTHER]**
15. Occupancy lock is irreversible by cascade: once a match is occupancy-locked, an upstream winner change does not silently replace who plays it. The TO must explicitly **vacate the subtree** or **deny cascade** (standings/prize note only). **[TIME][OTHER]**
16. “Who was announced as playing match *M*” and “who the live DAG now says should play *M*” and “who actually reported in *M*” are three facts and may differ; the product stores all three. **[TIME][OTHER]**
17. Swiss matches for round *r* do not exist before `PairSwissRound(r)` commits. There is no hidden full schedule. **[TIME]**
18. Swiss pairing for round *r* reads only **official** prior results (confirmed / admin-resolved / forfeit / walkover). An open dispute in round *r−1* blocks pairing unless the TO marks a temporary official score. **[OTHER]**
19. An entry’s Swiss standing is computed over the unfiltered official results of that phase, not over the rows the caller happens to be allowed to see. **[SECRET][OTHER]**
20. A user is active on at most one entry in a tournament at any timestamp. **[TIME][OTHER]**
21. A user may play match *M* for an entry only if they have an `entry_members` interval covering `M.occupants_locked_at` with role `starter` or `playing_sub`. **[TIME][OTHER]**
22. **[MONEY]** Prize-pool **display total** before lock is the live formula: organizer guaranteed + sum of **net** non-void contributions in window + sum over sponsors of `min(cap, ratio × eligible community net)`.
23. **[MONEY][TIME]** After `pool_state = locked`, payout math uses `pool_snapshots` for that lock, not the live formula. Later contributions are refused (or, if the TO opted in, routed to `overflow_for_tournament_id` and do not change this event’s snapshot).
24. **[MONEY]** A contribution is never updated in place. Voids and chargebacks are new reversing rows. Gross, processor fee, and net are stored on the original row.
25. **[MONEY][OTHER]** Payouts do not run while any match in a prize-bearing phase is non-terminal, any dispute is open, or any protest window is still open, unless the TO records `force_finalize_reason`.
26. **[MONEY]** Placement is a function of the final official graph (elim loss round / RR–Swiss order with published tiebreaks), applied to the locked snapshot, with remainder cents given one-at-a-time to the best placement.
27. **[MONEY]** Paying a placement twice is refused. The payout ledger is append-only. A corrected placement after payout requires an explicit clawback row and a new pay row, not an edit.
28. **[SECRET]** Hidden seeds (`seeds_public = false`) do not leak through match labels, check-in counts, or “who got a bye” before generation publishes.
29. **[OTHER]** Cutoff writes no playoff occupants except the entries the published ranking function returns from official pool/Swiss results.
30. Every official result change, roster change, generation, pairing, pool lock, dispute decision, and payout writes an audit row with actor, at, and before/after hashes.

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1 — Three occupants (the membership-at-a-time trap)**

*Wrong version.* `matches.player_a_id`, `player_b_id`, `winner_id`. On confirm, copy `winner_id` into the next match’s player column. Double elim also copies the loser into some `loser_next` id.

*Why it is wrong.* A slot is a **pointer** (winner of WB-5, loser of WB-5, seed 12, BYE, “GF2 if LB champion won GF1”). Copying a player id throws away the pointer. After a dispute reverses WB-5, the losers-bracket match that already used that loser still names the old person, and the new loser is nowhere. Grand-final reset cannot even be expressed: GF2 is not “always the two GF1 players”; it **exists** only under a condition. Swiss makes the columns a lie in the other direction: there is no “next match” to copy into.

*Observation.* 8-man double elim. WB-5 is confirmed A over B; B drops to LB-3 and **plays and wins** while A’s opponent in WB-final has not played. Dispute flips WB-5 to B. Shallow copy either (a) leaves B in two places and A in none, or (b) overwrites LB-3’s history so the match B already won now lists A, who never played it. Correct behavior: LB-3 has `locked_occupant = B`, `played_as = B`, `derived_occupant = A` (dirty). The TO must vacate LB-3’s subtree or refuse the cascade. One column cannot hold that.

**Trap 2 — First report wins, and cascade races**

*Wrong version.* Either entry posts a score; that score is the result; downstream fills.

*Why it is wrong.* Promise 8 is two-party consensus. First-write-wins makes a misclick or a liar fill the DAG. Concurrent opposite reports without a serial lock on the match produce two “confirmed” winners. A downstream match that went `ready` between the two commits inherits a slot that flips, violating occupancy lock (invariant 15).

*Observation.* A reports 2–1, B reports 1–2 in the same 50ms. Spectators must not see a winner. Both current reports exist. Match is `disputed`. Downstream slots stay empty. A third report from A “editing” to 2–1 is not a new match; it is still A’s current report.

**Trap 3 — Checked-in *now* versus checked-in *at generation***

*Wrong version.* `SELECT * FROM entries WHERE tournament_id = ? AND checked_in = true` at whatever moment the TO’s client runs generate, and allow uncheck after the bracket exists.

*Why it is wrong.* Check-in is a **time-bounded fact**. The field is the set at the close fence. A 17th entry checking in during generate, or unchecking after seeing a bad seed, must not change a committed DAG. The shallow boolean cannot answer “were they in?” tomorrow, after they leave the venue.

*Observation.* 16 checked-in, TO starts generate (creates 15 matches). Entry 17’s check-in lands. If generate was not fenced on `check_in_state = closed` and a generation id, you get 16 matches for 17 people, or a 16-man bracket that omits someone who “is checked in.” Next day, `checked_in = false` for a no-show who **did** play round 1: historical occupancy is gone. The generation’s `entry_snapshot` is the fact.

**Trap 4 — Swiss as a prebuilt bracket, and standings as a visible-row count**

*Wrong version.* Build all rounds at start (random or “estimated”), or pair round 3 while round 2 still has open disputes, or compute “my Buchholz” from the matches the caller can read.

*Why it is wrong.* Swiss pairing is an algorithm over **official** results of rounds `1..r-1`. Prebuilt matches are not Swiss (they ignore results and rematch constraints). Pairing through a dispute bakes a contested winner into the next matching. Buchholz is a **structural** sum over opponents’ match points. If the read is visibility-filtered, a player who cannot see others’ matches gets Buchholz 0 and a wrong pairing and a wrong public table.

*Observation.* Four entries, all 1–1 going into round 3. A vs B already played. Adjacent pairing would rematch them; the algorithm must swap. Entry C’s disputed round-2 match is still open: PairSwissRound must refuse. Public standings show C as 1–1 or 2–0 depending on a ghost result — both are lies. The TO’s override that sets a temporary official score is a written act, not a UI filter.

**Trap 5 — Prize pool as a number the TO types**

*Wrong version.* `tournaments.prize_cents` incremented on donate; sponsor match is a second number; payout uses whatever is in the field on Sunday; a refund `UPDATE`s the donation row.

*Why it is wrong.* The live total is a **formula** over append-only contributions and sponsor rules. Matching is `min(cap, ratio × net community in window)`, not a stored integer the TO can drift from the ledger. Refunds and chargebacks are reversing rows. Payouts use a **lock snapshot**. A dispute that swaps 1st and 2nd after 1st was paid is a clawback, not an edit of a “paid” flag.

*Observation.* Community net $400; sponsor “100% up to $500” → match $400, total $800 + guarantee. A $50 chargeback after lock: live formula would show $700 matched-down; snapshot is still $800; payout must block on `shortfall_cents` until the organizer makes whole or re-snapshots with a recorded consent. If 1st was already paid 50% of $800, you cannot silently pay the new 1st another 50%.

**Trap 6 — Roster as a current list; captain as whoever clicks**

*Wrong version.* `team.member_ids[]`. Any member reports. A sub added Friday is eligible for Thursday’s match because they are on the list now. One Discord account on two teams is caught by eyeball.

*Why it is wrong.* Eligibility is **interval membership covering occupancy lock**. Reporting authority is captain or `caller` at report time. A ringer added after lock played under a list that did not contain them. Two entries sharing a user is a uniqueness invariant over intervals, not over the current array.

*Observation.* Match locked 18:00 with roster {Ada, Beau, Cora}. At 18:10 captain swaps Beau for sub Dee. Dee’s report is accepted by a naive app. Ada’s second team, which lost at 17:00, still lists Ada as current because nobody set `valid_to`. Standings and payouts then attach to the wrong body of humans. The interval `[valid_from, valid_to)` relative to `occupants_locked_at` is the fact.

**Offline merge (cross-cutting).** Two devices, same captain, report 2–1 and 2–0 while offline; opponent reports 2–1 online. Merge is not CRDT-average and not last-timestamp-wins. Server: one current report per entry; the first that arrives is current; the second is a replacement **only if** the match is still `awaiting_counter_report` and the opponent has no current report; otherwise the replacement is an edit that can **open** a dispute if it breaks agreement. Money and DAG generation never merge offline.

**Derived (not stored, or stored only as cache with a version):** live pool formula; Buchholz; elim placement from loss-round; slot labels (`Winner of WB-5`); public checked-in count = `entries` in the **snapshot** once generated, else `status = checked_in`. GF-2 `state = not_required` when GF-1 official winner came from winners side.

**Append-only / versioned / time-bounded.** Append-only: reports, check_in_events, contributions, reversals, pool_snapshots, payout_ledger, audit_events, dispute_resolutions, generations (as rows), standings_snapshots. Versioned: `entry_members` intervals, `tournaments.version`, `matches.result_version`, `phases.active_generation_id`. Time-bounded: check-in window, member intervals, sponsor eligible window, protest window, report window, seed list (life of generation), pool snapshot.

**Relationships (graph).** `match_slots.source_match_id → matches.id` (the DAG edges). Winners-bracket match fans out to at most two slots (next WB winner slot, LB drop slot). GF-1 winner/loser feed GF-2 only if `conditional` fires. RR/Swiss slots have `source_type` assigned, no `source_match_id`.

**Elim construction (frozen).**

*Seed list of size `P = 2^k`:* start `[1]`; while length `< P`, replace the list `L` with the concatenation of `(s, P_next+1-s)` for each `s` in `L` where `P_next = 2×|L|`. Pair adjacent entries as round-1 matches, codes `WB-1..WB-P/2`. Higher WB rounds: winner-of pair `(2i-1, 2i)`. Positions `> n_checked_in` are BYE.

*Double elim, `P` players after byes.* WB: `P-1` matches, `k` rounds. LB: `P-2` matches, `2(k-1)` rounds, alternating drop rounds and minor rounds. WB round-1 losers fill LB round 1 in **reverse bracket order**. WB round `r≥2` losers fill the drop round `2(r-1)` in reverse bracket order against LB survivors. GF-1: WB champion vs LB champion. If `grand_finals = reset`, GF-2 exists with `conditional = gf2_if_lb_won_gf1`, slots `winner_of GF-1` and `loser_of GF-1`.

*Round robin (circle).* If `n` odd, a BYE entry is added for scheduling only. `n-1` rounds (after padding), each entry plays each other once. Slot sources `rr_assigned`.

### 5. The hard operations

Notation: `CAS(row, version)` means read version, write `version+1` or abort `conflict`.

#### 5.1 RegisterEntry

**Inputs.** `tournament_id`, `display_name`, for team: `member_user_ids[]`, `named_sub_user_ids[]`.  
**Auth.** Signed-in user. Solo: caller becomes captain+sole starter. Team: caller becomes captain; members must exist; sizes within min/max.  
**Steps.** (1) Load tournament; refuse if now outside registration window or `status` not `open`/`check_in`. (2) Count entries with status not `withdrawn`; refuse if `>= cap`. (3) Refuse if caller already captains or is an overlapping member of another entry. (4) Insert `entries`, `entry_members` intervals with `valid_from = now`, `valid_to = null`. (5) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 2–4.  
**Eventual.** Public registrant list fan-out.  
**Optimistic UI.** “Registered” with rollback on cap/conflict.  
**Conflict.** Unique member interval or cap: `registration_rejected`. Two devices: one entry, second call is idempotent if same captain.

#### 5.2 CheckIn / UndoCheckIn

**Inputs.** `entry_id`, `action` `in|out`, optional `client_scan_id`.  
**Auth.** Captain or any starter of the entry, or a TO.  
**Steps.** (1) Load tournament; require `check_in_state = open` **and** `now ∈ [opens, closes]` (both). (2) Require entry `registered` or `checked_in`. (3) Insert `check_in_events`. (4) Set `entries.status` to `checked_in` or `registered`, `checked_in_at` set/cleared. Undo refused if it would drop below `min_roster` who have accepted — N/A for solo. (5) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 3–4.  
**Eventual.** Public check-in count if `show_checkins_public`.  
**Optimistic UI.** Toggle immediately.  
**Conflict.** Close job vs check-in: state flag wins, check-in refused. Double in: second event stored, status remains `checked_in` (idempotent).

#### 5.3 CloseCheckInWindow *(scheduled + TO)*

**Inputs.** `tournament_id`, `actor` `system|to_user_id`.  
**Auth.** System at `check_in_closes_at`, or TO early.  
**Steps.** (1) `CAS(tournament.version)`. (2) If already `closed`, return. (3) Set `check_in_state = closed`, `status = live` if `starts_at` reached else `check_in` stays until generate. (4) Entries still `registered` → `no_show` (they remain historical registrations). (5) Enqueue `LockPrizePool` if `prize_lock_at` is `on_check_in_close`. (6) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 1–4.  
**Eventual.** Push “check-in closed, N in” to TO and captains.  
**Optimistic UI.** None for system; TO sees spinner.  
**Conflict.** Check-in in flight: serialization on tournament row; check-in retries, sees closed, fails.

#### 5.4 GeneratePhase *(bulk, atomic DAG)*

**Inputs.** `phase_id`, `seed_method`, optional `manual_order: entry_id[]`.  
**Auth.** TO of the tournament.  
**Steps.** (1) Lock tournament and phase (`CAS` both versions / row locks). (2) Require check-in closed. (3) Require phase `pending` or previous generation `retired` with zero `occupants_locked_at`. (4) Snapshot `S = entries` with `status = checked_in` (or winners of prior phase if `seq > 1` — those ids come from `placements` of the cutoff, not a live query of `checked_in`). (5) If `|S| < 2` (or `< phase.min_entries`) abort. (6) Compute `seed_order`: manual must permute `S`; rating uses `rating_at` frozen in snapshot (not live rating); random uses `random_salt = H(generation_id)`. (7) If `kind` is elim: `P = next_pow2(|S|)`; build seed positions 1..P (null entry = BYE); insert `generations` + `seeds`. (8) Insert **all** `matches` for the shape (WB, LB, GF1, GF2 if reset, or all RR matches via circle method, or **zero** Swiss matches). (9) Insert **all** `match_slots` with pointers only; `derived_entry_id` null except `source_type=seed` or `bye`. (10) Materialize round-1 (or all RR) `derived_entry_id` from seeds. (11) While a match has one real derived occupant and one BYE (or RR bye): set official result walkover, `result_version++`, `played_as`, lock occupants, recurse fill. Forbid BYE vs BYE. (12) Set matches with two real derived occupants to `ready`. (13) Point `phase.active_generation_id`, `status = generated`. (14) Spec hash; audit.  
**Atomic.** **1–13 as one transaction.** A crash leaves no generation. Intra-graph FKs: insert matches first with nil pointers, then slots, or use deferred constraints.  
**Eventual.** Spectator bracket subscription.  
**Optimistic UI.** None. Progress spinner.  
**Conflict.** Second TO: `already_generated`. Check-in close racing: must already be closed. `|S|` changed: impossible if close is fenced.

#### 5.5 ReportMatchResult

**Inputs.** `match_id`, `wins_slot_0`, `wins_slot_1`, `client_mutation_id`.  
**Auth.** A user whose `entry_members` interval covers **now** with role `captain|caller|starter` (solo) on `locked_entry_id` of one slot. Not a named_sub unless they are `playing_sub` covering lock time.  
**Steps.** (1) Lock the match row. (2) Require `state ∈ {ready, in_progress, awaiting_counter_report}` and both `locked_entry_id` set (lock occupants on first report if not yet: copy `derived → locked` if derived complete; if derived incomplete, refuse). Set `occupants_locked_at` if null. (3) Validate scores: integers ≥0, one slot reached `wins_needed`, the other `< wins_needed`. (4) Supersede the reporter’s current report if `superseded_at IS NULL`; insert new current report. If opponent has no current report, `state = awaiting_counter_report`, set `report_due_at` if null, return. (5) If opponent current report **equals** this score: `ApplyOfficialResult(match, scores, source=agreement)`. (6) Else: `state = disputed`; insert `disputes`; **do not** fill slots. Notify TO.  
**`ApplyOfficialResult` (subroutine, same transaction).** Set official scores, winner slot, `state` `confirmed` or `walkover`/`forfeit`, `result_version++`, `played_as_entry_id` on both slots from `locked_entry_id`, `protest_until = now + protest_window_sec`. For each slot in other matches with `source_match_id = this` and `source_type` winner/loser/conditional: recompute `derived_entry_id`. If that match has `occupants_locked_at` null, copy derived to display cache; if both slots derived, `state = ready`. If GF-2 condition false, set GF-2 `not_required`. If a target match is already occupancy-locked and derived ≠ locked, mark `derivation_dirty` (stored as `derived_from_result_version` mismatch) and **do not overwrite locked**. Recurse only along unlocked matches (bye auto-advance included).  
**Atomic.** Report insert + state change + `ApplyOfficialResult` cascade.  
**Eventual.** Spectator projection; opponent device.  
**Optimistic UI.** Reporter sees own score and “waiting for opponent.” Never show advancement until `confirmed`.  
**Conflict.** Opposite reports: dispute, not last-write-wins. Double submit same `client_mutation_id`: idempotent. Edit after opponent agreed: treated as new current report that **breaks** agreement → dispute and **reverse** official result **only if** no downstream occupancy lock; else dirty + TO queue (same as Trap 1).

#### 5.6 ResolveDispute

**Inputs.** `dispute_id`, `decision`, scores if `set_score`, `vacate_from_match_id` if vacate.  
**Auth.** TO.  
**Steps.** (1) Lock match + all matches in the generation (or the downstream subgraph). (2) Require dispute `open`. (3) `set_score`: `ApplyOfficialResult(..., source=admin)`; if any dirty locked downstream remains, refuse unless decision is `vacate_subtree` or `deny_cascade_keep_played`. (4) `vacate_subtree`: every downstream match with this match as ancestor: if it has a current report or official result, set `vacated`, clear official scores, increment `result_version`, clear `locked_*` and `played_as_*` except where `deny`… not this branch; recompute derived from the new official winner; re-apply bye walkovers on unlocked matches. (5) `deny_cascade_keep_played`: set official scores on **this** match for placement/prize notes; leave downstream `locked_*` and results intact; record dirty as acknowledged. (6) `forfeit_slot`: winner is the other locked entry. (7) Close dispute; resolution row; audit.  
**Atomic.** The chosen subgraph rewrite.  
**Eventual.** Spectator; affected captains.  
**Optimistic UI.** None.  
**Conflict.** A new report during resolve: match lock serializes; report after `admin_resolved` is refused. Concurrent TOs: one wins CAS.

#### 5.7 PairSwissRound *(algorithm + bulk)*

**Inputs.** `phase_id`, `round_index`, `allow_temporary_officials` bool.  
**Auth.** TO, or system at scheduled round time.  
**Steps.** (1) Lock phase. Require `kind = swiss`, generation active, `round_index` is `1 + max existing swiss round` (strict sequence). (2) If any match in prior rounds has `state ∈ {awaiting_counter_report, disputed, ready, in_progress}`: abort `prior_round_open`, unless TO passed temporary officials on those ids (each written as `admin_resolved` first). (3) Compute standings over **all** official results in this generation (unfiltered): match points (win=1, bye=1, loss=0), Buchholz = sum of opponents’ match points (bye opponent contributes 0), game-win %, opponent match-win %, then `random_salt` tiebreak. (4) If odd active entries, assign bye to the lowest-ranked with `bye_count = 0` (else lowest). (5) Sort remaining. Pair adjacent (0–1, 2–3, …). If pair is a rematch, try swap with the next unpaired entry (float down), backtracking up to 2000 attempts; if exhausted, pair with `is_rematch=true` and surface TO warning. (6) Insert `matches` (state `ready`, slots `swiss_assigned`, locked immediately to the paired entries) + `swiss_pairings` + `standings_snapshots`. (7) Bye match: walkover apply. (8) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 1–7.  
**Eventual.** Round list to players.  
**Optimistic UI.** None.  
**Conflict.** Double pair: unique `(generation_id, round_index)` / sequence check. Result arriving mid-pair: phase/match locks; pair aborts and TO retries.

#### 5.8 ContributeToPool *(external processor)*

**Inputs.** `tournament_id`, `gross_cents`, `idempotency_key`, `processor`.  
**Auth.** Any signed-in user, or anonymous with a processor customer id.  
**Steps.** (1) Refuse if `pool_state ≠ open` unless overflow policy applies. (2) Insert `contributions` `pending`. (3) Create processor PaymentIntent / order with metadata `contribution_id`; return client secret. **Webhook `payment_settled`:** (4) verify signature; (5) set `settled`, store `fee_cents`, `net_cents`; (6) if pool already locked, do not mutate snapshot; apply overflow or mark `stranded_cents` for refund job. **Webhook `chargeback`:** insert `contribution_reversals`; if locked, add `shortfall_cents` on the current snapshot, set `pool_state = shortfall`, halt payouts.  
**Atomic.** Each webhook handler vs the contribution row (unique `processor_event_id`). Live formula is a read, not a stored increment.  
**Eventual.** Public pool widget (1–2s).  
**Optimistic UI.** “Payment in progress” — **never** increment the displayed pool on client ack.  
**Conflict.** Double webhook: unique event id. Contribute vs lock: if lock commits first, contribution settles to overflow/refund, not into snapshot.

#### 5.9 LockPrizePool *(scheduled + TO)*

**Inputs.** `tournament_id`.  
**Auth.** System at `prize_lock_at`, or TO.  
**Steps.** (1) `CAS` tournament. (2) If already locked, return snapshot id. (3) Sum `net_cents` of settled contributions minus reversals with `at ≤ now`. (4) For each `active` sponsor rule, `eligible = sum of net in [eligible_from, eligible_to]`; `matched += min(cap, eligible × match_bps / 10000)` using integer floor. (5) `total = guaranteed + community_net + matched`. (6) Validate payout rules: percent_bps sum 10000, or fixed sum `≤ total`. (7) Insert `pool_snapshots`; `pool_state = locked`. (8) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 1–7.  
**Eventual.** Widget shows “Prize pool locked: $X.”  
**Optimistic UI.** None.  
**Conflict.** Late settlement: see 5.8. Two lockers: CAS.

#### 5.10 SubstituteRosterMember

**Inputs.** `entry_id`, `out_user_id`, `in_user_id` (must already be `named_sub` on this entry).  
**Auth.** Captain.  
**Steps.** (1) Refuse if any match for this entry in the **current** phase has `occupants_locked_at` and `allow_mid_match_sub = false`. If mid-match allowed, the sub applies only to matches not yet locked. (2) Refuse if `in_user_id` has an overlapping active membership on another entry in this tournament. (3) Close `out` interval (`valid_to = now`) if they were `starter`; insert `in` as `starter` or `playing_sub` with `valid_from = now`. (4) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 2–3.  
**Eventual.** Roster on the team page.  
**Optimistic UI.** Captain sees new list; rollback on ringer conflict.  
**Conflict.** Two captains (shouldn’t) or captain + TO: interval exclusion wins. Offline captain: may not apply to already-locked matches after reconnect.

#### 5.11 ApplyCutoffAndGenerateNextPhase *(bulk)*

**Inputs.** `from_phase_id`, `to_phase_id`.  
**Auth.** TO (or system if `auto_advance`).  
**Steps.** (1) Lock tournament. Require `from` complete: every match terminal (`confirmed|admin_resolved|forfeit|walkover|not_required`), no open disputes, all `protest_until` passed. (2) Snapshot standings **per group** with published keys: RR/Swiss rank; ties broken by head-to-head then game-win % then `random_salt`; if still tied **inside cutoff boundary**, abort `needs_tiebreak_match` (TO generates a one-match mini phase — same GeneratePhase path, `n_bracket=2`). (3) Take `cutoff_take_per_group` from each group. Cross-seed into `to` spec (standard: A1 vs D2, B1 vs C2, … stored in `group_assignments`). (4) Write `placements` for eliminated entries (ranks after the cutoff field, using group records). (5) Run GeneratePhase (5.4) for `to` using this frozen list as `S`, **in the same transaction**.  
**Atomic.** 1–5.  
**Eventual.** Playoff bracket appears.  
**Optimistic UI.** None.  
**Conflict.** Late dispute: step 1 fails. Vacate after cutoff is Trap 1 across phases: playoff occupancy lock blocks silent rewrite.

#### 5.12 GetBracketProjection *(read)*

**Inputs.** `phase_id`, `as_of` optional (TO/audit only).  
**Auth.** If tournament `public`/`unlisted` with the link: spectator. Extra fields for TO and for an entry in a match.  
**Steps.** (1) Load active generation. (2) For each match, for each slot: if spectator, occupant display = `locked_entry_id` name if locked else `derived_entry_id` name if derived and match not dirty else placeholder from `source_type` + source `code`; never scores unless state in `{confirmed, admin_resolved, forfeit, walkover}`; never reports. (3) For a participant of the match: include own current report; include opponent current report only if own current report exists or `now ≥ report_due_at`; include `state`. (4) For TO: dirty flags, both reports, derivation mismatch. (5) Swiss/RR: include latest `standings_snapshots` lines (public). (6) Public checked-in count: after generate, `|entry_snapshot|`; before, count `status=checked_in` (this is a **resource** count, not filtered to the caller’s rows).  
**Atomic.** Snapshot read at a generation `result_version` horizon (repeatable read).  
**Eventual.** Live channel may lag ≤1s.  
**Optimistic UI.** n/a (read).  
**Conflict.** n/a. A dirty slot is shown to TO as dirty, to spectators as the **locked** name (who is actually playing), not the derived name.

#### 5.13 FinalizePlacementsAndPayout *(bulk + external)*

**Inputs.** `tournament_id`.  
**Auth.** TO.  
**Steps.** (1) Require `pool_state = locked` and `shortfall_cents = 0`. (2) Require all prize-bearing phases complete (same terminal conditions as 5.11) unless `force_finalize_reason` set. (3) Compute placements: DE/SE from loss-round (GF winner 1st, GF loser 2nd, LB final loser 3rd, …); RR/Swiss from final snapshot; already-written cutoff remainders stand. Insert `placements`. (4) Using snapshot `total_cents`, allocate: subtract fixed rules; split remainder by percent_bps; leftover cents to best placement first. (5) For each placement with `amount > 0`, insert `payout_ledger` `pending` if no prior `pay` row for `(tournament, entry, placement, snapshot)`. (6) For each pending: call processor transfer to `payout_recipient_user_id`; on success `sent`. On failure leave `pending` and do not mark tournament `paid`. (7) If all sent, `pool_state = paid`, tournament `paid`. (8) Audit.  
**Atomic.** 1–5 (ledger intent). Processor sends are then **exactly-once** via unique `processor_transfer_id` and row state.  
**Eventual.** Recipients see “paid.”  
**Optimistic UI.** None.  
**Conflict.** Second payout: unique pay row. Placement change after pay: new `clawback` + new `pay`; never UPDATE amount.

#### 5.14 AutoForfeitOverdueMatches *(scheduled, bulk, periodic)*

**Inputs.** none (job every 15s over live tournaments).  
**Auth.** System only.  
**Steps.** (1) Select matches where `state = awaiting_counter_report` and `now > report_due_at`, limit 100, `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`. (2) For each: if exactly one current report, `ApplyOfficialResult` from that report with `source=deadline_forfeit_of_silent_side` (silent entry forfeits; reporter’s score stands if valid, else 1–0 forfeit). If **zero** reports and `now > report_due_at + grace`, mark both no-show, TO queue, do not invent a winner. (3) Same cascade rules as 5.5. (4) Audit per match.  
**Atomic.** Per match (not the whole batch).  
**Eventual.** Bracket.  
**Optimistic UI.** n/a.  
**Conflict.** A late report in flight: match lock; if the report commits first, state is no longer awaiting and the job skips.

### 6. Scale and cost

**A successful regional platform (year 3).** 40k tournaments/year, ~110/day; 8k organizations; 600k users; 1.2M entries; 4M matches; 7M reports; 900k contributions; GMV $8M/year. Concurrent **live** tournaments at peak (weekend evening): 400. Peak one event: 2048-entry Swiss, 11 rounds, ~1k matches/round, 15k spectators on the stream overlay hitting the same phase.

**Row sizes per hot event**

| Event | Entries | Matches | Slots | Reports (≤2×) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32-man DE + reset | 32 | 62 | 124 | ~120 |
| 256-man SE | 256 | 255 | 510 | ~500 |
| 4×8 RR → 8 DE | 32 | 4×28 + 14 | ~250 | ~240 |
| 2048 Swiss × 11 | 2048 | ~11×1024 | ~22k | ~22k |

**Rates.** Check-in: 256 entries in 10 minutes (bursty). GeneratePhase: one transaction inserting ~100–2000 rows. Report: 20–80/sec in a big Swiss round close. Spectator reads: 15k clients subscribe to one `phase_id`; each official result fans out to those clients; a WB confirm in DE touches 2 slots (WB next + LB drop), then maybe a bye chain of length 1–2. Contribute: low QPS, spiky at start. PairSwissRound: 1 per round, reads 2k standings lines, writes 1k matches.

**What gets expensive**

- **GeneratePhase / cutoff** — single transaction with self-FKs; 2k-row insert is fine; 8k-entry elim (impractical product, 13-round DE) is the cliff. Cost is integrity, not bytes.
- **ApplyOfficialResult cascade** — walk of a chain, not a table scan; worst DE confirm still touches < 10 matches unless a TO vacates a subtree (256-man vacate from round 1 is hundreds of rows — rare, must be one transaction).
- **PairSwissRound** — O(n log n) sort plus backtracking; 2048 is easy CPU; the cost is **holding locks** on the prior round’s matches while reading all official results. Buchholz is O(n × rounds) per pair.
- **GetBracketProjection** — 2048 Swiss cannot send 22k slot rows to mobile every confirm. Need a round-scoped view (current round + my match + standings 200 lines) plus an elim tree view that is the full DAG for ≤512 and collapsed-by-round for larger.
- **Standings / Buchholz / public counts** — if implemented as “sum what the caller can see,” they are both slow and **wrong** (Trap 4). They must be snapshot rows or an elevated resource aggregate.
- **Spectator fan-out** — 15k × 62-match DE is a pub/sub problem; recomputing placeholders per subscriber is waste. Publish a generation-horizon version; clients request a diff.
- **Payout batch** — 256 processor transfers; rate limits, not row counts.
- **Auto-forfeit job** — skip-locked 100 at a time; cheap if indexed on `(state, report_due_at)`.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**1. Atomic insert of a self-referential DAG whose size is a function of `n`.** Ordinary “create match” mutations die here. The product needs one transaction that inserts a variable-size graph with deferred FKs, bye walkover recursion, and a generation fence. *Concession:* pre-allocate a 256-man bracket and hide unused matches. **Cheat boundary:** when `n` is not a power of two, hidden matches must still be **byes in the standard seed positions**, not leftover ghost players; and DE loser-drop edges must still exist. A fixed 8-man template sold as “any size” is a cheat.

**2. Occupancy is a graph function with a lock, not a column write.** Confirm is “recompute a bounded downstream cone, but stop at occupancy locks, and maybe vacate.” This is not `UPDATE matches SET player = $winner WHERE id = next_id`. *Concession:* disallow disputes after the winner’s next match starts (no cascade, no vacate). **Cheat boundary:** you may shorten the protest window; you may not keep the **promise that both players can contest an error** if the UI still shows a “dispute” button that cannot restore the correct LB drop. If you store only player ids, you cannot even ask the question.

**3. Algorithmic bulk writes (Swiss pairing, circle RR, DE mapping, cutoff ranking).** These are not user-authored rows. They read a whole official-result set and write a whole matching. *Concession:* TO uploads a CSV of pairings. **Cheat boundary:** the product may let a TO **edit one pairing** after the algorithm runs; it may not advertise Swiss if round 1–N were all generated at registration. Cutoff that lets the TO pick eight names without a recorded ranking function cheats placements (money).

**4. Two-party consensus state machine on append-only reports.** Confirmation is an agreement operator over two current reports, with edits, deadlines, and admin override, serialized on the match. *Concession:* TO-only reporting (LAN with refs). **Cheat boundary:** if players are told they report scores, first-report-wins or TO-always-edits is a cheat of promise 8. Spectator-visible unconfirmed scores are a secrecy cheat.

**5. Money as formula, then snapshot, then append-only payout, with placements from the graph.** The pool is not a counter field. Sponsor match is not a second counter. Placement is not a TO-typed integer except as override with audit. *Concession:* TO types the seven payout amounts after the event (no crowdfunding). **Cheat boundary:** if the page still shows live matching and public contributions, the snapshot+ledger must exist. Editing a contribution row, or paying from a live SUM after a chargeback without `shortfall`, is a cheat of the money invariants.

A backend that only offers “queries and mutations over rows” will fake 1–3 by looping in application code **inside one transaction with deferred constraints and graph walks**. That is allowed if those walks are the real implementation of the invariants — it is a cheat if the loop is best-effort after commit (“we’ll fill the next match in a background job”) because a crash then yields a half-applied bracket, which invariant 4 forbids.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice (high confidence these are real product rules).** Power-of-two elim with byes on missing seeds; the recursive seed-placement list; double-elim losers bracket with drop rounds; optional GF reset; circle method for RR; Swiss as per-round matching with rematch avoidance and lowest-without-bye bye; check-in ≠ registration; two-player report with TO override (start.gg, Challonge, many Discord TOs); Matcherino-style community pool plus percentage match-up-to-cap; paying the captain; occupancy not changing after a match has started (TO folklore; software often gets this wrong).

**Specified tightly here so another implementer cannot guess.** Exact DE loser-drop order (reverse bracket order); GF-2 pre-created as a conditional node rather than inserted later; three occupant fields (`derived`, `locked`, `played_as`); dirty-derivation + mandatory vacate-or-deny on reverse; Swiss refuses to pair through open disputes; Buchholz over unfiltered official results; pool lock snapshot vs live formula vs shortfall; contribution reversals as new rows; cutoff as the same atomic generator fed by a frozen ranking; report visibility (own immediately, opponent after you submit); auto-forfeit of the silent side only when exactly one report exists.

**Invented for the exercise (plausible, not a clone of one vendor).** Treating `derivation_dirty` as a first-class spectator-hidden TO state; `deny_cascade_keep_played` as an explicit money/graph split; stranded post-lock contributions; requiring tiebreak **matches** rather than TO coin-flip when cutoff is tied (some TOs coin-flip; we refuse). FIDE Dutch pairing is richer than the adjacent-plus-2000-backtrack specified; a later implementation may swap the matcher **if** rematch avoidance and bye rules stay.

**Medium confidence.** Exact spectator lag (“one second”) is an SLO, not a law. Processor fee handling (net vs gross as the match base) matches honest crowdfunding but some products match on gross — the design picks net and freezes it. Non-power-of-two DE **without** padding to `2^k` is a zoo of shapes; padding is the standard simplification and is **not** a cheat of the axis, because the DAG remains irregular (loser edges, GF2, byes, cascades).

**Low confidence / out of scope on purpose.** Per-game (map) scores, FFA/battle-royale, multi-game events under one pool, tax forms, split payouts inside a team, rating recomputation, anti-cheat. Adding them would not exercise the axis harder than Trap 1 + Swiss + pool snapshot already do.

The axis is load-bearing in the happy path: every elim match a spectator looks at is a computed slot; every event the TO starts is an atomic DAG (or an atomic Swiss round); every score a player submits is a consensus step that may dirty a locked cone; every dollar on the page is a formula until a snapshot says otherwise.
