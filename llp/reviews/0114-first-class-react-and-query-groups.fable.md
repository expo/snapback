# LLP 0114 super-refine — Round 1 review

## 1. Overall assessment

LLP 0114 has the right architecture and an unusually disciplined security/totality posture. Its core composition claims are correct and verifiable: the React surface stays a `useSyncExternalStore` projection over the framework-neutral store (`packages/snapback-react/index.mjs` is 121 lines today and delegates everything to `packages/snapback-client/external-store.mjs`), the wire owners stay the connection grant and `LiveStreamManager` (LLP 0034 §2.2's one-socket/both-lanes rule), no second cache or transport is invented, and many of its pinned details match shipped code exactly — the `ExternalConnection`/status/freshness unions (`external-store.d.mts:1-3`), retained-until-proved-current (`external-store.mjs:241-264`, `index.mjs:5113-5127`), the microtask idle linger (`external-store.mjs:445-452`), rollback-from-latest-authoritative (`external-store.test.mjs:214`), the unsubscribe-before-manager-detach ordering (`packages/snapback-contract/index.mjs:3581-3621`), anonymous-register refusal and pre-mount bundle acquisition (`src/server.rs:16298-16305`, `src/bundle.rs:46-50`).

The spec's central weakness is that it repeatedly presents **net-new seams as existing composition**, and several of those seams as written **conflict with the normative corpus or with the spec itself**. The authority partition seam (`AuthorityPartitionV1`, `getAuthoritySnapshot`/`subscribeAuthority`, the dev partition, the "authority-source capability") exists nowhere and contradicts LLP 0059's coordinator/participant model and its deliberate exclusion of provider/generation facts from client authority. The mutation `ambiguous`/`retryable` vocabulary is net-new against a Confirmed/Rejected-only wire enum and a store that today mislabels every failure `rejected`. Presence permission facts are sourced from a generated permission profile that codegen does not emit, replacing a caller-permission callback the spec never mentions retiring. The RQS registries lack the provenance fields the group `boot` needs, the claimed mode-availability capability report does not exist (LLP 0091 explicitly disclaims capability negotiation), and the partition-keyed store identity contradicts LLP 0092 §0C.5's authority-neutral alias-key rule. Finally, the demo acceptance clause is not dischargeable against this repository: Signal Desk is out-of-capsule, no in-repo React demo carries both lanes, optimistic updates, or RQS, and "preserving atomic mutation-plus-activity [with] optimistic rollback" collides with the spec's own single-target optimistic bound.

None of this says the design direction is wrong — most of the new seams are the *right* new seams. It says the spec must stop describing them as projections, assign them owners and same-landing corpus amendments, and resolve the identified conflicts. That is normal round-1 super-refine output.

## 2. Strengths

- **Correct ownership layering, verified.** React owns only the projection; the store owns lifecycle; the headless client owns the wire. This matches LLP 0012's as-built registration (`llp/0012:836-861`), LLP 0064 §2's binding-value boundary, and the measured lesson in `issues/closed/20260726-what-a-framework-binding-should-actually-contain.md` ("the hook is the easy 10%") — the spec deliberately owns the lifecycle/failure-mode list that ticket says bindings should own.
- **Accurate restatement of hard-won invariants.** The presence teardown ordering (server unsubscribe before dropping the last `manager.onState` demand) restates the shipped ENG-22527 fix verbatim (`snapback-contract/index.mjs:3581-3621`); connection-only deliveries never settling optimistic layers matches `external-store.mjs:269-271` and the existing React test (`package.test.mjs:102-124`); reducer-failure-before-transport follows the ENG-24495 lineage; "boot is provenance, not consistentAt" is exactly right against LLP 0092's single-commit invariant (`llp/0092:300-303`).
- **Genuine glue deletion.** `useSnapbackQueries` would delete `demos/shindig/surfaces/react/src/useLiveRows.ts` (82 lines) and `demos/all-aboard/surfaces/react/src/store.ts` (181 lines); `useSnapbackPresence` targets exactly what `demos/cursors-web/surfaces/react/src/useCursorRoom.ts` (477 lines) hand-rolls.
- **Security/totality pass shows.** Removing caller-authored user/authority/permission inputs, finite group bounds, store caps with reclaim-only-idle, refusal-before-upstream-demand, value-free diagnostics, and ephemeral exclusion from durable/RQS/offline state are all the right closures, and the ephemeral exclusion matches code (`client-store.mjs`/`rqs*.mjs` contain zero ephemeral coupling).
- **Deterministic aggregation.** The group status/freshness/connection tables are total and ordered; `errors` surfacing during partial states is honest.
- **Honest capability language.** `Online`-only, no local-first claims from retained rows, per-target and per-OS non-inference — consistent with LLP 0012's Online-vs-Parity rule and LLP 0064 §7.
- **Unusually executable acceptance**, including Strict Mode, both mutation orderings, four-not-eight upstream attempts, and fencing — the right shape, even where individual clauses need repair (below).

## 3. Concerns

### C1. The authority seam is asserted, not owned — and conflicts with LLP 0059
**Severity: Critical — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: `AuthorityPartitionV1`, `getAuthoritySnapshot`, `subscribeAuthority`, the "explicit dev partition", the "authority-source capability", and all eleven `SNAPBACK_REACT_*` codes exist only in the spec (repo-wide grep). What exists is four deliberately separate artifacts — `ClientAuthorityV1` (`llp/0059:1639-1651`), `AuthorityCaptureV1` (`llp/0059:1653-1666`), `AuthorityDescriptor` (`src/manifest.rs:3397-3403`), `principalScope` via async `currentAuthorizationScope()` (`packages/snapback-client/index.mjs:2341-2365`) — plus the `AuthCoordinator` participant barrier (`auth-coordinator.mjs:1982`, phases quiesce/blank/clearMemory/commitDurable/resume). Specific conflicts: (a) the spec's partition binds "tenant/provider/delegation facts", but `llp/0059:1667-1670` *deliberately* excludes provider/generation from client authority and no tenant/delegation field exists anywhere; (b) the spec's "provider authority listener" model cannot satisfy 0059's requirement that `blank` is a coordinator-driven participant phase preceding the first destructive step (`llp/0059:2092-2134`, `:2500-2502`) and that resume-time publication goes through `stagePublication` (`:2408-2414`); (c) a synchronous `getAuthoritySnapshot` must not throw, but `capture()` throws `SNAPBACK_CLIENT_AUTHORITY_CLEARING` while clearing (`auth-coordinator.mjs:1021-1026`) — the blank/clearing partition value is undesigned; (d) if the partition included `custodyRevision`, ordinary bearer rotation would re-key every store — the exact mistake `llp/0059:1690-1694` names; the spec doesn't exclude it; (e) dev mode has no client-issued dev partition today — the shipped fallback is caller-supplied `devUserId` plus `authority = userId ?? 'default'` (`snapback-react/index.mjs:55`, `external-store.mjs:98`), which is what the spec forbids.
Resolution criteria: a same-landing LLP 0059 (and LLP 0012 registration) amendment that (i) defines the partition artifact and its exact members — which of epoch/key/incarnation/descriptor are in, `custodyRevision` explicitly out — and its rotation triggers; (ii) registers the React/store layer as a coordinator participant (or routes it through an existing participant) rather than a listener, restating the replacement sequence in 0059's phase vocabulary; (iii) defines the clearing-state partition value for a non-throwing sync read; (iv) defines the dev partition; (v) homes the capability in a real surface (e.g., the provider-introspection `requiredCapabilities` handshake, `provider-introspection.mjs:292-344`). The implementation sequence (spec:771-781) must schedule this seam; today it never appears.

### C2. Legacy-provider compatibility contradicts the authority-source requirement; the spec's own token rule is violated by its own changes
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: spec:189-190 ("A provider without the exact authority-source capability refuses before constructing a store") vs spec:645-649 ("Legacy providers that implement only name-based query/mutation methods keep working… They may also use query groups and the connection coordinator"). Legacy providers have no authority source; today's stores are keyed by caller `authority` with a literal `'default'` fallback. Both statements cannot hold. Separately, spec:639-643 says existing snapshots remain compatible and "a change to the existing query-store observer or snapshot meaning requires a new exact token" — yet the spec itself changes shipped snapshot semantics: it drops the published `protocol` and `identity` fields (`external-store.d.mts:11-12`), widens the closed `mutation.status` union with `ambiguous` (+`retryable`) (`external-store.d.mts:20,27`), redefines `getServerSnapshot` from "current live snapshot" (`external-store.mjs:331`) to a pinned construction snapshot, and upgrades shallow freezing (`external-store.mjs:14-37`) and unconditional re-publish (`:199-208`) to "deeply immutable"/"referentially stable until a fact changes".
Resolution criteria: define the legacy identity lane explicitly (a deprecated caller-authority partition scoped to name-based providers only, or drop the legacy-keeps-working claim); enumerate every behavior change to the shipped store and state per change whether it rides the existing token, an additive store, or forces a token event; add migration notes for the guide-taught caller `provider`/`userId`/`authority` form (`guide/clients/react.md:42-49`).

### C3. Mutation outcome vocabulary is net-new and its honesty rules lack mechanisms
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: the wire outcome enum is `{Confirmed, Rejected}` only (`src/live_wire.rs:625-628`); the store today labels *every* thrown call `'rejected'` (`external-store.mjs:387`) — precisely the uncertainty-collapse LLP 0043 forbids (`llp/0043:199-206`), which the spec fixes without saying it is fixing a defect or how; `ambiguous`/`retryable` exist nowhere (the sole `retryable` in the client is the sender-proof `indeterminate` error, `index.mjs:142-155`); `retryable` as defined requires composing `availability.queuedReplay.available` (`index.mjs:3157-3165`), descriptor-protocol negotiation, and authority-capture equality — unstated; a supplied id doubling as `clientMutationId` must satisfy `REPLAY_OPAQUE_ID_PATTERN`/max-bytes (`src/manifest.rs:3418-3428`) — unmentioned; and resolving a predecessor attempt's promise with the old authority's terminal `Result` (spec:203-205) conflicts with LLP 0059's exposure fence ("cannot… publish an A result", `llp/0059:2457-2463`).
Resolution criteria: specify the proof rule for `rejected` (receipt presence + outcome) and the transport-failure→`ambiguous` mapping; pin id-grammar validation with a diagnostic; define `retryable`'s derivation; and reconcile the predecessor-promise disposition with 0059 (most likely: reject with a structured supersession/ambiguous error and never deliver predecessor result values), or record an explicit 0059 carve-out.

### C4. Presence permission and key-mode facts are sourced from artifacts that don't exist, and the `self` options fight the spec's own hygiene rule
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: generated ephemeral references carry `keyMode/scopeFields/defaultTtlMs/maxTtlMs` but **no permission profile** (`packages/snapback-codegen/index.mjs:4944-4960`; publish/subscribe policies live only in `schema.manifest.json`); today's room permissions are a caller-supplied `ephemeralPermissions(name, args)` callback (`snapback-contract/index.mjs:532, 3650`) that graffiti uses to mint `roomPermission` client-side (`demos/graffiti/surfaces/contract/main.ts:202-204`); the spec's "permission facts come from the generated reference and target grant" therefore names two sources that respectively don't exist and aren't specified. `self.customKey` exposes a key mode whose authorization LLP 0013 keeps refused (`llp/0013:947`; Session/Custom degrade `self` to false, `snapback-contract/index.mjs:3218-3222`), and `self.ownerUserId`/`sessionId` are caller-authored identity inputs under a spec that says hooks "accept no caller-authored user". The publisher heartbeat (`refreshEveryMs`, refcounts, shared heartbeat) has zero implementation and no LLP 0013 counterpart — legitimate new store ownership, but presented alongside existing behavior without being marked as new.
Resolution criteria: name the codegen change (permission profile onto ephemeral references) and the grant-side permission path as owned deliverables with their governing LLPs (0013/0011/0034); pin per-key-mode availability (custom refuses with a capability diagnostic until 0013 changes); derive user/session keys from the bound authority where possible and specify server-side validation for any remaining caller-supplied key input, or trim v1 to connection-keyed `self`; mark the heartbeat model explicitly as new room-store ownership.

### C5. Bundle-boot provenance needs registry/wire facts that don't exist; the mode-availability capability claim has no backing artifact; registered adoption has an unstated precondition
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: the registries expose `{route|connection, generation, versionStream, size, lookup, consume}` — no `commitVersion`, no `source`, no entry-key enumeration (`packages/snapback-client/rqs-preload.mjs:57-75, 99-132`), and register-mode auth-scoped entries have `meta: null` (`llp/0091:406-416`), so "every child consumed an entry from the same complete bundle identity" is currently unprovable per entry. The wire has two modes (`read|register`; "inline" is Channel A's embedded read-mode body, `llp/0092:525-527`) — the three-way `boot.source` needs a defined derivation. "Target capability reports govern whether read, inline, or register mode is available" (spec:401-402) has no artifact: the closed capability registry has no RQS id (`src/manifest.rs:3783-3821`), LLP 0091:444 says "No capability negotiation", and Cloudflare's register unavailability is discovered only via a 403 refusal (`cloudflare/src/coordinator.js:1222-1255`). And registered adoption is refused whenever the auth-invalidation protocol is negotiated, falling back to a fresh subscribe (`packages/snapback-client/index.mjs:6632`; recorded follow-on `llp/0092:822-826`) — so the acceptance clause "register adoption starts no fresh subscribe" is unimplementable for sessions-mode clients until that lands.
Resolution criteria: name the registry/per-entry-provenance extensions as owned client work with an LLP 0090/0092 registration; define `source` derivation; restate mode availability honestly (explicit-refusal discovery with read retry) or add a capability id through a real 0091/0092 amendment; name the invalidation-lane adoption dependency or scope the acceptance to the configurations where adoption is admitted.

### C6. Partition-keyed store identity contradicts LLP 0092 §0C.5's authority-neutral alias key; the SSR "verified/exact authority" condition conflicts with 0090/0091
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: 0092's checked-promotion rule exists precisely so anonymous-minted (Channel A) preloads are adoptable under an authenticated session — the alias key `rqs:authority-independent` "changes store identity only, never invocation authority" (`llp/0092:238-274`); tokens are authority-neutral (`llp/0090:391-395`); `authPartition` is a reserved v2 slot, absent in v1 (`llp/0091:78-82`); and the shipped registry key is name+JCS-args with deliberately no authority component (`rqs-preload.mjs:6-9`). The spec's flat rule that different authority identities "never share… RQS adoption" plus `getServerSnapshot`'s "exact authority… supplied before render" condition cannot be evaluated against an authority-free bundle and would defeat the cross-authority adoption 0092 mandates. "Verified" also overclaims: client-side verification is digest+argsHash only, and 0090 v0.3 explicitly excludes client-side authenticity against coherent tampering (`llp/0090:16`; `rqs-store.mjs:74-120`).
Resolution criteria: reconcile with 0092 §0C.5 — either adopt the alias-key sentinel for authority-independent queries with the privacy argument stated (such queries are by construction anonymous-safe), or add a dated, explicit override of §0C.5 for the React surface; define "verified" as the actual check set; restate the server-snapshot condition in bundle-identity (not authority) terms.

### C7. The maintained-demo acceptance clause is not dischargeable against this repository
**Severity: High — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: Signal Desk exists only as an out-of-capsule working copy (`issues/shelved/20260727-backend-bakeoff-improvement-program.md:13`; `demos/signal-desk` absent; the four query names and `incidentPresence` appear nowhere but the spec). No in-repo React demo has both durable and ephemeral lanes, optimistic updates, or RQS; durable-version separation is proven only in Contract/headless suites (`demos/graffiti/tests/acceptance/headless.mjs:149,191,231`). "Preserving… atomic mutation-plus-activity, optimistic rollback" is either unsatisfiable or misleading: the write-plus-activity helper is an open, LLP-gated ticket (`issues/20260727-relation-and-activity-patterns.md`), and an activity feed is a second query store, colliding with the spec's own single-target optimistic bound (spec:446, 468-470; today's codebase rule at `snapback-contract/index.mjs:541-542`). React browser suites are Chrome-gated and outside `scripts/verify-local-proof`; the Expo per-OS receipts (`guide/clients/react-expo.md:32-38`) would be invalidated by migrating the demo they witness.
Resolution criteria: name a demo that exists in-repo (or state explicitly that the comparison fixture is external and define the in-repo proof that substitutes); split the clause into preserved properties vs new capability; state the intended optimistic shape for mutation-plus-activity (e.g., optimistic target + authoritative activity, explicitly); say how the demo proof and re-earned Expo receipts enter the enforced gate.

### C8. The one-connection-label rule ignores the corpus's recorded failure mode
**Severity: Medium — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: every React demo mints a fresh connection id per subscribe/effect, and cursors-web documents why: reusing one id across mount/unmount/mount "races the old socket's server-side teardown against the new subscription — the first live run of this arm lost one direction of convergence to exactly that" (`demos/cursors-web/surfaces/react/src/useCursorRoom.ts:155-162`). LLP 0034 §10.1's generation-guarded teardown has since closed the server-side race, but the spec neither cites it nor argues why one provider-owned label is now safe for both lanes under Strict Mode/HMR churn.
Resolution criteria: address the recorded failure explicitly (cite the §10.1 guard and the provider-scoped demand/linger design); add acceptance for same-label socket reopen after linger expiry and for room registrations surviving Strict Mode replay under the shared label.

### C9. No conditional subscription (`skip`) and no per-entry group args in the normative shapes
**Severity: Medium — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: LLP 0012 names "treat `skip` as a full unsubscribe, not a pause" as reference adapter behavior (`llp/0012:791`); hooks cannot be called conditionally in React, and neither `useSnapbackQuery` nor `useSnapbackQueries` offers skip/enabled. The group section's entry shape shows only `{query}` (spec:305-313) — args never appear, though scoped queries (team-chat's `roomMessages`, partiful's `eventDetails`) require them and the types-fixture acceptance implies they exist.
Resolution criteria: pin the group entry shape including `args`; add a skip/enabled affordance with full-unsubscribe semantics per LLP 0012 (for single and per-group-entry), or declare conditional subscription out of scope with the sanctioned workaround named.

### C10. Shared mutation-ledger projection semantics and terminal-attempt finiteness are unpinned
**Severity: Medium — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: equivalent hooks "observe one ledger" with combined `pendingCount` (spec:437-441), but which attempt drives `status`/`error`/`result`/`lastSettlement` under concurrency is undefined; whether differing `optimistic` configs across equivalent hooks refuse or coexist is undefined; the caps bound only *nonterminal* attempts (spec:625) and reclaim terminal *acknowledged* attempts only when a **new identity** would refuse — a long-lived subscribed ledger's `attempts` history is therefore unbounded; and the spec never names who drives LLP 0043 settlement acknowledgement (the seam exists: `acknowledgeMutations`, `index.mjs:4142-4147`).
Resolution criteria: define the ledger→snapshot projection (e.g., pending-if-any, else latest settlement by settle order); scope `optimistic` config to the call site and define conflict behavior; bound terminal attempt retention per ledger; name the acknowledgement driver.

### C11. Refusal presentation (throw vs error-state) is never pinned
**Severity: Medium — MATERIAL.**
Evidence: the spec uses "refuses" for at least eight classes (missing provider, authority source, reference kind, group shape, capability, connection-id mismatch, self conflict, capacity) without saying which throw during render and which surface as `status: "error"` snapshots. Today the hook throws a bare `TypeError` with no stable code for a missing provider (`snapback-react/index.mjs:59-61`). Acceptance tests cannot be written, and app error-boundary strategy cannot be chosen, without this.
Resolution criteria: a per-diagnostic presentation table (render-throw vs error snapshot vs rejected promise), consistent with `SNAPBACK_EXTERNAL_STORE_INCOMPATIBLE`'s existing throw behavior.

### C12. Presence/room semantic details diverge from shipped constants and need pinning
**Severity: Medium — DETAIL.**
Evidence: the "fixed 250 ms room/publisher linger" (spec:616-617) sits against the shipped `EPHEMERAL_IDLE_LINGER_MS = 30_000` rebind policy (`snapback-contract/index.mjs:3141-3146`) and numerically collides with the 250 ms retry bases; RN background timer throttling interacts with a fixed-ms linger (LLP 0064 §5). "One manager-demand registration" is imprecise (demand is one `onState` listener per room record; the socket is shared). Entry expiry rendering ("not rendered past `expiresAtMs`") needs a clock basis (server-authored `expiresAtMs` vs local `receivedAtMs` anchoring; LLP 0013 warns against synchronized-clock dependence, `llp/0013:196-210`). Equivalence for shared `self` declarations (canonical serialization?) and `PresencePublishOptions`/`PresenceRemoveOptions` are undefined.
Resolution criteria: reconcile or justify the linger constant (and its RN semantics); pin room connection vocabulary and poll-transport behavior; fix the wording; define the expiry clock rule and `self` equivalence; define the option types.

**Severity: Low — DETAIL.**

## 4. Suggestions

- **Pin optimistic-layer retirement to durable versions, not arrival order.** Confirmed mutation receipts already carry the non-null durable commit version (LLP 0034 §3.1); retiring a confirmed layer when an authoritative delivery's version ≥ the receipt version is strictly better than "first delivery strictly after confirmation" (the shipped conservative fallback, `external-store.mjs:151-157`) — it eliminates both the transient duplicate (ENG-23385) and the early-retire flicker from a pre-commit delivery arriving late.
- **Home the new capabilities in the existing handshake.** `validateProviderAdapterHandshake`'s `requiredCapabilities` (`provider-introspection.mjs:292-344`) is the natural declaration surface for authority-source and presence capabilities; referencing it avoids inventing a fourth capability vocabulary.
- **Reconsider a generated group reference.** The driving ticket (`issues/20260727-typed-live-query-groups.md`) asked for a generated group/route reference with closed child refs; the spec chose caller-authored maps. A generated route-group reference would also give `boot` a natural 1:1 alignment with LLP 0092 route bundles. If the inline map is the deliberate v1, say why and leave the generated form as future work.
- **Make the room linger a policy, not a constant** — e.g., a Strict-Mode-safe floor plus the existing configurable idle linger for navigation churn, with RN background semantics stated.
- **Name the corpus registrations in acceptance** the way LLP 0101 does (it names LLP 0094 §0C/§0E and LLP 0095 fixtures): LLP 0059 (partition/participant), LLP 0012 (artifact/diagnostic registration), LLP 0092 (registry provenance), LLP 0013 (heartbeat pattern note), LLP 0064 (surface/receipt rows), and the specific guide/capability row ids.
- **State the mutate() promise contract explicitly** (settlement vs rejection classes, including authority replacement), and consider surfacing presence publish-budget facts (the generated `sustainedHz`/burst) so the 15 Hz-style throttle check cursors-web hand-rolls stops being app glue.
- Keep the shipped `protocol`/`identity` snapshot fields (or version their removal deliberately).

## 5. Open questions

1. Which events rotate `AuthorityPartitionV1` — same-user re-login, stream reset, dev↔sessions transition — and is blank-then-refetch on each acceptable UX? (LLP 0059's transition table distinguishes blank vs wipe; credential refresh must not rotate.)
2. Is group-store identity shared across components with identical maps, and what is label normalization?
3. What does `useSnapbackPresence` do under `transport: "poll"` or on a target without the socket lane — refuse with `SNAPBACK_REACT_CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, or degrade?
4. Who drives LLP 0043 settlement acknowledgement in the React flow, and does ack-eviction interact with `lastSettlement` retention?
