## Findings

### 1. High — opening-cut stop is not gated on `opened_all`

- **Where:** `snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:876–877` (the post-refill stop at `:881` is the true drain path).
- **What is wrong:** D2.3 is implemented for every pop, including after an opening cut. `opened_all` is recorded (`:861`) and the candidate already requires it (`:890`), but the loop still `break`s on the first popped lane whose buffer is empty and whose refill is illegal because the meter is already cut. C2 (≤8 lanes, id-ending position, unchanged classification) and D3 (opening cut answers as today, terminal) are not met.
- **Scenario:** Two lazy lanes, `last 100`, `.body = 'hit'`, index ending in `id`. Lane 1’s newest row is a hit, then 9,000 rejects cut the opening; lane 0 holds a full buffer. Heap order pops lane 1 first. Before: 100 rows, `complete: false, cut: 'meter', next: null`. After: **1 row**, same flags (lane report; `merge_continuation.rs:286–299`). Those 99 buffered sibling rows are omitted from an incomplete page that still has no `next`. A walk that re-opens into this shape (first page or a later continuation that itself opening-cuts) ends short. This is not a silent `complete: true` skip and cannot mint `next` (`opened_all` is false; `has_more && uncapped` is false).
- **Why the lead’s fix is sound:** If `!opened_all`, skip the refill and keep popping heap/buffer heads, as the pre-change `capped_at` skip did. The record predicate cannot become true: the `has_more` arm needs an uncut meter, the drain arm needs `opened_all`. No `next` means no later exclusive resume to skip or repeat a row. When `opened_all` is true, keep stopping after the pop whose next head cannot be established — that is the drain rule D5.1–D5.3 pin.
- **Smallest correct change:**
  1. At `read.rs:877`, set `drain_cut` and `break` only if `opened_all`. If `!opened_all` and already capped, do not refill; fall through and keep popping known heads.
  2. In `control_c1_opening_cut_with_a_short_nonempty_buffer_stays_terminal` (`merge_continuation.rs:297`), replace `assert!(!ids(&page).is_empty())` with `assert_eq!(ids(&page).len(), 100)` so C2’s answer is pinned. Do not keep the current “terminal incompleteness, not an identical prefix” weakening.
