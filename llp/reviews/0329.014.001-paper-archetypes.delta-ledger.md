## Emerging synthesis after 4 of 6 (orchestrator, 00:59)

Coverage: the v0 *core* (typed bounded reads/writes with real control flow, rules in the language, `maintain`, constraints, `schedule`, `channel`) carries the large majority of operations in every app (auction 14/23 plan + 2 schedule; booking ~34/39; ledger 10/12; Clique 41/59 incl. schedule/maintained/channel/effect). No app needed a second query class or an opaque handler. The retired demands list is long (sum, patchWhere, ranges, appendOnly, immutable, maintained balances, intervals, ctx.now).

The residue is NOT more operators. It falls into five places, each a *dimension of the model*, not a rung:
A. **Structural facts vs caller facts (K2 generalized).** Counts, ranks, "is it full", "highest bid": any reduction over a scan under a non-tautologous rule is a caller-slice fact; writing it as a resource fact is an admitted wrong program unless computed under system visibility (schedule) or declared as a disclosure (maintain + readable). Must cover loop tallies. → E_STRUCTURAL_COUNT/RANK as one family: "reduction escapes its visibility."
B. **Time is state.** Windows ("live", "future", "last 90 days", "sealed until close") are statuses flipped by schedules; rules and `maintain.where` are time-independent; visibility transitions are data transitions (write a new table at the transition). Schedules need: live `at`, reschedule-on-change, `if` guards, args from trigger, absent-`at` = no enqueue, system-only callables, generation of the trigger.
C. **Constraints with `where`, across columns, across tables, in-transaction aggregates.** Partial unique/cap/exclusion; self-row exemption; cross-column FK equality; `balanced`/`sumEquals`; conditional `immutable`; `(old,new)` update predicates with per-field grants. These are what make invariants bind every writer.
D. **Missing kinds for a complete app.** identity/credentials; assets (intent/finalize/one-use/revoke/purge); commit-triggered effect outbox; calendar as an effect seam or bounded operator. Plus in-transaction composition (`db.run`/private tx fns).
E. **Authorization ≠ retention ≠ disclosure.** Three policies the spec collapsed into one read rule: what the server will serve now (rule), what a client may keep after it ends (retention policy, per table, content revocations override), what aggregate numbers are disclosed beyond their sources (readable on maintain). Clique's expelled-member history and the ledger's former-member balances both need the second.
Plus spec completeness: optional/enum/bounded-list types, money arithmetic law, published ceilings, cardinality propagation, settlement (OCC vs LWW).

Ideal-design thesis (draft): a *small* language + a *rich schema*. The language stays as in v0 (it was never the bottleneck once control flow is admitted); the schema grows the five dimensions above as first-class declarations the engine enforces, and the certificate reports them. "Refuse, do not degrade" then has exactly one failure mode left — the app needs a fact the schema cannot declare — and every archetype's residue was one of those, not a missing operator.
