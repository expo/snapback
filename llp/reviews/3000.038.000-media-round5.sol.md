Verdict: NOT READY

Five MATERIAL issues remain in the round-5 code/design contract.

1. **Upload admission bypasses the browser’s locked session-authority check.** `DeviceUploads` sends `admit_upload`, but both web hosts capture and authorize only `admit`. Consequently, logout or account replacement after the facade check, while byte reading or admission waits, can still commit an unadmitted capture. A later login can replay that capture. The facade’s initial check does not replace the authoritative check held through persistence. Add that check without putting upload bytes in localStorage. See [uploads.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/uploads.ts:104), [web.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/web.ts:329), and [web-worker-device.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/web-worker-device.ts:184).

2. **Shared/orphan terminal history loses immutable upload identity.** `terminalReceipts()` reconstructs receipts without `upload_identity`; `shareReceipts()` persists that projection before source deletion. Meanwhile, `upload_terminal()` checks only the destination device’s own history. After orphan cleanup, even a retained shared receipt cannot enforce same-input admission: changed input can become queued, and unchanged input cannot immediately recover its original terminal outcome. Preserve the private identity alongside shared receipts and consult it during admission; strip it only at public presentation. See [web-receipts.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/web-receipts.ts:8) and [upload.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/src/device/upload.rs:68). A read-only source probe confirmed the identity is discarded.

3. **Quota-safe orphan transfer violates FIFO.** Both adopters `continue` after an upload entry receives `E_UPLOAD_QUOTA`, then transfer later entries from that source. For source order `[upload A, delete/edit B]`, B can therefore enter the destination and execute before A. Later recovery appends A behind B. Keeping A’s bytes at the source preserves custody but does not preserve the promised ordering. Defer the remaining source suffix, or retain an equivalent durable ordering barrier. See [web.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/web.ts:546) and [web-worker-orphans.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/web-worker-orphans.ts:66).

4. **Oversized placing arguments cannot complete early-refusal finalization.** Upload admission bounds bytes but does not bound placing arguments. Both `/m/...` and `/assets/intent/.../finish` have the same 1 MiB request ceiling; finalization retransmits the unchanged arguments plus the refusal. An admitted oversized argument therefore causes both requests to receive 413 before receipt recording. The pin and FIFO head remain unresolved, while local discard refuses because `begin_send` already committed. Bound admissible envelopes before custody, or provide bounded finalization identity. See [upload.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/crates/snapback4-device/src/device/upload.rs:47), [serve.rs](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/crates/snapback4/src/serve.rs:903), and [uploads.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/uploads.ts:165).

5. **Server cancellation does not settle a recovered unresolved local upload.** When inventory recovery cancels an intent that another device still holds, that device’s next upload receives `E_UPLOAD_CANCELLED`. `prepare()` stores it as a permanent `blocked` state and subsequently stops; it never enters acknowledged local cancellation settlement. This contradicts §3’s recovery promise and retains local bytes and the FIFO hold until another explicit discard. Route this tombstone outcome through ordinary cancellation settlement. See [uploads.ts](/home/ccheever/projects/sb4-w9-media/snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/uploads.ts:182). A read-only source probe confirmed the entry remains blocked with its bytes.
