### 1. Product contract

Ashrail is a ranked, turn-timed, hidden-information table game for six. A player queues, is seated with five others of nearby rating, and plays a **match** of **three independent rounds** of Night Six. Each round the server shuffles a nine-card role deck, deals one hidden role to each seat and three face-down **center** cards, runs a sealed night, a discussion clock, a sealed vote, then a reveal. Round points accrue; the match has a total finishing order; that order updates Glicko-2 ratings. Anyone signed in may sit on the **rail** (spectate) a public in-play or completed match. There is no cash. The scarce quantities are **hidden facts**, **committed randomness**, and **global rating**.

**Night Six, closed rules.** Deck, in this order before shuffle: `wolf, wolf, seer, robber, troublemaker, mason, mason, villager, drunk`. Seats are `0..5`; center slots are `6,7,8`. You **wake as your dealt role**. Night submissions are simultaneous; **resolution order is canonical and is not submission order** (see invariant 7 and operation 7). Teams and the win check use **end-of-night** roles, not dealt roles. After the vote, every seat in the unique highest vote-count dies; if several seats share that unique-max count, **all of them die**; if zero votes were accepted, nobody dies.

- Let `inPlayWolves` be seats whose **end-of-night** role is `wolf`.
- Village wins if (a) at least one dead seat is in `inPlayWolves`, or (b) `inPlayWolves` is empty **and** nobody died.
- Otherwise wolves win.
- A seated player scores **1 round point** if they are on the winning side (wolf if `wolf` at end-of-night, village otherwise), else **0**. A wolf in the center is not a seated wolf.

Match finish: points desc, then fewest deaths in the three votes, then **higher match-lock rating**, then lower seat index. The last two tiebreaks make a total order. Rating uses that order and the **six match-lock rating snapshots**, never live ratings.

**Who uses it.** Ranked players; rail spectators; the server clock (phase closer, matchmaker, rail publisher, leaderboard rebuilder); the push vendor (APNs/FCM) as a side channel that must not be more informed than the player API.

**User-visible promises**

1. A ranked match is always six human seats, three rounds, this ruleset. No bots, no host with extra buttons, no mid-match join as a player.
2. Before each deal the table publishes a **shuffle commitment**. After that round’s reveal anyone can recompute the nine cards from the revealed seed and the six nonces and see that they match what was dealt. The deal is not “whatever the server inserted.”
3. Your dealt role is delivered only to you until that round’s reveal. Center cards are delivered to no player except through a legal night snapshot (Seer, lone Wolf peek).
4. Night is one 20-second window. Every seat may submit one sealed envelope. “Has submitted” is public; the payload is not.
5. Vote is one 25-second window with the same sealed/public split. You may not vote for yourself.
6. Phase clocks are **server clocks**. The client countdown is decorative. A night or vote envelope is accepted if `receivedAt ≤ phaseEndsAt + 250ms`.
7. If you submit nothing, night is a legal no-op (Drunk still swaps center slot 6 by rule 6’s default); vote is abstain. Timeouts never roll extra dice.
8. Illegal-because-hidden envelopes are **accepted and become no-ops at resolve**. Illegal-because-public requests (wrong phase, self-vote, not seated) are refused with a public reason. The API never says “you are not the Seer.”
9. You may be signed in on several devices. You have at most one waiting queue ticket and at most one seat. The **first accepted** envelope for `(roundId, phase, generation)` sticks; a second is `already_acted`. Last-write-wins is not the product.
10. There is no offline table. A queued or seated client that cannot reach the server simply misses the window. Reconnect inside a window restores **your** private view (role, snapshots, your envelope if already accepted) and the public clock; it does not re-deal, re-shuffle, or unseal others.
11. Rail spectators see occupancy, handles, match-lock display ratings, phase, clocks, who-has-submitted bits, and public chat-less table state **live**. They see roles, night payloads, vote targets, and the seed **only after a durable rail-copy write**, which is scheduled 15 seconds after the player reveal. A seated player who also opened the rail still gets the **player** projection. You cannot call GetTableView “as” another user.
12. After reveal, the shuffle seed, permutation, dealt cards, end-of-night cards, night effects, and votes are auditable by seats immediately and by the rail after the rail copy.
13. Matchmaking locks six tickets in one rating neighborhood that expands with wait (section 5). Your **match-lock rating** is copied onto the seat at that instant and is what the match will rate against.
14. The leaderboard is global among players with at least five completed ranked matches this season. Rank 1 is the highest display rating, ties broken by `gamesPlayed` desc then `userId` asc. Your rank is among **all** such players, not among friends or the table. A snapshot at most 60 seconds stale is an allowed presentation; a friends-only rank is not.
15. Mid-match rating of anyone at the table does not change. Completing **another** match on a second device is impossible: one seat max. Completing matches back-to-back applies rating events in `ratingVersion` order.
16. Disconnect after deal does not abort the match. If fewer than four seats have submitted entropy by the entropy window’s end, the match **aborts**, tickets return to the queue as new waiting tickets, and no rating event is written.
17. Reveals, deaths, and rating changes are not optimistic on the client. “Locked in” on an envelope you just sent is the only optimistic pixel, and it reverts on `already_acted`, `phase_closed`, or `not_seated`.
18. A later model can implement this without inventing card values, timers, or win rules: every constant is in this document.

**Concurrency, offline, multi-device.** Concurrent actors are: six seats, N rail spectators, the phase-close job, the matchmaker, the rail publisher, the rating writer, and a second device of the same user. Offline play is refused, not merged. Two devices do not CRDT a vote; they race to a single insert with a unique `(roundId, phase, userId)` key.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

1. **[secrecy]** A fact that is hidden for a caller is omitted from that caller’s projection; it is never a distinguishing error, a missing slot, a filtered-to-zero count, or a different legal-action list.
2. **[secrecy][time]** Shuffle `serverSeed` is readable by system only until the round’s player-reveal write exists; then by seated users; then by rail only after the rail-copy write.
3. **[secrecy]** `RoundCard.dealtRole` for a seat is readable by that seat from deal onward, by nobody else until player-reveal. Center `dealtRole` is readable by no seat except as copied into a snapshot effect that names them as the viewer.
4. **[secrecy][time]** End-of-night roles, night payloads, and vote targets follow the same two-step publish as the seed: player-reveal write, then rail copy 15 seconds later.
5. **[secrecy]** The Seer’s snapshot is the role(s) **as of deal**, copied by value at night resolve step 3, and never a live pointer at the target slot.
6. **[secrecy]** Wolf-team and mason-team are the sets of seats **dealt** that role, written as snapshot effects for exactly those seats. They are not `COUNT(*)` over role rows.
7. Night envelopes are resolved in this order, using a working map that starts as the nine dealt cards: (1) wolf team / lone-wolf peek, (2) mason team, (3) seer snapshot, (4) robber swap and stolen-role snapshot, (5) troublemaker swap of two other seats, (6) drunk swap with a center slot (default slot 6). Submission timestamps do not reorder this list.
8. You act as your **dealt** role. A payload whose type the dealt role cannot perform becomes `none` at resolve, including after you have been robbed in the working map.
9. **[time]** `phaseEndsAt` and `phaseGeneration` on the round row are the only legal clocks for accepting envelopes and for the closer. A client clock cannot close a phase.
10. **[time]** The closer is idempotent: `UPDATE rounds SET phase=next, phaseGeneration=g+1 WHERE roundId=R AND phase=expected AND phaseGeneration=g` returning 0 rows means another closer already won; the loser writes nothing.
11. One accepted night envelope and one accepted vote envelope per `(roundId, userId)`. Inserts are append-only; there is no patch of payload.
12. **[secrecy]** “Submitted” is a boolean derived from envelope existence. It is public. Payload fields are not.
13. **[rating]** A user’s `ratingMu/Phi/Sigma` change only by appending a `RatingEvent` and applying it to the user row at `ratingVersion+1`. Handlers that patch rating fields directly are forbidden.
14. **[rating][others][time]** Rating inputs for a match are the six `MatchSeat.startMu/startPhi` copied at match lock. Later matches of those opponents do not rewrite this match.
15. **[rating][others]** Display rank of user U is `1 + |{V : V is eligible ∧ (V.displayRating > U.displayRating ∨ (V.displayRating = U.displayRating ∧ (V.gamesPlayed > U.gamesPlayed ∨ (V.gamesPlayed = U.gamesPlayed ∧ V.userId < U.userId))))}|`. The domain is not visibility-filtered.
16. **[others]** A user has at most one `QueueTicket` in `waiting` and at most one `MatchSeat` on a match with `status=in_play`.
17. **[time]** A waiting ticket older than 90 seconds without a match is marked `expired` (the user may re-enqueue). Expansion of the rating window does not delete the ticket.
18. Round cards, shuffle audits, envelopes, night effects, votes, round results, rating events, and rail copies are **insert-only**. The working map at night resolve is not a client-writable table.
19. **[secrecy]** A spectator row grants rail projection of that match, never seat projection, never another user’s snapshots.
20. **[time]** Match abort (invariant-adjacent to 16 in §1) writes no `RatingEvent` and no finish places.
21. **[rating]** Players with `gamesPlayed < 5` are omitted from the published top-100 and from “my rank,” and are still matchmade on internal Glicko.
22. Deal is a pure function of `combinedSeed` (defined in operation 5). Two deals of the same round are impossible; a reconnect reads existing `RoundCard` rows.

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1 — Role-as-of is an interval, not `player.role`. (temporal; the coterie analogue)**

- **Wrong:** One `role` column on the seat, updated in place as Robber and Drunk swap. Seer UI reads `seats[target].role` whenever it renders. Win check reads the same column. Masons count live `role='mason'`.
- **Why it is wrong:** Three different instants are load-bearing in every round. Seer information is **dealt-as-of**. Wolf/mason teams are **dealt-as-of**. Win, round points, and “what I am during the day” are **end-of-night**. A Villager who robbed a Wolf **is a Wolf for the vote and for rating**, and is **not** a Wolf for the wolf-team peek (they did not wake as Wolf). A Seer who peeked seat 3 and then that seat was robbed still “saw Villager” even if seat 3 is now Wolf. An enum cannot say this.
- **Observation:** Round log: seat 0 dealt Seer, peeked seat 3 (dealt Villager); seat 1 dealt Robber, robbed seat 3. If the Seer’s client shows Wolf, or if seat 3 is on the wolf team chat/snapshot, the implementation collapsed the interval. If a robbed Wolf still receives `wolf_team`, same bug in the other direction.

**Trap 2 — The shuffle seed is hidden information with a reveal schedule. (secrecy × randomness)**

- **Wrong:** `INSERT round_cards` with `ORDER BY RANDOM()` (or application `Math.random()`), store only the resulting roles. Or store `serverSeed` on the round row with the same read policy as `phase`.
- **Why it is wrong:** Randomness is an **input** to deal, not a side effect of insert. Without a pre-deal commitment, the server can grind a Wolf deal after seeing who is seated. If the seed is readable under “owner of the match,” every seated client (or a buggy replica, or a spectator query) can recompute everyone’s cards during night. Reconnect that re-runs `RANDOM()` deals a second hand.
- **Observation:** Two clients of the same seat, reconnecting 200ms apart, report different dealt roles; or a spectator’s network tab contains `serverSeed` before reveal; or permutation reconstructed from the post-reveal audit does not match `RoundCard`.

**Trap 3 — Simultaneous collect, ordered resolve, one closer. (concurrency × time)**

- **Wrong:** Apply a night action as soon as it arrives. Or resolve in `receivedAt` order. Or let any player press “close night.” Or run two closer replicas without a generation fence.
- **Why it is wrong:** If Robber’s swap applies when it arrives, a Seer who submitted later snapshots a post-rob world (illegal). If Troublemaker is applied before Robber because their packet won the race, the working map is the wrong game. If two closers both apply Drunk’s swap, center and seat can double-swap back to identity. A seated “host close” in a ranked match can wait until five envelopes exist and then not close, or close early to freeze an opponent out of the 250ms grace.
- **Observation:** Identical six envelopes, replayed, produce two different `EndRole` maps on two servers; or `NightEffect.seq` is 1..N twice; or a vote accepted at `phaseEndsAt+180ms` is dropped on one replica and counted on another.

**Trap 4 — Refusals, counts, ranks, and holes leak the deck. (visibility)**

- **Wrong:** Night submit of `seer_player` as a Villager returns `403 not_seer`. `GET /roles?round=R` with row-level “you may read your row” plus a count. Leaderboard rank computed in a friends visibility scope. Omitting center slot 8 from a Seer who only peeked 6 and 7, while a non-Seer sees three placeholders.
- **Why it is wrong:** `403 not_seer` tells the caller they are not Seer (and, if the button was shown by a bug, tells a shoulder-surfer). A visibility-filtered count of `dealtRole='wolf'` returns 0 or 1 for a lone client: a Wolf does not learn their partner; a Villager learns they are not a Wolf (sometimes they already know) **and** a naive “wolves remaining” widget shows 0 or 1 to everyone. Rank-among-visible-rows is not global rank. A projection that **omits** a slot versus **nulls a role field** is a distinguishable encoding of “you peeked this.”
- **Observation:** A test client that is dealt Villager posts `seer_player` and gets 403, while a Seer gets 200. A Wolf partner query returns `count=1`. Two GetTableView JSON trees differ in **key set** (not just values) across roles.

**Trap 5 — Match-lock rating versus live rating; finish order versus last round’s team. (temporal × rating × other people’s data)**

- **Wrong:** Glicko inputs are `users.ratingMu` as of the moment the closer runs. Or each round writes a rating change. Or a player’s place is “won last round.” Or two concurrent match closers for the same user both read `ratingVersion=12` and both write `13`.
- **Why it is wrong:** Opponents’ concurrent matches would change the expected score of **this** match after lock. Rating every round lets a player throw round 3 once first place is mathematically locked and dump rating on purpose with extra events. Place is a function of **three-round points plus the published tiebreaks**. Lost update on `ratingVersion` drops a match that actually happened: leaderboard and the audit log disagree.
- **Observation:** Player U plays match A and match B that close 50ms apart (should be impossible under one-seat, but is possible if abort+requeue or if the unique seat constraint is missing). `RatingEvent` has A then B, but `users.ratingMu` equals apply(B only). Or a table that locked at 1510/1490/… is rated against 1560 because U’s other table finished first.

**Trap 6 — Two devices are a race to first-accept, not a merge. (merge × authority)**

- **Wrong:** CRDT or last-write-wins on `votes.payload`. Phone folds (abstain) at T=24.1s, laptop votes seat 2 at T=24.4s, merged state is seat 2. Or a rail spectator’s client is allowed to POST a vote if it has the matchId. Or device B replays an optimistic envelope after the closer ran and the closer “helpfully” inserts it.
- **Why it is wrong:** A vote is an irrevocable ranked action. Spectator authority is watch-only. Late replay is a new write against a closed generation.
- **Observation:** `Vote` unique key missing, two rows for one voter; or a spectator token successfully inserts a vote; or an envelope with `phaseGeneration=3` is accepted while the round is on `phaseGeneration=4`.

### 4. Data model

Types: `id` = 128-bit opaque; `i32`, `f64`, `text`, `bytes`, `timestamptz`, `bool`. Enums listed. Derived fields say **derived**. Unless noted, rows are updatable only on the status/version fields called out.

**users.** `userId` PK; `handle` unique text; `createdAt`; `ratingMu` f64 default 1500; `ratingPhi` f64 default 350; `ratingSigma` f64 default 0.06; `ratingVersion` i32 default 0; `gamesPlayed` i32 default 0; `lastRatedAt` nullable; `displayRating` **derived** = round(`ratingMu`) as i32. Glicko-2 τ = 0.5.

**devices.** `deviceId` PK; `userId` FK; `pushToken` text nullable; `platform` `apns|fcm|none`; `lastSeenAt`; unique `(userId, deviceId)`.

**queue_tickets.** `ticketId` PK; `userId` FK; `status` `waiting|matched|cancelled|expired`; `enqueuedAt`; `expiresAt` = enqueuedAt+90s; `bucketCenter` i32 = displayRating at enqueue; `ratingMuLock` f64; `ratingPhiLock` f64; `matchedMatchId` nullable FK; unique partial `(userId) WHERE status='waiting'`. Time-bounded by `expiresAt`.

**matches.** `matchId` PK; `status` `in_play|complete|aborted`; `seasonId` text (e.g. `2026-H2`); `createdAt`; `lockedAt`; `completedAt` nullable; `abortReason` nullable `entropy_quorum|internal`.

**match_seats.** PK `(matchId, seatIndex)` with `seatIndex in 0..5`; `userId` FK; `startMu`, `startPhi`, `startDisplay` copied at lock; `deaths` i32 default 0; `roundPoints` i32 default 0; `finishPlace` nullable i32 `1..6`; `ratingEventId` nullable. Unique `(matchId, userId)`. Unique partial `(userId) WHERE match.status='in_play'` enforced by joining match status or by `users.inPlayMatchId` nullable FK (either is fine; pick `users.inPlayMatchId` as a denormalized lock column written in the same transaction as seating).

**rounds.** `roundId` PK; `matchId` FK; `roundIndex` `0..2`; unique `(matchId, roundIndex)`; `phase` `entropy|night|day|vote|reveal|complete`; `phaseGeneration` i32; `phaseEndsAt` timestamptz; `commitHash` bytes; `serverSeed` bytes nullable (ACL: system until player-reveal); `combinedSeed` bytes nullable; `dealtAt`, `nightClosedAt`, `voteClosedAt`, `revealedAt`, `railCopiedAt` nullable timestamps.

**shuffle_audits.** `roundId` PK/FK; `commitHash` bytes; `serverSeed` bytes nullable; `nonces` bytes[6] (index = seat; empty 0-length means timeout-fill); `permutation` i32[9] nullable; `combinedSeed` bytes nullable. **Append-only**; nulls filled once by the deal closer, seed copied to rail via rail copy, not by patching ACL on this row for spectators.

**entropy_nonces.** PK `(roundId, seatIndex)`; `userId`; `nonce` bytes (32); `receivedAt`. **Insert-only.**

**round_cards.** PK `(roundId, slot)` `slot in 0..8`; `dealtRole` `wolf|seer|robber|troublemaker|mason|villager|drunk`. **Insert-only.** Slots 0..5 seats, 6..8 center.

**end_roles.** PK `(roundId, slot)`; `endRole` same enum. **Insert-only**, written only by night closer. **Derived** from `round_cards` + ordered `night_effects`, then stored as the legal end-of-night fact.

**night_envelopes.** PK `(roundId, userId)`; `generation` i32; `receivedAt`; `payload` jsonb `{type, targetSeat, centerSlotA, centerSlotB, swapSeatA, swapSeatB}` with `type` `none|seer_player|seer_center|rob|trouble|wolf_center|drunk_center`. **Insert-only.** Read: owner until player-reveal; system always.

**night_effects.** PK `(roundId, seq)` `seq` 1,2,… in canonical order; `kind` `wolf_team|wolf_peek|mason_team|seer_snapshot|robber_swap|troublemaker_swap|drunk_swap`; `viewerUserIds` id[] (who may read this row before player-reveal); `body` jsonb (see operation 7). **Insert-only.**

**votes.** PK `(roundId, userId)`; `generation` i32; `targetSeat` i32 nullable (`null` = abstain, not used: abstain is **no row**); `receivedAt`. **Insert-only.** Self-vote is not stored; it is rejected as a public illegal.

**round_results.** `roundId` PK/FK; `deadSeats` i32[]; `villageWon` bool; `points` i32[6] indexed by seat; `revealedAt`. **Insert-only.** Player-readable from insert. Spectators do not read this table.

**rail_copies.** `roundId` PK/FK; `copiedAt`; `body` jsonb (the public reveal document: dealt, end, effects with no viewer restriction, votes, seed, permutation, deadSeats, villageWon, points). **Insert-only.** Spectator-readable. Written only by PublishRailReveal.

**spectators.** PK `(matchId, userId)`; `joinedAt`; `leftAt` nullable. Does not grant seat rights.

**rating_events.** `ratingEventId` PK; `userId`; `matchId`; `fromVersion` i32; `toVersion` i32; `place` i32; `muBefore`, `phiBefore`, `muAfter`, `phiAfter`; `createdAt`. **Insert-only.**

**leaderboard_rows.** PK `(seasonId, userId)`; `asOf`; `rank` i32; `displayRating` i32; `gamesPlayed` i32. Rebuilt in bulk. **Derived** off users. Eligible: `gamesPlayed ≥ 5` and at least one `rating_events` in this `seasonId`.

**Relationships.** Match 1—6 seats; match 1—3 rounds; round 1—9 dealt cards, 0—9 end roles, 0—6 night envelopes, 0—* night effects, 0—6 votes, 0—1 result, 0—1 rail copy, 0—1 audit. User 0—1 waiting ticket, 0—1 in-play seat, 0—* spectator rows.

**Time-bounded values.** `queue_tickets.expiresAt`; `rounds.phaseEndsAt`; rail delay (`revealedAt+15s` until `rail_copies` exists); season on leaderboard; `serverSeed` secrecy interval; spectator `leftAt`.

### 5. The hard operations

Payload types for night are those on `night_envelopes.payload`. **Timeout-fill nonce** for seat s is `SHA-256(serverSeed || "fill" || matchId || roundIndex || s)` as 32 bytes.

Fisher–Yates stream: `combinedSeed = SHA-256(serverSeed || nonce0 || … || nonce5 || matchId || roundIndex)` where each nonce is 32 bytes (timeout-fill if missing). `permutation` of `[0..8]` via Fisher–Yates; each random integer from `SHA-256(combinedSeed || counter)` as a big-endian 32-bit, counter increments, **rejection sampling** so the range is unbiased. Fixed deck array D = `[wolf,wolf,seer,robber,troublemaker,mason,mason,villager,drunk]`. Slot i receives `D[permutation[i]]`.

---

**Op 1 — EnqueueForMatch** (write)

- **Inputs:** `userId`, `deviceId`.
- **Auth:** caller is that user.
- **Steps:** (1) Abort if `users.inPlayMatchId` is not null. (2) If a `waiting` ticket exists, return it unchanged (idempotent). (3) Insert `queue_tickets` with `bucketCenter = displayRating`, `ratingMuLock/PhiLock` copied from the user row **now**, `expiresAt = now+90s`, `status=waiting`.
- **Atomic:** unique waiting ticket + the read of rating fields.
- **Eventual:** matchmaker pickup.
- **Optimistic UI:** show “in queue, 0:90.” Do not show bucket width or number of other tickets (that count is a structural leak of the pool).
- **Conflict:** unique violation → return the existing waiting ticket. In-play seat → `in_match`.

**Op 2 — CancelQueue** (write)

- **Inputs:** `userId`.
- **Auth:** caller is that user.
- **Steps:** Update ticket `waiting → cancelled` where `userId` and `status=waiting`. If 0 rows, no-op success.
- **Atomic:** the status flip. If matchmaker has already flipped to `matched` in the same instant, 0 rows; caller is seated and CancelQueue does not unseat.
- **Eventual:** none.
- **Optimistic UI:** leave the queue screen; if a match-found push arrives, honor it (the cancel lost).
- **Conflict:** already matched → `already_matched` with `matchId`.

**Op 3 — MatchmakeTick** (background, every 1s)

- **Inputs:** none (system).
- **Auth:** system only.
- **Steps:** (1) Select waiting tickets where `expiresAt > now`, order by `enqueuedAt` asc, limit 2000. (2) For each ticket in that order, let `wait = now - enqueuedAt`. Window half-width W = 200 + 200*floor(wait/15) capped at 1000 (so 200 at 0s, 400 at 15s, …, 1000 at 60s+). (3) Greedy pack: take the oldest ticket T, collect other waiting tickets with `|bucketCenter - T.bucketCenter| ≤ W_T` (use **T’s** window), oldest first, until 6 including T. (4) If fewer than 6, skip T this tick. (5) On 6: in one transaction, update those six tickets `waiting → matched` **only if still waiting**; if not all 6 rows updated, abort this pack and continue. Create `matches` (`in_play`, `lockedAt=now`). Assign `seatIndex` by sorting the six `userId` on `SHA-256(matchId || userId)` ascending (no extra RNG). Insert six `match_seats` with `startMu/startPhi/startDisplay` from **the ticket lock fields**, not from live users. Set `users.inPlayMatchId`. Insert `rounds` index 0, `phase=entropy`, `phaseGeneration=0`, `phaseEndsAt=now+10s`, generate 32-byte `serverSeed` from OS CSPRNG, set `commitHash = SHA-256(serverSeed || "ashrail-v1")`, copy `serverSeed` onto the round row (system-readable). Insert `shuffle_audits` with that commit, seed still system-only. (6) Tickets not packed and `expiresAt ≤ now` → `expired`.
- **Atomic:** each successful pack (6 tickets + match + 6 seats + user locks + round 0 + audit). Packs in one tick are sequential.
- **Eventual:** notify six devices (op 13).
- **Optimistic UI:** none (no client).
- **Conflict:** a CancelQueue between select and update: pack aborts, those users remain as they are.

**Op 4 — SubmitEntropy** (write)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, 32-byte `nonce`.
- **Auth:** caller has a `match_seats` row on this round’s match; `rounds.phase=entropy`; `now ≤ phaseEndsAt+250ms`; `generation` matches.
- **Steps:** Insert `entropy_nonces` for the caller’s seat. Duplicate → `already_acted`.
- **Atomic:** the insert.
- **Eventual:** none.
- **Optimistic UI:** “entropy locked.”
- **Conflict:** unique key; phase closer already advanced → `phase_closed`.

**Op 5 — CloseEntropyAndDeal** (scheduled; fire at `phaseEndsAt` of entropy)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, expected `phaseGeneration`.
- **Auth:** system only.
- **Steps:** (1) Fence: `UPDATE rounds SET phase='night', phaseGeneration=g+1, phaseEndsAt=now+20s, dealtAt=now WHERE roundId=R AND phase='entropy' AND phaseGeneration=g`. 0 rows → return. (2) If this is round 0 of the match and count of `entropy_nonces` + (implicit) connectedness: **quorum** is “≥4 distinct seats have a nonce row.” If `<4`, instead set match `aborted`, clear `inPlayMatchId`, insert new `waiting` tickets for the six (rating lock = **original ticket locks**), skip deal. (3) For each seat without a nonce, compute timeout-fill nonce into the audit array (do not fabricate a user nonce row). (4) Compute `combinedSeed` and `permutation` as specified. Fill `shuffle_audits`. (5) Insert nine `round_cards`. (6) Do not write `end_roles` yet.
- **Atomic:** fence + abort-or-(audit fill + 9 cards + phase night).
- **Eventual:** op 13 pushes “night opened.”
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** second closer loses the fence. Never insert cards without winning the fence.

**Op 6 — SubmitNightAction** (write)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, `payload`.
- **Auth:** seated caller; `phase=night`; generation match; within grace. **Do not** check payload against dealt role.
- **Steps:** (1) Public-illegal → refuse: unknown type name, `targetSeat` not in `0..5` when required by type, `targetSeat` = self for `seer_player|rob`, `swapSeatA=swapSeatB` or either = self for `trouble`, center slots not in `6..8` or not distinct for `seer_center`. Those constraints are **public** (they do not depend on role). (2) Insert envelope. Duplicate → `already_acted`.
- **Atomic:** insert.
- **Eventual:** public submitted-bit (may be a denormalized `nightSubmittedMask` i32 on `rounds` updated in the same transaction).
- **Optimistic UI:** lock in the chosen form locally; do not show others’ payloads.
- **Conflict:** unique key or fence.

**Op 7 — CloseNightAndResolve** (scheduled; fire at night `phaseEndsAt`)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, expected generation.
- **Auth:** system only.
- **Steps:** (1) Fence to `phase='day'`, `phaseEndsAt=now+90s`, `nightClosedAt=now`. 0 rows → return. (2) Load 9 `round_cards` and ≤6 envelopes. Missing envelope = `type=none`. (3) Working map M[0..8] = dealt roles. Let `dealt(seat)=M[seat]` at start (copy). Invalid-for-**dealt**-role payloads: set to `none` (Seer types require dealt seer; `rob` dealt robber; `trouble` dealt troublemaker; `wolf_center` dealt wolf **and** exactly one seat has dealt wolf; `drunk_center` dealt drunk). (4) seq=1: `wolf_team` body `{seats: [seats with dealt wolf]}`, `viewerUserIds` = those seats’ userIds. If that set size = 1 and payload is `wolf_center` with valid slot, also seq++ `wolf_peek` body `{slot, role: dealt(slot)}`, viewer = that one user. (5) seq++ `mason_team` body `{seats: [dealt mason]}`, viewers = those users (size 0, 1, or 2). (6) If seer payload `seer_player`: seq++ `seer_snapshot` body `{kind:'player', seat, role: dealt(seat)}`, viewer = seer. If `seer_center`: body `{kind:'center', slots:[a,b], roles:[dealt(a),dealt(b)]}`, viewer = seer. (7) If rob: swap M[robberSeat] and M[target]; seq++ `robber_swap` body `{from,to,stolen: M[robberSeat] after swap}`, viewer = robber. (8) If trouble: swap M[a] and M[b]; seq++ `troublemaker_swap` body `{a,b}` (no roles), viewer = troublemaker (so they can see their action stuck; they still do not see cards). (9) Drunk: slot = payload.centerSlotA if valid else 6; swap M[drunkSeat] and M[slot]; seq++ `drunk_swap` body `{seat, slot}` **without the new role**, viewer = drunk. (10) Insert `end_roles` from M. (11) Day has no envelopes.
- **Atomic:** fence + all effects + 9 end_roles.
- **Eventual:** pushes; player GetTableView now returns end-of-night **own** role (from `end_roles` of their slot) and their snapshot effects.
- **Optimistic UI:** none. Do not locally apply your rob.
- **Conflict:** fence. Recompute-from-log of the same envelopes must equal stored `end_roles` (audit test).

**Op 8 — SubmitVote** (write)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, `targetSeat`.
- **Auth:** seated; `phase=vote`; generation; grace.
- **Steps:** If `targetSeat` not in 0..5 or equals self → public refuse `illegal_target`. Insert vote. No row means abstain (do not insert a null).
- **Atomic:** insert.
- **Eventual:** public submitted-bit.
- **Optimistic UI:** “vote locked.”
- **Conflict:** unique; phase closed.

Day→vote is a scheduled fence at day `phaseEndsAt`: set `phase='vote'`, `phaseEndsAt=now+25s`. No hidden facts change. Count this **OpenVote** as part of the same closer family as op 7’s clock; implement as a tiny **Op 8a — OpenVote** (scheduled, system, fence only). It is required so vote envelopes cannot arrive during day.

**Op 9 — CloseVoteAndSettle** (scheduled; fire at vote `phaseEndsAt`)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, expected generation.
- **Auth:** system only.
- **Steps:** (1) Fence to `phase='reveal'`, `voteClosedAt=now`, `revealedAt=now`. (2) Tally votes by `targetSeat`. `deadSeats` = all seats with count = max count, or `[]` if no rows. (3) `inPlayWolves` from `end_roles` slots 0..5. Compute `villageWon` per §1. `points[s]=1` iff seat s is winning side. (4) Insert `round_results`. Increment `match_seats.roundPoints` and `deaths` (death +1 if seat in `deadSeats`). (5) Make `serverSeed` player-readable by the existence of `round_results` (ACL rule: seated user may read `rounds.serverSeed` if `revealedAt` is not null). Do not insert `rail_copies` here. Enqueue **Op 9b** at `revealedAt+15s` and enqueue **Op 13**. (6) If `roundIndex < 2`: insert next `rounds` row `phase=entropy`, new CSPRNG `serverSeed`, new commit, `phaseEndsAt=now+8s+10s` (8s reveal pause then 10s entropy; put `phaseEndsAt=now+18s` and let clients show a 8s freeze because `revealedAt` of the previous round is set — simpler: `phaseEndsAt=now+18s` with `phase=entropy` immediately, clients still show previous reveal until they have it). **Product:** 8-second mandatory reveal view, then 10-second entropy: set next round `phase=entropy`, `phaseEndsAt=now+18s`, and **do not accept** SubmitEntropy until `now ≥ previous.revealedAt+8s` (explicit extra predicate). (7) If `roundIndex=2`: compute finish places by §1 tiebreaks. For each seat in place order, append `rating_events` using Glicko-2 **FFA**: place i beats j if `place(i)<place(j)`; no draws because tiebreaks total-order. Update each user with `WHERE ratingVersion=startVersion` matching the version read at the beginning of this rating block; if any user fails the version predicate, abort the **rating subtransaction** and retry from a fresh read of that user only (match is already complete). Set `gamesPlayed += 1`, `inPlayMatchId=null`, `matches.status=complete`, `finishPlace` on seats.
- **Atomic:** fence + result + seat counters + (if last round) match complete + six rating events + six user rating versions. Next-round insert is in the same transaction for non-final rounds.
- **Eventual:** rail copy; leaderboard rebuild; pushes.
- **Optimistic UI:** none for deaths or rating. Clients may animate when GetTableView returns `round_results`.
- **Conflict:** fence; ratingVersion retry.

**Op 9b — PublishRailReveal** (scheduled)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`.
- **Auth:** system only.
- **Steps:** If `round_results` missing, no-op. If `rail_copies` exists, no-op. Insert `rail_copies.body` = full reveal document. Set `rounds.railCopiedAt`.
- **Atomic:** insert copy.
- **Eventual:** spectator GetTableView.
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** unique `roundId` on rail_copies.

**Op 10 — GetTableView** (read) — the load-bearing read

- **Inputs:** `matchId`, caller `userId`.
- **Auth:** any signed-in user. Projection depends on membership.
- **Steps:** Load match, seats, current round, caller’s spectator row, caller’s seat if any. Build a document with a **fixed key set** for all callers:

```
{ matchId, status, seasonId, seats: [{seatIndex, userId, handle, startDisplay, roundPoints, deaths, finishPlace}],
  roundIndex, phase, phaseEndsAt, phaseGeneration, commitHash,
  mySeat, myDealtRole, myEndRole, myNightEnvelope, myVote,
  snapshots: [], submittedNight: [6 bools], submittedVote: [6 bools],
  centerKnown: [{slot, role}|null, null, null],
  reveal: null | RevealDoc,
  railPending: bool }
```

Fill rules: `submitted*` from envelope existence (public). `my*` fields null if caller is not seated. `myDealtRole` from `round_cards` if dealt. `myEndRole` from `end_roles` if night closed. `snapshots` = `night_effects` where caller ∈ `viewerUserIds`, **only if** night has closed (during night, even your own payload is echoed in `myNightEnvelope` but wolf_team does not exist yet). `centerKnown` always length 3; fill a slot only from a snapshot body that names it for this caller. `reveal`: if seated and `round_results` exists, full `RevealDoc` (dealt all 9, end all 9, all effects, all votes, serverSeed, permutation, deadSeats, villageWon, points). If not seated and `rail_copies` exists, the same from `rail_copies.body`. Else `reveal=null` and `railPending=true` iff seated=false and results exist but rail copy does not. Never return another seat’s `myDealtRole`. Never omit keys.
- **Atomic:** read-your-writes for the caller’s own just-inserted envelope.
- **Eventual:** rail copy may lag 15s+job delay. Leaderboard not in this document.
- **Optimistic UI:** client may overlay its in-flight envelope on `myNightEnvelope` until ack.
- **Conflict:** none (read). A caller who compares JSON **key sets** across two accounts must see equality.

**Op 11 — JoinRail** (write)

- **Inputs:** `matchId`.
- **Auth:** signed in. If caller is seated at this match, return success but do not insert a spectator row (their GetTableView is already the player projection).
- **Steps:** Upsert `spectators` with `leftAt=null`.
- **Atomic:** upsert.
- **Eventual:** they poll or subscribe GetTableView.
- **Optimistic UI:** open rail layout immediately; `reveal` stays null until rail copy.
- **Conflict:** none of interest.

**Op 12 — GetLeaderboard** (read)

- **Inputs:** `seasonId`, caller `userId`, `cursor` optional (rank).
- **Auth:** signed in.
- **Steps:** (1) Return `leaderboard_rows` for `seasonId` ordered by `rank` asc, `take 100` from cursor. (2) Caller’s card: if `users.gamesPlayed < 5`, `{eligible:false, rank:null, displayRating}`. Else `{eligible:true, rank, displayRating, gamesPlayed}` from `leaderboard_rows` for that user (not computed from a friends filter). If the snapshot is missing (brand new 5th win), `rank=null` with `asOf` of the table and the client text “rank updates within 60s” — **not** a live `COUNT(*)` over a visibility-filtered users query.
- **Atomic:** none required.
- **Eventual:** snapshot lag ≤ 60s (op 14).
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** none.

**Op 13 — DispatchPhasePushes** (background, **external**: APNs/FCM)

- **Inputs:** `roundId`, `kind` `entropy|night|vote|reveal|match_found|aborted`.
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:** (1) Seats for the match. (2) Devices with `lastSeenAt < phase-open − 2s` and a `pushToken`. (3) Send `{kind, matchId, phaseEndsAt}` **without roles, without payloads, without seed**. (4) Treat vendor 4xx invalid token by clearing `pushToken`. Vendor outage is logged; it does not extend `phaseEndsAt`.
- **Atomic:** none with the game fence (push is after commit).
- **Eventual:** the whole op.
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** duplicate send is allowed (clients dedupe by `phaseGeneration`).
- **External boundary:** the vendor is a dumb pipe. If a misbuilt payload included `dealtRole`, that is a secrecy bug in **this** operation, not in APNs.

**Op 14 — RebuildLeaderboard** (bulk periodic, every 60s)

- **Inputs:** `seasonId`.
- **Auth:** system.
- **Steps:** (1) From `users` where `gamesPlayed ≥ 5`, compute `rank` by invariant 15. (2) Replace `leaderboard_rows` for that season in one transaction (write to a new `asOf` set then swap, or truncate-insert). N is ~80k eligible at scale; this is an all-rows rewrite of a small table, not a scan of round history.
- **Atomic:** the swap of the snapshot.
- **Eventual:** GetLeaderboard.
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** overlapping rebuilds: take `FOR UPDATE` on a `season_locks` row (`seasonId` PK, `rebuildStartedAt`).

### 6. Scale and cost

Successful deployment, not a toy:

| Thing | Size / rate |
|---|---|
| Accounts | 250,000 |
| Season-eligible (`gamesPlayed≥5`) | 80,000 |
| Peak in-play matches | 1,500 (9,000 seated) |
| Peak queue waiting | 4,000 tickets |
| Peak rail | 25,000, of which one streamer match holds 8,000 |
| Match duration | ~3 × (10+20+90+25+8)s ≈ 7.5 min + entropy fills |
| Peak match starts | 1,500 / 450s ≈ **3.3/s** (20 seats/s) |
| Peak round deals / night closes / vote closes | **~10/s each** |
| GetTableView | 9,000 seats × 2 Hz + 25,000 rail × 0.5 Hz ≈ **30,000/s** |
| Leaderboard reads | 200/s |
| `round_cards` rows/day | 3.3 matches/s × 3 rounds × 9 × 86400 ≈ **7.7e6** |
| Envelopes+votes/day | ~5e6 |
| `rating_events`/day | 3.3 × 6 × 86400 ≈ **1.7e6** |

**What gets expensive**

- **GetTableView at 30k/s** is a per-caller computed document (role, snapshots, two reveal classes). A shared cached JSON for the match is **wrong** (trap 4). Cache key must be `(matchId, roundIndex, phaseGeneration, railCopiedAt, viewerClass, userId)` and still split player vs rail.
- **Streamer fan-out:** one `rail_copies` insert invalidates 8,000 subscribers at once. That is the hot write. Player reveal must not ride that bus.
- **MatchmakeTick** scanning 4,000 waiting tickets every second is cheap; the cost is **contention** on the oldest tickets (everyone tries to pack the same head). Keep one matchmaker leader.
- **RebuildLeaderboard** sorting 80k users every 60s is cheap CPU; serving “my rank” as a live `COUNT` against 80k on every profile view is the expensive-and-wrong alternative.
- **Glicko FFA** at match close: 15 pairwise updates × 6 players, 3.3 matches/s — fine. Retry on `ratingVersion` is rare if the one-seat lock holds.
- **Night resolve** is O(1) (9 slots). It is **latency**-sensitive (must finish before day UI), not throughput-sensitive.
- **Audit table growth** is append-only ~O(10^7) rows/day; hide behind roundId point reads, never “scan shuffles of the season” in a user path.
- **Push vendor:** 9,000 potentially stale devices × 5 phase transitions per match is a large external bill if you push everyone; the `lastSeenAt` filter is what makes it affordable.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**1. Per-caller projection of the same nine cards, with a fixed key set.** Ordinary “query rows you own” yields either (a) only your role row, so wolf_team and mason_team and “wolves remaining” become counts of `{0,1}`, or (b) all role rows, i.e. the deck is public. The product needs **written snapshots with a viewer list** plus a document assembler that nulls rather than omits. *Concession:* drop hidden partners (no wolf/mason teams, Seer peeks face-up). That is a different game. *Cheat:* keep the marketing (“hidden roles, ranked”) and ship owner-only rows with a client that “just doesn’t render” other people’s fields.

**2. Randomness as a committed, system-only input; deal is a pure function.** Ordinary mutations create card rows from client data or from `RANDOM()`. Here a CSPRNG seed is written **before** nonces, hashed, then combined, then expanded with unbiased Fisher–Yates, then **never drawn again**. Reconnect is a read. *Concession:* a casual party mode with host-dealt cards and no rating. *Cheat:* ranked matches that insert roles without a public `commitHash` that verifies after reveal.

**3. Scheduled closers with generation fencing and canonical batch apply.** Ordinary backends have user writes; they do not have a system actor that, at `phaseEndsAt`, atomically consumes up to six sealed rows, applies a **non-commutative scripted order**, and publishes derived facts. Host-closes and client-side night order fail ranked integrity. *Concession:* untimed, host-facilitated Night Six (a tabletop helper). *Cheat:* ranked clocks where the first player into `phase=day` runs the resolver in application code without a fence.

**4. Two-step publish (player reveal write, then rail copy) instead of `now() > revealedAt+15s` in the read ACL.** Clock-in-the-read-rule races with replica time and with spectators who query early. The unusual-but-honest form is **another scheduled insert** (`rail_copies`) that **is** the spectator capability. *Concession:* no rail, or rail only on completed matches. *Cheat:* live spectator JSON that includes `end_roles` “because they promised not to look,” or a delay enforced only in a first-party client.

**5. Global rank and match-lock snapshots as unfiltered, versioned facts.** Rank is `topK` + dense position over ~80k rows, not over readable rows. Rating apply is a compare-and-swap on `ratingVersion` plus an append-only event. *Concession:* show percentile buckets (top 1/5/10%) updated hourly, and rate using live ratings. Hourly buckets are honest if advertised. *Cheat:* claim live global #4123 computed from friends-visible users, or rate the match against post-match μ of opponents who finished another table first.

**Softening that is a cheat, restated.** If you still claim (i) hidden partners and peeks, (ii) ranked fairness, (iii) reconnect identity of the deal, (iv) a rail that does not spoil, and (v) a global rating number, then snapshots, committed shuffle, system closers, rail copies, and unfiltered rank are not optional polish. They are the product.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice:** Glicko-2; sit-and-go matchmaking with expanding rating windows; server-authoritative phase clocks and grace; first-write-wins unique action keys; reconnect as read; spectator delay as a **publish** rather than a clocked ACL; append-only hand history; TrueSkill/Glicko FFA via pairwise expansion; push as a dumb channel; leaderboard materialization; abort-on-quorum-fail.

**Standard genre rules, specified tightly rather than invented:** six-player hidden-role night with Seer / Robber / Troublemaker / Drunk / Wolves / Masons / center cards is the One Night social-deduction family. Win when wolves are out of play only if nobody dies is that family’s usual empty-wolf case. Canonical night order is that family’s usual order. This document **closes** the rules (nine-card deck, tie-all-die, Drunk default slot 6, no Tanner/Drunk-as-hidden-identity extra roles, no daytime talk structure) so an implementer does not guess.

**Invented for the exercise (to keep the axis load-bearing, not to be cute):** the 15-second **rail_copies** table as a first-class capability boundary; refusing role-illegal night actions only at resolve; timeout-fill nonces bound to the committed seed (so a missing nonce cannot stall or bias); seat assignment from `SHA-256(matchId || userId)` instead of a second RNG; match-lock Glicko snapshots on the ticket; GetTableView’s **fixed key set**; three-round match with total order tiebreaks so rating never needs draws; entropy quorum abort of four.

**Not invented, but often skipped in shallow apps, and here mandatory:** shuffle commit-and-reveal; role-as-of intervals; wolf/mason **written** teams; Seer snapshots by value.

I have not run this game, not proven the Glicko pairwise encoding is the best FFA rule, and not load-tested 8,000-rail fan-out. The traps are checkable by the observations in §3; those do not depend on production traffic.
