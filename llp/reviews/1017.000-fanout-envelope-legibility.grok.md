- **§3.4.** `E_STEPS`, `E_OPERATOR_REQUIRED`, and the 0.0.4 enriched-lane observation are routed instead of swallowed. That is disciplined scope.
- **§4 anti-goal and §5 last bullet.** The diary sentence is a precise anti-goal. Refusing to claim grok-4.6 would have scored 82 is the honest ceiling for N=1.
- **Astra shape in §1.** The `first 500` merge and the chunk-job digest are concrete enough that D2’s celebrity paragraph has a real pattern to generalize, and the 167,157-action green replay is the right kind of existence proof that the envelope is expressible.

## Concerns

**1. Blocker — D1 is an example, not a spec, and does not compose with the parent diagnostic.**  
1017 §3.3 wraps every reason as `` `for` over … at `<site>` may end before its range does: `<reason>` ``. Reasons in that table are short clauses. D1 drops a multi-sentence tutorial (caps, metering, 51st insert, three alternatives, two guide pointers) into `<reason>`. The result is ungrammatical and unscannable; the RFC never says whether this text is the reason, a new hint field, the rewrite field, or a change to the wrapper. The same section never gives the format string (`{table}`, `{columns}`, `{n}`, `{n+1}`). The worked text says “51st,” which is `n+1` only for the grok loop. A faithful implementer can hardcode 51, leave the guide page at `bound/loop`, or emit a paragraph that no fixture grammar expects — and still believe they implemented D1.  
**Resolve:** Replace the example with a full amended 1017 §3.3 table: per-arm `reason` template, `rewrite` template, and which diagnostic field carries teaching. Keep `<reason>` a clause. If alternatives must appear at the refusal, add an explicit third field or state that they live only in the structured guide target. Interpolate `n` and `n+1`; add fixtures at `n ∈ {1, 50, 500}`.

**2. Blocker — the error → guide join, which is the actual “at the refusal” path, is unspecified.**  
1017 plumbed `E_STRUCTURAL_FANOUT` as family `bound`, page `bound/loop`, with `protocol.rs::certification_family_guide` called out so it does not fall through to `opaque/subset`. D1’s prose names `guide bounds` and `guide fanout`. D2 adds `fanout.md`. Nothing says which single page the structured `guide` field points at, what happens to `bound/loop` (already shared with `E_UNBOUND_LOOP`, and whose text “covers both”), or how a builder who follows the code’s guide link rather than a parenthetical in the reason ever reaches D2. Acceptance §4 requires that the refusal *and the topics it anchors* suffice. That join is currently undefined.  
**Resolve:** One sentence in D1: `E_STRUCTURAL_FANOUT`’s published guide page becomes `fanout` (or stays `bound/loop` and `fanout.md` is only cross-linked — pick one). List the plumbing rows that change (`families.source.json`, `diagnostics.ts`, `protocol.rs`). State what `bound/loop` still teaches so it cannot keep the old fan-out story.

**3. Major — the field evidence is real; the causal lead in §1 is not the best reading of it.**  
The quotes do not show a builder who thought “50 is a mysterious platform maximum and therefore I am stuck.” They show a builder who inferred, correctly, that a `cap` on `followed` would refuse the celebrity population and the scale log, then called that a platform gap, and separately treated merge lanes as capped at 50 without a merge refusal (this rule is a mutation-loop check; a `followingFeed` query never hits `fanout.rs`). Diary line: *“no documented way to fan out digest `newPostsInFollowing` to an uncapped follower set.”* Essay: write-time fan-out without that cap cannot express 500/900, *“a platform gap, not an authoring miss.”* That is failure (c), plus (b) for the merge number. Bracketing “50 is the number you chose” does not unlock the digest: `cap 500 by followed` is still the wrong shape.  
§1’s line *“The difference was not capability but legibility”* is also stronger than two different models at different effort (medium vs xhigh) can bear, and it collides with §5’s non-claim. “Nearly the entire gap” between 67 and Convex’s 80 is asserted without the rubric.  
**Resolve:** Lead with (c): the refusal was correct and complete as a *no*, incomplete as a *where instead*. Keep (a) as a real defect (a bare pasted N *is* a platform-shaped claim) but not as the mechanism that forfeited the milestone. Soften §1 to “the documented miss was failing to see the paved shapes,” which already matches §5. Drop or source the overall-score attribution. Land the run under `benchmarks/runs/` (or hash the four quotes) so the evidence is not only a fleet path.

**4. Major — D1’s proposed message does not contain the sentence the RFC says is load-bearing.**  
D2: *“the lane bound is yours; there is no platform lane constant.”* D1 names `merge` / `maintain` / `chunk` but never says a merge lane bound is an author envelope subject to the read budget. Grok’s final report bundled *“following-feed merge and any follower fan-out are capped at 50.”* A builder who reads only the refusal can still conclude merge is a 50-lane recipe.  
**Resolve:** If teaching must work at the refusal, the no-cap reason or hint must say that read-side lane bounds are author-declared and are not the echoed N. If that sentence is too long for the wrapper, the structured guide target must be `fanout` and acceptance (a) must not claim the refusal text alone is enough.

**5. Major — D2 does not actually edit the smoking-gun `merge.md` lines.**  
The complete `merge.md` in the packet is: worked following-feed `first 50`; *“The recipe admits 50 follows; more is `capped`.”* That sentence is a false identification of a query bound with a schema cap. The primary worked example also stays at 50, so the pattern-match in (b) survives.  
**Resolve:** Make D2 specify the replacement sentences, not a delta. Rewrite or delete “more is `capped`.” Either change the merge exemplar to `first 500` with the read-budget caveat, or keep 50 only as an illustration that 50 is *a* choice. **6. Major — stale LLP pointers in other 1017 §3.3 arms are in the same defect class as the one D1 fixes, and are left live.**

## Suggestions

- Prefer a *short* no-cap reason that does (a) — `` `{table} has no cap over {columns}; {n} is this loop's bound, not a platform maximum` `` — and do (c) by retargeting the structured guide page to `fanout`. That matches how the parent diagnostic was designed (reason + rewrite + guide page) and avoids fighting the colon-wrapper. Put metering and the celebrity anti-pattern in `fanout.md`, not in `<reason>`.
- In `fanout.md`, make the decision table a procedure, not a list of four mechanisms: feed projection → merge-on-read; per-group counter you could cap → `cap` + loop; per-group counter you must not cap → `maintain` and/or `chunk` job; whole-table / backfill → `chunk` job. Astra’s digest should appear as maintain+job, not as “or merge.”
- State in D1 that merge/maintain/chunk are not pasteable one-liners, which is why they are not the rewrite until J3 can paste `enqueue job { … }`. That closes open question 3 without preempting 1016.
- Use the medium-vs-xhigh split as evidence *for* a teaching bug (more budget did not find the door) without claiming equal capability. That is stronger than “not capability but legibility” and still honest.
- When rewriting `merge.md`, say what “more” does: extra follows are omitted from this query’s lanes, they are not refused at insert, and a larger `first` is the author’s envelope until `check` refuses the read budget.
