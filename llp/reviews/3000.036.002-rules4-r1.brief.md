insert and delete writes. Keep N_WRITE_DENIED for proved impossible admitted
paths and deny/omitted rules. No unknown expression becomes a contradiction.

caller-filtered program probe proves the same physical rule probe, including
additional equality tests; the reverse implication is never used. Normalize
keys through real unique indexes and compare known scalar/snapshot terms.

invalidate the witnessed rows; jobs remain exempt. Unknown or oversized
branch products stay quiet. The advisory never changes admission or runtime.
