## 16. PAGE3: finish the ordinary private-page plans

Commissioned 2026-10-13; implementation in this worktree, review pending.
PAGE2 left 18 notes in 11 of the 50 odd wave-10 final schemas: 12
party/static-grant pages, three compound rules, two hidden parent sources
and one pure membership. This amendment extends the existing plan rather
than asking builders to merge lists themselves. It changes no read authority,
replicated data or runtime resource ceiling.

**Static grants and whole-group reads.** An owner-or-readable-static-grant
rule uses the existing party/grant merge on synced union tables too. The
server evaluates the positive readable grant once; its holder reads the
original whole range, everyone else the party ranges. An explicit `first` or
`last` is a page bound, not a table cap: a role holder may page an unbounded
table, under the usual request meter. An implicit whole-group read is expanded
only if its original fixed prefix has an applicable stored cap or fully
pinned total unique, at or below the examined ceiling. That original bound
applies to the union. A cap on owner does not bound a staff's whole table.
A fully pinned total unique may supply a one-row lane with the implicit id
position, even when its physical index has no unpinned component.

**Full union devices.** The same descriptor and scope serve server and device
cursors. On a device a full union target's grant lane reads its held authorized
rows, with the ordinary acquisition, coverage and meter checks; it does not
probe a remote grant. That held range covers every other target lane, so the
others are not opened. Missing acquisition stays unknown. Online-only and
single-arm narrowed sync retain their existing grant deferral. This amends
LLP 3000.036.000.000 §5.3's planner restriction, while preserving its holding
and acquisition invariants.

**Pure membership.** Remove the syntactic party requirement from §15. A rule
`exists members[.id, viewer]` or `exists members[.parent, viewer]` has a system
range and exact membership descriptors, no party descriptors. All source
readability, unique/cap proofs, total filters, index/key bounds, shared
positions and discovery/head fences remain. If the source discovery is cut,
no target rows or cursor are emitted. An empty complete discovery means an
empty complete page. Point uniqueness still does not cap memberships.

**Positive compound rules.** Distribute positive `and`/`or` into at most seven
alternatives plus the original system range. Each alternative retains all
of its row equality conditions. It contains either one exact membership
witness, or row equality pins and positive row-independent guards. A membership
conjunct supplies precisely its own authority; all remaining conjuncts must
be true by the target lane's pins. A pinned membership target instead uses
a readable page guard, without discovering hidden source rows. No negated
probe, arithmetic, fallible predicate or second varying source component is
admitted. Candidates are speculative and fall back when the physical plan
or bound-IR validator cannot prove the whole admitted shape.

A new guarded-range descriptor evaluates `page_guard_decision` on its own
pins. A true readable guard opens only that range, not the other alternatives;
a false guard opens none. It does not cover other lanes. The system range
covers every lane only for the system. Status/party lanes, discovered parent
lanes and guarded lanes share one ordered id-tied position and deduplicate
there, as in §§3–6. Thus if `R = ∨i (Pi ∧ Gi ∧ Mi)` and W is the total page
predicate, each lane returns exactly `W ∧ Pi ∧ Gi ∧ Mi`; their union is W ∧ R.
Guard facts and membership sources must be readable, as in §15, so neither
empty results nor completeness reveal hidden witness rows. Read-meter cuts
and frontier rules are unchanged; heap exhaustion remains a refusal.

A nullable small enum's `field != null` has a finite positive expansion into
its declared non-null values. This retains null exclusion exactly; ordinary
nullable inequalities against other values keep their original scan. The
wave-10 archive with four species therefore gets four enum ranges plus its
owner, readable reviewer and system ranges. Compound volunteer-open and
parent-teacher/status rules preserve each status condition when opening lanes.

**Readable static predicates.** A `where` disjunction may repeat a readable
static point, such as `.gleaner = viewer or exists coordinators[viewer]`.
The point must be a total unique with fixed terms, readable by those pins or
its identical self-witness rule, without a lifetime or read-through boundary.
It remains in the original predicate for every caller, including the system.
The physical point can cut even though its logical predicate is total. The
branch frontier therefore excludes an interrupted filter candidate; it may
continue only from an earlier fully evaluated candidate, so retry cannot
skip the interrupted row. A source its holders cannot read keeps its scan.

**Compatibility and model.** `branch-pages-v2` fences guarded descriptors,
held-union merge behavior and fully pinned point-lane admission; it is derived
from bound plans, serialized as an optional operation fact, and older engines
refuse it through the ordinary handshake. Existing descriptor bytes remain
unchanged when none of these new lane types is present. Membership-only plans
retain the existing membership capability rules. The Lean surface model
removes its party requirement, and models the admitted positive rule ranges;
unsupported fragments remain explicitly named rather than accepted by the
oracle. Its logical leaf soundness/coverage proof includes pure membership.

**Hidden-source boundary and exact repairs.** PAGE3 does not scan private
parent rows whose owner cannot necessarily read them. No cap/index alone
repairs that absence proof. Copy the parent's immutable owner onto the child,
keep that copy immutable, and check the copy against the parent in the child's
insert rule (and in every trusted seed/backfill). Replace the parent-owner
read-rule leaf with the copied owner comparison. The copy preserves authority
only while the parent owner is immutable; a mutable owner needs transactional
propagation or the original rule. Existing data must be backfilled before
adoption. The diagnostic names the actual table, reference and owner column
and supplies exact source edits. It never recommends making parents public.
