## New defect

### N1 — Medium: zero-count rows permanently consume the global routing-accounting capacity

**Location:** [feeds.md:234](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/feeds.md:234), with the cap at [feeds.md:94](snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/feeds.md:94).

Unfollowing the last shared author leaves a `pullCounts` row with `used: 0`. No recipe operation deletes it.

**Concrete scenario:** One promoted author retains its self-route. Over time, 31,999 distinct readers follow and then unfollow that author. Only one pull route remains, but all 32,000 count-row slots are occupied. The next new reader’s follow inserts its route, then fails the count-row upsert and rolls back. Ordinary unfollows cannot recover this capacity. Readers need not register as authors to follow, so the 1,000-author cap does not prevent this scenario.

**Smallest fix:** Permit reader-owned count deletion and delete the count row when removing its final route; otherwise decrement it. Add a churn-at-cap regression demonstrating that a departed reader releases the accounting slot.
