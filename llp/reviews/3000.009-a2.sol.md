## Initial pass — NEEDS REVISION

The reviewer followed lead-owned corrections while reviewing and identified
four material defects at the pins below:

1. **HIGH, stale supervised claims across adoption** (`6471f5f68`). Worker
   authentication and claiming were separated by an engine unlock. Adoption
   published new work before the old child's service family was revoked;
   an old poll could take it, run stale code or terminally reject the new
   contract. Fixed in `8d66c9be4`: persisted supervised-session revision,
   authentication/fence/claim under the same engine guard as activation,
   and revocation before process-stop delay. Manual seats remain independent.
2. **MEDIUM, unchecked external dependency changes** (`3b71c9aee`). The
   checker followed external imports/config but the watcher did not. Fixed
   in `6471f5f68`: checker-observed graph and failed-resolution candidates
   are watched even after failed checks. Real dev edits/deletion/refill,
   missing-import and config recovery have an HTTP/child-process witness.
3. **MEDIUM, scheduled service rule mismatch** (`0e5c84935`). A validly named
   service could pass check but fail its action's rule at runtime. Fixed in
   `3b71c9aee`: the binder invokes the same pure admission rule under that
   seat, with accepted/refused rule cases.
4. **MEDIUM, media result bounds/shape** (`8d66c9be4`). A result such as
   `{"photo":{"id":"x","bytes":"not-a-number","secret":"leak"}}`
   could settle under `image <=1`. Fixed in `357c29d39`: recursive exact
   supplied-fact matching against the asset ledger, actual kind/byte bound,
   unknown/extra-field refusal, no added metadata or byte grant, and store
   failures propagated instead of classified as invalid data.

The reviewer reported no follow-up-only finding remaining. Its initial
verdict remained NEEDS REVISION pending separate bounded delta qualification.
