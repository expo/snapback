**6. M5 Disappearing messages — refused-with-rewrite to schema fence plus effect.**

```ts

```

`E_TIME_DEPENDENT_RULE messages.read — row rules must be snapshot-pure; Date.now() changes visibility without a traced event. Declare messages.visibility({notAfter:"notAfter",onExpire:"tombstone"}) and sweep byNotAfter through a bounded mutation. Passing caller time is not a repair.`

Rerun-and-diff first hurts Following: celebrity events invalidate many subscriptions whose merges can each examine 11k rows. V1 coalesces by version and shares actor-head subplans. Failure there promotes the merge to IVM without changing source.

### 3. The spike

Build one vertical repository in seven days; any failed item falsifies the converged design rather than earning another query flavor.

- [ ] **Freeze semantics.** Write the IR, value model, ordering/overflow rules, certificate schema, and trace/receipt schema in at most two implementation-facing documents. Pass: no operation-specific opcode. Fail: any Clique operation needs host JavaScript or unspecified semantics.
- [ ] **Compile real TypeScript.** Lower `thread`, `send`, Relay `boardView`, maintained `boardSummary`, Split `ledger`, bounded `ancestors`, and both shape-keyed and computed-key dependent reads. Pass: helpers inline without losing provenance or bounds. Fail: manual plan annotations are required.
- [ ] **Refuse honestly.** Golden-test `Unbound.count`, dynamic tables, `Date`, `while`, recursion, unbounded helper calls, hidden-value escape, and bounded-input allocation explosion. Pass: each diagnostic names the cost and a runnable safe repair. Fail: `G` can be bypassed through strings, arrays, or helpers.
- [ ] **Execute twice.** Run the IR in Rust inside a real SQLite transaction and in the TypeScript client evaluator on Hermes/JSC/V8. Pass: a frozen corpus byte-matches result, trace, errors, overflow, and collation. Fail: any semantic mismatch or hydration replay disagreement.
- [ ] **Prove closure behavior.** Hydrate a private thread, its policy verdict, and N+1/N+2 evidence; predict an offline send; introduce a computed mention; delete twice from a full page; revoke membership before reconnect. Pass: exact, then `partial`, then authoritative rejection/purge occur at the specified boundaries. Fail: invented completeness, hidden-range leakage, or queued-write authorization from a verdict.
- [ ] **Exercise maintenance.** Test insert/delete/group move, retry, overflow, online backfill with concurrent changes, and a 50-recipient send. Pass: same-commit totals and idempotent recovery. Fail: drift or unbounded write work.
- [ ] **Measure and observe agents.** Replay 100k messages while live subscriptions run, then give two fresh agents only the one-page grammar and ask for chat, Relay, and Split. Pass: bounds remain population-independent, live latency stays within target, and neither agent reaches for `effect`, `public`, or larger caps to escape a diagnostic. Fail: special cases, stalled subscriptions, or materially worse correction loops than the Convex control.

### 4. What to keep from Snapback as built

**Survive unchanged**

- `crates/snapback-build-identity`: content-derived generation identity is exactly the rebuild’s custody primitive.
- `snapback-kernel` SQLite connection/version journal: pinned snapshots, one transactional writer, and monotone versions remain the authoritative base.
- `crates/snapback-sync/src/ephemeral.rs`: separate TTL realtime state is the right typing/transport boundary.
- `snapback-harness`, `snapback-acceptance`, LLP 0189, and LLP 0201: green receipts, affected verification, and the Clique benchmark remain the qualification system.

**Survive as ideas, re-implemented**

- `packages/snapback-codegen`: keep digest-verified generation and typed diagnostics; replace profiles/manifests with the TS-subset compiler and certificate.
- `crates/snapback-sync`: keep resumable snapshots, range routing, and coalescing; rebuild around traced ranges rather than current RQS fragments.
- `materialization-store*.mjs`, `local-read-admission.mjs`, and `optimistic-rebase.mjs`: retain local custody and settlement concepts; replace them with the versioned range store, verdict cache, evaluator, and eight states.
- `outbox-shared.mjs` and `snapback-kernel/storage/settlement.rs`: retain durable order, identities, and authoritative settlement; adapt envelopes to mutation IR and prediction receipts.
- LLP 0053/0059 auth epochs and retention fences: re-express as scoped verdict expiry, `maxOfflineAge`, reconnect purge, and store-key destruction.
- `projection_rebuild.rs` and current maintained-projection work: generalize into compiler-owned `maintain.count/sum` with proved backfill and move semantics.
- `crates/snapback-activation`: retain atomic generation activation, but bind schema, plans, rules, evaluator, and generated clients as one identity.

**Delete**

- `crates/snapback-runtime` query/mutation Hermes/Ibex and workerd/V8 execution paths: application JavaScript leaves the authoritative data plane.
- The query-flavor/profile matrix in `packages/snapback-codegen`: one bounded language replaces named shape products.
- Authored read/write/retainability manifests, elevated reads, and handler-declared dependency graphs: certificates and traces derive them.
- Current RQS/QMK fragment, AuthorizedLease, and per-query completeness machinery: the normalized range store plus scoped verdicts replaces them.
- App-authored optimistic reducers and specialized `live_delta` eligibility: the mutation IR and client evaluator own prediction and rerun behavior.

### 5. Residual risk

1. **The restricted TypeScript may be an uncanny imitation rather than a good language.** First-month signal: fresh agents repeatedly hit provenance/cardinality diagnostics, enlarge `take`/`G`, weaken rules, or move query work into effects; time-to-green and correction loops lose materially to the Convex control.

2. **Client exactness may fail at the evaluator/authorization boundary.** First-month signal: any cross-engine result/trace mismatch, hydration replay quarantine, hidden-verdict fuzz escape, or more than a small minority of ordinary offline transitions becoming unexpectedly `partial`.

3. **Bounded reads may merely move the scaling failure into writes and reruns.** First-month signal: 50-member sends contend on maintained rows, 500-follow merges dominate subscription CPU, authority-partitioned search backfills lag, or the 100k replay cannot keep subscriptions within the declared latency target.
