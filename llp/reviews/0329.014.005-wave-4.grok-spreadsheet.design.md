### 1. Product contract

Harbor is a collaborative spreadsheet that companies use as a live operational database. Commissions, inventory, headcount plans, pipeline, FX-converted board packs, and warehouse counts all live in workbooks whose formulas *are* the business logic. It is not a grid UI over a hidden SQL schema. The grid, the formulas, and the calculated numbers *are* the database.

**Who uses it**

- **Workbook owner / IT**: creates workbooks, billing, share-links, feed secrets, UDF deploy.
- **Elevated author (FP&A, controller)**: writes formulas that may read the whole workbook, including salary and margin columns others must not see, and publishes totals onto dashboard sheets.
- **Department editor (sales ops, warehouse, recruiting)**: edits literals and formulas only inside granted rectangles.
- **Range-restricted contractor**: sees and edits a slice (for example pipeline columns A–F); must not learn salary, cost, or hidden-sheet presence through values, counts, or error types.
- **Exec viewer**: reads dashboard sheets on phone and laptop; never edits.
- **Field user**: iPad in a warehouse or airplane; edits for hours offline; expects merge without corrupting the model.
- **Integration operator**: attaches HTTP/CSV/SQL feeds that dump into feed-owned ranges on a schedule.

A successful workbook looks like “FY26 Comp”: 18 sheets, millions of populated cells, a commission rate in `Assumptions!B4` that fans out to hundreds of thousands of dependents, month tabs selected with `INDIRECT($A$1&"!B2")`, an interest block that iterates, `TODAY()` in aging columns, a Salesforce dump every 60 seconds, and 40 people in the file at month close.

**User-visible promises (18)**

1. A cell holds a literal or a formula. What people read is a **computed value** produced by a workbook-wide calculation, not a number the last editor typed into a “value” field (unless the cell is a literal).
2. Editing a cell updates every dependent cell. If that is 620,000 cells, all 620,000 update. The product does not cap fan-out as a feature.
3. Formulas include ordinary refs and ranges, cross-sheet refs, named ranges, `INDEX`/`MATCH`/`XLOOKUP`, `SUMIF`/`SUMIFS`/`SUMPRODUCT`, `FILTER`/`UNIQUE`/`SORT` (they **spill**), `INDIRECT`, `OFFSET`, `ROW`/`COLUMN`, and a Sheets-like `QUERY` over a range. **Data-dependent references are ordinary month-close practice**, not a power-user corner.
4. `INDIRECT("A5")` is a string. Inserting a row above row 5 does **not** rewrite that string. A real `A5` ref in an AST **does** shift. After an insert, those two diverge, matching Excel.
5. Circular references iterate when the workbook has iteration on: default `max_iterations = 100`, `change_threshold = 0.001`, evaluation order inside a cycle is (sheet order, row, col). The seed for the first iteration is the previous committed values of those cells (0 if never calculated). The seed is recorded so the run is reproducible.
6. `RAND`/`RANDBETWEEN` are **sticky**: they do not reshuffle because someone edited an unrelated cell. They redraw only if that cell’s formula text changes, a direct precedent of that cell changes, or a user runs Recalc All. Every viewer of a committed calculation sees the same random numbers.
7. `NOW()` is the timestamp of the calculation run, identical for every `NOW()` in that run. `TODAY()` is that timestamp’s calendar date in the workbook’s IANA timezone and **changes at local midnight even if nobody edits**.
8. External feeds write **literals into a feed-owned range** on a schedule. Users reference the dump; they do not type into it. A failed fetch keeps last-good values and marks the range stale; it does not blank the model.
9. User-defined functions are pure in their arguments (timeout 100 ms) unless declared feed-shaped, in which case they are cached by `(name, args)` and refreshed like feeds. UDFs cannot open the grid behind the caller’s back.
10. Named ranges are first-class, versioned, and follow Excel grow/shift rules on insert/delete. Formulas bind the name, not a frozen rectangle copied at parse time.
11. Range permissions are a **secrecy boundary**, not a polite “please don’t edit.” A principal without read on a cell does not receive its value, formula text, or computed number. Hidden sheets they cannot read do not appear in the sheet list.
12. Two formula classes exist and are visible in the UI: **user formulas** (sandbox: may only read cells the formula’s authority principal can read; otherwise `#PERM!`) and **elevated formulas** (full workbook read; only elevated authors can write them). This is how a dashboard total of salaries can be published without letting a contractor write `=HR!H5`.
13. A user formula that aggregates a range it cannot fully read returns `#PERM!`. It does **not** sum/count the visible subset. Partial aggregates are lies.
14. Many people edit at once. Each workbook has a single sequencer. You see other people’s committed calculations, their cursors, and (on cells you can read) that a cell is dirty and calculating. You never see a viewport that mixes two calculation runs (new `A1` with old `B1 = A1*2`).
15. Offline: a device may apply up to 5,000 local ops over 8 hours. On reconnect, content and structural ops merge; **computed values are not merged** — they are thrown away and recalculated from the merged formulas, literals, sticky volatiles, iteration seeds, and latest feed snapshots. Same-cell content conflicts surface; the grid does not silently become a per-device calculator.
16. Multi-device: laptop, phone, and iPad converge to the same computed values for a given calc run. Undo is **this principal’s inverse op**, not a global rewind. If someone else already overwrote that cell, undo does not clobber them; it offers the old value in a conflict tray.
17. History: every content change is kept. Frozen snapshots (manual, or nightly) store numbers as they were shown, with the calc-run metadata needed to explain them. Restoring *formulas* and pressing calculate uses **today’s** `TODAY()`, feeds, and ACLs, unless the user asks for a frozen-values copy. Those two are different buttons.
18. Scale: 10 million populated cells per workbook, 200 sheets, 1,000 named ranges, 500 range grants. A single rate-cell edit that dirties 620,000 formulas is in-contract. Viewport of ~1,000 cells paints in under 200 ms from a committed calc run; a 620k recalc finishes in 2 seconds for on-screen cells (rest of the closure in the same calc run, under 30 seconds), or the UI stays on the previous consistent run with a “Calculating…” banner.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

1. Only RecalcDirty writes computed values; users, paste, undo, restore-of-formulas, and feeds never patch a computed number directly.
2. Every reader of a committed calc run, on any device, sees the same computed value for a given cell. **[TIME]**
3. A viewport, export, or API page is labeled with one `calc_run_id` and contains no cells from another run. Torn graphs are not shown.
4. The dirty set of a recalc is the reverse-dependency closure of the seed, expanded by dynamic references discovered **during** evaluation, then expanded again, up to 8 waves; it is not the set of cells the user just typed into.
5. Static references are edges from the AST. `INDIRECT`/`OFFSET`/computed-`INDEX`/computed-`QUERY` also produce **dynamic** edges recorded from the last successful eval and re-resolved every wave. **[TIME]**
6. Insert/delete of rows or columns shifts stored cell keys, absolute refs, named ranges, ACLs, comments, spill occupancy, and feed targets per Excel grow/shift rules, and does not rewrite `INDIRECT` strings.
7. Iteration starts from the recorded seed of the previous committed run for that cycle (0 if none), runs in stable cell order, stops at threshold or `max_iterations`, and stores both the seed used and the iteration count. Path-dependent cycles are reproducible from that record. **[TIME] [MONEY]**
8. `RAND`/`RANDBETWEEN` values live in a sticky table keyed by cell and formula-text hash; unrelated recalc does not redraw them. **[MONEY]** (lotteries, random assignment, sampling).
9. `TODAY()` is the workbook-timezone date of the calc run’s `sequencer_now`, and a midnight job must start a calc run when that date changes even if the op log is idle. **[TIME] [MONEY]** (aging, interest, close calendars).
10. Feed-owned cells are system literals. User edits there are refused. A fetch failure leaves last-good cells and a staleness timestamp. **[TIME] [OTHER PEOPLE'S DATA]**
11. A principal without `can_read` on a cell does not receive formula text, literal, computed value, or formatted text for that cell. **[SECRECY] [OTHER PEOPLE'S DATA] [MONEY]**
12. User-formula evaluation may read only cells that `authority_principal_id` can read **at eval time**. If ACL has shrunk since authoring, the result is `#PERM!`, not a cached secret. **[SECRECY] [TIME]**
13. Elevated formulas evaluate over the unfiltered workbook. Only `can_author_elevated` plus `can_elevated_write` on the cell may create or edit them. **[SECRECY] [MONEY]**
14. Aggregates (`SUM`, `COUNT`, `COUNTA`, `AVERAGE`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `RANK`, `QUERY` counts, pivot counts) in a user formula that cannot read every required precedent return `#PERM!`, never a visibility-filtered number. **[SECRECY] [MONEY]**
15. `#PERM!` and “no such sheet” and “typo in sheet name” are the same `#REF!` to a non-elevated principal, so hidden-sheet existence does not leak through error type. **[SECRECY]**
16. Spill occupancy is derived: a spill root owns a rectangle; collision with a non-empty cell or another spill is `#SPILL!` at the root and blanks (not deletes content of) blocked occupants. Occupancy is not a user-editable field.
17. Named-range resolution uses the version valid at the start of the calc run. Renaming or retargeting a name is a content op that dirties every formula that mentions it.
18. `op_log` is append-only and densely sequenced per workbook. `cell_content` is a materialization of the log. Two ops on the same workbook are ordered; there is no concurrent commit of two sequences. **[TIME]**
19. Computed history for audit is a frozen snapshot (content + computed + `sequencer_now` + sticky rands + iteration seeds + feed snapshot ids + UDF versions + ACL version). Replaying formulas “as of now” is a different operation and will not match the board pack if feeds or `TODAY()` moved. **[TIME] [MONEY]**
20. UDF code runs as system in a sandbox with only its arguments; it does not inherit the invoking user’s grid reads, and it does not run “as the owner” with a silent extra ACL. Feed-shaped UDFs read secrets only the operator stored on the feed. **[SECRECY] [OTHER PEOPLE'S DATA]**

### 3. The traps, ranked

**Trap 1 — The graph is data, and it mutates during calculation (temporal + admission).**

Wrong version: treat dependencies as schema. Parse formulas once, store `A1 → B1` edges, recalc along that static DAG when content changes.

Why it is wrong: `=INDIRECT($A$1&"!B2")` with `A1 = "2026-07"` points at July. At month close someone types `2026-08` into `A1`. The edge set of tens of thousands of cells **changes as a result of a value**, not a formula-text edit. `OFFSET`, computed `INDEX` rows, `FILTER` criteria, and `QUERY("select A where B > "&C1)` are the same class. Spill height is a function of the result array, so *which cells even exist as computed values* is data. A second wave of eval is required when newly resolved refs were not in the subgraph. Eight waves and then `#REF!` is a product rule because dynamic graphs can thrash.

Observation: after changing the month name, commission totals still show July, or a naive engine never dirties August because no formula *text* changed. A second observation: insert a row, and `A5` refs move but `INDIRECT("A5")` does not — any engine that “normalizes everything to refs at parse” cannot reproduce the workbook finance already has.

This is the archetype’s equivalent of active-versus-historical membership: **membership in the dependency graph is a fact at a calc run, not a property of a cell row.**

**Trap 2 — The unit of admission is not a cell (fan-out + derived writes).**

Wrong version: admit `EditCells` as a patch of N content rows. Treat recalc as a view layer, a client loop, or a background “best effort.”

Why it is wrong: the user-visible promise is that dependents update. The write-set of computed values is the reverse closure, which is routinely 10⁴–10⁶ cells, includes cells the editor **cannot read or write**, and is not known until dynamic refs resolve. Admitting “patch this one cell” admits an incorrect database. Admitting “patch the whole workbook” cannot be the authorization unit because contractors write one cell in column B. The real split is: **user-admitted unit = bounded content/structural op on granted rectangles; system-admitted unit = RecalcDirty over a data-dependent closure; read unit = a rectangle at one calc_run, ACL-filtered.**

Observation: editor changes `Assumptions!B4` from 0.08 to 0.085. Contractor’s viewport still shows old commissions, or worse, 20% of rows have new numbers and 80% have old (torn run). Second observation: the editor does not have read on `HR!H` but an elevated dashboard cell they *can* read must still update; if recalc runs only as that editor, the dashboard freezes.

**Trap 3 — Cached values and visibility-filtered aggregates (visibility, including counts).**

Wrong version: if you can see a cell, send its last computed value; if a formula ranges over cells you cannot see, `SUM`/`COUNTA` over the rows the ACL leaves behind.

Why it is wrong: two legitimate products collide. FP&A wants `=SUM(HR!H:H)` on a dashboard the exec can read without reading salaries (intentional aggregate over the unfiltered domain). A contractor with write on `Scratch!A1` must not learn `HR!H5` by typing `=HR!H5` or `=INDIRECT("HR!H5")`, and must not see a `COUNTA(HR!H:H)` of 12 instead of 400 and believe headcount is 12. Visibility-filtered aggregates are **admitted, confidently wrong integers**. Cached values without an authority class are an exfiltration bus: any writable visible cell becomes a read of the whole workbook.

Observation: contractor `=HR!H5` displays $187,400. Or `=SUM(H:H)` on a partially granted column returns the sum of the ten visible rows; payroll is run from that number. Or an API client scans the grid, gets `{value: null}` vs `{access: "denied"}` vs `#PERM!` vs `#REF!` and maps the HR sheet’s existence and occupied cells.

**Trap 4 — Volatiles and iteration are functions of time and of previous runs (temporal + determinism).**

Wrong version: `NOW`/`RAND`/`TODAY` evaluate in the client at render; circulars iterate from whatever numbers happen to be in RAM; each device calculates independently.

Why it is wrong: the sheet is a database. Two execs opening the same board pack must see the same `RAND` assignment and the same iterated interest. Excel’s “any recalc redraws all volatiles” reshuffles a territory assignment when someone edits a comment. Iteration that starts from RAM is path-dependent in a way you cannot audit: the sixth concurrent editor’s laptop “wins” a cycle because it had different seeds. `TODAY()` that only moves when a user opens the file leaves weekend aging wrong until Monday.

Observation: Device A and Device B show different lottery columns after both were offline. Recalc All on one device reshuffles commissions for everyone. Interest expense in the 10-K backup does not match a replay from formula text alone because seeds were not recorded. Aging bucket still says 29 days at 02:00 local after midnight.

**Trap 5 — Offline merge of structure, formula text, and values (merge + concurrency).**

Wrong version: last-writer-wins on a JSON document of the grid, or CRDT each cell’s display string including computed numbers; or rebase cell addresses by string (`C6` stays `C6` after an insert at row 5).

Why it is wrong: Device A inserts a row at 5. Device B (offline) sets `C6 = 42` and `D6 = =C6*2`. Merge must attach B’s literal to `C7` and rewrite B’s formula’s **coordinates** using OT against the insert, while the AST’s *relative* offsets stay put. Computed `84` on B is not a third field to merge — after merge C7 is 42 and D7 recalculates to 84 *or something else* if A also changed C. Two devices both spilling `FILTER` into the same rectangle produce `#SPILL!`, not a concatenated array. Two devices editing the same cell need a conflict; two devices editing different cells that share a cycle need **one** new iteration from recorded seeds, not a merge of the two devices’ iterated outputs.

Observation: warehouse iPad inserts 40 rows of counts; FP&A laptop had been editing what they still call row 200; after sync the notes sit on the wrong SKU. Or both devices’ computed interest are averaged / LWW’d and no longer satisfy the cycle equations.

**Trap 6 — Who a formula acts as (authority).**

Wrong version: evaluate every formula as the workbook owner (Apps Script’s habitual “run as me”), or as the current viewer, or as “the system, with the viewer’s ACL,” one global choice.

Why it is wrong: owner-authority lets a contractor’s `=HR!H5` succeed. Viewer-authority makes the exec’s dashboard `SUM` of salaries return `#PERM!` or a partial sum depending on who opens it — **the same cell shows different numbers to different people**, breaking promise 2 and making a live database impossible. System-with-viewer-ACL during *eval* couples computed values to the reader, so you cannot store one `computed_current`. The product users want is dual authority: **stored computed values are unique per cell per calc run; user formulas are sandboxed by the author’s principal at eval time; elevated formulas are sandboxed by workbook-full read; viewers may read the stored number iff they can read the cell.** Scheduled recalc has no “current user”; it must use each formula’s stored `authority_principal_id`.

Observation: exec and contractor open the same dashboard cell and see different totals. Or a midnight tick recalculates user formulas as system and fills contractor-visible cells with salaries. Or a UDF `=TAX(H5)` fetches an HTTP API with the owner’s token and posts the salary to a URL the contractor put in another cell.

### 4. Data model

Coordinates are 0-based (`A1` = row 0, col 0). Populated cells are sparse. Maximum populated cells per workbook: 10,000,000. Maximum sheets: 200. Formula text max 8,192 characters. Formula call depth max 512. Dynamic waves max 8.

**org** — `org_id` uuid PK, `name` text, `created_at` timestamptz.

**principal** — `principal_id` uuid PK, `org_id`, `kind` enum(`user`,`group`,`link`,`system`), `email` text null, `display_name` text.

**workbook** — `workbook_id` uuid PK, `org_id`, `title` text, `timezone` text (IANA), `iteration_enabled` bool, `max_iterations` int (1–1000, default 100), `change_threshold` float64 (default 0.001), `recalc_mode` enum(`automatic`,`manual`), `now_refresh_seconds` int null (null = `NOW` only when its cell is already dirty), `latest_op_id` uuid, `latest_calc_run_id` uuid, `acl_epoch` int, `created_by`, `created_at`. One sequencer per workbook.

**sheet** — `sheet_id` uuid PK, `workbook_id`, `name` text (unique among non-deleted sheets in the workbook), `order_index` int, `row_count` int (default 1,000, max 1,048,576), `col_count` int (default 26, max 16,384), `hidden` bool, `deleted_at` timestamptz null. Versioned by ops (rename, reorder, resize, hide).

**cell_content** (hot current; materialization of `op_log`) — PK `(workbook_id, sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx)`. `content_kind` enum(`literal`,`formula`). `literal_type` enum(`number`,`text`,`bool`,`blank`,`error`) null. `literal_number` float64 null, `literal_text` text null, `literal_bool` bool null, `error_code` text null. `formula_text` text null, `formula_ast` jsonb null (**derived** from `formula_text` at write: refs stored as `{sheet_id, row, col, abs_row, abs_col}` where relative `row`/`col` are **offsets from the host cell**). `formula_class` enum(`user`,`elevated`) null. `authored_by` uuid, `authority_principal_id` uuid (sandbox principal; equals author for user formulas). `provenance` enum(`user`,`feed`,`restore`,`system`) default `user`. `feed_id` uuid null. `updated_op_id`, `updated_by`, `updated_at`. Not append-only.

**cell_revision** — **append-only**. `revision_id` uuid PK, copy of content fields, `op_id`, `created_at`. Full history of content; not of computed values.

**computed_current** — **derived**, writable only by RecalcDirty. PK `(workbook_id, sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx)`. `calc_run_id`, `value_type` enum(`number`,`text`,`bool`,`blank`,`error`,`spill_root`). `number_value` float64, `text_value` text, `bool_value` bool, `error_code` enum(`#DIV/0!`,`#REF!`,`#NAME?`,`#VALUE!`,`#N/A`,`#CIRCREF!`,`#SPILL!`,`#PERM!`,`#TIMEOUT!`,`#FEED!`), `spill_height` int, `spill_width` int.

**calc_run** — **append-only**. `calc_run_id` uuid PK, `workbook_id`, `triggering_op_id` uuid null, `reason` enum(`edit`,`feed`,`midnight`,`recalc_all`,`merge`,`restore`,`acl`,`udf`,`now_tick`), `op_seq_fence` int, `sequencer_now` timestamptz, `today_date` date, `status` enum(`running`,`committed`,`aborted`), `cells_dirtied` int, `cells_evaluated` int, `waves` int, `iteration_max_used` int, `started_at`, `completed_at`. Reproducibility metadata: `udf_version_map` jsonb, `named_range_epoch` int, `acl_epoch` int, `feed_snapshot_ids` uuid[].

**iteration_seed** — PK `(calc_run_id, sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx)`, `seed_number` float64. Recorded **inputs** to a cycle, copied from the previous committed `computed_current` for those cells.

**sticky_volatile** — PK `(workbook_id, sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx)`. `kind` enum(`rand`,`randbetween`), `formula_text_hash` bytea, `value` float64, `low` int null, `high` int null, `bound_calc_run_id`. Time-bounded in the sense: invalid when formula hash changes.

**dependency_edge** — **derived**, replaced per evaluated cell each calc run. PK `(workbook_id, from_sheet, from_row, from_col, to_sheet, to_row, to_col, edge_kind)` with `edge_kind` enum(`static`,`dynamic`,`named`,`spill_occupy`,`volatile`). `last_calc_run_id`. Reverse index `(to_*)` is the fan-out index.

**volatile_index** — **derived**. Cells whose AST contains `NOW`,`TODAY`,`RAND`,`RANDBETWEEN`, `INDIRECT`,`OFFSET`, or a volatile UDF. Used by midnight/now ticks without scanning millions of formulas.

**named_range** — **versioned** (close `valid_to`, insert new row). `named_range_id` uuid, `workbook_id`, `name` text, `scope` enum(`workbook`,`sheet`), `sheet_id` null, `ref_sheet_id`, `start_row`,`start_col`,`end_row`,`end_col` int, `is_formula` bool, `definition_formula` text null (when the name *is* an `OFFSET(...)` definition), `valid_from` timestamptz, `valid_to` timestamptz null, `op_id`. Unique `(workbook_id, name)` among open versions (`valid_to` is null). Time-bounded.

**range_acl** — **time-bounded**. `acl_id` uuid PK, `workbook_id`, `principal_id`, `sheet_id`, `start_row`,`start_col`,`end_row`,`end_col`, `can_read` bool, `can_write` bool, `can_elevated_write` bool, `granted_by`, `valid_from`, `valid_to` null, `revoked_at` null. Evaluation uses grants where `valid_from ≤ sequencer_now` and (`valid_to` is null or `> sequencer_now`) and `revoked_at` is null. Workbook-level flags on a join table **workbook_principal**: `can_share`, `can_author_elevated`, `can_edit_structure`, `role` enum(`owner`,`editor`,`commenter`,`viewer`).

**acl_event** — **append-only** grant/revoke/expiry.

**share_link** — `link_id`, `workbook_id`, `token_hash`, maps to a `principal` of kind `link` with a range_acl, `expires_at` timestamptz null, `revoked_at` null. Time-bounded. Expiry is a durable revoke job (not clock-compared at read in a racy way): a sweeper sets `revoked_at` and bumps `acl_epoch`.

**op_log** — **append-only**. `op_id` uuid PK, `workbook_id`, `parent_op_id` uuid, `seq` int dense, `actor_id`, `device_id`, `client_op_id` uuid (idempotency unique with workbook), `op_type` enum(`edit_cells`,`insert_rows`,`insert_cols`,`delete_rows`,`delete_cols`,`named_range`,`acl`,`feed_write`,`udf_put`,`recalc_all`,`restore`,`snapshot`,`merge_marker`), `payload` jsonb, `committed_at`, `calc_run_id` null.

**feed** — `feed_id` uuid PK, `workbook_id`, `name`, `kind` enum(`http_json`,`csv_url`,`sql`,`connector`), `config` jsonb (URL, JSON path, connector id — **no raw secrets**), `secret_id` uuid (stored in a secret table readable by system and the operator only), `target_sheet_id`, `target_start_row`,`target_start_col`, `write_mode` enum(`overwrite_range`), `interval_seconds` int, `last_snapshot_id`, `last_status` enum(`ok`,`stale_error`), `last_error` text null.

**feed_snapshot** — **append-only**, time-bounded. `snapshot_id` PK, `feed_id`, `fetched_at`, `body_hash`, `parsed_rows` int, `parsed_cols` int, `valid_from`, `valid_to` null.

**udf** — **versioned**. `udf_id`, `workbook_id`, `name` (unique among open versions), `language` enum(`lambda`,`js_sandbox`), `source_text`, `is_volatile` bool, `is_feed_shaped` bool, `timeout_ms` int default 100, `version` int, `valid_from`,`valid_to` null.

**udf_cache** — for feed-shaped only. PK `(udf_id, args_hash)`, `result_jsonb`, `fetched_at`, `expires_at` (time-bounded).

**spill_occupancy** — **derived**. PK occupied cell; `root_sheet`,`root_row`,`root_col`.

**number_format** — range + pattern text (`$#,##0.00`). Display-only; does not affect calc except as `TEXT()`.

**protection_lock** — rectangles even writers cannot edit without `can_edit_structure` unprotect. Not a secrecy boundary.

**frozen_snapshot** — **append-only**. `snapshot_id`, `workbook_id`, `reason` enum(`manual`,`nightly`,`export`), `at_calc_run_id`, `created_by`, `created_at`. Blob or side tables of content+computed for cells (unfiltered stored server-side; **reads** of a snapshot are ACL-filtered at read time with the *requesting* principal, except owners reading for disaster recovery).

**comment**, **presence** — not on the axis; presence is `(session_id, workbook_id, principal_id, device_id, sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx, last_seen)`.

**Relationships.** Workbook 1–N sheets, 1–N ops, 1–N calc_runs. Cell keys point at sheets. Edges, computed, sticky, seeds point at cell keys. ACL and named ranges point at rectangles. Feeds overwrite rectangles of `cell_content` with `provenance=feed`. `workbook.latest_calc_run_id` is the unique committed view. `authority_principal_id` on a formula cell points at `principal`.

**Derived vs user-authored.** User-authored: `cell_content` (except feed provenance), `named_range` definitions, `range_acl`, `udf` source, `feed` config, `workbook` calc settings. Derived: `formula_ast`, `computed_current`, `dependency_edge`, `volatile_index`, `spill_occupancy`, `iteration_seed` (copied from derived), `cell_content` for feed dumps (system-authored literals).

**Append-only:** `op_log`, `cell_revision`, `calc_run`, `acl_event`, `feed_snapshot`, `frozen_snapshot`. **Versioned / time-bounded:** `named_range`, `udf`, `range_acl`, `share_link`, `udf_cache`, `feed_snapshot.valid_*`.

### 5. The hard operations

Shared helpers: `transform(op, intervening)` is 2D OT (insert/delete rows/cols shift keys and absolute coords; same-cell content: later seq wins). `read_acl(principal, cell)` is true iff some unrevoked `range_acl` covers it with `can_read`, or workbook role is owner. Sequencer: one serial lock per workbook for committing ops; RecalcDirty takes a calc lock that orders after the `op_seq_fence`.

---

**1. ReadViewport** (read)

Inputs: `actor_id`, `workbook_id`, `sheet_id`, `r0,c0,r1,c1` (inclusive), optional `want_calc_run_id` (default latest committed).

Authorization: actor has some role on the workbook. Hidden sheets with no read grant: refuse as “no such sheet” (same as unknown id).

Steps: (1) Load `calc_run` = wanted or `latest_calc_run_id`; if `status≠committed`, use previous committed. (2) Enumerate keys in rectangle that exist in `cell_content` or `computed_current` or `spill_occupancy`. (3) For each key, if not `read_acl`, emit `{row, col, access:"denied"}` with **no** value, formula, or error distinction. (4) Else emit content (if any) + computed from that `calc_run` (join `computed_current` if ids match, else historical computed stored on that snapshot; if neither, `{status:"calculating"}` only when the cell is in an in-flight dirty set **and** the client asked for latest — still do not mix: the whole viewport then either stays on previous run or is marked calculating as a unit). (5) Include sheet `row_count`/`col_count` (dimensions are not secret). (6) Do not include `COUNTA` of the sheet.

Atomic: single read of one `calc_run_id`. Eventually consistent: presence. Optimistic: none. Conflict: none; if a newer run commits mid-read, this response stays on the chosen id.

---

**2. EditCells** (write)

Inputs: `actor_id`, `device_id`, `workbook_id`, `parent_op_id`, `client_op_id`, `edits[]` of `{sheet_id, row_idx, col_idx, content_kind, literal_* | formula_text, formula_class}` , max 50,000.

Authorization: each cell covered by `can_write` (and `can_elevated_write` + workbook `can_author_elevated` if `formula_class=elevated`); not protection-locked; not `provenance=feed`. User formulas: every **static** ref and named-range rectangle in the AST ⊆ actor’s current read set; else refuse that cell with a permission error and write nothing for it. Dynamic-only refs (`INDIRECT` of a string literal the parser cannot resolve) are allowed through and become `#PERM!` at eval if they escape.

Steps: (1) If `client_op_id` exists, return stored result. (2) If `parent_op_id ≠ latest_op_id`, OT-transform `edits` against intervening ops. (3) Parse formulas; store `formula_ast` with relative offsets. (4) Set `authored_by` and `authority_principal_id` = actor for user formulas; elevated keeps `authority_principal_id` = actor as well (elevated skip the read filter). (5) Write `cell_content`, append `cell_revision`, append `op_log` with `seq++`, set `latest_op_id`. (6) Seed dirty = edited keys ∪ reverse-`static`/`dynamic`/`named`/`spill_occupy` from `dependency_edge` ∪ spill roots that occupy an edited cell. (7) Enqueue RecalcDirty(`seed`, `op_seq_fence=seq`, `reason=edit`). (8) If `|seed|≤20,000` **and** no `INDIRECT`/`OFFSET` in the seed’s ASTs, run RecalcDirty before releasing the sequencer; else release after step 5 and let the calc worker run.

Atomic: steps 3–5 (content + revision + op_log + latest_op_id). Recalc may be the next serial step. Eventually consistent: other devices’ viewports, search. Optimistic UI: the editor’s client shows typed literals immediately; it may locally recalc the cone **only if every precedent in that cone is readable by the actor**, badged “local”; other users keep the previous calc run until RecalcDirty commits. Concurrent conflict: later seq overwrites same-cell content; earlier actor gets an overwrite event with both texts. Insert-vs-edit is OT, not LWW.

**3. RecalcDirty** (system write; the axis)

Inputs: `workbook_id`, `seed[]` cell keys, `reason`, `op_seq_fence`, `triggering_op_id`.

Authorization: **system only**. Never as a user. User “Recalc All” is op 13-shaped: an `op_log` row that seeds this.

Steps: (1) Acquire calc lock. If `latest_op.seq > op_seq_fence`, abort and restart with fence bumped and seed unioned with intervening ops’ seeds. (2) Insert `calc_run` `status=running`, `sequencer_now=now()`, `today_date=date(sequencer_now, workbook.timezone)`, copy `acl_epoch`, open named-range versions, current UDF versions, current feed snapshot ids. (3) Copy `iteration_seed` from `computed_current` for every cell that had `edge_kind` cycle membership last run (or skip if none). (4) `dirty := seed`. If `reason=recalc_all`, add all `volatile_index` cells. If `reason=midnight`, add `TODAY` cells from `volatile_index`. (5) `wave := 0`. While `dirty` nonempty and `wave < 8`: (a) Expand `dirty` by reverse edges (`static`,`dynamic`,`named`,`spill_occupy`,`volatile`) until closed. (b) Tarjan SCCs on the subgraph; topo-sort SCCs. (c) `discovered := ∅`. (d) For each SCC in topo order, stable-sort cells by `(sheet.order_index, row, col)`: **singleton without self-loop:** evaluate AST. Reads of precedents go to the scratch map, else `computed_current`. `INDIRECT`/`OFFSET`/computed range args resolve using **already-evaluated** values this run; collect resolved cells; add `dynamic` edges in scratch. If `formula_class=user` and any resolved cell fails `read_acl(authority_principal_id)`, result `#PERM!` (do not put the secret in scratch for that cell; elevated cells that need the real precedent still read it via system). If `formula_class=elevated`, unfiltered reads. `RAND`: if sticky missing or hash mismatch or `reason=recalc_all` or a **direct** precedent of the RAND cell is in `dirty`, draw and write `sticky_volatile`; else reuse. `NOW` = `calc_run.sequencer_now`. `TODAY` = `calc_run.today_date`. UDFs: pure eval or `udf_cache` lookup. Arrays: set spill occupancy; collision → `#SPILL!` at root; add dependents of blocked occupants to `discovered`. Fan-in functions (`SUM`, `QUERY`, …) read the full rectangle; for user formulas, if any required cell in that rectangle is unreadable → `#PERM!`, **not** a partial sum. **SCC size>1 or self-loop:** if `!iteration_enabled` all `#CIRCREF!`; else for `i in 1..max_iterations` evaluate in stable order starting from seeds (first pass) then scratch; stop if max `|Δ| < change_threshold`. (e) Any resolved ref not in the subgraph goes to `discovered`. (f) `dirty := discovered`; `wave++`. (6) If `wave=8` and `discovered` still nonempty, those cells `#REF!`. (7) **Swap:** write `computed_current` for all evaluated keys, replace `dependency_edge` and `volatile_index` and `spill_occupancy` for those keys, set `calc_run.status=committed`, `workbook.latest_calc_run_id`. (8) Notify viewport subscribers with the new id.

Atomic: step 7 (computed + edges + occupancy + latest_calc_run_id + calc_run committed). Evaluation scratch is private. Eventually consistent: frozen-snapshot sampler, webhooks. Optimistic: none. Concurrent conflict: generation fence in step 1; never two committed calc runs from overlapping fences.

---

**4. InsertRowsOrCols** (write, structural)

Inputs: `sheet_id`, `axis` enum(`row`,`col`), `at_index`, `count` (1–5,000), plus sequencer fields.

Authorization: `can_edit_structure`.

Steps: (1) OT against intervening ops. (2) Shift `cell_content` keys with `index >= at_index` by `count` (and revisions are not rewritten; future restores use op replay). (3) Shift absolute AST nodes (`abs_row`/`abs_col` true and index ≥ `at_index`) on **all sheets** that mention this sheet; relative offsets unchanged. (4) Do **not** rewrite formula **text** of `INDIRECT` string literals. (5) Grow/shift `named_range`, `range_acl`, `protection_lock`, `number_format`, `spill_occupancy`, `feed` targets, comments per Excel: a rectangle that begins before `at_index` and ends at or after it grows; a rectangle that begins at or after it shifts; a rectangle wholly before it is unchanged. (6) `op_log` + `seq++`. (7) RecalcDirty seed = all formula cells on this sheet ∪ all cells with edges into the shifted interval ∪ all `ROW()`/`COLUMN()`/`INDIRECT`/`OFFSET` in `volatile_index` for this workbook (conservative but honest; a 1.1M-formula sheet recalc is in-contract).

Atomic: 2–6. Eventually consistent: computed (via RecalcDirty). Optimistic: local grid insert on the actor’s device. Conflict: two inserts at the same index serialize; the second OT-shifts. Insert vs cell-edit: the edit’s coordinates transform.

---

**5. UpdateNamedRange** (write)

Inputs: `name`, `scope`, rectangle or `definition_formula`, create/update/delete.

Authorization: `can_edit_structure`.

Steps: Close previous version (`valid_to=now`), insert new version, `op_log`, RecalcDirty all formula cells whose AST contains the name (index via `edge_kind=named`; if missing, scan is unacceptable at 1.1M — **named edges must exist**; on first parse of a formula using a name, EditCells writes them).

Atomic: version close+open + op. Optimistic: name box updates. Conflict: two retargets LWW by seq; both dirties union.

---

**6. GrantRevokeRangeAcl** (write)

Inputs: `principal_id`, rectangle, flags, `valid_from`/`valid_to`, or revoke `acl_id`.

Authorization: `can_share` or owner. Cannot grant elevated-write without being owner. Cannot grant read the granter does not have.

Steps: (1) Insert `range_acl` or set `revoked_at`, append `acl_event`, bump `workbook.acl_epoch`. (2) Share-link expiry is this op issued by the sweeper. (3) RecalcDirty seed = all **user-formula** cells with any edge into the rectangle (their `#PERM!` vs value may change). Elevated formulas are not re-eval’d for ACL (their values do not depend on viewer ACL); but viewport subscriptions must drop newly-unreadable cells immediately even before recalc.

Atomic: acl row + epoch + event. Recalc of `#PERM!` may lag by the calc SLA; **viewport omission of revoked cells must not lag** — read path filters on `acl_epoch` live, not on computed. Optimistic: sharer sees the collaborator in the list. Conflict: concurrent revoke/grant on same principal+rectangle: seq order, last state wins; both bump epoch.

---

**7. BulkPaste** (bulk write)

Inputs: a dense 2D block, up to 50,000 non-empty cells, `as` enum(`values`,`formulas`,`formulas_and_recalc`).

Authorization: same as EditCells per cell (checkerboard ACLs). A single unwritable cell does not fail the batch; it is reported in `skipped[]` and left unchanged.

Steps: one `op_log` row, one `cell_content` write set, one RecalcDirty. Formula texts that were copied from another origin rewrite relative refs as Excel paste does (offset by delta of top-left).

Atomic: the whole paste content. Optimistic: local fill. Conflict: OT per cell against intervening inserts; same-cell LWW per seq.

---

**8. RefreshFeed** (external system + scheduled)

Inputs: `feed_id`, `reason` enum(`schedule`,`manual`).

Authorization: system for `schedule`; `can_edit_structure` or feed operator for `manual`. Secrets never leave the server.

Steps: (1) HTTP/SQL fetch **outside** the workbook lock (timeout 15s, max 8 MB). (2) On failure: set `last_status=stale_error`, do not touch cells, do not dirty. (3) Parse to a grid, max 100,000 cells. (4) Acquire sequencer. (5) Close previous `feed_snapshot.valid_to`, insert new snapshot. (6) Overwrite the target rectangle with literals `provenance=feed`, `authored_by=system`; clear feed-provenance cells that used to lie in the old rectangle and now lie outside the new size. (7) `op_log` `feed_write`. (8) RecalcDirty the rectangle.

Atomic: 5–7. Fetch is not atomic with write. Optimistic: none (manual refresh shows a spinner). Conflict: user edits in the rectangle are already illegal; concurrent manual+schedule: seq, both overwrite with their parsed grids (last fetch wins). Scheduler: one job per `interval_seconds` per feed; ~8,000 feeds at 60s ≈ 133 fetches/s fleet-wide.

---

**9. MidnightTodayTick** (scheduled / background)

Inputs: none. Runner buckets workbooks by `timezone` whose local date just changed.

Authorization: system.

Steps: For each such workbook: RecalcDirty(`reason=midnight`, seed = `volatile_index` where function is `TODAY` or a UDF marked date-volatile). If `now_refresh_seconds` is set, a separate 60s job seeds `NOW` cells.

Atomic: each workbook’s RecalcDirty swap. Eventually consistent: across the fleet, workbooks in `America/Los_Angeles` tick within 60 seconds of local midnight, not at the same instant as `Europe/London`. Optimistic: none. Conflict: if a user edit is in flight, fence restart unions the TODAY dirty set with the edit.

---

**10. MergeOfflineBatch** (merge write)

Inputs: `device_id`, `ops[]` (client order, each with `parent_op_id`, `client_op_id`, payload), max 5,000.

Authorization: actor owns the device session and still has write on the cells **as of merge time** (ACL may have been revoked while offline: those ops fail individually with `#PERM!` and do not apply).

Steps: (1) For each op in order, OT against committed ops since its parent, then apply the matching server operation **without** running RecalcDirty each time (content, insert, named range, etc. still commit to `op_log`). (2) Collect union of seeds and `conflict[]` (same-cell overwrites of a **third** party while offline, feed-owned, ACL). (3) One RecalcDirty(`reason=merge`, seed=union). (4) Return new `latest_calc_run_id` and conflicts.

Atomic: each transformed op is its own op_log append; the batch is not one transaction (a crash mid-merge resumes by `client_op_id` idempotency). The **calc run** at the end is one snapshot. Eventually consistent: the offline device’s computed numbers are discarded. Optimistic: device already showed local calc; on completion it **replaces** the grid with the server run and banners “Updated from server” if any computed number the user can read changed. Conflict: listed; computed LWW is forbidden.

---

**11. PutUdf** (write)

Inputs: `name`, `language`, `source_text`, `is_volatile`, `is_feed_shaped`.

Authorization: `can_author_elevated`. Sandbox validator rejects network, filesystem, and grid handles unless `is_feed_shaped`, in which case network is only via `secret_id` allowlisted on a feed record.

Steps: Close previous UDF version, open new, `op_log`, RecalcDirty all cells whose AST calls `name` (index required, same as named ranges).

Atomic: version switch + op. Optimistic: formula autocomplete updates. Conflict: two deploys serialize; recalc once at the end of the second.

---

**12. CreateFrozenSnapshot** (read/write hybrid; bulk/periodic)

Inputs: `reason` (`manual`,`nightly`,`export`). Nightly is scheduled per org.

Authorization: `can_view` for export-of-what-I-can-see; owner for disaster snapshot that stores unfiltered blobs. Nightly job is system, stores unfiltered server-side.

Steps: (1) Wait until no `running` calc_run (or snapshot the latest committed). (2) Copy `calc_run` metadata (seeds, sticky, feeds, udf versions, `sequencer_now`, `acl_epoch`). (3) For export to a user: stream ACL-filtered computed+content (same omission rules as ReadViewport). For nightly: store unfiltered. (4) `op_log` `snapshot`.

Atomic: snapshot row points at an already-committed `calc_run_id` (no torn copy). Eventually consistent: blob storage. Optimistic: “Snapshot saved.” Conflict: none.

---

**13. RestoreContentToOp** (write)

Inputs: `at_op_id` or `snapshot_id`, `mode` enum(`replay_formulas_and_recalc_now`,`materialize_frozen_sheet`).

Authorization: `can_edit_structure`.

Steps: **replay mode:** rebuild `cell_content` from `cell_revision`/`op_log` through `at_op_id` (named ranges and ACLs are *not* silently restored unless flags `restore_acl`, `restore_names` are set — default off, because restoring ACL from last year re-opens contractors). Append a new op `restore` (do not rewrite history). RecalcDirty(`reason=restore`, seed=all formula cells) using **current** feeds, `TODAY()`, sticky policy (RAND hashes match restored formula text so old randoms return **if** sticky rows still exist; else redraw and warn). **materialize mode:** create a new sheet `Restore {sequencer_now}` filled with **literals** from the frozen computed values the actor may read; live sheets untouched; no recalc of the live graph.

Atomic: the restore op + content write; recalc is RecalcDirty. Optimistic: none. Conflict: concurrent edits after `at_op_id` remain in the log after the restore op (restore is a forward op, not a rewind); that is visible in history.

### 6. Scale and cost

**Fleet (successful mid-market deployment).** 25,000 orgs, 1.2 million workbooks, 80,000 daily active users, 15,000 peak sessions, ~400 content ops/s fleet-wide, ~8,000 feed subscriptions at 60s (≈133 fetches/s), nightly snapshots of ~50,000 “hot” workbooks.

**Hot workbook (month close, “FY26 Comp”).** 18 sheets, 4.2 million populated cells, 1.1 million formulas, 2,400 `INDIRECT`, 18,000 `OFFSET`/`INDEX`, 11,000 `FILTER`/`XLOOKUP`, 80 named ranges, 22 range ACLs, 9 feeds, 14 UDFs, 40 concurrent editors, 4 offline devices. Max fan-out: 620,000 dependents from `Assumptions!B4`. Month-tab `INDIRECT` rewires ~85,000 edges. One circular SCC of 36 cells × 100 iterations = 3,600 evals. `SUMIFS` over 200,000-row ranges are common (fan-**in** 2×10⁵ per formula).

**Storage (one hot workbook, order of magnitude).** Content ~0.8–1.5 GB; `computed_current` similar; ~4 edges/formula → ~4.4 million edges ~0.2 GB; `cell_revision` grows ~2–20 GB/year depending on paste behavior (BulkPaste of 50k every hour is the killer); `op_log` millions of rows. Cannot store `computed_current` for every calc run; history of numbers is frozen snapshots + replay from content and `calc_run` metadata.

**What gets expensive**

| Operation | Why it hurts at this size |
|---|---|
| RecalcDirty after `Assumptions!B4` | 620k evals, some `SUMIFS`/`QUERY` each scanning 10⁵–10⁶ cells; CPU-bound; must still be one calc_run |
| RecalcDirty after month-name `INDIRECT` | Graph rewrite of 85k edges + extra waves; worst case 8 waves × large subgraph |
| InsertRows at top of a 1.1M-formula sheet | Key shift + absolute AST walk + conservative full-sheet dirty |
| BulkPaste 50k formulas | Parse + ACL per cell + one huge seed |
| GrantRevokeRangeAcl | May dirty all user formulas pointing into a column; plus live viewport drop |
| MergeOfflineBatch of 5,000 ops including inserts | OT against hours of server ops, then one giant recalc |
| MidnightTodayTick | Thousands of workbooks want a calc run at once; aging columns fan out |
| RefreshFeed 100k cells / 60s | Literal overwrite + recalc of everything that `VLOOKUP`s the dump |
| ReadViewport | Cheap if computed is materialized and ACL is interval-indexed; expensive if it recomputes on read or scans grants naively |
| Restore replay | Rebuild 4.2M cells from revisions then full recalc |

The cost center **is** the axis: derived-write fan-out of a data-dependent graph, not the content patch.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

**1. The write-set of the product-correct mutation is computed by running user formulas, including data-dependent refs.** An ordinary “queries + mutations over rows” backend admits `EditCells` as N row patches. Harbor’s correct commit is those N content rows **plus** an unbounded, eval-discovered set of computed rows, dynamic edges, spill occupancy, and sticky volatiles, including rows the actor cannot read. There is no static query that names the write-set before the engine runs. INDIRECT means the engine may add writes in wave 2 that wave 1 did not mention.

*Concession:* recalc only the edited cell, or cap dependents at 10,000, or forbid `INDIRECT`/`OFFSET`/`FILTER` spill. *Cheat boundary:* you still claim Excel/Sheets-scale live database, month-tab INDIRECT, or “changing the rate updates commissions.” Viewport-priority evaluation is legitimate **only if** the committed swap is still one calc_run and nobody is shown a torn mix.

**2. Evaluation does unbounded reads under system authority (fan-in) while authorization is rectangle-shaped.** `SUM(A1:A200000)`, `QUERY`, `SUMIFS`, and circular iteration are the product. A backend that only admits bounded, visibility-composed reads cannot evaluate a formula without either cheating (partial sums — Trap 3) or leaving the engine. Dual-authority evaluation on the same graph (user sandbox vs elevated unfiltered) is a second unusual requirement: one stored number, two read regimes **inside** the engine, plus a third regime at the viewport.

*Concession:* no aggregates over ranges the caller cannot fully read, and no elevated class (dashboards cannot publish salary totals). *Cheat boundary:* you still offer “share this dashboard, not the HR sheet,” or you return visibility-filtered `COUNT`/`SUM` as if they were real.

**3. Deterministic, sequenced calculation across devices, including functions that are specified as non-deterministic, plus path-dependent iteration.** Sticky `RAND`, recorded iteration seeds, `NOW`/`TODAY` from the calc run, feed snapshot ids, UDF versions — a calc run is a transaction with a clock and a RNG log. Ordinary row mutations have no place for “this derived column is a pure function of those rows except when it isn’t, and everyone must match.”

*Concession:* no `RAND`, `NOW` as a feed cell the system writes once a minute, iteration off. *Cheat boundary:* you still ship `RAND()` in the function list, collaborative random assignment, or Excel-compatible circular interest, but each laptop calculates at render.

**4. Structural OT on a 2D addressed graph, then recompute — never merge derived values.** Merge is not CRDT-on-JSON and not LWW on displayed numbers. Ops transform coordinates, absolute ASTs, named ranges, ACLs, and spill rectangles; `INDIRECT` strings stay; then RecalcDirty. Offline is in-contract for warehouse users.

*Concession:* no offline, or no row insert while anyone is offline, or “offline is a downloaded CSV.” *Cheat boundary:* you still promise airplane editing of a live formula workbook.

**5. Derived-data snapshot isolation at 10⁵–10⁶ writes per keystroke.** `latest_calc_run_id` is an MVCC timestamp for the whole graph. Readers pin it. A row store that updates computed cells in place without a run id will tear. A row store that versions every computed cell every keystroke will not fit (4.2M cells × close-week edit rate).

*Concession:* eventual computed values, spinner everywhere, no audit of “what the board saw at 16:00.” *Cheat boundary:* you still promise “the number on screen is the number” and frozen board packs that match live history.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice.** Excel’s calculation model (static vs volatile vs iterative, A1 vs relative storage, insert/shift of names, `INDIRECT` not rewriting), documented in vendor behavior and in Sestoft’s spreadsheet-implementation literature. Google Sheets-style collaborative sequencing and presence. Sparse grids. Materialized computed values plus a dirty closure. Tarjan SCCs for cycles. OT for rectangular inserts (EtherCalc, early Google Wave papers). Feed-as-literal-dump. Sandboxed UDFs. Frozen snapshots for audit. Range protection as a product surface (in Sheets/Excel it is weak; the *UI* is standard).

**Deliberate product choices, not industry-universal, but what this design claims users of a *database* sheet want.** Dual formula authority as a real secrecy boundary (Sheets/Excel do not actually provide this; they leak through formulas and Apps Script-as-owner). Sticky `RAND` that does not redraw on unrelated edits. Viewport pinned to one calc run (Excel *does* tear during calculate). Feed-owned locked ranges. Iteration seeds recorded for replay. `#PERM!` rather than partial aggregates. Midnight `TODAY()` without an open client. Restore-of-formulas vs materialize-frozen-sheet as two buttons.

**Invented for the exercise (plausible, not measured).** The numeric SLAs (620k in 2s / 30s), fleet sizes, wave cap of 8, dirty threshold of 20,000 for synchronous recalc, 5,000-op offline cap, 50,000 paste cap, 100,000-cell feed cap. The exact ACL error-collapsing to `#REF!` for non-elevated principals. `now_refresh_seconds` as a setting.

**High confidence** that Trap 1–3 are load-bearing in real Sheets-as-database workbooks (month-tab `INDIRECT`, rate-cell fan-out, dashboard SUM of a hidden column, contractor exfiltration). **High confidence** that merging computed values offline is wrong. **Medium confidence** on sticky-RAND as the majority user want — some power users want Excel-identical volatile redraw; the design picks the database interpretation and puts Recalc All on the other side. **Medium confidence** on the 20,000 sync threshold; it is an engineering cut, not a product cut. **Low confidence** that any specific hot-workbook cell count is typical rather than upper-quartile.

The axis is not an edge case in this product: **every ordinary edit is a graph mutation whose correct write-set is discovered by evaluation, must be deterministic across devices, and cannot use the cell as the unit of admission.**
