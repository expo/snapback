### 1. Product contract

**Who uses it.** Sellers list one item. Buyers place a maximum, BIN when it is still offered, pay, receive, return. A scheduler closes listings and expires unpaid orders. Support may freeze an order to `disputed` but cannot bid, edit maxima, or change `endsAt`.

**Currency and time.** Money is integer USD cents. Authorization and settlement use server Unix milliseconds. A device clock never admits a bid, BIN, close, feedback window, or return window.

**Listing.** Quantity 1. Optional reserve. Optional Buy-It-Now. Flat shipping. Return window 0, 14, or 30 days. Duration 1, 3, 5, 7, or 10 days, fixing `endsAt = publishedAt + durationDays * 86400000` at publish. After the first accepted bid, start, reserve, BIN, shipping, duration, and return window cannot change. The seller may only append to the description.

**Proxy.** A buyer submits a **maximum**. The engine stands them at the lowest legal price that keeps them winning, up to that max, using §4’s increment table. The typed amount is not the public price except for a lone bidder at start (or at reserve, when their max meets it). Maxima only **raise**. Earlier `firstBidAt` wins a tied max; a later raise does not improve time priority.

**Hard close.** No anti-snipe extension. A bid the server timestamps at or after `endsAt` is refused even if the buyer tapped at `endsAt - 80ms`. A bid timestamped before `endsAt` is accepted even if the UI showed `0s`. Sniping is the product; the last milliseconds race the server clock and every other writer on that listing.

**BIN vs first bid.** BIN exists iff `binPriceCents` is set, `state = live`, `now < endsAt`, and `bidCount = 0`. First accepted bid and BIN are mutually exclusive. A $1 bid on a $1 start legitimately kills a $400 BIN. Seller defense is a higher start, a reserve, or no BIN.

**User-visible promises**

1. The live **current price** is the second-price proxy standing produced by §5.2’s `recompute`, not “the last number someone typed.”
2. Connected clients see that price, `bidCount`, `uniqueBidderCount`, `reserveMet`, `hasReserve`, `highBidderId`, BIN availability, and time remaining **within 1 second of the commit** that changed them. Anonymous visitors see the same public projection.
3. `bidCount` and `uniqueBidderCount` are facts about the listing, identical for every viewer, including logged-out. They are never “how many of *my* bids.”
4. The **current high bidder’s unused headroom** (`maxCents - currentPriceCents`) is secret from every other principal, including the seller, **forever** — after close, after payment, in bid history, in invoices, in notices. Losing maxima are not similarly secret: second-price standing publishes them (Trap 1).
5. Bid history shows public stands (`bid_events`), not maxima. A silent raise by the current high bidder (max goes up, price and leader do not) produces **no** public event and does **not** increment `bidCount`.
6. If you lose high-bidder status, you receive an **outbid** notice even if all your devices are offline. You do **not** receive an outbid notice when someone else bids below your max and merely raises the standing price.
7. You cannot bid on your own listing. You cannot BIN your own listing.
8. You can only raise your max. Submitting a number ≤ your current max is refused.
9. Equal maxima: the earlier `firstBidAt` remains high; the later bidder is accepted as a challenge that may lift the price to that max but does not take the lead.
10. Hard close on the server clock, as above. The UI countdown is decorative.
11. First accepted bid kills BIN; BIN and first bid cannot both succeed.
12. `hasReserve` is public; `reservePriceCents` is seller-only. `reserveMet` is public. A single bidder whose max ≥ reserve is stood at the reserve, not at the start.
13. After a sale (close with reserve met, or BIN), the buyer pays **hammer + shipping** into escrow. The seller is not paid until release.
14. Funds release when the buyer confirms delivery, or automatically 3 days after `state` becomes `shipped` if no return is open. A return opened while funds are held keeps the hold.
15. One buyer→seller and one seller→buyer feedback row per completed order, in a 60-day window from `completedAt`. Ratings feed a public score.
16. Insertion fee 35¢ at publish (not refunded on cancel or no-sale). Final value fee `min(floor((hammer+shipping)*1325/10000), 75000)` cents at sale, invoiced monthly.
17. Seller may cancel only while `live`, `bidCount = 0`, and `now < endsAt`.
18. **Offline:** the app may cache a listing view. It must not queue bids, BIN, payments, or cancels for later replay. A submit requires the network; a timeout is followed by a server read, never by assuming win or loss.
19. **Multi-device:** the server max is authoritative. Two devices racing raises: the higher max commits; the lower, if it arrives after, is refused as not a raise. A device must not write a stored local max that would clobber a higher server max.
20. **Optimistic UI:** after tap, show “Submitting…”. Do not show “You’re winning at $Y” or jump to an order confirmation until the server ack. In the last 60 seconds this is load-bearing, not polish.

Caps (enforced at write): 4,096 bidders/listing; 64 raises per pair; 8,192 `bid_events`; max bid 10⁹ cents; 50,000 watches.

### 2. Invariants, in plain language, before any code

Tags: **[money]**, **[secret]**, **[time]**, **[foreign]** (other people’s data).

1. **[money][secret]** A listing’s public `currentPriceCents` always equals `recompute(...)` over the full set of `proxy_bids` for that listing (empty set → no bids, price displayed as `startPriceCents`, `highBidderId` null). No other writer may set `currentPriceCents`.
2. **[secret]** Only the current high bidder has an unrevealed max, and only the unused headroom is unrevealed. The engine may write a public `bid_event` that equals an exhausted bidder’s `maxCents` at the moment they lose the lead. Nobody’s `maxCents` is readable as a field except by that bidder (and by the engine inside a listing-serialized bid/BIN/close transaction).
3. **[foreign]** `bidCount` equals the number of `bid_events` rows for the listing. `uniqueBidderCount` equals the number of `proxy_bids` rows. Both are the same integer for every reader.
4. **[money][time]** A `placeMaxBid` or `buyItNow` whose transaction `now` is ≥ `listing.endsAt` refuses, even if `state` is still `live`. Bidding liveness is a clock fact; `state` is a settlement fact.
5. **[money][time]** Close is idempotent: at most one terminal sale per listing. `orders.listingId` is unique. A listing is sold at most once, either by BIN or by close with a winner, never both.
6. **[money]** BIN is committable only when `bidCount = 0` and `binPriceCents` is non-null and `state = live` and `now < endsAt`. After any `bid_event` exists, BIN refuses `BIN_UNAVAILABLE`.
7. **[money]** The seller’s `userId` never appears as `proxy_bids.bidderId` or as a BIN buyer on their listing.
8. **[secret][time]** `firstBidAt` is set once, on insert of the proxy row, and is the leadership tie-break (then `bidderId` lexicographic as a last resort). `lastRaiseAt` must not be used to rank.
9. **[money]** A bidder’s new max must be strictly greater than their existing max if any; otherwise `MUST_RAISE`. If they are not the current high bidder, the new max must also be ≥ `nextMinBid` (start price when `bidCount = 0`, else `currentPriceCents + increment(currentPriceCents)`).
10. **[money]** Reserve, if set, is met iff the high max ≥ `reservePriceCents`. When that holds, `currentPriceCents ≥ reservePriceCents` (the engine stands the high bidder at reserve even with no challenger). `reservePriceCents` is never copied onto a public row.
11. **[money][foreign]** An outbid notice with `dedupeKey = outbid:{listingId}:{writeVersion}` is inserted in the **same commit** as the bid that changed `highBidderId`, addressed to the previous high bidder, and only then. Price-only changes do not outbid.
12. **[money]** The proxy engine may change the listing’s public standing and may append a `bid_event` attributed to the incumbent who is not the request principal, but only up to that incumbent’s stored `maxCents`, and only inside the serialized listing transaction.
13. **[time]** Policy on a listing (start, reserve, BIN, shipping, duration, return window, increment table identity) is frozen at publish for display and frozen against mutation after the first bid. Changing a seller default must not rewrite live or ended listings.
14. **[money][time]** After `ended_sold` or `sold_bin`, exactly one order exists with `totalCents = hammerCents + shippingCents`. Escrow release moves `paid_held` → `completed` once; double release is refused.
15. **[foreign][time]** Feedback is append-only, one row per `(orderId, role)`, only after `order.state = completed`, only by the two parties, only while `now < completedAt + 60 days`. A party cannot edit or delete the other party’s row.
16. **[money]** Fee entries are append-only. Insertion is written at publish; FVF at sale. An invoice sums currently uninvoiced entries for one seller and one UTC calendar month and does not invent amounts.
17. **[time]** A return may be opened only by the buyer, only while `now < deliveredAt + returnWindowDays * 86400000` (and `returnWindowDays > 0`), and only if the order is `shipped`, `delivered`, `paid_held`, or `completed`.
18. **[money]** Wallet-external payments are idempotent on `orderId`: at most one successful capture per order, amount exactly `totalCents`.
19. **[foreign]** A user’s public `feedbackScore` is the sum of `rating` over feedback **to** them; `feedbackCount` is that row count. Readers who cannot see a comment can still see the aggregates.
20. Silent max-raise (Invariant 9 raise by the current high that does not change `recompute` output) must not increment `publicEpoch`, must not append `bid_events`, and must not notify watchers. It must increment `writeVersion`.

### 3. The traps, ranked

#### Trap 1 — The public price is a fold over private maxima (visibility + derived value)

**Wrong version.** Treat `placeMaxBid(amount)` as “set current price to `amount` if it beats current,” and store typed amounts as bid history. Or compute `uniqueBidderCount` by counting `proxy_bids` the caller may read.

**Why it is wrong.** The typed amount is a ceiling. Alice max $50, start $1 → public $1, not $50. Bob max $20 → public $20.50 (`increment(2000)=50`), Alice still winning, Alice never typed $20.50. Headroom is the secret; publishing typed maxima destroys it. A bidder can read **their** proxy row (0 or 1). A visibility-filtered bidder count is 0 or 1 on a 400-bidder listing — an admitted, **wrong** integer. `bidCount` / `uniqueBidderCount` / `currentPriceCents` are resource facts, maintained on the listing in the same commit as the private write, or counted with engine privilege over the unfiltered set.

**Observation.** After Alice $50 and Bob $20, every viewer sees $20.50 and `bidCount ≥ 2`, never 50. Alice’s standing: `maxCents=5000`, `isHighBidder=true`. Bob’s: `maxCents=2000`, `isHighBidder=false`. A bidder-scoped unique count of `1` fails the public page.

Second-price leakage is **intended**: when Carl bids $80, Alice is exhausted and her $50 is reconstructible (`currentPriceCents = 5100`). The invariant is the **current** leader’s headroom, not eternal secrecy of every max.

#### Trap 2 — Bidding liveness is a clock; `state` is a settlement flag (temporal)

**Wrong version.** Admit bids whenever `state = live`. Close is a sweep that sets `state = ended_sold` when it notices `now ≥ endsAt`. Or admit bids when the client countdown is positive.

**Why it is wrong.** Between `endsAt` and the sweep, `state` is still `live`. Last-second snipes that serialize after `endsAt` would stick, possibly after a winner was already chosen, or would create a bid the close never saw. Conversely, treating the UI clock as authority rejects legal snipes (device 12s slow) and accepts illegal ones (device 12s fast). This is the membership-vs-history shape: **“open for bids” is not a stored enum.** It is `state = live ∧ serverNow < endsAt`. Settlement is a later, idempotent write that must re-check both.

**Observation.** Freeze a listing with `endsAt = T` and `state = live`. At `T + 50ms`, before the sweep, `placeMaxBid` must return `AUCTION_ENDED`. At `T - 50ms`, it must be eligible even if the client rendered 0s. After close commits `ended_sold`, a second close is a no-op; `orders` still has one row.

Related: ranking by `lastRaiseAt` lets a later raise steal a tied max. Sort `maxCents DESC, firstBidAt ASC, bidderId ASC`. `firstBidAt` never moves.

#### Trap 3 — Five writers, one listing (concurrency)

**Wrong version.** `placeMaxBid`, `buyItNow`, `cancelLiveListing`, `closeExpiredListings`, and a seller revise each read, decide, and write without a single serialization point. Or they serialize bids with each other but not with BIN/close.

**Why it is wrong.** The preconditions differ: BIN needs `bidCount = 0`; cancel needs `bidCount = 0`; bid needs `now < endsAt`; close needs `now ≥ endsAt`; a max-raise needs the caller’s proxy row. Two of these committing is how you get a paid BIN **and** an auction winner, or a cancelled listing with a winning bid, or a close that misses the snipe that committed 3ms earlier.

**Observation.** On a live listing with BIN, fire `placeMaxBid(startPrice)` and `buyItNow` at the same millisecond. Exactly one commit: either `bidCount ≥ 1` and `BIN_UNAVAILABLE` for the other, or `state = sold_bin` and `AUCTION_ENDED`/`NOT_LIVE` for the other, and exactly one order. Fire 200 `placeMaxBid`s in the last 2 seconds plus a close at `endsAt`: every accepted bid has `bid.now < endsAt`; the winner is `recompute` of the set that committed; losers after `endsAt` see `AUCTION_ENDED`; one order.

#### Trap 4 — The engine bids as Alice while serving Bob’s request (authority)

**Wrong version.** `bid_events.bidderId` must equal the authenticated principal of the request. Or: any request may insert a `bid_event` for any user.

**Why it is wrong.** Proxy standing **is** an act on Alice’s behalf, authorized by Alice’s stored max, triggered by Bob. If you forbid attribution to non-callers, you cannot show Alice’s automatic stand and you are forced to lie about who is standing. If you allow arbitrary attribution, the seller (or anyone) can forge Alice’s bid history. The grant is narrow: inside the listing-serialized engine, after `recompute`, the engine may append `proxy_stand` or `exhausted` events for the **incumbent high bidder** (or just-exhausted high bidder) up to their stored max.

**Observation.** Alice winning at $10 with max $50. Bob submits max $20. History contains a row attributed to **Alice** at $20.50, and Alice did not send a request. A security log that requires `event.actorId = request.principalId` for every insert will drop that row or reject the bid — both product-wrong. A handler that lets Bob pass `onBehalfOf=Alice` as an input is also wrong.

#### Trap 5 — Outbid is a leadership edge, not a bid event (commit-triggered alerts)

**Wrong version.** Email the previous high bidder on every accepted bid. Or have the client watch the listing and notify itself. Or enqueue the push before commit.

**Why it is wrong.** Bob bidding $20 under Alice’s $50 max must **not** outbid Alice; it must raise the public price. Only Carl at $80 should notify Alice. If the client is the notifier, Alice’s closed laptop never learns. If the push is sent pre-commit, a retried/rolled-back bid notifies her of a loss that did not happen. Duplicate retries without `dedupeKey` notify twice.

**Observation.** Sequence: Alice $50, Bob $20, Carl $80. Notice table contains **one** `outbid` to Alice, keyed to the writeVersion of Carl’s commit, none to Alice for Bob, none to Bob (Bob never led). Alice offline still has the notice row. A rolled-back Carl attempt leaves no notice.

Watcher “price changed” alerts may coalesce per listing per 10s outside the bid transaction. Do not put 10k watcher rows in the hot commit.

#### Trap 6 — Raise-only maxima and no offline bid queue (merge / multi-device)

**Wrong version.** Last-write-wins on `proxy_bids.maxCents`. Or enqueue `placeMaxBid` when offline and flush on reconnect.

**Why it is wrong.** Phone sets max $80, commits. Stale tablet then sends $50 from a form filled 30s earlier. LWW stores $50, silently lowering a max (forbidden) and possibly flipping the leader and the public price. An offline queue is worse: a bid flushed after `endsAt` is a post-close write; a bid flushed after the user already BIN’d on another device is a second purchase path; a bid flushed after the user raised higher on another device lowers or double-applies.

**Observation.** Device A shows local draft max $50; device B committed $80; A submits. Server returns `MUST_RAISE` (50 ≤ 80), public price unchanged, max remains $80. Airplane-mode tap on Bid does not create a durable local write that later syncs; it fails.

Types: `Id` string ULID, `UserId` string, `Time` int64 Unix ms, `Cents` int64. Enums are strings. No floating money.

|---|---|
| [0, 100) | 5 |
| [100, 500) | 25 |
| [500, 2_500) | 50 |
| [2_500, 10_000) | 100 |
| [10_000, 25_000) | 250 |
| [25_000, 50_000) | 500 |
| [50_000, 100_000) | 1_000 |
| [100_000, 250_000) | 2_500 |
| [250_000, 500_000) | 5_000 |
| [500_000, ∞) | 10_000 |

- `id` UserId PK
- `handle` string unique, public
- `suspended` bool
- `feedbackScore` int **derived** (sum of `feedback.rating` where `toId = id`)
- `feedbackCount` int **derived**

- `binPriceCents` Cents | null; if set, `≥ startPriceCents + increment(startPriceCents)` and `≥ reservePriceCents` if reserve set
- `shippingCents` Cents ≥ 0
- `returnWindowDays` 0 | 14 | 30
- `durationDays` 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 | 10
- `publishedAt` Time, `endsAt` Time  **time-bounded offer window** `[publishedAt, endsAt)`
- `state` `draft | live | sold_bin | ended_sold | ended_unsold | cancelled`
- `currentPriceCents` Cents **derived**, public
- `bidCount` int ≥ 0 **derived**, public

- `maxCents` Cents  **secret, bidder-only**
- `firstBidAt` Time  immutable

- `raiseCount` int  1..64

Index: `(listingId, maxCents DESC, firstBidAt ASC)` to fold top-2; `(bidderId, lastRaiseAt)`.

#### `proxy_bid_revisions` **append-only**, secret, bidder-only
- `id` Id PK

- `requestId` string  idempotency; unique `(listingId, bidderId, requestId)`

- `id`, `orderId` unique while open (at most one open return per order)

- `id`, `sellerId`, `listingId`, `orderId` | null

- `amountCents`, `createdAt`, `invoiceId` | null

#### `invoices`
- `id`, `sellerId`
- `periodStart`, `periodEnd` Time  (UTC month, time-bounded)
- `totalCents`, `state` `open | paid | overdue`
- `createdAt`

#### `description_appends` **append-only**
- `listingId`, `body`, `createdAt`  (seller, after first bid)

**Relationships.** Listing→seller; `proxy_bids` N:1 listing, unique `(listingId,bidderId)`; one order per listing; feedback/returns/payments hang off orders; fee entries → listing/order and optionally invoice.

**Derived (same transaction as sources):** listing `currentPriceCents`, `bidCount`, `uniqueBidderCount`, `highBidderId`, `reserveMet`, `publicEpoch`; user `feedbackScore`, `feedbackCount`; `fee_entries.invoiceId` when invoiced.

**Mutable:** `listings`, `proxy_bids`, `orders`, `returns`, `invoices`, user aggregates, `watches` (CAS on `writeVersion` where specified). **Append-only:** `proxy_bid_revisions`, `bid_events`, `notices`, `order_events`, `payments`, `feedback`, `fee_entries`, `description_appends`, except `notices.deliveredAt` and `fee_entries.invoiceId`.

**Time-bounded:** offer `[publishedAt, endsAt)`; payment due; feedback 60d from `completedAt`; return from `deliveredAt`; auto-release `shippedAt+3d`; invoice period.

**Public projection:** listing fields except `reservePriceCents`. No `maxCents`.

### 5. The hard operations

Shared helper, pure, used by §5.2 and §5.5:

```
increment(p):  # table in §4

recompute(startPrice, reservePrice, proxies):
  # proxies: [{bidderId, maxCents, firstBidAt}]
  if proxies is empty:
    return {highBidderId: null, currentPrice: startPrice,
            reserveMet: reservePrice is null, secondBidderId: null}
  ranked = sort proxies by maxCents DESC, firstBidAt ASC, bidderId ASC
  high = ranked[0]
  if ranked.length == 1:
    if reservePrice != null and high.maxCents >= reservePrice:
      return {high.bidderId, currentPrice: reservePrice, reserveMet: true, second: null}
    return {high.bidderId, currentPrice: startPrice,
            reserveMet: reservePrice is null, second: null}
  second = ranked[1]
  beat = second.maxCents + increment(second.maxCents)
  price = min(high.maxCents, beat)
  met = (reservePrice is null) or (high.maxCents >= reservePrice)
  if met and reservePrice != null:
    price = max(price, min(reservePrice, high.maxCents))
  return {high.bidderId, price, met, second.bidderId}

nextMinBid(listing):
  if listing.bidCount == 0: return listing.startPriceCents
  return listing.currentPriceCents + increment(listing.currentPriceCents)
```

Every listing write below takes a **listing-scoped exclusive lock** (or a serializable transaction whose conflict key is `listingId`) for the whole procedure. On lock, re-read the listing. `now` is frozen once at the start of the transaction.

#### 5.1 `publishListing` (write)

- **Inputs:** `sellerId`, `title`, `description`, `categoryId`, `startPriceCents`, `reservePriceCents?`, `binPriceCents?`, `shippingCents`, `returnWindowDays`, `durationDays`.
- **Auth:** caller is `sellerId`, not suspended, no invoice `overdue`.
- **Steps:** (1) Validate price inequalities in §4. (2) Insert `listings` with `state=live`, `publishedAt=now`, `endsAt=now+durationDays*86400000`, `currentPriceCents=startPriceCents`, `bidCount=0`, `uniqueBidderCount=0`, `highBidderId=null`, `reserveMet=(reservePriceCents is null)`, `hasReserve=(reservePriceCents != null)`, `writeVersion=0`, `publicEpoch=0`. (3) Insert `fee_entries` insertion 35¢. (4) Commit.
- **Atomic:** listing + insertion fee.
- **Eventual:** search index of live listings.
- **Optimistic UI:** “Publishing…” then navigate on ack.
- **Conflict:** new id. Retry with same client `listingId` returns the existing row.

#### 5.2 `placeMaxBid` (write) — the ordinary hot path

- **Inputs:** `listingId`, `bidderId`, `maxCents`, `requestId` (client ULID).
- **Auth:** caller is `bidderId`, not suspended, not `listing.sellerId`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Lock listing; freeze `now`; load listing.
  2. If `requestId` already in `proxy_bid_revisions` for this pair: return the original ack (idempotent), no new fold.
  3. If `state != live` or `now >= endsAt`: refuse `AUCTION_ENDED`.
  4. If `uniqueBidderCount >= 4096` and caller has no `proxy_bids` row: refuse `LISTING_FULL`.
  5. Load caller’s `proxy_bids` row if any. If exists and `maxCents <= row.maxCents`: refuse `MUST_RAISE`. If `raiseCount >= 64`: refuse `MAX_RAISES`.
  6. If caller is not `highBidderId` (including no row) and `maxCents < nextMinBid(listing)`: refuse `TOO_LOW` with `minRequired = nextMinBid` (this is public information).
  7. Snapshot `prev = {highBidderId, currentPriceCents, reserveMet, bidCount, uniqueBidderCount}`. Load **all** `proxy_bids` for `listingId` (≤ 4096).
  8. Upsert caller’s `proxy_bids`: on insert set `firstBidAt=now`, `raiseCount=1`; on raise set `maxCents`, `lastRaiseAt=now`, `raiseCount++`. Append `proxy_bid_revisions`.
  9. `next = recompute(startPriceCents, reservePriceCents, proxies including caller)`.
  10. Build `events` (empty if silent raise: `next` equals `prev` in high, price, reserveMet):
      - If `prev.highBidderId is null`: one event `(caller, next.currentPrice, first)`. If `next.reserveMet` and listing `hasReserve`, kind `reserve_meet` instead.
      - Else if `next.highBidderId == prev.highBidderId` and `next.currentPrice > prev.currentPrice`: `(caller, min(caller.maxCents, next.currentPrice), challenge)` and `(prev.highBidderId, next.currentPrice, proxy_stand)`.
      - Else if `next.highBidderId != prev.highBidderId`: `(prev.highBidderId, prevHigh.maxCents, exhausted)` and `(caller, next.currentPrice, challenge)`. If reserve newly met, use kind `reserve_meet` **instead of** `challenge` (one row, do not double-count).
  11. Insert `events`. Set listing `currentPriceCents=next.currentPrice`, `highBidderId=next.highBidderId`, `reserveMet=next.reserveMet`, `bidCount=prev.bidCount+events.length`, `uniqueBidderCount=count(proxies)`, `writeVersion++`. If `events` non-empty: `publicEpoch++`.
  12. If `prev.highBidderId != null` and `next.highBidderId != prev.highBidderId`: insert `notices` `{type:outbid, userId: prev.highBidderId, dedupeKey: outbid:{listingId}:{writeVersion}}`.
  13. If `bidCount` was 0 and events non-empty, BIN is now dead (derived from `bidCount`, no extra flag write required).
  14. Commit. Ack `{accepted:true, isHighBidder: next.highBidderId==caller, currentPriceCents, bidCount, uniqueBidderCount, reserveMet, maxCents, endsAt}`.
- **Atomic:** proxy upsert, revision, listing derived fields, bid_events, outbid notice. No interval where price moved without the notice row, or notice without the leader change.
- **Eventual:** push/email of the notice; watcher coalesced “price changed”; search index of current price.
- **Optimistic UI:** “Submitting…”. **Forbidden:** “You’re winning at $Y” from local math. On timeout, call §5.7; do not resubmit without the same `requestId`.
- **Conflict:** another writer holds the listing lock. Wait, re-read, retry from step 3, up to 16 times, then `BUSY`. If the competing writer was BIN or close, retry observes terminal state and refuses `AUCTION_ENDED` / `NOT_LIVE`. Two raises from one user: higher max should be sent with a new `requestId`; the lower arriving second gets `MUST_RAISE`.

#### 5.3 `buyItNow` (write, competing path)

- **Inputs:** `listingId`, `buyerId`, `requestId`.
- **Auth:** caller is `buyerId`, not seller, not suspended.
- **Steps:** Lock listing. Idempotency: if `requestId` already produced an order, return it. If `state != live` or `now >= endsAt`: `AUCTION_ENDED`. If `binPriceCents is null`: `NO_BIN`. If `bidCount > 0`: `BIN_UNAVAILABLE`. Set `state=sold_bin`, `endedAt=now`, `winnerId=buyerId`, `hammerPriceCents=binPriceCents`, `highBidderId=buyerId`, `currentPriceCents=binPriceCents`, `writeVersion++`, `publicEpoch++`. Insert `orders` (`source=bin`, `state=awaiting_payment`, `paymentDueAt=now+4d`, totals = BIN + shipping). Insert FVF `fee_entries`. Insert notices `bin_bought` to buyer and `you_sold` to seller. Commit.
- **Atomic:** listing terminal state + order + FVF + notices.
- **Eventual:** push delivery; search removal.
- **Optimistic UI:** spinner; no order screen until ack.
- **Conflict:** first bid wins → `BIN_UNAVAILABLE`. Close wins after `endsAt` → `AUCTION_ENDED`. Unique `orders.listingId` is a backstop, not the primary guard.

#### 5.4 `cancelLiveListing` (write, competing path)

- **Inputs:** `listingId`, `reason`.
- **Auth:** caller is `sellerId`.
- **Steps:** Lock listing. `state==live` and `bidCount==0` and `now < endsAt`, else `HAS_BIDS` or `NOT_LIVE`. Set `state=cancelled`, `endedAt=now`, `cancelReason`, `writeVersion++`, `publicEpoch++`. No insertion refund. Commit.
- **Atomic:** state change.
- **Eventual:** search removal.
- **Optimistic UI:** confirm dialog, then wait for ack.
- **Conflict:** first bid or BIN beats cancel → `HAS_BIDS` or `NOT_LIVE`.

#### 5.5 `closeExpiredListings` (scheduled + bulk)

- **Inputs:** none (worker). Batch `K=100`.
- **Auth:** system only. Not invocable by users. Must not use a user `sellerId` as actor on `order_events`.
- **Steps:** (1) Select up to 100 listings where `state=live` AND `endsAt <= now`, ordered by `endsAt`. (2) For each, lock listing, re-read. If `state != live`: skip (idempotent). If `now < endsAt`: skip (woke early). (3) `next = recompute(...)` on current `proxy_bids` (must match stored derived fields; if mismatch, repair from `recompute` then settle — defensive). (4) If `uniqueBidderCount==0` or `hasReserve and not reserveMet`: `state=ended_unsold`, `winnerId=null`, `endedAt=now`, versions++. Notice seller `you_sold` is **not** sent; no order. (5) Else: `state=ended_sold`, `winnerId=highBidderId`, `hammerPriceCents=currentPriceCents`, `endedAt=now`. Insert order `source=auction`, `awaiting_payment`, `paymentDueAt=now+4d`. Insert FVF. Notices `you_won` (buyer), `you_sold` (seller). (6) Commit per listing (do not one-transaction the batch: one failure must not roll back other closes). (7) Same worker, second query: orders `awaiting_payment` AND `paymentDueAt <= now` → lock order+listing, set `unpaid_cancelled`, notice both parties. (8) Third query: orders `shipped` AND `autoReleaseAt <= now` AND no open return → run the release body of §5.11 as system.
- **Atomic:** per listing, settlement + order + FVF + notices. Per order for unpaid expiry / auto-release.
- **Eventual:** push; search; invoice remains monthly.
- **Optimistic UI:** listing page already shows “ended” from `now >= endsAt` (clock) before this job; it must not show a winner until this commit. “Ending…” is allowed; “Sold to X for $Y” is not, until `state` is `ended_sold`.
- **Conflict:** a snipe holds the lock with `now < endsAt`, commits, then close runs and sees the new set — **correct**, that snipe counts. A snipe arriving after `endsAt` waits, then refuses. Double-scheduled workers: second sees non-`live` and skips.

Cadence: every 200ms. ~25k endings/day (~0.3/s) with minute-aligned bursts.

#### 5.6 `getListingLiveView` (read)

- **Inputs:** `listingId`. Optional `publicEpoch` for long-poll/live.
- **Auth:** none (public).
- **Steps:** Load listing by id. If `draft` and caller ≠ seller: `NOT_FOUND`. Strip `reservePriceCents`. Include `secondsRemaining = max(0, endsAt - serverNow)` as a **display** field, not an authority. Include `binAvailable = (binPriceCents != null and state==live and bidCount==0 and serverNow < endsAt)`. Include `acceptingBids = (state==live and serverNow < endsAt)`. Do not include any `maxCents`.
- **Atomic:** single-row read at a version. `bidCount` on this row must match `count(bid_events)` at that writeVersion.
- **Eventual:** a replica may lag; product SLA is 1s for connected live queries. Stale `currentPriceCents` behind a committed higher bid is a **bug**, not eventual consistency, if still served after 1s on a live connection.
- **Optimistic UI:** n/a (read). Cached screens must badge “may be stale” if `fetchedAt` older than 5s on a live listing in its last 15 minutes.
- **Conflict:** n/a. Live subscribers key off `publicEpoch`. Silent max-raises do not wake them.

#### 5.7 `getMyAuctionStanding` (read)

- **Inputs:** `listingId`.
- **Auth:** authenticated. Seller gets seller view; bidder gets bidder view; others get the public view plus `isHighBidder=false`, no `maxCents`.
- **Steps:** Load public listing. If caller has `proxy_bids` row (owner read): return `maxCents`, `firstBidAt`, `raiseCount`, `isHighBidder = (highBidderId==caller)`, `headroomCents = max(0, maxCents-currentPriceCents)` if high else 0, `nextMinBid` if not high. If caller is seller: also `reservePriceCents`. Never return another user’s `maxCents`.
- **Atomic:** standing is listing derived fields + caller’s proxy row; if `isHighBidder` and caller’s max < `currentPriceCents`, the replica is torn — refuse to display and retry (should be impossible after §5.2’s single commit).
- **Eventual:** none for the bidder on a live connection.
- **Optimistic UI:** do not locally compute `isHighBidder` from a guessed second price.
- **Conflict:** n/a.

#### 5.8 `getBidHistory` (read)

- **Inputs:** `listingId`, `cursor`, `limit` ≤ 50.
- **Auth:** public if listing is not `draft`.
- **Steps:** Page `bid_events` by `(createdAt, id)`. Join `users.handle`. Do not join `proxy_bids`.
- **Atomic:** a page may omit an event that commits mid-page; clients reconcile with `writeVersion` on the listing.
- **Eventual:** acceptable for history pagination ≤ 1s.
- **Optimistic UI:** none.
- **Conflict:** n/a.

#### 5.9 `captureBuyerPayment` (write, external processor)

- **Inputs:** `orderId`, `paymentMethodHandle` (processor token).
- **Auth:** caller is `order.buyerId`.
- **Steps:** (1) Load order; require `awaiting_payment` and `now < paymentDueAt`. (2) Insert `payments` row `pending` with `idempotencyKey=order:{orderId}`, `amountCents=totalCents`. If a `succeeded` row exists, return success. (3) Call processor `Charge(amount=totalCents, idempotency_key=order:{orderId}, metadata=orderId)`. (4) On success: `payments.status=succeeded`, order `paid_held`, `paidAt=now`, `order_events`, notice seller `payment_received`. (5) On processor timeout: leave `pending`; a webhook `payment_intent.succeeded` with the same idempotency key runs step 4. (6) Duplicate webhook: unique/idempotent no-op.
- **Atomic:** local state transition `awaiting_payment → paid_held` once. The processor charge is **not** in the DB transaction; the idempotency key is the join.
- **Eventual:** webhook may complete a charge the client timed out. UI polls order state.
- **Optimistic UI:** “Processing payment…” — never “paid” until `paid_held`.
- **Conflict:** two devices pay: one `pending` insert wins unique key; the other receives the same succeeded order. Unpaid-expiry concurrently: if expiry commits first, charge must **refund** (compensation); do not set `paid_held` on a cancelled order. If charge commits first, expiry no-ops.

#### 5.10 `confirmShipment` (write)

- **Inputs:** `orderId`, `trackingNumber`.
- **Auth:** caller is `sellerId`; order `paid_held`.
- **Steps:** Set `shipped`, `shippedAt=now`, `autoReleaseAt=now+3d`, `deliveredAt` still null, `trackingNumber`, `order_events`, notice buyer `shipped`.
- **Atomic:** those fields + notice.
- **Eventual:** push.
- **Optimistic UI:** allowed to show “Marking shipped…”; tracking must round-trip.
- **Conflict:** buyer already `disputed` or `refunded` → refuse. Double-click: idempotent if already `shipped` with same tracking.

#### 5.11 `confirmDeliveryAndReleaseEscrow` (write)

- **Inputs:** `orderId`.
- **Auth:** caller is `buyerId`, or system auto-release from §5.5.
- **Steps:** Require `shipped` or `delivered`, no open return, not `disputed`. If funds not released: set `completed`, `deliveredAt=now` if null, `completedAt=now`, `releasedAt=now`. Append `order_events`. This is the money-release: **no second release**. (Payout to seller’s external balance is out of process; a payout row keyed by `orderId` unique is the backstop.)
- **Atomic:** state `completed` + `releasedAt` not null, once.
- **Eventual:** actual bank payout.
- **Optimistic UI:** pending until ack; do not credit the seller locally.
- **Conflict:** auto-release vs buyer confirm: one wins, the other no-ops on `completed`. Open return: `RETURN_OPEN`.

#### 5.12 `leaveFeedback` (write)

- **Inputs:** `orderId`, `rating` -1|0|1, `comment`.
- **Auth:** caller is buyer or seller on a `completed` order; `now < completedAt + 60d`; no existing row for that `role`.
- **Steps:** Insert `feedback`. Recompute `users.feedbackScore` and `feedbackCount` for `toId` in the same commit (`score += rating`, `count += 1`).
- **Atomic:** feedback row + denormalized counters.
- **Eventual:** public profile live query.
- **Optimistic UI:** author’s own row may show immediately; public score waits for ack.
- **Conflict:** unique `(orderId, role)` — second submit returns the existing row.

#### 5.13 `openReturn` (write)

- **Inputs:** `orderId`, `reason`.
- **Auth:** caller is buyer; `returnWindowDays > 0`; `now < deliveredAt + window` (if `deliveredAt` null, window starts at `shippedAt`); order in `shipped|delivered|paid_held|completed`.
- **Steps:** Insert `returns` `requested`. If order is `paid_held` or `shipped` (not yet released), it stays unreleased (`autoReleaseAt` cleared). If already `completed`, insert a compensating payout clawback **request** (do not silently un-complete; support/dispute path). Notice seller `return_opened`.
- **Atomic:** return row + hold extension.
- **Eventual:** support tooling.
- **Optimistic UI:** none for money; “Submitting return…” only.
- **Conflict:** auto-release racing the return: if release commits first, this follows the `completed` branch. If return commits first, §5.11 refuses.

#### 5.14 `generateMonthlySellerInvoices` (periodic bulk)

- **Inputs:** `periodStart`, `periodEnd` (UTC month), optional `sellerId` for repair.
- **Auth:** system, first Monday 00:10 UTC, or operator repair.
- **Steps:** For each `sellerId` with `fee_entries` where `invoiceId is null` and `createdAt in [periodStart, periodEnd)`: insert `invoices` `open` with `totalCents = sum(amountCents)`; patch those entries’ `invoiceId`; notice `invoice_ready`. Batch 500 sellers per transaction. Skip sellers whose invoice for that period exists (idempotent unique `(sellerId, periodStart)`).
- **Atomic:** per seller: invoice row + assignment of that seller’s entries. Do not assign an entry twice (`invoiceId` null check in the update).
- **Eventual:** emailing the invoice PDF; charging the seller’s card (out of scope; `state` stays `open` until a separate pay op).
- **Optimistic UI:** n/a.
- **Conflict:** unique `(sellerId, periodStart)` — loser skips. An in-period FVF that arrives mid-run is billed on a repair pass, never twice.

### 6. Scale and cost

Successful regional marketplace, not eBay-scale:

| Thing | Size |
|---|---|
| MAU | 500,000 |
| Live listings | 80,000 |
| Listings published / ended per day | ~25,000 each |
| Mean bids per listing | 3 |
| Hot listing, last 60s | 80–400 accepted bids, 200–800 attempted |
| Peak `placeMaxBid` QPS global | ~200 |
| Peak `placeMaxBid` QPS **one listing** | ~80 for ~10s |
| Distinct bidders on a hot listing | 50–400 (cap 4,096) |
| Watches on a hot listing | 2,000–20,000 (cap 50,000) |
| Concurrent live readers on a hot listing | 5,000–30,000 |
| `proxy_bids` rows total | ~15M / year |
| `bid_events` | ~20M / year |
| `notices` | ~8M / year (mostly outbid + lifecycle) |
| Close jobs | 25k/day, bursts of tens per second at minute boundaries |
| Payment captures | ~12k/day |
| Monthly invoice run | ~40k sellers with activity |

**Expensive at that size**

1. **`placeMaxBid` on a hot listing.** Lock hold must stay a few milliseconds (top-2 + caller + previous high for events; unique count incremental). Do not send push, search, or charge under the lock. Live fan-out: 30k `publicEpoch` invalidations × 200 bids ≈ 6M wakeups/minute if you push the full listing.

2. **Watcher alerts inside the bid transaction.** 20k inserts per bid is a cheat-to-death. Coalesce outside.

3. **`closeExpiredListings` minute bursts.** Must not table-scan 80k live rows; the `(state, endsAt)` index is required. Per-listing transactions, not one 100-listing transaction.

4. **`getBidHistory` on a 8k-event listing.** Cursor pagination only; never `ORDER BY createdAt` without `(listingId, createdAt, id)`.

5. **Monthly invoice `sum`.** Index `(sellerId, invoiceId, createdAt)`. Monthly full aggregate is fine; a dashboard that re-sums unbounded history on every view is not.

6. **Feedback aggregates.** Maintain on write; do not `SUM(rating)` on every profile view.

`recompute` over 400 integers is cheap versus lock wait and live invalidation.

### 7. Where a backend would have to be unusual

Row-level queries/mutations plus a cron do not give this product:

**R1. A serialized write that folds a private set into a public row and an outbox, as one commit.** `placeMaxBid` is not “insert bid row.” It is: write a secret, read the unfiltered secret set (or the maintained top-2 plus counts), write public derived fields, write 0–2 public events possibly attributed to a non-caller, write 0–1 notice to a non-caller, bump two different versions. A mutation that can only patch rows the caller owns cannot update Alice’s public stand while serving Bob, cannot bump `bidCount` as a resource fact, and cannot insert Alice’s outbid notice.

*Concession:* drop proxy; each bid is a public English bid and the price **is** the highest typed amount. Honest only if you stop calling maxima private and stop advertising second-price standing. *Cheat:* keep proxy in the UI copy but recompute price in a later job (“eventual current price”). Under hot-item contention that **is** the product, users bid on a stale number and win/lose wrong.

**R2. Predicate-conditional competing writers on one row.** Bid / BIN / cancel / close are four procedures whose guards are different predicates on the same listing (`bidCount=0`, `now<endsAt`, `now≥endsAt`, `state=live`). They need one serialization point and commit-if-predicate, not “read then later write.” Unique `orders.listingId` is necessary and **not sufficient** (it does not refuse the bid that arrived after BIN but before the order row was visible to the bid’s snapshot).

*Concession:* no BIN, and close by flipping a flag that bids do not consult (only `endsAt`). Removing BIN is honest if you do not sell BIN. Ignoring `endsAt` in the bid path is a cheat: it is how snipes after close stick.

**R3. Resource-scoped counts and derived public values that are not visibility-filtered.** `bidCount`, `uniqueBidderCount`, `currentPriceCents`, `reserveMet` are about the listing. Evaluating them over “rows I can read” yields 0, 1, or “unknown.”

*Concession:* hide all counts and show only “bids have been placed: yes/no.” That is a different, poorer marketplace. *Cheat:* show a count that is actually “my bids” labeled as “bids.”

**R4. Commit-triggered alerting with transactional outbox.** Outbid is an edge on `highBidderId` in the same commit, delivered later at-least-once using `dedupeKey`. Client-presence watchers are not a substitute for offline losers.

*Concession:* in-app only when the user has the listing open. Honest if you do not promise outbid emails. *Cheat:* promise them and implement them as a live query the victim must be subscribed to.

**R5. A system-scheduled writer with user-like effects, fenced by clock and state.** Close must create orders, fees, and notices under **system** authority, idempotently, without being callable as the seller. Bid admission must use the same server `now` as close. A user-invocable “close my listing” that skips `bidCount=0` is a different op (`cancelLiveListing`) with a different guard.

*Concession:* manual seller-close only, no clock close — not an auction. *Cheat:* a client timer that calls `close` when it thinks time is up (device clock, Trap 2).

**R6. Split versioning.** Silent max-raise must CAS against BIN/close (`writeVersion`) without waking 30k public subscribers (`publicEpoch`). One version field either leaks “the leader raised” or fails to serialize.

A product change is legitimate if you would still want it with an infinitely capable backend (no proxy because you want a fairground auction). It is a cheat when the UI still promises live second-price, BIN race, offline outbid, and hard-close sniping while the implementation dodges R1–R5.

### 8. Confidence

**Standard practice (do not reinvent):** eBay-style proxy / second-price standing with a published increment table; first bid kills BIN; hidden reserve with public met/unmet and auto-stand to reserve; hard close (US eBay, which is why sniping exists); listing-level serialization for a hot item; transactional outbox; idempotent payments with a processor key; append-only ledger for fees; 1:1 order per listing; raise-only maxima; earlier-bid-wins ties; escrow until delivery.

**Specified here with real-but-simplified numbers:** this increment table (not a live scrape of eBay’s current table); FVF 13.25% capped at $750; insertion 35¢; 4-day unpaid window; 3-day auto-release; 60-day feedback; return windows 0/14/30; duration set {1,3,5,7,10}; caps 4096 / 64 / 8192; USD only; quantity 1 only.

**Deliberately omitted (and not required of an implementer):** bid retraction (it is a probing attack on headroom); anti-snipe extensions (that is a different auction; the sealed-bid / soft-close family); Best Offer; multi-quantity; seller-financed holds; shill detection beyond “seller cannot bid as this userId”; tax; relist-with-one-click; partial refunds; chargebacks.

**Disambiguations eBay’s public docs leave open (implement as specified):** two version integers; silent raise with zero public signal; `exhausted` / `proxy_stand` event kinds; watcher coalesce vs transactional outbid; `recompute` total order including `bidderId`.

**Fiat:** `recompute` uses `increment(second.maxCents)`, not a cent-by-cent walk of the table. Boundary crossings (e.g. $24.90 → $25.40 vs $26.00) follow this spec. Do not “improve” them.

High confidence Traps 1–3 and R1–R3 are ordinary (every two-bidder listing). Trap 5 is ordinary (any outbid). Medium that attributed `proxy_stand` is the right history shape versus caller-only events; the engine must still act as Alice for **price**. Dropping attributed events is allowed only if `recompute` and leadership-edge outbid stay.
