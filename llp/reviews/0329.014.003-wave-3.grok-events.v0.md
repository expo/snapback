The original paper’s 14 ops are not 14 plans. v0’s `scan`+`if`+`maintain`+`readable`+`schedule`+`effect` close **single-row** RSVP, offer, grant, and check-in **facts**. What remains is not missing `count`. It is **a set-valued principal join that rules cannot state, a rank over hidden rows, hidden rows consuming `take` on the names page, clock predicates the rules forbid, and fan-outs that are jobs.**

### 2. Schema in v0

Sentinels: no `optional(t)` → `0` / `''` means absent. Enums are `text({ max })`. `json` is opaque to rules and indexes — `grants[]`, `fieldVersions`, `rule`, `overriddenFields`, `changes` are exploded. No `tree`. `never` is the missing-effect. `sys = () => principal.kind === 'system'` (the only kind §3 names). Interval `to` open = `0`. Discriminant unique (partial unique is absent): live rows store `liveKey = 'L'`, ended rows store `liveKey = id`.

Live “rows become mine” **without** copying `userAccess` is **inexpressible in rules**. Closure copies **are** the explicit re-attribution I20 asked for, at the cost of making P2 a write. |

### 6. Language delta

Ranked by whether Doorlist’s central invariants stay slogans without them.

1. **Set-valued principal closure in rules.** Algebraic `exists/get` with *shape* keys can ask “is *this* contact bound to viewer” (the id is on the row) and cannot ask “does viewer have *any* grant on this event among bound contacts.” Smallest addition: either (a) `existsAny(index, { principalIn: viewer.bindings })` with `bindings` a `cap`-bounded host-maintained set, or (b) a `maintain.rows` / derived-table form that is what `userAccess` fakes in programs. *General* (every app with agents, teams, or pre-identity). Amends §3. Without it Trap 1’s correct shape is not a rule.

2. **Column-level read / projection.** Rules are per effect per table. Field grants (hostLabel, address, location, `secretHash`) require splits. Smallest addition: `read` may name a projection per role, or `table.view('door', cols).rules({ read })`. *General* (the design called this routine). Amends §3. Splits work today; they are the T1 denormalization.

3. **Link-holder and anonymous principals.** `ctx.viewer` + `principal.kind === 'system'` is the whole kind lattice. Smallest addition: `principal.kind ∈ {system,user,contact,link,anon}` and a runtime that binds link possession to `contact` **without** unlocking `roleGrants`. *General*. Amends §3 / §4.3. Workaround: args carry `accessLinkId` (secret in the query log; prediction `partial`).

4. **Rank / prefix-count maintained aggregate.** `maintain.count` is a group total, not `count(seq < mine)`. Smallest addition: `maintain.rank(rsvps, { by:['eventId'], order:['waitlistSeq'], where: waiting, as: waitlistRank, readable })` written onto the source row or a side row. *General* (queues). Amends §2 / §4.4. Until then “#3” is a 5k `sys` rewrite or a product-change.

5. **MAC / bearer-token seam.** `db.verifyMac(secretHash, args.mac)` or server-minted non-client `id()`. *General* (tickets, magic links). Amends §4.2 next to `consumeAssetGrant`. HMAC-in-JS is `E_OPAQUE`. Unguessable `id()` is a product substitute (client-mintable ids are the wrong default for a QR).

6. **Savepoints / per-item outcomes.** Bulk invite wants 500 independent unique-constraint results. Smallest addition: `insertSoft` that returns `ok|conflict` without aborting, sealed (no hidden-row identity). *General*. Amends §4.2 / `E_CONSTRAINT`. Client-loop of `inviteOne` is the v0 path.

7. **Partial `unique` / partial `cap` / `where` on exclusion.** Live invitation, live binding, undelivered coalesce key, one open offer per rsvp. Discriminant columns are workarounds. *General*. Amends §2.

8. **Authenticated point-get without `public` or world-`true`.** `contactPrincipals` must be gettable by `(channel,address)` inside `inviteOne` and not listable. `read: _ => true` is `E_PUBLIC_IMPLICIT`. Smallest addition: `read: 'point'` or rule that is only admitted when the enclosing site is `get(unique)`, not `scan`. *General*. Amends §3 / §4.2.

9. **`db.run(namedMutation)` in the same tx** with summed certificates. Op 3→Op 5, Op 13→Op 3, Op 12 following-occurrences. *General*. Amends §4.2. Paste is the v0 path; certificates lie if you pretend the paste is one site.

10. **Inbound host→`system` mutation and `callable:system`.** Webhooks, OTP completion, delivery callbacks. Spec has effects calling named mutations but not the inbound principal. *General*. Amends §1 / §7.

11. **`optional(t)`, `int({ min, max })` on columns and args, `text({ enum })`.** Sentinels collide with epoch, with `id()`, and with unlimited capacity `0`. *General*. Amends §2, §4.3.

12. **Calendar / TZ operator or the effect is the only legal producer of occurrence instants.** *General* for series. Amends §4.1 / §7. Same finding as booking.

13. **Schedule `at` on 0 / open timestamps does not enqueue; mutation-triggered `at: now` is the promote bus.** Spec-silent. *General*. Amends §7. Otherwise every event enqueues promote at epoch, or `promoteAt=now` never runs.

14. **`capSum({ by, of, max })` or a check constraint on maintained scalars.** I4 cannot be `cap`. *General* (inventory). Amends §2. Until then I4 is a `require` every admit can forget.

15. **Publish `G`.** Promote `take:5000` (exam≤5002), manifest 20k, fan-out 500. If `G.examined=10^4` they pass; if 200 they `E_BOUND` and the diagnostic will propose shrinking the product. Amends §5.

16. **`maxOfflineAge` as an app ceiling, not only 24h/7d.** Door manifests. Amends D-D.

Not missing, despite the paper: `maintain`+`readable`, `appendOnly`, `immutable`/`immutableExcept`, `(old,next)`, `exclusion`, `interval`, `schedule`/`system`, `effect` as a kind, `ctx.now` in mutations, OCC on the trace, hidden-rows-consume-`take` as a stated ruling, `public(reason)`. Those were the rungs. v0 shipped them. The residue is **principal union, projection, rank, tokens-as-tickets, partial constraints, and jobs.**

The analogue of “maybe the waitlist should not tell you how many people are in front of you” is **exactly P4/P5 as written.** That is a coherent product only if Doorlist publishes ticket numbers. It is not acceptable as a way to keep I6 *and* a personal rank *and* guest-hidden rows.

Changing dest-style disclosure (`readable` on `confirmedSeats` for every invitee) is **required** by P4 and is **not** a cheat — it is the K2 declaration. Omitting `readable` and counting under visibility **is** the cheat.

### 8. Where the design is wrong

Not taste. Forced by v0 spelling.

1. **`effectivePrincipals` / `effectiveGrants` are specified as lookup functions of `(user, event, t)`, but every v0 rule is a point predicate over shape keys.** Per-row “is this mine” works (`exists(binding, [row.principalId, viewer])`). Per-event “am I staff / may I `get` this event” does not, unless a closure row exists. The design already rejected backfill `userId` (Trap 1) and already required that rows not be re-keyed (I20) while P2 still says the rows “become theirs.” Those three cannot be jointly implemented as rules. Confirming observation: grant co-host to a contact, then bind that contact to user U; `getEventPage` as U either (a) succeeds because a `userAccess` row was written (the explicit act, not a live join), (b) succeeds because the rule scanned U’s contacts (`E_RULE_UNBOUNDED` / illegal loop), or (c) returns miss. If (c), P2 is false. If (b), `check` is wrong. Run that bind and read the certificate’s `readSites`.

2. **Waitlist position and the names page are both specified as ordinary reads over a visibility-filtered table, and both are false under ruling 2/8.** Position is a rank over rows the guest may not name. Names share an index with hidden guests; hidden rows consume `take`, work must not scale with invisible counts, and mixed rules strip receipt extents. Confirming observation: 90 hidden going + 10 named; guest `listGuestNames take:20` returns ≤10 and is `partial` with no extent — it is not “the names page.” A waitlisted guest with 30 hidden parties ahead who `count`s visible waitlisted rows sees `#1`; if `check` refuses, Op 7 step 2 as specified is not a plan. Storing `waitlistRank` and updating everyone behind a leave is a 5k write under the event lock — the cost the design already accepted for promote-when-free=1, now also on every leave.

3. **I13/I21/check-in/RSVP-deadline are clock predicates, and P4’s “same number the write path used” is not a constraint.** Rules cannot contain a clock (K5). `ctx.now` is bound at **intent** and reused, so an offline cancel minted 49h before start still passes a `now ≤ startsAt−48h` `require` after the window closes. The 180-day claim horizon and 90-day grant reconfirm are time-windowed aggregates, inexpressible as `maintain where` (ruling 11). Independently, two `rsvp going` writers that never `get` `confirmedSeats`/`seatGen` overbook: `maintain` updates in the same commit **after** the insert, it does not refuse the insert. Confirming observation: (a) mint `requestGuestRefund` at T−49h, stay offline 3h, replay — if it refunds, I13 is false under §4.3; (b) two parties of 2 at `free=2` without reading the aggregate — if both `going`, I4 is false; (c) claim a contact invited to an event with `endsAt` 181d ago — if those rows are readable without an `inClaimHorizon` flag, I21 is false.
