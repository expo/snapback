## Material findings

**1. The 78,000 target contradicts the proposal’s own evidence rule.**

The candidate already measures **79,272**, which is 1,272 bytes *above* the proposed target. The proposal correctly refuses to credit unmeasured public-Words retirement, then sets a target that is only reachable by counting that (or some other unmeasured) reduction. LLP 1012’s earlier targets sat *above* the then-current complete measurement (46,838 vs 48,000; 62,624 vs 64,000). This one does not.

Fix: omit a numeric target until Words/legacy deletion is measured on the same five roots, or set any target ≥ the last complete measurement (79,272). Do not encode an unmeasured bet in the target while forbidding that bet in the ceiling rationale.

**2. The 80,000 ceiling is the current unfinished candidate plus a rounding crumb, not a remaining-work budget.**

80,000 − 79,272 = **728 bytes** (0.9%). The 66,000 decision on 62,624 WIP left **3,376 bytes** (~5%) “for final review repairs.” This amendment does not.

That matters because LLP 1018.000 still lists unfinished work on the same commission (public Words removal is a deletion; remaining client corrections and other integration fixes may add). A subsequent method on Twin / range store / offline runtime / projection / receipts can consume 728 gzip. 80,000 is the number that makes 79,272 green, plus rounding. It is not shown to cover “fully integrated LLP 1018.000 work.”

Fix, pick one:

- Defer the ceiling until after the already-commissioned Words/legacy deletions, then pin ceiling/target from that measurement with explicit repair headroom; or
- If a number is needed now, state an explicit remaining-work allowance (same posture as the 66k decision), not 728 bytes of slack; or
- Call 80,000 a temporary WIP cap and require a re-pin after remaining commissioned edits, so the next 200-byte fix is not another scope amendment.

**3. Most of the 14,000 raise is not the forwarding slice.**

From the 66,000-era WIP (62,624) the five-root entry is already **77,571 before forwarding** and **79,272 after** (+1,701). The 13,272 deficit vs 66,000 is almost entirely already-landed 1018.000 work, not the +1,701 forwarding delta. Paying gzip to keep bounded-work indexes and retained durable proofs is the right *kind* of trade versus deleting those semantics. I do not have a measured smaller architecture in the allowed evidence that is preferable to that trade. Alternatives 1, 3, and 4 (move bytes out of the entry; scan instead of inverse extents; drop historical proofs) are correctly rejected on instrument and semantics.

What I cannot independently confirm from the two JSON files: that Unicode packet/codec experiments *grew*, or that tagged immutable proofs have no safe body deletion. Those remain author-summarized. They are not required to justify *a* raise; they are required if the claim is “no smaller architecture exists.”

## Not blocking, but keep honest

- Five-root instrument retained; relocating required bytes still does not count. That matches LLP 1012.
- Full-client +6,691 gzip is disclosed and is **not** the binding ceiling. It is also ~4× the five-root forwarding delta, so user-facing download growth is larger than the governed number. Do not treat 80,000 as an app-download budget.
- Runtime resource limits unchanged is the correct non-trade.
- 21.2% / 14,000 arithmetic is correct: 14,000 / 66,000.

## What would make this READY

A revision that (a) stops proposing 78,000 while 79,272 is the last complete five-root number and Words is unmeasured, and (b) either waits for that measurement or sets a ceiling that is a remaining-work decision rather than 79,272 rounded up. After that, recording 66,000 as historical and 80,000-class as the new explicit scope decision in LLP 1012, LLP 1018.000, and `rules/NOT-DOING.md` is the right enactment path. A measurement still does not enact it.
