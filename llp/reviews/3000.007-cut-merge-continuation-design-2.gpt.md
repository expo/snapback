Revision 2 repairs several central problems: the `id` suffix precondition, `has_more` gating, exhaustion after a cut, and late non-uniform eligibility are the right changes. Findings:

1. **High — D2.3 must stop after duplicate pops too.**  
   [Design:77](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:77), [read.rs:854](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:854).

   With more than eight lanes, A and B both head at key 100. A emits 100 and successfully refills to 90, consuming most remaining budget. B’s duplicate 100 is popped without an emission; its refill cuts before establishing its next head, 95. If the new stopping rule runs only “after an emission,” the engine can pop and emit A’s 90, then stop on another refill and issue `next=90`. B’s 95 is skipped.

   **Smallest fix:** apply the refill/unknown-head stop after every heap pop, independently of `seen.insert` and whether the pop emitted a row. Add this exact overlapping-lane cut case.

2. **High — D4’s sole-cause classification does not prove S1’s discharge.**  
   [Design:131](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:131), [runner.rs:462](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/runner.rs:462), [read.rs:305](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:305), [pages.rs:279](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/pages.rs:279).

   A cursor-owning merge drain-cuts after returning row R. A one-to-one result shape then reads `profiles[R.authorId]`. The exhausted examined meter makes that read return null. All tables are uniformly readable; there is no horizon, overflow, lost page position or unknown presence. D4 therefore permits `cut:'meter'` with the merge’s `next`.

   Following that cursor returns later rows and eventually a complete terminal page. S1 discharges the earlier page, but R’s missing profile remains in the accumulated result. The meter was the only *kind* of incompleteness; the continuation did not repair all its consequences.

   **Smallest fix:** strengthen the invariant to “every incomplete contribution is covered by this continuation.” Track cuts outside the resumable page traversal, including result shaping, and return `cut:null` while preserving a safe `next` when that proof fails. Preserve raw continuation/loss facts through finalization; checking only the final encoded cursor is insufficient.

   This requires updating the cards’ promise of precise device reasons. I found no corresponding functional regression in the two chat apps: they use `hasMore` and row ids, not `cut`, to paginate.

3. **Medium — D5 requires an impossible small-lane drain-cut fixture and overstates feature-off discrimination.**  
   [Design:143](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:143), [read.rs:840](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:840), [meter.rs:166](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/meter.rs:166).

   At eight lanes or fewer, each lane buffers `take+1` rows during opening. Before any lane can exhaust that buffer, the merge must encounter more than `take` distinct rows and stop at the page boundary. Sparse filtering and expiry therefore cut opening, not a later refill. Heap steps abort rather than produce the proposed capped refill.

   Moreover, opening-cut/no-`next` and ordinary overlapping-id deduplication already pass without D.

   **Smallest fix:** make small-lane cases explicit unchanged-behavior controls; require positive drain-cut regressions on the one-head branch. Alternatively, deliberately change batching and review that additional change. Separate positive feature tests from regression controls, and construct cheap opening heads/lookahead followed by expensive refills.

4. **High — O2 cannot represent a word boundary inside a fixed matching scope.**  
   [Design:167](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:167), [Design:188](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:188), [read.rs:437](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:437).

   O1 admits fixed lanes alongside the generated specification. Let the first fixed matching scope contain 33 live matching words. `F(1)` means the entire fixed scope is finished; `W(k,w)` requires a lane-source row that this scope does not have. Closing after word 32 therefore has no faithful position. Using `F(1)` skips word 33; retaining B repeats the window.

   **Smallest fix:** add a fixed-scope word position, such as `FW(i,w)`, including its ordering, witness and size rules; or explicitly exclude fixed matching scopes from O.

5. **High — O3/O4 have reachable states with neither a legal continuation nor a defined lossy exit.**  
   [Design:224](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:224), [Design:290](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:290), [meter.rs:23](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/meter.rs:23), [read.rs:579](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:579).

   Two examples:

   - With `take=4096`, the examined reserve is 8194, exceeding the default 8192 ceiling before any unit is attempted. Even an empty first lane fails reservation. There is no last witnessed position, and the “unit cannot complete” rule does not describe a unit that was never attempted.
   - An initial source-index run contains more than one read’s allowance of expired rows before its first live source row. Discovery cuts before producing a legal S witness. Naming an expired key violates O2; retrying the same start repeats the work; the proposed degenerate rule has no live k to skip.

   Rejecting a zero-width shrink does not supply a transition. Likewise, `take=0` needs an explicit policy rather than relying on `values` advancing.

   **Smallest fix:** define all no-witness outcomes, including reserve-only failure and source-discovery failure. Require feasible reservation or choose an adaptive reserve, and specify a truthful terminal incomplete result when safe progress cannot be represented. Do not promise later-lane reachability for that terminal case. The termination proof must include these branches.

6. **Medium — the current-request witness rule conflicts with retaining window boundaries.**  
   [Design:203](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:203), [Design:250](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:250), [read.rs:636](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:636).

   A token ends at `W(k,w)`. The previous page returned that word’s last qualifying row, while other words in the window still have rows. Reopening every lane `after values` produces no head for w, yet O4 requires subsequent tokens to retain the same end. O2 requires that W to produce a live head **in this request**.

   Similarly, if an S endpoint’s source row expires, digest mismatch calls for a restart retaining an endpoint O2 now forbids.

   **Smallest fix:** distinguish introducing a boundary from carrying an already supplied boundary. Define endpoint revalidation and removal/expiry handling explicitly. Any independent re-witnessing read must be metered.

7. **High — O4’s window-local digest cannot support the stated whole-walk coverage promise.**  
   [Design:259](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:259), [read.rs:828](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:828), [rows.rs:103](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/rows.rs:103).

   Finish a window through source key 10. An unvisited source row at key 20 selects a lane containing R. Move that source row’s ordering key to 5 before the next window opens. R itself remains live, readable, unchanged and continuously selected by the merge throughout.

   Subsequent windows start after 10, never discover that source row and never hash its disappearance from the unvisited range. The walk can finish with `lossy=false`, omitting R. No digest collision or forged token is needed.

   **Smallest fix:** either narrow the promise to rows whose selecting lane and source position also remain stable throughout the walk, or add an authorized change witness covering movement across the walk’s frontier. Restarting only the current window cannot repair this case. §A’s claim that the digest provides the necessary restart effect is too broad.

8. **Medium — O3 changes the word limit, and O7’s witness rule does not establish its paired-world equality claim.**  
   [Design:233](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:233), [Design:368](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:368), [NOT-DOING.md:36](snapback4/NOT-DOING.md:36), [read.rs:425](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:425), [read.rs:589](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/read.rs:589).

   Consider 33 live first-term words where only word 33 contains a row satisfying the second term. Today word expansion stops before it. Under “only witnessed heads count,” the first 32 produce no heads, so O can examine word 33 and answer in one window. This changes both the bound and answers outside the reserve band.

   Separately, hold live data and expired-row count constant, but redistribute expired text between repeated and distinct matching words. Distinct-word seeks consume different work. Near a ceiling this changes the last completed visible source unit, hence the window end and the digest of visible source keys. No expired word need appear literally in the token.

   **Smallest fix:** count first-term word lanes independently of later filtering, with witnesses defined accordingly. Reconcile the expired-work exception with O8’s equality assertion: either explicitly permit those workload-dependent observations or provide an expiry-independent discovery mechanism. “Expired content is not copied into a token” is a narrower claim than observational equivalence.

9. **Medium — O6 does not always bind a token to its viewer.**  
   [Design:344](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:344), [rules.rs:101](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/rules.rs:101), [cursor.rs:107](snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/cursor.rs:107).

   A uniformly readable public lane source can have an empty prefix, or a shared organization prefix supplied as an argument. Two viewers then have identical `src` descriptors and evaluated prefixes. Replaying the first viewer’s token does not yield the promised `Scope` refusal.

   This need not widen authorization if every lane is still authorized, but the stated binding and acceptance test are unsupported.

   **Smallest fix:** bind viewer identity explicitly, or restrict eligibility to a source prefix proven to distinguish viewers. Also validate position tags against the actual lane structure, fixed-lane indices and matching mode; typed positions plus `end>start` are insufficient.

10. **High — S3’s cut-based activation fails precisely on D4’s mixed-cause walks.**  
    [Design:447](REVIEW-DESIGN.md:447), [react.tsx:127](snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/react.tsx:127), [react.test.mjs:375](snapback4/packages/snapback4/test/react.test.mjs:375).

    On a device beyond its horizon, every windowed page has `cut:null` under D4. A message appearing under words on both sides of a window boundary is returned twice, but S3 never activates. A live re-answer introducing a horizon can also switch deduplication off for an already accumulated walk.

    Conversely, a non-null cut does not establish a valid identity field: the hook accepts `readonly unknown[]`, and existing tests exercise strings. A terminal cut also activates the proposed rule without any continuation having been followed. Reused projection ids remain subject to data loss when a cut occurs.

    **Smallest fix:** identify window-walk deduplication independently of public cut disclosure, and define a verified stable item identity. Preserve generic/identity-free page behavior explicitly; do not treat missing ids as one shared identity. Add horizon, live cut-to-null, primitive and reused-id regressions.

For the author’s questions: **Q1 yes**, land a corrected D separately; it needs no token change or SDK fill. **Q2 eight attempts is reasonable**, provided replacements share the budget and exhaustion leaves a truthful, manually resumable state. **Q3 defer** single-scope matching until it has a representable position. **Q4 retain the ruling’s terminal completeness**, once the coverage promise is supportable. **Q5 retain the 32-word limit**, counted independently of final matches. **Q6 prefer preserving a safe `next` and withholding the cut reason**, with the stronger continuation-coverage invariant from finding 2; withholding `next` sacrifices useful offline progress.

Before another readiness pass, acceptance also needs explicit v1-to-v3 transition behavior, preservation of an authored lane source’s `first N` boundary across windows, both ordering directions, all relevant meter axes, forged/replayed endpoint states, and SDK fill exhaustion under repeated live replacements. Existing React assertions that followed cuts remain aggregated must change intentionally. The §A dispositions for progress, witness exclusion, coverage, discharge, deduplication and feature-off discrimination remain only partially resolved.
