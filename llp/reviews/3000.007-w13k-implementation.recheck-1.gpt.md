Both join events select the latter span, then both are classified continuous because the former ends at sequence 2. Catch-up at 1600 delivers **no acquisition and no marker**, advances past both, and leaves a horizonless device answering empty-complete. One `seed_at` batch can generate this through successive upserts of the same member ([engine/mod.rs:913](/Users/ccheever/projects/snapback-w13-review-f/repo/snapback4/crates/snapback4-core/src/engine/mod.rs:913)).
