## Agreed by two seats (Fable, Grok)

The Draft's §7.2 — durable owner record, owner generations, idempotent cross-store import, old-client fence — was a second protocol that the Draft itself listed as unsolved, and it was not needed for anything the Draft promised. Both proposed the same replacement from different ends: the online journal is the only dispatcher while it holds any unsettled intent; the device outbox admits only once that journal is empty and the core is ready; the flip is monotonic per document; no import.

Grok's supporting claim, verified: `valid()` in `snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/app-journal.ts` checks only known fields, so an added ownership field would pass unseen and an older runtime would keep replaying `pending`; bytes that fail `valid()` are quarantined to a `.damaged.*` key and an empty journal installed. A new ownership record therefore could not fence old clients or make them fail closed. The replacement rule needs no record.

## Single-seat, checked

- **Fable: the coordinator sits one layer too high.** Verified: `local-query.ts` carries `Candidate` origins `local | server | remembered | composed` and publishes under server-cut-versus-watermark ordering with the LLP 3000.023/3000.028 fences; `createLocalClient` awaits `openLocalDevice` (`local.ts:175`) and binds clock, media, admission authority, outbox listeners and delivery to that core. Held. Disposition: §6 prefers a late-bound core inside the one local client; app-level coordinator is the fallback.
- **Fable: the 1 s cold target is below the transfer floor.** Verified and understated: `react-dom-client.production.js` gzips to 110,481 B and `react.production.js` to 4,626 B in the worktree's pinned builds, so SDK (82 KB) plus React is about 200 KB, or about 1.0 s of transfer alone at 200 KB/s before five or so 150 ms round trips and 4× throttled execution. Held. Disposition: §9 target derived from the floor (median near 2 s as the honest first target); §8 names SSR as the only sub-second path on that profile.
- **Grok: cursor affinity keeps a paged inbox online for its page session.** Already stated in the Draft's §6. No change.

## Split

Disposition: first increment evaluates at open; mid-session evaluation is a later increment gated on a measured long-lived session.
