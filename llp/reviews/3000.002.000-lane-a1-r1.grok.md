**Blocker** — `snapback4/packages/snapback4/src/local.ts:875` (applied from `sendPost` at `local.ts:613`)

A progressive client treats a later program generation or store id as a terminal refusal for the rest of the session. `onlineEvidence` runs on every `/q/` for the whole progressive lifetime, and on the pre-attach `/changes` poll. On `store_id` mismatch or `json.generation !== generation` it sets `partitionDenied` and `coreFailure`, calls `answers.refuse`, and returns failure. `E_GENERATION` is given `family: "store"`. `follow` stops on any `partitionDenied` (`local.ts:968`). `attachCore` returns without opening (`local.ts:1621`). Scenario: startup loaded schema generation 1 and published a server row. A deploy (or a `/changes` payload, or the next `/q/` denial/success, all of which carry `generation`, `store_id`, and `seq`/`head`) reports generation 2. Every watch flips to `{ denied: E_GENERATION }`. The next `query` dies in `local-query.ts` `checkpoint` on `partitionDenied`. The engine never attaches, and sync never adopts the new program. The signed-in client stays dead until the process is reopened. The same sequence on a non-progressive client adopts through `observeIncarnation` and sync (`local.ts:746`). Do not set `coreFailure`, do not `answers.refuse` with a synthetic "reopen the app" refusal, and do not turn the `/q/` into `{ ok: false }`.
