1. **High — §§9–10: P1 is not distinguishable from noise under the proposed experiment.**

Furthermore, **93 is the fitted 0.2.16 endpoint; 96 is its observed score**—“its 0.2.16 fit (93 → 96)” conflates them. 3. **Medium — §1: “beyond noise” is an uncalibrated heuristic.**

   Equally spaced least squares is reasonable for describing **change per observed run**. It does not estimate change per day, release, or byte of exposure, and cannot identify causation here.

   The SD is calculated around the series mean, so it combines trend and variation; it is not an independently measured noise distribution or the uncertainty of the slope. For ten equally spaced observations, the rule corresponds to approximately **|correlation| > 0.319**. Nothing supplied establishes that as a defensible error-rate threshold.

   Leave-one-out tests sensitivity to individual observations, not statistical significance. The implementation also renumbers the remaining observations after removal. Preserving their original positions does not overturn the six falling verdicts, but the method should be stated accurately. [Fit implementation](/Users/ccheever/lanes/f188/benchmarks/scripts/score-trend.mjs:22)
