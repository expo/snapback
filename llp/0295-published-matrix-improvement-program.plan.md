## Summary

The published Snapback 0.1.24 matrix demonstrated that the product's
architectural center is now buildable across three model families, two
authoring profiles, and two client stacks: all six Clique lanes completed
M1-M3 green, all twelve lanes completed, and 35 of 36 milestone cells were
green. It also demonstrated that agents still pay too much to reach that
center. The repeated costs are lifecycle reconstruction, auth custody and
transition debugging, Familiar dialect discovery, app-visible projections
over private authority, provider connection management, browser reruns, and
source inspection when a guide or diagnostic stops one fact short.

A paired current-source Clique run on 2026-08-20/21 corroborated that diagnosis
after the 0.1.24 matrix: both profiles again completed M1-M3 green, but Familiar
lost its M1 advantage in M2/M3 while the builder reconciled live projections,
membership authority, and capability exports. It confirmed that the Familiar
Guide's nullable-document/value result forms and membership `resourceField:
"_id"` spelling disagree with current generated/runtime contracts. The run
also found one six-subscription composition-sensitive live delay and a 489 MB
debug binary. The delay is now classified as LLP 0200 overload rather than a
client defect, but its pre-inconsistency client diagnostic remains open. The
binary remains a routed investigation, not an established cause or release
regression. Their value here is that they sharpen the program's order: make
the declared path coherent and live under ordinary composition before adding
more surface vocabulary.

The 2026-08-22 candidate-0.1.25 Air rerun strengthens the same conclusion
through the public-package-shaped path. Both lanes finished M1-M3 green from a
sealed candidate closure: Explicit in 3,485 seconds and Familiar in 5,129
seconds. The core remained strong—server-derived identity, exact generated
references, live two-client updates, typed assets, membership enforcement, and
identity-bound verification all worked. The remaining correction loops again
clustered outside domain logic: fixed-port supervisor integration, sessions
lifecycle, Guide/runtime disagreement, client-side loss of declared refusal
codes, hydration/settlement timing, and benchmark navigation assumptions.
Candidate packaging did not remove those seams; it made their ownership
clearer.

LLP 0277 is the published-matrix evidence register; the current-source run has
its own retained record named in `Related`. This Plan is the higher-level
response. It does not replace the subsystem owners already carrying mechanisms.
It answers three cross-cutting questions they cannot answer independently:

1. What end-to-end authoring journey should every supported client/profile
   share?
2. Which product and documentation decisions should be made before local
   tickets are executed in parallel?
3. How will the program prove that it reduced correction loops, wall clock,
   and token usage rather than merely moved ceremony between packages?

The program has two lanes. **Straight repairs**—a package cycle, relay
allowlist omission, codegen/runtime mismatch, precise diagnostics, and bounded
guide recipes—can proceed through their filesystem tickets without waiting for
this Plan. **Design work** waits for the Phase 0 rulings below, then lands
through the owning LLPs. This avoids repeating LLP 0207's failure mode: a
coordination document must not become a second owner for mechanisms that
already have one.

harness streams. It supports a prioritized improvement program, not a profile
winner or comparative population claim. In particular:

- host load heavily contaminated Grok Clique Familiar and Opus Clique
  Explicit wall time;
- the Opus Explicit lane was interrupted and resumed;
- harness token vocabularies differ across Codex, Grok, and Claude;
- lane totals do not attribute tokens to milestones or correction episodes;
  and
- correction-loop labels were authored by independent agents and are not yet
  normalized enough to sum as a publication statistic.

extension of the published matrix. It pinned unpublished source commit

## Objectives

1. **One paved first hour.** A published-package agent reaches password auth,
   durable custody, a live two-client query, and an additive schema change
   without source inspection, custom middleware, a custom binding environment,
   or a destructive reseed.
2. **Preserve the differentiators.** Explicit authority, exact generated
   references, runtime-enforced result contracts, membership transitions,
   typed assets, ephemeral state, build-bound documentation, and remediation-
   bearing refusals stay intact.
3. **Make Familiar familiar end to end.** Familiar may retain a deterministic
   restricted runtime, but its first-read contract, generation phases, client
   hooks, and runtime interpretation must agree. Familiar-looking code may
   either work or refuse before output with the supported rewrite.
4. **Make Explicit purposeful rather than repetitive.** Policy decisions stay
   authored; mechanically derivable facts and recurring lifecycle ceremony do
   not require transcription. LLP 0275's four-class ceremony taxonomy governs.
5. **Reduce agent cost.** Lower semantic correction actions, output tokens,
   time to M1 green, restart/reseed cycles, and full browser reruns under a
   matched, load-isolated protocol.
6. **Keep evidence honest.** Instrument defects and external Exact defects are
   classified separately from Snapback product failures; no green count hides
   structurally unobservable product behavior.
7. **Prefer coherence to surface area.** For every advertised paved operation,
   the first-read Guide, public imports and types, generator, runtime, client
   projection, and refusal remediation describe one contract. A capability is
   not paved merely because a lower-level escape can assemble it.

## Non-goals

- Selecting Familiar or Explicit as the default from this N=1 matrix.
- Broadening the handler runtime merely to imitate unrestricted Node or
  Convex. Portability, determinism, and declared capability boundaries remain
  product constraints.
- Making private authority tables public to avoid projection work.
- Replacing Exact's issue tracker or LLP 0279 with Snapback-side duplicate
  tickets.
- Turning every benchmark-specific selector failure into product API design.
- Optimizing prose volume while leaving correction loops intact. The retained
  report artifacts are visible cost, but they are a small fraction of total
  model output.
- Stripping the debug executable or suppressing warnings merely to improve a
  size/count metric. Developer artifact posture must preserve qualified launch,
  useful crash/debug evidence, and actionable migration information.

## Program invariant: vertical paved-path coherence

The dominant remaining failure shape is not absence. It is a semantic fact
changing as the agent crosses layers: a Guide example is rejected by generated
types, generation accepts a result the runtime rejects, a capability exists but
is absent from the profile entrypoint, or a correct auth refusal names only one
of several custody stores. Each layer can be locally defensible while the
journey is globally broken.

Every Phase 1 or W1-W4 closure that changes an advertised authoring path
therefore names a **vertical contract fixture** for that path. The smallest
adequate fixture proves, from packed external packages:

1. the first-read Guide spelling is retrievable with the diary's plausible
   wrong vocabulary;
2. that spelling imports and typechecks from the advertised profile entrypoint;
3. clean first-pass generation accepts it, or refuses before output with one
   supported rewrite;
4. the runtime accepts exactly the generated result/authority contract;
5. the relevant headless and framework client surfaces preserve the same
   nullability, identity, and lifecycle meaning; and
6. one negative case reaches a stable diagnostic naming the semantic fact and
   repair.

This is a test shape, not a new mechanism owner. Subsystem suites continue to
own their layers; the vertical fixture is the integration receipt that prevents
a local fix from moving the mismatch one boundary downstream.

Where an advertised path is advertised on the Expo/native client arm, a
vertical fixture named under this invariant covers that arm too — guide
spelling, types, generation, runtime, native client projection, and negative
diagnostic — not only the web projection. LLP 0294's 2026-08-21 tickets supply
the current concrete cases; this Plan's own qualification (the W3 fixture
catalogue and the R1 probes) consumes whatever evidence those owners produce.
A native-arm fixture missing at qualification time is 0295-side Phase 1/W1-W4
work, never a retroactive expansion of a 0294 ticket's acceptance.

### Phase-order ruling

Work is commissioned in this order, even when independent tickets land in
parallel:

1. **Liveness and identity correctness:** auth transitions, foreground versus
   housekeeping writes, and ordinary multi-subscription consistency.
2. **Vertical contract coherence:** Guide/types/codegen/runtime/client parity
   for paths already advertised.
3. **Lifecycle consolidation and composition:** fixed-origin development,
   private-authority projections, and provider connection ownership.
4. **Ceremony and cost reduction:** generated conveniences, warning density,

## Risks

1. **Umbrella without authority.** Mitigation: every mechanism names an owner;
   this Plan owns sequencing and scorecard only.
2. **Optimizing the benchmark instead of the product.** Mitigation: Phase 1
   paths must be ordinary published-package journeys; benchmark-only selector
   work stays in LLP 0281.
3. **Reducing tokens by hiding guarantees.** Mitigation: outcome assertions and
   generated authority evidence remain fixed; only semantic actions and
   ceremony may fall.
4. **Choosing Familiar from syntax preference.** Mitigation: no default ruling
   before N>=3, and results stay client-stack scoped.
5. **Telemetry becoming a new tax.** Mitigation: D0.6 uses harness-native
   accounting and generated run records; W5 requires a bounded/redacted design
   before runtime instrumentation.
6. **Fast-moving main invalidates rerun setup.** Mitigation: qualify and run
   against exact published closures; optimistic landing remains ordinary, and
   claim evidence binds scoped package/instrument identities rather than a
   quiet HEAD.
7. **A workaround becomes the de facto contract before root cause.** Mitigation:
   1D classifies the six-subscription observation before W4 standardizes the
   relational singleton shape; both independent and aggregate query forms get
   explicit supported bounds.
8. **Vertical fixtures ossify incidental syntax.** Mitigation: fixtures bind
   semantic facts and advertised entrypoints, not internal lowering. A profile
   may narrow or change its spelling through its owning LLP, but all layers and
   the migration diagnostic move together.
