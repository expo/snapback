## 1. Outcome and ownership

An ordinary application should express search, counts, rankings and periodic
work through a small number of declarations and business rules. Snapback
should own their continuation, derived-state maintenance and durable execution.
The proposed completion criterion is **application responsibilities removed,
with correctness and runtime cost measured beside the reduction**.

This document extends its parent and reopens none of its accepted rulings.
The merge contract remains [LLP 3000.007.000](./3000.007.000-merge-position-and-drain-cut-continuation.spec.md);
maintained-column semantics remain [LLP 3000.007.001](./3000.007.001-maintained-columns.rfc.md).
Their reviews, remaining gates and existing implementation ownership stand.
What is added here is priorities, the grouped-page residual, distinct ranking
requirements, and a common builder acceptance experiment. It does not reopen
the implemented increment of [LLP 3000.008](./3000.008-holistic-product-excellence.rfc.md).

Finish the search and direct-digest surface first. Make maintained columns
the next core addition, after the horizon repair and their own activation
gate. Treat ranking by expiring activity as a separate experiment. Measure
the completed application and one subsequent change.

## 3. Maintained fields remove score maintenance

**Recommendation:** build LLP 3000.007.001's narrow first scope. A count or
sum grouped by a reference to its parent is readable and indexable on that
parent, unwritable by an application program, and maintained in the source
write's transaction. Zero-contribution parents remain ordinary rows.

Proposed syntax, not a compiling example or a shipped capability:

```text
table posts:
  ...
  likeCount: count likes by postId
  by byLikes: likeCount, at, id
```

The source's rules still decide who may contribute, including uniqueness and
ownership of likes. Making a count visible through a public parent deliberately
discloses that aggregate; it does not make the source rows public. A safe
target cannot compensate for forgeable source facts.

The application should lose score-copy writes, score refresh jobs, manual
aggregate backfills and permissions added solely to maintain a score. The
engine covers source changes, imports, redefinition and parent lifecycle
through the declared derivation. The sibling RFC owns the details; generic
privileged writes are not the solution.

Keep non-expiring sources, no cascading maintained fields, and no device
prediction of the maintained column in the first scope. Distinguish predicting
a like from predicting its authoritative count. The
[watermark defect](../issues/20260917-snapback4-stream-page-serves-images-newer-than-its-watermark.md)
must be repaired and qualified before extending count prediction.

Respect the sibling's horizon and real-device activation gates. Measure bytes
per contribution and index churn on hot, large parents. Boundary-aware sync
and absolute field patches are later options already noted there; neither is
silently added to this increment.

Ruling 31's broader derived-copy note follows its false-positive census. It
offers working rewrites, remains a note, and is not proof that every surviving
derived field is safe.

## 4. Search preserves continuation through presentation

**Recommendation:** finish W13e's one-cursor search forms and remove the
superseded two-cursor helper as provided by LLP 3000.007 §20.22.7. Test online history and
held-device search separately, including coverage and freshness limits.

The additional gap is grouping a returned message page for display. Prefer
preserving its original opaque continuation through grouping proven to retain
every input row. Preserve the originating page's incompleteness and cut
information as well.

The cursor still continues **messages in source order**. A conversation may
appear in multiple pages; grouping a message page does not promise all matches
in each conversation, distinct-conversation pagination, or a snapshot across
requests. The UI can combine those group fragments by conversation id.
A distinct-conversation query uses its own indexed scan and matching existence,
with its own continuation. The two operations must not share a cursor.

Do not extend this automatically to arbitrary transformations. Sorting one
page does not establish global sorted order; filtering or truncation may lose
rows. When the binder cannot establish a supported transformation, a diagnostic
should identify the lost continuation and show a compiling rewrite that
returns the page directly and groups its data in presentation.

Acceptance includes sparse matches, empty intermediate pages, equal keys,
membership changes, edits, and horizon/meter/word cuts. Static-fixture walks
must neither skip nor duplicate matches. Concurrent changes retain the
documented live-pagination semantics. Opening cuts and non-uniform scans
retain their existing restrictions; one cursor does not mean every cut can
continue. Following a cursor must not silently clear incompleteness.

A scoped design and affected tests precede implementation; this section does
not settle a new serialized page representation.

## 5. Two ranking requirements, two contracts

| Application requirement | Recommended expression and completion |
| --- | --- |
| Posts created in the trailing day, ranked by current likes | Maintained column plus time-bucketed index and a merge over intersecting buckets. Include zero-like posts and deterministic ties without a ranking table or refresh job. |
| Posts of any age, ranked by likes received in the trailing day | The bounded live calculation remains the current path. An exact indexed server count query is a separate measured experiment. |
| Arbitrary model-computed score with acceptable lag | Keep the system-written scheduled/native scoring path, with score freshness and recovery documented. |

For recent posts, select the exact trailing interval within the intersecting
buckets, excluding future-dated imports. Buckets avoid whole old ranges, but
boundary filtering still costs work. Pin the clock within each read and
arrange live invalidation at the next relevant time boundary. A continuation
is not a snapshot: score movements and a changed bucket set retain the
documented restart/refresh behavior. Verify time advancement, ties, zero
likes, unlikes, old high-scoring rows and a crowded boundary bucket.

For the activity-window experiment, a physical stored count can upper-bound
its live count while expired contributions await sweeping. A server could
scan descending bounds, recompute live candidates, and stop only when every
remaining bound cannot beat the current top-K, including the promised tie-break.
Qualification must establish a complete authorized group universe and sound
bounds at one read snapshot. Meter exhaustion yields an incomplete or
unavailable answer, never certified global winners.

This algorithm is not qualified by the cited probes. Measure ordinary and
adversarial unswept populations against a full live oracle, before/after
partial sweeps and as time advances without writes. Signed sums have no
equivalent monotone bound. Device snapshots do not carry the server's physical
upper bounds, so this experiment makes no offline exact-ranking promise.
Produce a feasibility result before adding syntax or aggregate classes.

## 6. Background work reads the live data directly

**Recommendation:** make the period-seeking digest on the jobs card the
default, and verify that fresh builders discover it. Share the eligibility
helper with the UI where their meanings agree; derive recipients' readable
conversations from their memberships. A job's system authority does not itself
enforce each recipient's audience.

Within the documented envelope, one recipient's chunk computes the digest
from live rows and emits in that transaction. Expired-but-unswept rows,
tombstones, own messages and out-of-period messages follow the business rule.
No expiry-staging clone or cleanup mutation is needed. Use the card's actual
meter accounting, including scan attempts, lookahead and unswept candidates;
explicit per-chat bounds are not unbounded counts.

A chunk's cursor, writes and emits commit together. Resuming the same run
after a crash differs from starting a replacement run, which may revisit
already-emitted recipients. Make `(recipient, period)` the explicit business
idempotency key at the provider/worker boundary, or use the card's atomic
emission marker where appropriate. A stable key alone cannot make a
non-idempotent provider exactly once.

When a recipient exceeds the chunk envelope, use the bounded accumulation
alternative and state its restart/replacement behavior. Count that table and
its reconciliation as application machinery: an honest residual to measure,
not a reason to add staging to the ordinary case. The expiring mutation-page
refusal and LLP 3000.007 §21.9's decision stand.

Presence-dependent notifications remain ruling 32's separate contract work.
Record intent durably and let presence defer it. An authenticated re-poll after
the commit can implement a stated reconnect-suppression policy; it is not
evidence that the message was delivered or read. A delivery-sensitive policy
needs a corresponding acknowledgement and its own design. Qualify restart
and scoped wakeups: stopping unrelated polls from waking on every commit
must not invalidate the notification policy.

## 7. Sequence and acceptance

| Step | Work | Evidence needed to call this step complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Finish search/card/helper work and qualify the direct digest | Installed-package examples; ordinary search traversal; recipient eligibility, expiry and restart witnesses; discovery by fresh builders |
| B | Repair horizon coverage; run the maintained-column activation prototype | The [horizon reproduction](../issues/20260917-snapback4-device-page-past-horizon-answers-from-scattered-rows.md) is repaired on both stores, and the sibling's gates pass, including actual HTTP adoption |
| C | Implement maintained columns and recent-post ranking | Correct values through source writes and adoption; no application score writes or maintenance; measured stream/storage cost |
| D | Resolve grouped-page continuation and presence policy in their owning lanes | Grouping retains position and uncertainty; unsupported transforms have working rewrites; durable notification outcomes under disconnect/restart and scoped wakes |
| E | Run the builder comparison and one subsequent application change | The metrics and lifecycle assertions below, retaining failures and unrun cases |
| F | Evaluate activity-window count ranking | Oracle agreement when complete, bounded work and honest exhaustion; a feasibility record before product expansion |

These are dependencies and scoped completion records, not extra push gates
or duplicate lanes. A and existing correctness work can progress independently.
F does not block the simpler shapes. Predicting maintained fields on devices
remains a later increment.

## 8. Measure application work and the next change

Reuse the [bounds bar](../snapback4/loopbar/BOUNDS.md) and its four tasks:
global search grouped by chat, Following, roster/unread totals and Trending.
Add the digest as a fifth task in that instrument. Keep Following as a control
and an explicit residual; this proposal does not presume its delivery
architecture disappears. No new harness or registry is needed.

Use at least three fresh builders per compared package pin, the same model
configuration or balanced mix, logical fixture, brief and instrument. Builders
get only the installed package and ordinary discovery surfaces, without a
briefing that dictates the desired design. Record host load, installation
problems, timeouts and invalid trials. The existing four valid 0.2.27 before-runs
remain evidence; rerun the baseline where an instrument change or added digest
prevents a matched comparison. Historical single-builder holistic scores are
not this experiment's result.

| Measure | Proposed target for the ordinary documented envelope |
| --- | --- |
| Custom membership/result cursor coordination | Zero |
| Application score-copy writes and refresh jobs for declared counts/sums | Zero |
| Broad write permissions added to maintain a derived field | Zero, checking actual write paths as well as rules |
| Digest staging/cleanup machinery | Zero; larger-envelope accumulation reported separately |
| Manual aggregate backfill and repair code | Zero for engine-owned derivations |
| Tables, jobs, copied/generated recipe code, chosen numeric bounds, cards consulted and correction loops | Report before/after per task; disclose unchanged or increased responsibilities |
| Requests, examined/index work, cold rows/bytes, retained rows, stream bytes and storage growth | Report beside ergonomic gains on the same workload |

Copied or generated recipes still count when the application owns their
tables, policy choices and recovery lifecycle. A shorter wrapper does not
prove those obligations disappeared. Separate product rules, UI,
fixture/verification code and substrate work; retain source ranges for
inspection. Do not reduce reported code by moving it into the harness or
dropping required behavior.

After each successful build, make one assigned ordinary change: exclude
soft-deleted posts from a count, change the ranking window, or alter digest
eligibility. Use a pre-existing device with queued writes, disconnect/reopen
it, deploy, and reconnect. Record migration/adoption work, correction loops
and manual reconciliation. The
[program-only retention defect](../issues/20260917-snapback4-program-only-generation-drops-retained-history.md)
blocks any affected lifecycle assertion; a fresh replica cannot substitute.

Require oracle agreement and authorization failures where applicable.
Record incomplete reads and their UI treatment rather than counting any
returned top ten as correct. A skipped native restart is unrun; an application
work reduction with higher replication cost reports both. Report per-run
results, medians and ranges. This small sample establishes practical builder
evidence, not statistical superiority over another backend.

Use Workroom's votes/counts, search and invitation/reminder journeys as the
next generality check within its existing brief. Do not force a chat-specific
recipe onto it to improve these counters.

## 9. Decisions still open

1. The smallest supported grouped-page form and its representation. Prefer
   preserving the source message cursor for proven grouping, with a
   diagnostic/rewrite for unsupported transformations.
2. Whether the activity-window threshold experiment earns a product surface;
   its result cannot justify signed-sum or offline claims.
3. Whether measured digest rerun bookkeeping warrants a first-class keyed
   emission form. First assess existing worker-key/marker paths; this
   section introduces no keyword.
4. The precise presence suppression policy under scoped wakeups. Reconnect
   evidence and delivery acknowledgement remain distinct.
5. Whether whole-parent count-update costs justify the sibling's later
   stream optimizations.

No implementation, model review, publication, new ceiling, generic view
maintenance, jobs framework or client vocabulary is commissioned by this
Draft. Existing rulings retain their authority. Report completion per step;
compiling examples or a higher holistic score alone do not complete this work.
