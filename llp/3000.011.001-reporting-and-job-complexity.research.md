# LLP 3000.011.001: Reporting and jobs require too much application machinery

**Type:** Research
**Status:** Draft
**Systems:** Snapback 4 aggregates, reporting, scheduled jobs, chunk progress, resource meters
**Author:** Codex / GPT-6 (for Charlie Cheever)
**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Revised:** 2026-09-22 (initial findings)
**Related:** [Parent](./3000.011-recent-builder-friction.research.md), [Improvement proposal](./3000.011.001.000-digests-beyond-one-chunk.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.007.002](./3000.007.002-common-application-shapes.rfc.md), [LLP 3000.002](./3000.002-end-to-end-resource-bounds.research.md)

## 1. Problem

Maintained lifetime counts are compact. Reporting that combines time windows,
membership, unread state and retry-safe external work requires more design:
indexes, bounded sweeps, intermediate totals, explicit progress and emission
deduplication. A small successful application test does not establish that the
chosen shape fits the busy-chat workload.

## 2. Evidence

The [0.2.30 M8 diary](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-18-snapback4-clique-astra-0.2.30/astra/lane-diary.md)
describes a digest as the largest change in implementation shape from a
conventional SQL service: multiple bounded sweeps with atomic chunk progress.
It also records a separate ranking table and backfill for exact trending ties.

The [0.2.31 M8 follow-through](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-20-snapback4-clique-astra-0.2.31/astra/lane-diary.md)
found a per-chat count exceeding the 16 MiB heap meter after the initial UI
proof was green. Narrowing the projection did not fix it. The builder rewrote
the algorithm into period-message chunks, per-message admission markers and
per-chat totals. The [run record](../benchmarks/runs/2026-09-20-snapback4-clique-astra-0.2.31/README.md)
records 33,949 chunks and 580,996 writes for the completed replacement, followed
by a zero-write rerun. These are that application's measured costs, not a
lower bound on what every digest must cost.

The builder attributed the bound failure to its design against a documented
limit. The refusal was useful. The residual product cost is needing to invent
and validate the replacement machinery after the simpler implementation
seemed finished.

## 3. Current surface and existing owners

The [jobs card](../snapback4/crates/snapback4-lang/src/guide/jobs.rs) already
offers a direct period-seeking digest, a membership-paged alternative, durable
chunk cursors and explicit guidance that a busier individual chat also needs
message paging. Its simpler recipe has a measured envelope; it is not a
promise that `count` can traverse arbitrary history.

[LLP 3000.007.002 §6](./3000.007.002-common-application-shapes.rfc.md#6-background-work-reads-the-live-data-directly)
already recommends that direct recipe and recognizes accumulation beyond its
envelope. The new residual is a complete, reusable path for the busy single
chat, including replacement-run semantics. This family should improve that
path rather than introduce staging to every ordinary digest.

Ranking ownership stays with the maintained-column and common-shapes RFCs.
Nothing in the latest diary proves those designs have shipped. A new digest
proposal is not a reason to create another aggregate model or reopen the
refusal of expiring pages in ordinary mutations.

## 4. Interpretation and confidence

The sharp edge is crossing from one atomic read-and-emit operation into a
multi-transaction computation. Resuming one job, starting a replacement job,
and retrying an external provider call have different identities and effects.
The current primitives make them possible but leave their composition to the
application. System-principal jobs also require explicit recipient authority.

Confidence is high in the observed failure and rewrite. There is no profile
here establishing whether streaming aggregation, a different index or another
algorithm would outperform the replacement. Increasing a heap ceiling would
move the failure point and would not remove these lifecycle responsibilities.

## 5. Desired outcome

Builders should choose the documented direct form within its envelope, then
adopt one complete message-paged form when the workload exceeds it. They
should supply business eligibility and effect payloads without inventing
cursor, retry and emission bookkeeping from scratch.

The [child RFC](./3000.011.001.000-digests-beyond-one-chunk.rfc.md) proposes
that bounded recipe and the static/concurrent semantics it must disclose.
It must be measured against both the simple direct recipe and the latest
builder's replacement; merely generating the same expensive program is only
an authoring improvement, not a runtime improvement.
