## Summary

Waves 1 and 2 ([LLP 0329.014.001](./0329.014.001-paper-archetypes.research.md),
[LLP 0329.014.002](./0329.014.002-paper-archetypes-wave-2.research.md)) wrote
fourteen applications in the v0 language and found the same thing each time: the
language's core carries the operations, and the residue is a *dimension of the
model* — thirteen so far — rather than a missing operator; one archetype
(semantic search) forced a second **read-site** class, two ended at declared
boundary kinds, none needed a second query class.

Wave 3 stretches along axes none of the fourteen exercised: **fan-out scale**
(one hot range with 100k live subscribers; public quotes to a million watchers),
**derived aggregates with unbounded fan-in** (one player's score in 100k lineups),
**standing queries as product objects** (saved searches that alert),
**symmetric two-party secrets** (a match exists only when both swiped),
**pre-identity and external principals** (invite a phone number; a guest from
another organization), **per-principal data volume** (a 100k-photo library on a
device), **schedules at population scale** (millions of reminders), **order-book
matching as a program**, **live public values derived from private inputs**
(current price from secret proxy maxes), and **write-rate limits as
constraints**.

Method as before: one family designs each application deep with no Snapback
vocabulary (brief: `llp/reviews/0329.014.002-wave-2.brief.design.md`); a
different family writes it in v0.1 and scores it with the spec's §11 rubric
(brief: `llp/reviews/0329.014.001-paper-archetypes.brief.md`). Predictions are
recorded below **before any design existed**.

## Pre-registered predictions (orchestrator, 2026-08-29 02:05 PDT, before any design existed)

1. **Live-stream chat.** No new language. The per-subscriber rerun model fails
   at 100k subscribers on one range (one message → 100k re-executions). The
   trace model already implies the fix — identical rules yield identical
   receipts — but the spec never states it: a **uniform-audience subscription
   class** (one evaluation, N deliveries; "broadcast") chosen by the engine when
   the rule is tautologous or hoisted for the audience. Slow mode and spam
   limits are a new constraint family, **rate limits** (`rateLimit({ by:
   viewer, per, max })`) evaluated at the write. Mod/VIP/sub roles are role
   rows in rules; sub-only emotes are entitlement disclosure; chat retention
   after a ban is the retention dimension. VOD chat replay is a query over
   `commitSeq`.
2. **Dating.** The verdict mechanism at scale: every card is a rule over a
   hidden input (the other side's swipe). A match exists iff both swiped —
   a rule reading one visible and one hidden row; its *existence* discloses
   the other's swipe by product intent. "Who liked you" is a declared
   entitlement disclosure. Block is the retention dimension sharpened:
   `purge-on-block` overrides keep-delivered-history on *both* devices.
   Proximity is a geohash **prefix scan** — range-decomposable, so *not* a
   second read-site class (the contrast with vectors is the point).
   Recommendations are an effect-materialized queue.
3. **Events with roles.** Field grants (`immutableExcept` by role) carry the
   load; the new construct is **pre-identity principals**: an invitation
   addressed to a phone number is a bearer capability whose later claim binds
   it to an identity — the identity kind gains `claim`/`merge`. Guest-list
   disclosure ("12 going" vs names) is K2 as a product choice; capacity and
   waitlist are booking's shapes.
4. **Real estate.** The invalidation engine as a product feature: a saved
   search is a **standing query** — a durable `(query, args, notify: effect)`
   object the server re-evaluates on writes. At a million saved searches this
   is the fan-out problem reversed (a reverse index of predicates), so the
   honest v0 form is a batch **job** over "listings since watermark" per
   search cohort, and a `watch` kind is the candidate addition. Faceted search
   is many indexes or the ranked-index site; MLS import is a job; price
   history is `appendOnly` versions.
5. **Open auction.** Per-item serialization is natural on a single writer; the
   new thing is a **live public value derived from private inputs** — current
   price = second-highest secret max + increment — which is a maintained
   top-2 over hidden rows with a declared `readable: public` (K2's admit
   route, continuous). Unlike the sealed auction, `maintain` is the right
   form here because the disclosure is live by design. Outbid alerts are the
   commit-triggered outbox; Buy-It-Now vs bid is OCC on the item row.
6. **Prediction market.** Matching is a bounded program: scan the best
   opposing orders `take K`, fill, rest the remainder — atomic, bound `K`; the
   product caps fills per order (`E_FANOUT` otherwise, queued remainder).
   Resolution is a **job** over all positions; best bid/ask is a maintained
   top-of-book with declared disclosure; position limits are sum-caps; PnL is
   maintained sums.
7. **Daily fantasy.** The maintain-versus-compute rule becomes explicit: a
   player's score fans into an unbounded number of lineups, so `maintain.sum`
   is refused at `E_FANOUT`; a lineup's score is **computed at read** (nine
   point reads — bounded and cheap); the contest leaderboard is a rank over
   *computed* values, which no maintained structure can hold → a periodic
   **derived-rank snapshot job**. Lock is a schedule-flipped state; payouts
   are a job.
8. **Brokerage.** Quotes to a million watchers are the broadcast class again,
   with a durable last-value snapshot; portfolio valuation is client-side pure
   computation over positions × quotes (the server maintains nothing);
   PDT is a rolling-window count over per-day maintained buckets (five point
   reads — no new construct); corporate actions are a versioned **job**; tax
   lots are ordered computation over a bounded page; execution is a saga.
9. **Photos.** The closure model meets a viewer's *own* 100k rows: paged
   receipts work, but local search over the whole library needs an
   **owner-partition retention policy** (`retain: 'all-owned'` — the retention
   dimension's third form). Assets at scale are the asset kind; "all photos of
   X" is a rule over an effect-derived label table (fine); dedup is a hashed
   unique; quotas are sum-caps; trash is a schedule.
10. **Calendar.** Three things: the **calendar seam** (zoned recurrence) is the
    largest gap, as booking found; free/busy is a **declared existence
    disclosure** (an `exists` over hidden rows with `readable`) — K2's boolean
    cousin; **external principals** (a guest from another organization) extend
    the identity kind. Reminders at population scale are fine iff the
    schedule kind is a durable timer *index*, not a scan; detail-level
    sharing is the column-audience split.

11. **Ride-hailing** (added 02:10 at the founder's request). No new language:
    nearest-driver dispatch is a geohash **prefix scan** (range-decomposable)
    plus a **system continuation** that makes one timed exclusive offer at a
    time (exclusive-open constraint + schedule with reschedule); positions are a
    broadcast channel with a durable last-known snapshot; surge is a public
    value derived from per-cell supply/demand **maintained counts** with a
    declared disclosure; the trip is an event-coupled state machine; fares are
    a saga. The stress is schedule volume (every offer is a timer).
12. **Food delivery.** The three-sided order is one row with three audiences
    and three writers: expect `E_SECRECY_MIX` splits and role-scoped field
    grants to carry it; batching is system work over a bounded candidate page;
    restaurant "busy"/prep-time is time-as-state; tips and refunds are ledger
    shapes; live tracking is a channel. No new dimension.
13. **Fast-casual ordering.** Group carts reuse the editor's **merge modes**
    (`append` lines, per-line `lww`); an 86'd item propagating into open carts
    is a live query joining cart lines to availability — the reverse
    dependency the invalidation engine already provides, *not* a standing query;
    slot capacity is booking's partial `cap`; kitchen queues are `alloc` +
    exclusive claim; POS is the outbox. No new dimension.
14. **Tournaments.** The "weird shape" is rows: a bracket is a fixed-shape DAG
    of match rows with computed slot positions, generated atomically as a
    bounded fan-out write (≤ 511 rows for 512 players under a `cap`); advancing
    a winner is an event-coupled transition writing the next slot; Swiss
    pairing per round is a bounded algorithm over ≤ N participants that will
    press on **`maxSteps`** (O(N²) pairing) and may need a system job; seeding
    is ranked selection under system visibility; `tree` is the *wrong*
    primitive here. Prize pools are ledger + job.
15. **User-defined databases.** The largest stretch of the wave. Under
    "schema-closed", user tables must be **EAV over fixed tables** with typed
    value columns and a generic `(propertyId, value)` index; that is
    expressible, but filters and formulas become an *interpreter inside a
    program* over json definitions (bounded by `maxSteps`, keys `computed`,
    prediction mostly `partial`), rules over EAV lean on the page ACL via
    `nearest`, and user-defined **rollups** have no route at all — `maintain`
    declarations are static. Prediction: the honest design needs a new
    dimension, **schema as data** — runtime-declared tables, indexes, and
    maintained aggregates with per-tenant activation and per-user-schema
    certificates — or an explicit product boundary ("rollups compute at read
    over relation pages ≤ N").

Cross-cutting: no archetype needs a second query class or a new read-site
class beyond the ranked-index one; two new **runtime** dimensions
(subscription fan-out class; standing queries), one new **constraint** family
(rate limits), three extensions of existing kinds (identity: claim/merge/
external; retention: owner partitions and purge-on-block; jobs: derived-rank
snapshots), and one new *rule* of the design — maintain when fan-in is bounded,
compute at read when fan-out is unbounded.

### What seven of fifteen already establish

- **The cross-cutting prediction holds so far.** No second query class; the
  ranked-index site gains a geo/knn sibling (the same class, a second
  instance); boundary kinds are the broadcast projection (Twitch, brokerage),
  `replica` (photos), and jobs everywhere.
- **The second axis of "a fact escaping its scope" is completeness, not only
  visibility.** Real estate, fantasy, and photos each found ruling 8 admitting a
  page reduction returned as a population fact (a facet `total`, `rank = i+1`,
  a storage meter) *even under a public or tautologous rule*. Scope =
  visibility × completeness; `E_STRUCTURAL_*` must refuse both escapes.
- **The intent clock is the most-repeated wrong answer in the family** — five
  applications (payments, game, realtime, fantasy, DoorDash): an offer, a
  dispute window, a lock, a bid. "Judged when received" is server acceptance
  time; expiries are schedule-flipped state; a lock is an OCC'd row.
- **Maintained *rows*, not only counts and sums.** Derived membership (idle
  drivers per cell), relationship projections (following feeds), current-
  snapshot pointers (listings), order statistics (rank), ordered reducers
  (queues): five reports ask for `maintain.rows` / `maintain.prefixCount` /
  pointer maintenance with an amplification certificate. `maintain` is a
  family, not two functions.
- **Projections with a declared audience** are the third answer to
  multi-audience rows, after "split by protection class" (health, realtime):
  when one entity genuinely has three views (an order), engine-maintained
  projections generalize the split.
- **`callable: 'system'` is not optional**: DoorDash alone had eleven
  client-callable holes; real estate adds a *per-mutation read view* so that
  "system is total" is not the only alternative.

### What thirteen of fifteen establish (tournaments and eBay pending)

The cross-cutting prediction held with one correction: **no second query
class** in fifteen more applications; the ranked-index site gained a geo/knn
sibling (same class, second instance — Uber, real estate, dating); the boundary
kinds are the **broadcast projection** (Twitch, brokerage, prediction market),
**`replica`** (photos), and **jobs** (everywhere). The one archetype
pre-registered as most likely to force a new dimension — schema as data — did
not: Notion reduced to EAV over fixed tables, an interpreter inside a program,
scope-split maintained aggregates, and a product boundary on runtime indexes.

What wave 3 adds to the model is not a fourteenth dimension so much as the
*second axis* of the first one and the *families* behind several others:

- **Scope = visibility × completeness.** Three applications (real estate,
  fantasy, photos) found ruling 8 admitting a page reduction returned as a
  population fact under a public or tautologous rule — a facet total,
  `rank = i + 1`, a storage meter. The structural-fact family refuses both
  escapes; Notion's `Visible<T>` (reductions only over *complete* domains) is
  its typed form.
- **Cardinality oracles.** Ruling 2 was hit a third time (dating, after health
  and editor): `hasMore` and page length count hidden rows. `hasMore` becomes a
  receipt-only extent; `E_CARDINALITY_ORACLE`; calendar adds `E_HIDDEN_PREFIX`
  (a scan whose rule hoists to false is a sealed refusal, never an empty page).
- **The intent clock, six times.** Offers, disputes, locks, bids, RSVP windows:
  "judged when received" is server acceptance time; expiries are schedule-
  flipped state; a lock is an OCC'd row; and the invocation-policy set
  (`online-only`, `maxReplayAgeMs`, `predict: false`, `queueAllowed`) is a
  legitimate per-mutation declaration because it refuses, never degrades.
- **`maintain` is a family.** Rows (derived membership, relationship
  projections, current-snapshot pointers), order statistics (`prefixCount`,
  rank), ordered reducers, interval coverage (free/busy), sharded sums with a
  disclosed root, decrease-only floors — with an amplification certificate and
  a declared audience each. Count and sum were the first two members.
- **Principals are sets and kinds.** Pre-identity contacts, link holders,
  anonymous devices, hosts, effect/webhook ingress, API scopes: rules need a
  bounded principal closure (`existsAny(index, { principalIn: viewer.bindings })`),
  site-conditioned rules (`read: 'point'`), and an identity kind with
  claim/merge/epoch.
- **Projections with a declared audience** (DoorDash) generalize the
  protection-class split for entities that genuinely have several views;
  inherited columns along a `ref` (Notion) generalize `tree.inherit`.
- **Time arithmetic is a value-model builtin**, versioned by a pinned
  `tzdbVersion` (calendar adjudicates booking, DoorDash, real estate).
- **Retention needs per-table leases** (`retain({ onDeny: 'purge', offlineLease })`)
  shorter than the store-wide fence (dating), and a `replica` declaration for
  owner partitions (photos).
- **Sequencing is declarable** (`serialBy(key)` with an engine-issued acceptance
  sequence — prediction market) and **standing queries** are maintained
  predicate posting lists behind a `commitSeq` barrier (real estate), not a
  `watch` kind.
- **Certificates gain negative and flow claims**: `independentOf(table)`
  (dating), taint checking that hidden inputs cannot escape through fields,
  codes, or failures (brokerage, game).

Writing the designs in a strict language again found bugs in the designs
themselves — in thirteen of thirteen this wave (reversed signs, self-defeating
promises, unowned re-resolution, leaking pass operations, contradicting
atomicity claims).
